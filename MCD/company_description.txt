MCD is the stock ticker for McDonald's Corporation, the world's leading global foodservice retailer. The company operates and franchises an extensive system of fast-food restaurants, primarily serving a menu of hamburgers, chicken products, breakfast items, and beverages. Founded in 1940 by Richard and Maurice McDonald as a small restaurant in San Bernardino, California, the business was revolutionized in 1955 when Ray Kroc joined as a franchise agent and subsequently purchased the company. Under Kroc’s leadership, McDonald's pioneered standardized, high-volume production methods and rapid global expansion. Today, the corporation generates revenue through a combination of company-operated restaurant sales and franchise fees, royalties, and rent payments. Headquartered in Chicago, Illinois, McDonald's serves tens of millions of customers daily across more than 100 countries, making its iconic golden arches and signature items, such as the Big Mac, enduring symbols of American culture and global commerce.
