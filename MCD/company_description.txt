MCD is the stock ticker for McDonald's Corporation, the world's leading global foodservice retailer. Founded in 1940 by Richard and Maurice McDonald as a barbecue restaurant in San Bernardino, California, the enterprise transformed following its acquisition by Ray Kroc in 1961. Kroc introduced the franchise model that fueled its international expansion and standardized operations. Today, McDonald's primarily operates through a business model built on franchising, real estate leasing, and company-owned restaurant sales. The company generates revenue through a combination of franchisee fees, royalties, and direct sales of its globally recognized menu items, anchored by core products like the Big Mac and World Famous Fries. Operating in over one hundred countries, McDonald's functions as both a dominant restaurant chain and a major real estate holder, owning the underlying land and buildings for a significant portion of its franchised locations.
