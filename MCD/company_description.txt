The company identified by the stock ticker MCD is McDonald's Corporation, the world's largest chain of hamburger fast-food restaurants. Founded in 1940 by Richard and Maurice McDonald as a barbecue restaurant in San Bernardino, California, the business underwent a major operational transformation in 1948 by introducing its "Speedee Service System," establishing the modern template for the fast-food industry. In 1955, businessman Ray Kroc joined the company as a franchise agent and eventually purchased the enterprise, turning it into a global powerhouse. McDonald's primarily operates through a franchising business model, generating revenue from rent, royalties, and fees paid by independent franchisees, as well as sales from company-operated restaurants. Today, the corporation serves tens of millions of customers daily across more than one hundred countries.
