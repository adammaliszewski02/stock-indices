MCD is the stock ticker for McDonald's Corporation, the world's leading global foodservice retailer. Founded in 1940 by Richard and Maurice McDonald as a barbecue restaurant in San Bernardino, California, the company revolutionized the industry in 1948 by introducing the Speedee Service System, establishing the foundation for the modern fast-food model. Ray Kroc joined the company as a franchise agent in 1955 and subsequently purchased the business, spearheading its global expansion. Today, McDonald's operates and franchises a vast network of restaurants serving millions of customers daily across over one hundred countries. The company generates revenue primarily through a combination of company-operated restaurant sales and franchise fees, rent, and royalties paid by independent franchisees. Its business model heavily emphasizes real estate holdings, owning the land and buildings for a significant portion of its restaurant locations. McDonald's is renowned for its standardized menu offerings, anchored by iconic products such as the Big Mac and World Famous Fries, and remains a dominant force in the quick-service restaurant sector.
