MCD is the stock ticker for McDonald's Corporation, the world's leading global foodservice retailer. Founded in 1940 as a restaurant operated by brothers Richard and Maurice McDonald in San Bernardino, California, the company revolutionized the industry in 1948 by introducing the Speedee Service System, laying the groundwork for the modern fast-food business model. Businessman Ray Kroc joined the company as a franchise agent in 1955 and subsequently purchased the enterprise, spearheading its global expansion. Today, McDonald's operates and franchises a vast network of restaurants serving millions of customers daily across more than 100 countries. The company generates revenue through a combination of company-operated restaurant sales and franchise fees, royalties, and rent payments. Renowned for its iconic menu items, including the Big Mac and World Famous Fries, McDonald's has evolved into one of the most recognizable and valuable brands in the world, continuously adapting its menu, technology, and real estate strategy to maintain its market dominance.
