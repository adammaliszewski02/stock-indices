MCD is the stock ticker for McDonald's Corporation, the world's leading global foodservice retailer. The company operates and franchises an extensive system of quick-service restaurants serving a localized menu of value-priced food and beverages. Founded in 1940 by Richard and Maurice McDonald as a barbecue restaurant in San Bernardino, California, the business was revolutionized in 1955 by Ray Kroc, who purchased the exclusive rights to the McDonald's name and opened the first franchised location in Des Plaines, Illinois. McDonald's pioneered the modern fast-food system, emphasizing standardized operations, speed, and affordability. Today, the corporation generates revenue through a combination of company-operated restaurant sales and franchise fees, rent, and royalties paid by independent operators. With tens of thousands of locations operating across over one hundred countries, McDonald's has evolved into a ubiquitous cultural icon and a dominant force in the global hospitality industry.
