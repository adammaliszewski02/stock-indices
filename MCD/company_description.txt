MCD is the stock ticker for McDonald's Corporation, the world's leading global foodservice retailer. Founded in 1940 as a restaurant operated by brothers Richard and Maurice McDonald in San Bernardino, California, the enterprise revolutionized the industry in 1948 by introducing the Speedee Service System, establishing the foundations for the modern fast-food business model. Businessman Ray Kroc joined the company as a franchise agent in 1955 and subsequently purchased the chain, driving its monumental global expansion. Today, McDonald's generates revenue through a combination of company-operated restaurants and, predominantly, franchise fees, royalties, and property leases paid by independent franchisees. The company operates a vast international footprint, serving millions of customers daily across more than 100 countries with a standardized menu anchored by signature offerings such as the Big Mac and World Famous Fries.
