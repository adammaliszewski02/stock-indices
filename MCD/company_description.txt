MCD is the stock ticker for McDonald's Corporation, the world's leading global foodservice retailer. Founded in 1940 as a restaurant operated by brothers Richard and Maurice McDonald in San Bernardino, California, the company revolutionized the industry in 1948 by introducing the Speedee Service System, laying the operational groundwork for the modern fast-food model. Ray Kroc joined the company as a franchise agent in 1955 and subsequently purchased the business, steering its explosive global expansion. Today, McDonald’s operates and franchises a vast network of restaurants serving millions of customers daily across more than 100 countries. The company generates revenue primarily through a combination of company-operated restaurant sales and franchise fees, which include rent and royalties paid by independent franchisees. Its business model heavily emphasizes real estate ownership, as the corporation often owns the land and buildings where franchised locations operate.
