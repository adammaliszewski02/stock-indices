Alphabet Inc., trading under the ticker GOOGL for its Class A common stock, is a global technology conglomerate and the parent company of Google. Founded in 1998 by Larry Page and Sergey Brin as an internet search engine, Google reorganized in 2015 to establish Alphabet, separating its core internet services from higher-risk, long-term ventures. 

The company generates the vast majority of its revenue from digital advertising through Google Services, which encompasses Google Search, YouTube, and the Google Network. This segment also includes hardware products, the Android operating system, the Chrome browser, and Google Play. Beyond advertising, Alphabet has expanded significantly into enterprise software and infrastructure through Google Cloud, offering computing power, data analytics, and artificial intelligence solutions. 

Additionally, Alphabet operates an "Other Bets" segment, which incubates emerging technologies in diverse industries, such as autonomous vehicles through Waymo and healthcare via Verily. Alphabet remains one of the world's most valuable companies, heavily focused on advancing artificial intelligence across its consumer and enterprise ecosystems.
