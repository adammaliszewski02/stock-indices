Alphabet Inc., trading under the ticker GOOGL, is a multinational technology conglomerate established as part of a corporate restructuring of Google on October 2, 2015. Google itself was founded on September 4, 1998, by Larry Page and Sergey Brin while they were Ph.D. students at Stanford University. Alphabet operates as a holding company for Google and several former Google subsidiaries, with Google serving as its largest subsidiary and core revenue generator. 

The company's primary business revolves around internet-related services and products. This encompasses the dominant Google Search engine, online advertising technologies, cloud computing via Google Cloud, software including the Android operating system and Chrome browser, and hardware such as Pixel devices. Additionally, Alphabet houses "Other Bets," a division comprising forward-looking and exploratory ventures in sectors like biotechnology (Verily), autonomous driving (Waymo), and deep artificial intelligence research (Google DeepMind). Through continuous innovation in artificial intelligence, digital advertising, and enterprise infrastructure, Alphabet remains a foundational pillar of the global digital economy.
