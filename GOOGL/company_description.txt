GOOGL is the ticker symbol for Alphabet Inc., a multinational technology conglomerate founded in October 2015 as the parent company of Google and several former Google subsidiaries. Google itself originated as a search engine research project in 1996 by Stanford University doctoral students Larry Page and Sergey Brin, officially incorporating as a company in September 1998. Today, Alphabet operates through Google Services, Google Cloud, and Other Bets. The core Google Services segment encompasses dominant digital advertising products, Android, Chrome, Google Maps, and YouTube. Google Cloud provides enterprise-grade infrastructure and data analytics platforms to global businesses. The Other Bets segment houses pioneering ventures in emerging industries, such as autonomous driving technology via Waymo and life sciences research through Verily. Alphabet generates the vast majority of its revenue through online advertising, hardware sales, cloud computing services, and digital subscriptions, establishing itself as one of the world's most influential technology enterprises.
