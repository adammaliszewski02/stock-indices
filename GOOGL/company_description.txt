GOOGL is the ticker symbol for Alphabet Inc., a multinational technology conglomerate founded in October 2015 as the corporate parent of Google and several former Google subsidiaries. Google itself originated as a search engine developed in 1998 by Stanford University Ph.D. students Larry Page and Sergey Brin. Alphabet's primary business encompasses internet-related products and services, including online advertising technologies, search engines, cloud computing, software, and hardware. Key operations include the Google search platform, the Android mobile operating system, YouTube, and Google Cloud. Additionally, Alphabet invests in high-risk, high-reward ventures through its "Other Bets" division, which includes autonomous driving technology developer Waymo and life sciences company Verily. Alphabet generates the vast majority of its revenue through digital advertising via Google properties and the Google Network. Shares of Alphabet are divided into multiple classes, with GOOGL representing Class A common stock, which carries voting rights of one vote per share.
