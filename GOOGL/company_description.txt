GOOGL is the ticker symbol for Alphabet Inc., a multinational technology conglomerate founded in 2015 as the parent company of Google and several former Google subsidiaries. Google itself originated in 1998, founded by Larry Page and Sergey Brin while they were Ph.D. students at Stanford University, initially launching as a revolutionary web search engine. Over the decades, Alphabet has expanded far beyond its foundational search and advertising business. Today, the company's diverse operations encompass cloud computing through Google Cloud, hardware development, operating systems like Android, and artificial intelligence research via DeepMind. Additionally, Alphabet manages its "Other Bets" portfolio, which includes pioneering ventures in autonomous driving with Waymo and life sciences with Verily. Alphabet generates the vast majority of its revenue through digital advertising, while continuing to position itself as a global leader in artificial intelligence, consumer software, and internet infrastructure.
