GOOGL is the ticker symbol for Alphabet Inc., a multinational technology conglomerate founded in October 2015 as the corporate successor to Google Inc., which was originally established in September 1998 by Larry Page and Sergey Brin. Alphabet primarily operates through its core subsidiary, Google, generating the vast majority of its revenue from online advertising via search, YouTube, and the Google Network. Beyond advertising, the company provides cloud computing services through Google Cloud, develops the Android mobile operating system, manufactures consumer hardware, and manages a diverse portfolio of "Other Bets," which includes ventures in autonomous driving through Waymo and life sciences through Verily. Alphabet maintains a dominant global presence in internet-related services and artificial intelligence research, continually investing in cutting-edge technological infrastructure to sustain its market leadership.
