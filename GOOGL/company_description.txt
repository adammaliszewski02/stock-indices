Alphabet Inc., trading under the ticker GOOGL, is a multinational technology conglomerate founded in October 2015 as the corporate parent of Google and several former Google subsidiaries. Google itself originated as a search engine developed in 1998 by Stanford University Ph.D. students Larry Page and Sergey Brin, eventually going public via an initial public offering in August 2004. Today, Alphabet operates as one of the world's most prominent technology companies, deriving the vast majority of its revenue from digital advertising through Google Search, YouTube, and the Google Network. Beyond advertising, Alphabet’s business encompasses a diverse portfolio of products and services, including cloud computing via Google Cloud, the Android mobile operating system, consumer hardware, and software applications. The company also invests in high-risk, long-term ventures known as "Other Bets," which include autonomous driving technology developed by Waymo and life sciences research through Verily. Alphabet maintains a dominant global presence in internet-related services, artificial intelligence development, and digital infrastructure, continually expanding its technological ecosystem to serve billions of individual and enterprise users worldwide.
