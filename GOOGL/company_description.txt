Alphabet Inc., trading under the ticker GOOGL for its Class A common stock, is a multinational technology conglomerate and the parent company of Google. Founded in 1998 by Larry Page and Sergey Brin while at Stanford University, Google originated as an innovative internet search engine. In October 2015, the company restructured, establishing Alphabet as the parent holding company to separate its core internet operations from ambitious, frontier ventures. 

Alphabet’s primary revenue driver is its Google Services segment, which dominates the digital advertising landscape through Google Search, YouTube, and Google Network. This division also encompasses the Android operating system, digital content sales through Google Play, and hardware products such as Pixel smartphones and Nest smart-home devices. 

Beyond consumer services, Alphabet is a major enterprise technology provider through Google Cloud, offering scalable cloud computing infrastructure, data analytics, and collaboration tools like Google Workspace. The company also invests heavily in artificial intelligence and speculative, early-stage businesses known as "Other Bets," which focus on revolutionary fields such as autonomous vehicle development via Waymo and life sciences through Verily.
