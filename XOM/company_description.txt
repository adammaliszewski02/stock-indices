Exxon Mobil Corporation (ticker: XOM) is a premier American multinational energy corporation and one of the world's largest publicly traded integrated oil and gas companies. Headquartered in Spring, Texas, the company is engaged in the exploration, production, transportation, and sale of crude oil and natural gas, as well as the manufacture and marketing of petrochemicals, plastics, and specialized petroleum products. 

The company traces its origins to John D. Rockefeller’s Standard Oil trust, founded in 1870, which later became the Standard Oil Company of New Jersey (later known as Exxon) and the Standard Oil Company of New York (later known as Mobil). These two entities merged in 1999 to form ExxonMobil. Today, XOM leverages its vast global infrastructure and technological expertise to meet current global energy demands while strategically investing in lower-emissions initiatives, including carbon capture and storage, hydrogen, and advanced biofuels, to navigate the evolving energy landscape.
