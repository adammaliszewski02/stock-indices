Exxon Mobil Corporation, trading under the ticker XOM, is a premier American multinational energy corporation and one of the largest publicly traded companies in the world. The company operates across the entire energy spectrum, engaging in the exploration, production, transportation, and sale of crude oil and natural gas. Additionally, ExxonMobil manufactures and markets commodity petrochemicals, plastics, and specialized petroleum products, while increasingly investing in lower-emission technologies such as carbon capture and biofuels. 

The company traces its origins to John D. Rockefeller’s Standard Oil trust, established in 1870. Following the landmark 1911 antitrust dissolution of Standard Oil, the business separated into several regional entities, most notably the Standard Oil Company of New Jersey (which later adopted the brand name Esso and eventually became Exxon) and the Standard Oil Company of New York (which became Mobil). The modern enterprise took shape in November 1999, when Exxon and Mobil completed a historic $81 billion merger, creating the integrated energy powerhouse known today. Headquartered in Spring, Texas, ExxonMobil continues to play a vital role in global energy markets, balancing traditional hydrocarbon operations with strategic transitions toward a lower-carbon energy future.
