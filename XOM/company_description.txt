Exxon Mobil Corporation, trading under the ticker XOM, is a premier American multinational energy corporation and one of the largest publicly traded companies in the world. Headquartered in Spring, Texas, the company operates across the entire energy spectrum, engaging in the exploration, production, transportation, and sale of crude oil and natural gas. Additionally, ExxonMobil manufactures and markets commodity petrochemicals, specialty products, and power generation solutions. 

The company’s corporate lineage traces back to John D. Rockefeller's Standard Oil trust, founded in 1870. Following the landmark 1911 antitrust dissolution of Standard Oil, the enterprise split into several regional entities, most notably the Standard Oil Company of New Jersey (later known as Exxon) and the Standard Oil Company of New York (later known as Mobil). These two industry giants officially reunited in a historic 1999 mega-merger, forming ExxonMobil. Today, XOM remains a dominant force in the global energy market, actively adapting its portfolio toward lower-emissions technologies, carbon capture, and advanced biofuels while continuing to meet vital global energy demands.
