Exxon Mobil Corporation, trading under the ticker XOM, is a premier American multinational energy corporation and one of the largest publicly traded companies in the world. Headquartered in Spring, Texas, the company operates across the entire energy spectrum, engaging in the exploration, production, transportation, and sale of crude oil and natural gas. Additionally, ExxonMobil manufactures and markets commodity petrochemicals, specialty products, and power generation solutions, while increasingly investing in lower-emission technologies such as carbon capture and advanced biofuels. 

The company’s corporate lineage traces back to John D. Rockefeller’s Standard Oil trust, founded in 1870. Following the landmark 1911 antitrust dissolution of Standard Oil, the enterprise split into several regional entities, most notably the Standard Oil Company of New Jersey (which later became Exxon) and the Standard Oil Company of New York (which became Mobil). These two major corporate descendants officially reunited in a historic merger in 1999, forming ExxonMobil. Today, the corporation leverages its vast global scale, technological expertise, and integrated business model to navigate evolving global energy demands while maintaining its historical legacy as a cornerstone of the modern energy industry.
