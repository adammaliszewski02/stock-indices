Exxon Mobil Corporation, trading under the ticker XOM, is a premier American multinational energy corporation and one of the world's largest publicly traded integrated oil and gas companies. Headquartered in Spring, Texas, the enterprise operates through upstream, product solutions, and low-carbon solutions segments, engaging in the exploration, production, transportation, and sale of crude oil, natural gas, and petroleum products, alongside chemical manufacturing. 

The company traces its corporate lineage directly to John D. Rockefeller's Standard Oil trust, founded in 1870. Following the landmark 1911 antitrust dissolution of Standard Oil, two of its major successor entities—the Standard Oil Company of New Jersey (which later adopted the brand name Esso) and the Mobil Oil Corporation (formerly Standard Oil Company of New York)—operated independently for decades. In 1999, these two corporate giants merged to form ExxonMobil, creating a dominant global energy powerhouse. Today, the corporation balances its historical legacy in fossil fuels with strategic investments in lower-emission technologies, including carbon capture and storage, hydrogen, and biofuels, to address evolving global energy demands.
