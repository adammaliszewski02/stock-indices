RTX Corporation, formerly known as Raytheon Technologies, is a leading global aerospace and defense company headquartered in Arlington, Virginia. Formed in 2020 through the merger of Raytheon Company and United Technologies Corporation, the organization operates through three primary business segments: Collins Aerospace, Pratt & Whitney, and Raytheon. The company provides advanced technology and services to commercial, military, and government customers worldwide. Its diverse portfolio includes the development of jet engines, avionics, cybersecurity solutions, guided missiles, and air defense systems. With a history tracing back to the early 20th century through its predecessor entities, RTX remains a critical player in the global industrial base, focusing on innovation in aerospace engineering, space exploration, and national security infrastructure. Through its integrated business model, RTX supports the modernization of global air travel and the advancement of sophisticated defense capabilities.
