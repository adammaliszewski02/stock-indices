RTX Corporation, formerly known as Raytheon Technologies, is a leading multinational aerospace and defense conglomerate headquartered in Arlington, Virginia. The company was formed in 2020 through the monumental merger of United Technologies Corporation and the Raytheon Company, bringing together legacy aviation and defense portfolios dating back to the early twentieth century. RTX operates primarily through three industry-leading business segments: Pratt & Whitney, Collins Aerospace, and Raytheon. Pratt & Whitney designs and manufactures aircraft engines and auxiliary power units for commercial and military applications. Collins Aerospace provides advanced aerostructures, avionics, and interior systems for the global aviation industry. Meanwhile, the Raytheon segment is a premier defense contractor specializing in integrated air and missile defense systems, radar, sensors, advanced electronics, and cutting-edge cybersecurity solutions. Serving commercial airlines, defense departments, and government agencies worldwide, RTX delivers critical technologies that ensure global security, advance civil aviation, and shape the future of aerospace exploration.
