RTX Corporation, formerly known as Raytheon Technologies, is a preeminent global aerospace and defense company headquartered in Arlington, Virginia. Formed in 2020 through the merger of Raytheon Company and United Technologies Corporation, the organization operates through three primary business segments: Collins Aerospace, Pratt & Whitney, and Raytheon. The company provides advanced technology and services to commercial, military, and government customers worldwide. Its diverse portfolio includes the development and manufacturing of aircraft engines, avionics, aerostructures, cybersecurity solutions, guided missiles, and air defense systems. With a legacy rooted in the pioneering innovations of companies like Pratt & Whitney, founded in 1925, and Raytheon, established in 1922, RTX maintains a critical role in global security and commercial aviation infrastructure. By leveraging deep engineering expertise and a commitment to research and development, RTX continues to address complex challenges in aerospace propulsion, space exploration, and integrated defense systems.
