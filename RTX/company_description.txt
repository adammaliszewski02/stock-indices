RTX Corporation, formerly known as Raytheon Technologies, is a leading multinational aerospace and defense conglomerate headquartered in Arlington, Virginia. Formed in 2020 through the mega-merger of United Technologies Corporation and the Raytheon Company—whose aerospace roots trace back to the 1920s—the company operates primarily through three industry-leading segments. Collins Aerospace provides advanced aerostructures, avionics, and interior systems for commercial and military aircraft. Pratt & Whitney designs and manufactures aircraft engines and auxiliary power units for global aviation. Raytheon delivers state-of-the-art defense solutions, specializing in air defense systems, radar, sensors, missiles, and cybersecurity technologies. Serving commercial airlines, military agencies, and government customers worldwide, RTX is a critical pillar of global defense infrastructure and aerospace innovation.
