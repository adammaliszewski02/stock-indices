RTX Corporation, formerly known as Raytheon Technologies, is a leading multinational aerospace and defense conglomerate headquartered in Arlington, Virginia. Formed in 2020 through the mega-merger of Raytheon Company and the United Technologies Corporation aerospace businesses, the company operates as one of the world's largest defense contractors and providers of commercial aviation services. RTX is organized into three primary business segments. Collins Aerospace specializes in aerostructures, avionics, and interior systems for commercial and military aircraft. Pratt & Whitney designs and manufactures aircraft engines and auxiliary power units for commercial and defense applications. Raytheon focuses on defense systems, including advanced missiles, radar, air defense batteries, and cybersecurity solutions. With a rich legacy tracing back to the early 20th-century origins of its predecessor companies—including the historic development of radar and iconic aviation engines—RTX continues to deliver critical technological innovations that support global defense, national security, and modern commercial air travel.
