RTX Corporation, formerly known as Raytheon Technologies, is a leading multinational aerospace and defense conglomerate headquartered in Arlington, Virginia. The company was formed in 2020 through the multi-billion-dollar merger of Raytheon Company and the aerospace businesses of United Technologies Corporation, combining a legacy of defense innovation dating back to 1922 with decades of commercial aviation expertise. 

RTX operates primarily through three industry-leading business segments: Pratt & Whitney, Collins Aerospace, and Raytheon. Pratt & Whitney designs and manufactures aircraft engines and auxiliary power units for both commercial and military applications. Collins Aerospace provides advanced aerostructures, avionics, interiors, and mechanical systems for the global aerospace industry. Meanwhile, the Raytheon segment focuses on defense electronics, air defense systems, cybersecurity solutions, and advanced missiles. 

Serving commercial airlines, military branches, and government agencies worldwide, RTX plays a critical role in shaping modern defense capabilities and air transport infrastructure. The corporation leverages cutting-edge research and development to address complex security and mobility challenges on a global scale.
