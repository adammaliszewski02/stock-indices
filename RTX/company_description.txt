RTX Corporation, formerly known as Raytheon Technologies, is a leading global aerospace and defense company headquartered in Arlington, Virginia. Formed in 2020 through the historic merger of Raytheon Company and the aerospace businesses of United Technologies Corporation, the company operates primarily through three industry-leading segments: Collins Aerospace, Pratt & Whitney, and Raytheon. RTX provides advanced technology and services to commercial, military, and government customers worldwide. Its comprehensive portfolio includes the manufacturing of commercial aircraft engines, avionics, and aerostructures, alongside the development of sophisticated defense systems, including air and missile defense, radar, cybersecurity solutions, and hypersonics. With a rich legacy of innovation tracing back to early aviation and defense pioneers, RTX plays a critical role in shaping modern commercial aviation and national security infrastructure.
