RTX Corporation, formerly known as Raytheon Technologies, is a premier American multinational aerospace and defense conglomerate headquartered in Arlington, Virginia. The company was formed in 2020 through the historic merger of Raytheon Company and the aerospace businesses of United Technologies Corporation, combining a legacy of defense innovation dating back to 1922 with foundational aerospace pioneers. RTX operates primarily through three industry-leading segments: Collins Aerospace, Pratt & Whitney, and Raytheon. Collins Aerospace provides advanced aerostructures, avionics, and interior systems for commercial and military aircraft. Pratt & Whitney is a world leader in the design, manufacture, and servicing of aircraft engines and auxiliary power units. Raytheon specializes in the development of integrated air and missile defense systems, advanced sensors, cyber solutions, and cutting-edge software for global defense customers. Serving commercial, military, and government sectors worldwide, RTX delivers critical technologies that ensure global security, advance commercial aviation, and drive scientific exploration in aerospace and defense.
