The stock ticker QCOM identifies Qualcomm Incorporated, an American multinational semiconductor and telecommunications equipment company headquartered in San Diego, California. Founded in 1985 by Irwin Jacobs and six co-founders, Qualcomm revolutionized wireless communications by pioneering Code Division Multiple Access (CDMA) technology, which became a foundational standard for 2G, 3G, and subsequent cellular networks. 

Today, Qualcomm operates as a fabless semiconductor company driven primarily by two core segments: Qualcomm CDMA Technologies (QCT) and Qualcomm Technology Licensing (QTL). Through QCT, the company designs and markets advanced integrated circuits and system software, notably its Snapdragon processors that power premium smartphones, automotive systems, and Internet of Things (IoT) devices. Through QTL, Qualcomm grants licenses for the use of its vast intellectual property portfolio, which covers essential patents underpinning modern wireless standards, including 4G and 5G. In recent years, Qualcomm has significantly expanded its footprint into edge artificial intelligence, connected automotive cockpits, and personal computing processors, cementing its position as a global leader in foundational wireless and high-performance, low-power computing technologies.
