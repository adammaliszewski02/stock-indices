Qualcomm Incorporated, trading under the ticker QCOM, is a leading multinational semiconductor and telecommunications equipment company headquartered in San Diego, California. Founded in 1985 by Irwin Jacobs and six co-founders, Qualcomm played a foundational role in the mobile communications revolution by pioneering commercial Code Division Multiple Access (CDMA) technology, which established key technical foundations for 2G, 3G, and subsequent cellular standards.

Today, Qualcomm operates through two primary divisions: Qualcomm CDMA Technologies (QCT) and Qualcomm Technology Licensing (QTL). QCT develops and markets integrated circuits and system software, most notably the flagship Snapdragon platform, advanced 5G modems, and RF front-end components utilized in smartphones, automotive systems, and Internet of Things (IoT) devices. QTL manages the monetization of the company’s extensive intellectual property portfolio, generating substantial revenue by licensing foundational patents covering modern wireless communications standards. Through continuous innovation in wireless connectivity, edge computing, and on-device artificial intelligence, Qualcomm remains a central architect of modern mobile and industrial digital infrastructure.
