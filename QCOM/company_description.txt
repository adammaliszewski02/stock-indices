The stock ticker QCOM identifies Qualcomm Incorporated, a prominent American multinational semiconductor and telecommunications equipment company headquartered in San Diego, California. Founded in 1985 by Irwin Jacobs and six colleagues, Qualcomm pioneered commercial Code Division Multiple Access (CDMA) technology, which revolutionized digital mobile communications and became foundational for 3G mobile networks. 

Today, Qualcomm operates as a fabless semiconductor designer through two primary business divisions: Qualcomm CDMA Technologies (QCT) and Qualcomm Technology Licensing (QTL). QCT develops and markets advanced integrated circuits and system software, most notably the Snapdragon mobile platform, cellular modems, and RF front-end components powering smartphones, automotive platforms, personal computers, and Internet of Things (IoT) devices. 

Through its QTL division, Qualcomm licenses its substantial intellectual property portfolio—including essential patents covering 3G, 4G, 5G, and Wi-Fi telecommunications standards—to device manufacturers worldwide. This dual model of chip design and IP monetization has established Qualcomm as a global leader in foundational wireless technologies, edge computing, and on-device artificial intelligence.
