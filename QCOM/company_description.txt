Qualcomm Incorporated, traded under the ticker QCOM, is a multinational semiconductor and telecommunications equipment company that designs and markets wireless telecommunications products and services. Founded in 1985 by Irwin Jacobs and a team of colleagues, the San Diego-based company pioneered code-division multiple access (CDMA) technology, which laid the foundational groundwork for modern digital wireless communications. 

Today, Qualcomm is a world leader in 3G, 4G, and 5G wireless technologies, deriving a significant portion of its revenue from the licensing of its extensive intellectual property portfolio and the sale of integrated circuits for mobile devices. The company’s core semiconductor business, Qualcomm Technologies, manufactures Snapdragon system-on-chip platforms that power a vast majority of the world's smartphones. In recent years, Qualcomm has leveraged its advanced processing and connectivity expertise to diversify its business operations into adjacent growth markets, including automotive platforms, Internet of Things devices, and high-performance personal computing hardware.
