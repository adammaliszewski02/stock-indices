Qualcomm Incorporated, trading under the ticker QCOM, is a multinational semiconductor and telecommunications equipment company that designs and markets wireless telecommunications products and services. Founded in 1985 in San Diego, California, by Irwin Jacobs and Andrew Viterbi, the company pioneered the commercialization of Code Division Multiple Access (CDMA) digital wireless technology. Over the decades, Qualcomm has established itself as a world leader in 3G, 4G, and 5G wireless technologies, supplying foundational semiconductor components and mobile processors, known as Snapdragon, to major smartphone manufacturers globally. Beyond mobile handsets, Qualcomm’s business model centers on licensing its extensive intellectual property portfolio and expanding into high-growth sectors such as the Internet of Things, automotive platforms, and personal computing. Through continuous research and development, the company remains a central driver of global wireless connectivity and digital infrastructure.
