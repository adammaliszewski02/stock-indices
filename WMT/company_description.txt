Stock ticker WMT corresponds to Walmart Inc., an American multinational retail corporation that operates a massive chain of hypermarkets, discount department stores, and grocery stores. Founded by Sam Walton in 1962 and incorporated in 1969, the company has grown from a single small-town discount store in Rogers, Arkansas, into the world's largest company by revenue. Walmart's core business model focuses on everyday low prices, supported by a highly efficient global supply chain and logistics network. The company encompasses multiple retail formats, including Walmart discount stores, Supercenters, Neighborhood Markets, and Sam's Club warehouse stores. In response to shifting consumer habits and digital competition, Walmart has significantly expanded its omni-channel capabilities, developing a robust e-commerce platform and digital marketplace. Additionally, the company offers various financial and health services. Operating globally, Walmart employs millions of associates and serves hundreds of millions of customers weekly through its physical stores and e-commerce websites.
