The company identified by the stock ticker WMT is Walmart Inc., an American multinational retail corporation that operates a massive chain of hypermarkets, discount department stores, and grocery stores. Founded by Sam Walton in 1962 in Rogers, Arkansas, the company has grown from a single small-town discount store into the world's largest company by revenue. Walmart incorporated as Wal-Mart Stores, Inc. in 1969 and officially changed its name to Walmart Inc. in 2018 to better reflect its transition into an omni-channel retailer integrating physical stores with e-commerce. Through its core segments, which include Walmart U.S., Walmart International, and Sam's Club, the company serves millions of customers globally every week. Walmart leverages its extensive supply chain logistics, everyday low pricing strategy, and digital marketplace to deliver a wide array of consumer goods, groceries, and technology products, maintaining a dominant position in the global retail industry.
