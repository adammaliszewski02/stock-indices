The stock ticker WMT represents Walmart Inc., the world’s largest retailer by revenue and a constituent of the Dow Jones Industrial Average. Founded by Sam Walton in 1962 in Rogers, Arkansas, and incorporated in 1969, the company transformed the retail landscape through high-volume discounting, efficient supply chain management, and its signature "Everyday Low Prices" operating philosophy. 

Walmart operates a massive global omnichannel network comprising three primary segments: Walmart U.S., Walmart International, and Sam's Club. Its footprint includes supercenters, discount stores, neighborhood markets, and membership-only warehouse clubs, alongside robust e-commerce platforms. The business retails a vast assortment of merchandise, including groceries, consumables, apparel, electronics, and home goods, while also providing pharmacy, optical, and financial services. 

In recent years, Walmart has strategically expanded beyond traditional brick-and-mortar retail by heavily investing in e-commerce infrastructure, automated fulfillment centers, third-party marketplace services, and its digital advertising arm, Walmart Connect. Serving hundreds of millions of customers weekly across thousands of locations worldwide, the company leverages its immense physical scale and technological investments to maintain its dominant position in global commerce.
