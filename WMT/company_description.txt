Walmart Inc., trading under the ticker WMT, is a multinational retail corporation that operates a chain of hypermarkets, discount department stores, and grocery stores. Founded by Sam Walton in 1962 and incorporated in 1969, the company has grown into the world's largest corporation by revenue. Headquartered in Bentonville, Arkansas, Walmart encompasses distinct business segments including Walmart U.S., Walmart International, and Sam's Club. The company serves millions of customers globally through its extensive physical store network and robust e-commerce platforms, offering a vast array of consumer goods, groceries, and financial services at everyday low prices.
