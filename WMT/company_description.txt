Walmart Inc., trading under the ticker WMT, is a multinational retail corporation that operates a massive chain of hypermarkets, discount department stores, and grocery stores. Founded by Sam Walton in 1962 in Rogers, Arkansas, and incorporated in 1969, the company has grown into the world's largest corporation by revenue. Walmart's core business model focuses on everyday low prices, supported by a highly efficient supply chain and logistics network. The company operates through three primary segments: Walmart U.S., Walmart International, and Sam's Club, which is its chain of membership-only warehouse clubs. In recent decades, Walmart has significantly expanded its digital footprint and e-commerce capabilities to compete in the modern retail landscape, offering omnichannel services such as curbside pickup, home delivery, and a dedicated membership program known as Walmart+. Additionally, the company is a major private-label producer, offering a wide array of consumer goods ranging from groceries and apparel to electronics and home furnishings, serving millions of customers globally each week through both its physical storefronts and digital platforms.
