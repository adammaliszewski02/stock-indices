Stock ticker WMT identifies Walmart Inc., an American multinational retail corporation that operates a massive chain of hypermarkets, discount department stores, and grocery stores. Founded by Sam Walton in 1962 in Rogers, Arkansas, the company incorporated as Wal-Mart Stores, Inc. in 1969 and officially changed its name to Walmart Inc. in 2018 to reflect its transition into an omnichannel enterprise. Walmart has grown to become the world’s largest company by revenue, employing over two million associates globally. Its business model centers on everyday low prices, supported by an extensive supply chain and logistics network. The company serves hundreds of millions of customers weekly through physical stores and robust e-commerce platforms, operating globally through numerous banners and subsidiaries, including Sam's Club warehouse stores.
