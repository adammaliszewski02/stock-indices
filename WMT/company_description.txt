Walmart Inc., trading under the ticker WMT, is a multinational retail corporation that operates a massive chain of hypermarkets, discount department stores, and grocery stores. Founded by Sam Walton in 1962 in Rogers, Arkansas, the company has grown from a single small-town discount store into the world's largest company by revenue. Walmart officially incorporated as Wal-Mart Stores, Inc. in 1969 and listed its shares publicly on the New York Stock Exchange in 1972. Today, its business model encompasses three primary segments: Walmart U.S., Walmart International, and Sam's Club, a chain of membership-only warehouse clubs. In addition to its extensive physical storefronts across the globe, the company has significantly expanded its digital footprint through robust e-commerce operations, marketplace platforms, and omnichannel services. Guided by its founding mission to help people save money and live better, Walmart leverages its vast supply chain infrastructure and logistics network to offer a wide assortment of consumer goods, groceries, and technology products at everyday low prices.
