UnitedHealth Group (UNH) is a diversified health care company based in Minnetonka, Minnesota, operating through two primary business platforms: UnitedHealthcare and Optum. UnitedHealthcare provides health insurance coverage and benefits services to individuals, employers, and government programs, including Medicare and Medicaid. Optum serves as a health services innovation platform, offering data-driven insights, pharmacy care services, and clinical care delivery to improve health outcomes and reduce costs.

The company traces its origins to 1977 with the founding of Charter Med Incorporated, which reorganized into United HealthCare Corporation in 1977 to manage the newly formed Physicians Health Plan of Minnesota. Over the subsequent decades, the organization expanded significantly through strategic acquisitions and internal development, evolving from a regional health maintenance organization into a global leader in the health care industry. Today, UnitedHealth Group is a component of the Dow Jones Industrial Average and is recognized as one of the largest health care companies in the world by revenue, playing a critical role in the administration and delivery of health services across the United States and international markets.
