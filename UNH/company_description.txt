UnitedHealth Group Incorporated (ticker: UNH) is a diversified healthcare and well-being company based in the United States, recognized as the largest healthcare company in the world by revenue. Traded on the New York Stock Exchange, the company operates through two primary platforms: UnitedHealthcare, which provides health care coverage and benefit services to individuals, employers, and Medicare and Medicaid beneficiaries, and Optum, which delivers information- and technology-enabled health services. Tracing its origins back to the 1977 founding of United Health Insurance Company as a restructuring of InterStudy, a health maintenance organization research firm established by Paul Ellwood in 1972, the enterprise officially adopted its current holding company structure in 1977. Over the subsequent decades, UNH expanded aggressively through strategic acquisitions—including the 2004 purchase of Oxford Health Plans and the later integration of Catamaran Corporation and Change Healthcare—to establish a vertically integrated model spanning insurance, pharmacy benefits management, care delivery, and health technology.
