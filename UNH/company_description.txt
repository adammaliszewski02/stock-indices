UnitedHealth Group Incorporated, traded under the ticker UNH, is a diversified American healthcare and well-being company. Tracing its origins to the founding of United Health and Life in 1977, the company formally established UnitedHealth Group in 1977 as a parent organization to better manage and expand its health services. Headquartered in Minnetonka, Minnesota, UNH operates through two primary platforms: UnitedHealthcare, which provides health care coverage and benefit services to individuals, employers, and Medicare and Medicaid beneficiaries, and Optum, which delivers information and technology-enabled health services, pharmacy care services, and direct patient care. By integrating health benefits with care delivery and data analytics, UnitedHealth Group has grown to become one of the largest healthcare companies in the world by revenue, serving hundreds of millions of customers globally and playing a foundational role in the modern healthcare ecosystem.
