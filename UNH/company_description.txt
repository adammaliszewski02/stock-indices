UnitedHealth Group Incorporated, traded under the ticker UNH, is a diversified American healthcare and well-being company. Tracing its origins to the founding of its predecessor, United Health and Life, in 1977, the company officially restructured into its current enterprise form in 1977 under the leadership of Richard T. Burke. Headquartered in Minnetonka, Minnesota, UNH operates primarily through two distinct platforms: UnitedHealthcare, which provides health care coverage and benefit services to individuals, employers, and Medicare and Medicaid beneficiaries, and Optum, its health services arm that delivers information and technology-enabled health services, pharmacy care services, and direct patient care. Recognized as the largest healthcare company in the world by revenue, UNH leverages its integrated model to improve clinical outcomes, streamline administrative processes, and reduce overall costs within the healthcare system.
