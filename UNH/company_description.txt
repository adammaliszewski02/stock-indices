UnitedHealth Group Incorporated, traded under the ticker UNH, is a diversified American healthcare and well-being company. Tracing its origins to the founding of United Health and Life in 1977, the company reorganized as UnitedHealth Group in 1998 to reflect its expanding scope. Headquartered in Minnetonka, Minnesota, it operates through two primary business segments. UnitedHealthcare provides health care coverage and benefits services to individuals, employers, and Medicare and Medicaid beneficiaries. Concurrently, Optira—which evolved from the company's health services operations—delivers information and technology-enabled health services, pharmacy care services, and direct patient care delivery. Through this integrated approach, the enterprise leverages data, analytics, and clinical expertise to improve the quality, affordability, and overall efficiency of the health system on a global scale.
