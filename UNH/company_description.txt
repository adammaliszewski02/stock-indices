UnitedHealth Group Incorporated (ticker: UNH) is a diversified healthcare and well-being company based in the United States, recognized as the largest healthcare company in the world by revenue. Traded on the New York Stock Exchange, the company operates through two distinct platforms: UnitedHealthcare, which provides health care coverage and benefits services, and Optum, which delivers information and technology-enabled health services. 

The enterprise traces its origins to the 1977 founding of Charter Med Incorporated, which was established as a corporate restructuring of a physician-led organization created three years earlier. In 1979, the corporate name was changed to United HealthCare Corporation, and it subsequently adopted its current holding company structure in 1998. Over the decades, UNH expanded significantly through strategic acquisitions, evolving from a traditional health maintenance organization into a comprehensive healthcare leader that integrates insurance, pharmacy benefit management, health services, and data analytics to serve tens of millions of individuals globally.
