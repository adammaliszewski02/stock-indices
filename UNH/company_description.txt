UnitedHealth Group Incorporated, traded under the ticker UNH, is a diversified American healthcare company and the largest healthcare provider in the world by revenue. Headquartered in Minnetonka, Minnesota, the company traces its origins to the 1977 founding of InterStudy, an organization that analyzed health maintenance organizations, which led to the establishment of United HealthCare Corporation in 1977. 

UNH operates primarily through two distinct business platforms: UnitedHealthcare, which provides health care coverage and benefit services to individuals, employers, and Medicare and Medicaid beneficiaries, and Optum, a health services enterprise that leverages data, technology, and clinical expertise to improve pharmacy care, healthcare delivery, and operational efficiency for the broader healthcare industry. Through this integrated care delivery and financing model, the company serves hundreds of millions of people globally, addressing complex healthcare challenges across the entire continuum of care.
