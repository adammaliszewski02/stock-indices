UnitedHealth Group Incorporated, traded under the ticker UNH, is a diversified American healthcare and well-being company. Tracing its origins to the founding of its predecessor, United Health and Life, in 1977, the company officially restructured into its current holding structure in 1977 before becoming a publicly traded entity on the New York Stock Exchange. Headquartered in Minnetonka, Minnesota, UnitedHealth Group operates through two distinct platforms: UnitedHealthcare, which provides health care coverage and benefit services to individuals, employers, and Medicare and Medicaid beneficiaries, and Optum, a health services business that leverages information and technology to deliver pharmacy care services, care delivery, and operational consulting. Through this integrated care model, the company has grown to become one of the largest healthcare enterprises in the world by revenue, serving hundreds of millions of people globally while advancing modern healthcare delivery and financial systems.
