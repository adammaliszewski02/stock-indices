United Parcel Service, Inc. (UPS) is a global leader in logistics, offering a broad range of solutions that include the transportation of packages and freight, the facilitation of international trade, and the deployment of advanced technology to efficiently manage the world's supply chain. Headquartered in Atlanta, Georgia, the enterprise was founded in 1907 as the American Messenger Company by 19-year-old James E. Casey in Seattle, Washington, starting with a mere hundred-dollar loan. Over the course of more than a century, the company evolved from a local messenger service into a multi-billion-dollar corporation. Today, UPS operates one of the largest and most efficient airline fleets in the world alongside a massive ground delivery network, serving millions of customers across more than 200 countries and territories. The company’s core business focuses on reliable time-definite delivery services, freight forwarding, contract logistics, and specialized healthcare supply chain management, driving commerce on a global scale while continually adapting to the demands of modern e-commerce and sustainable business practices.
