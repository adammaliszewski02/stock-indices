Costco Wholesale Corporation, trading under the ticker COST, is a prominent global membership-only warehouse club operator. Founded in Seattle, Washington, in 1983 by James Sinegal and Jeffrey Brotman, the company pioneered the warehouse club concept with the opening of its first location on Fourth Avenue South. Costco provides its members with substantial value by offering a carefully curated selection of nationally branded and private-label products, notably its Kirkland Signature brand, across a wide range of merchandise categories. These include groceries, electronics, apparel, and hardware, all sold at discounted prices in high-volume, self-service warehouse facilities. In addition to its retail operations, the company operates numerous gasoline stations, pharmacies, optical centers, and food courts. Costco generates the majority of its profitability from membership fees rather than traditional retail markups, allowing the company to maintain exceptionally low prices on goods. Headquartered in Issaquah, Washington, the corporation has expanded significantly over the decades, operating hundreds of warehouses globally across the United States, Canada, Mexico, the United Kingdom, and several Asian markets, establishing itself as one of the largest retailers in the world.
