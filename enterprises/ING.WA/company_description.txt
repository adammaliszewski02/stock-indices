ING Bank Śląski S.A., traded on the Warsaw Stock Exchange under the ticker ING.WA, is one of Poland’s leading financial institutions, offering a comprehensive range of banking and financial services to individual consumers, small and medium-sized enterprises, and large corporations. The bank provides retail banking, corporate banking, investment services, and specialized financial solutions such as leasing and factoring. Tracing its origins back to the Polish economic transformations of the early 1990s, the institution was established in 1988 as Bank Śląski in Katowice before transforming into a joint-stock company in 1991. Following its privatization and initial public offering on the Warsaw Stock Exchange in 1994, the majority stake was acquired by the Dutch financial services giant ING Group, leading to the adoption of the current name in 2001. Today, ING Bank Śląski is widely recognized for its strong emphasis on digital banking innovation, modern mobile platforms, and sustainable finance, operating an extensive branch and ATM network across Poland while serving millions of active clients.
