Cyfrowy Polsat S.A., trading under the ticker CPS.WA on the Warsaw Stock Exchange, is the leading integrated media and telecommunications group in Poland. Founded in 1999 as a satellite television platform, the company has evolved into a diversified conglomerate providing a comprehensive range of services, including pay-TV, mobile and fixed-line telecommunications, broadband internet, and content production. Through its strategic acquisition of Polkomtel, the operator of the Plus network, the group successfully integrated media and mobile infrastructure to create a unique multi-play service model. Today, Cyfrowy Polsat operates under the umbrella brand Polsat Plus Group, leveraging its extensive media assets—including the Polsat television network—and its robust telecommunications backbone to deliver converged digital services to millions of retail and business customers across Poland. The company remains a cornerstone of the Polish capital market and a pivotal player in the nation’s digital transformation.
