The ticker GPW.WA refers to Giełda Papierów Wartościowych w Warszawie, known internationally as the Warsaw Stock Exchange. Established in its modern corporate form in April 1991 following the fall of communism and the revival of the Polish capital market, the company operates the primary stock and derivative exchange in Poland. Its core business encompasses the organization of trading on regulated markets for equities, debt instruments, derivatives, and structured products, alongside the operation of alternative trading systems. Furthermore, the company provides comprehensive clearing and settlement services, market data dissemination, and indices calculation. As the leading exchange in Central and Eastern Europe, GPW plays a pivotal role in capital formation, regional financial integration, and the facilitation of corporate fundraising.
