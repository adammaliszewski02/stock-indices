Johnson & Johnson (ticker: JNJ) is a globally prominent American multinational healthcare company founded in 1886 by Robert Wood Johnson, James Wood Johnson, and Edward Mead Johnson. Headquartered in New Brunswick, New Jersey, the company operates as one of the world's largest and most broadly based healthcare enterprises. Its core business focuses on the research, development, manufacture, and worldwide marketing of innovative products across two primary sectors: Innovative Medicine and MedTech. Following the recent spin-off of its consumer health division, Kenvue, the company now concentrates entirely on prescription pharmaceuticals and advanced medical technology solutions. JNJ serves diverse healthcare needs ranging from oncology, immunology, and neuroscience to cardiovascular and orthopedic interventions.
