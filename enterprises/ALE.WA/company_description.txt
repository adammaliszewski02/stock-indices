The ticker ALE.WA corresponds to Allegro.eu, a prominent European technology company and the operator of Poland’s leading e-commerce platform, Allegro. Founded in Poznań in 1999 as a Polish online auction site, the company has evolved significantly over the past two decades. It transitioned from a domestic marketplace into a publicly traded multinational enterprise, completing its initial public offering on the Warsaw Stock Exchange in October 2020. Today, Allegro functions as a comprehensive online shopping ecosystem, offering a vast array of products across multiple categories through third-party merchants and its own direct retail operations. The company generates revenue primarily from marketplace commissions, advertising services, and subscription models like Allegro Smart!, which provides buyers with free delivery benefits. In recent years, Allegro has expanded its geographical footprint beyond Poland through strategic initiatives, including the acquisition of the international cross-border e-commerce platform Mall Group and the logistics provider WEJUNGA in 2022, thereby strengthening its presence across Central and Eastern Europe.
