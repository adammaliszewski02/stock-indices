JPMorgan Chase & Co., traded under the ticker JPM, is a leading global financial services firm and one of the oldest banking institutions in the United States, with historical roots tracing back to 1799 through predecessor entities like the Manhattan Company. Headquartered in New York City, the company operates as a multinational financial powerhouse, offering a comprehensive suite of services that includes investment banking, asset management, treasury and securities services, private banking, and commercial and consumer banking. Serving millions of consumers globally—including corporations, institutional investors, and governments—the firm conducts its primary operations through well-known segments such as Chase Bank for retail and commercial services, and J.P. Morgan for investment banking and wealth management. The modern enterprise is the result of numerous strategic mergers and acquisitions over centuries, most notably the 2000 merger between Chase Manhattan Corporation and J.P. Morgan & Co., followed by the pivotal acquisitions of Bear Stearns and Washington Mutual during the 2008 financial crisis. Today, JPMorgan Chase stands as the largest bank in the United States by total assets and is recognized as a globally systemic financial institution.
