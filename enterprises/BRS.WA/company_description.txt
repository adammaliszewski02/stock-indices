The ticker BRS.WA corresponds to Boryszew S.A., a prominent industrial manufacturing group headquartered in Warsaw, Poland, and listed on the Warsaw Stock Exchange. Tracing its origins back to a metal and wire plant established in 1911, the company has evolved significantly over more than a century. Today, Boryszew operates as a diversified holding company with a primary focus on the automotive sector, where it manufactures components such as interior elements, piping systems, and plastic parts for major global vehicle manufacturers. Beyond automotive operations, the group is a leading European producer of non-ferrous metals, specializing in the processing of zinc, lead, and aluminum, alongside manufacturing various chemical products and plastics. Through its extensive international network of production facilities, Boryszew serves diverse industrial markets globally, driven by strategic acquisitions and restructuring efforts undertaken since the late twentieth century to modernize its industrial portfolio.
