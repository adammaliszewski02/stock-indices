NVIDIA Corporation, traded under the ticker NVDA, is a pioneering American multinational technology company specializing in accelerated computing and artificial intelligence. Founded in 1993 by Jensen Huang, Chris Malachowsky, and Curtis Priem, the company initially revolutionized the PC gaming industry with the 1999 invention of the Graphics Processing Unit, or GPU. 

Over the decades, NVIDIA expanded its core architecture beyond graphics rendering to address complex computational challenges. By developing versatile parallel processing capabilities and proprietary software ecosystems like CUDA, launched in 2006, the company laid the foundational hardware and software infrastructure for modern machine learning, deep learning, and generative AI. 

Today, NVIDIA designs market-leading GPUs, system-on-a-chip units, and comprehensive software platforms utilized across diverse sectors, including cloud computing, data centers, autonomous vehicles, robotics, and healthcare. Driven by the explosive global demand for artificial intelligence infrastructure, NVIDIA has evolved into one of the world's most valuable technology enterprises, powering the critical systems that enable next-generation technological advancements.
