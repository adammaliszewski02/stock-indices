Chevron Corporation, trading under the ticker CVX, is an American multinational energy corporation and one of the world's largest integrated energy companies. Tracing its origins back to the 1879 formation of the Pacific Coast Oil Company, Chevron engages in every facet of the oil and natural gas industries. Its operations encompass hydrocarbon exploration, production, refining, marketing, transportation, petrochemical manufacturing, and power generation. Headquartered in San Ramon, California, the company operates globally, managing extensive upstream assets in prolific basins such as the Permian, alongside a robust downstream network of refineries, pipelines, and retail service stations. Additionally, Chevron is actively investing in lower-carbon technologies, including carbon capture, renewable fuels, and hydrogen, to support the global energy transition while maintaining its traditional fossil fuel operations.
