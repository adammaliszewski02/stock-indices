The stock ticker DAT.WA corresponds to DataWalk S.A., a prominent Polish technology company specializing in big data analytics, intelligence, and investigative software. Founded in 1998 and originally known as Piql Polska and later Asseco Data Systems spin-offs before rebranding, the company developed a patented, graph-based analytics platform designed primarily for the public sector, law enforcement, intelligence agencies, and financial institutions. DataWalk’s core software empowers organizations to ingest, integrate, and analyze massive volumes of disparate data from structured and unstructured sources to detect fraud, combat financial crime, enhance national security, and manage complex investigations. Headquartered in Warsaw, Poland, DataWalk transitioned from a domestic integrator to a global software vendor, scaling its operations internationally with a strategic focus on expanding its footprint in North American and European markets through a network of strategic partners and direct enterprise sales.
