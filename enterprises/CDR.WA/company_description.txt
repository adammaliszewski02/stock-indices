The ticker CDR.WA represents CD Projekt S.A., a prominent Polish video game development, publishing, and distribution company headquartered in Warsaw. Founded in May 1994 by Marcin Iwiński and Michał Kiciński, the enterprise initially operated as a retailer importing Western video games into Poland. The company transitioned into game development with the establishment of its studio, CD Projekt Red, in 2002. 

CD Projekt achieved major international acclaim and commercial success with its critically lauded dark fantasy role-playing game series *The Witcher*, based on the literary works of Andrzej Sapkowski. The release of *The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt* in 2015 cemented the company's status as a major global developer. In December 2020, the company launched the sci-fi action-RPG *Cyberpunk 2077*, which, despite a highly publicized and troubled launch, ultimately achieved robust long-term sales following extensive technical overhauls and the release of its acclaimed expansion, *Phantom Liberty*. Alongside game development, CD Projekt operates GOG.com, a prominent digital distribution platform for video games and films focusing on DRM-free titles. The company continues to develop several major projects, including sequels to both *The Witcher* and *Cyberpunk* franchises.
