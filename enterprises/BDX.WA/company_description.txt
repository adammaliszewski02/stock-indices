The ticker BDX.WA refers to Budimex S.A., one of Poland's largest construction and infrastructure groups. Founded in 1968, the company originally operated as a foreign trade enterprise before undergoing privatization in the 1990s, with Spain's Ferrovial becoming its majority shareholder through Cintra. Budimex operates primarily in the Polish market, delivering a diverse portfolio of complex engineering projects. Its core business encompasses infrastructure construction, including roads, railways, airports, and public transit systems, alongside commercial, residential, and industrial building. Furthermore, the company has expanded its operations into the energy sector, undertaking projects related to renewable energy and power transmission. Budimex plays a pivotal role in the modernization of Central Europe's transport and industrial infrastructure, leveraging decades of engineering expertise and technological innovation to execute large-scale public and private investments.
