The ticker ZAB.WA corresponds to Zakłady Azotowe w Tarnowie-Mościcach S.A., a prominent Polish chemical enterprise headquartered in Tarnów. Historically, the company traces its origins to the interwar period, established in 1927 as a state-owned facility vital to Poland's developing industrial sector and agricultural self-sufficiency, initially focusing on the production of synthetic fertilizers. Over the decades, the company evolved into a major player in the European chemical market, expanding its portfolio beyond nitrogen fertilizers to encompass engineering plastics, polyamide, and various industrial chemicals. In 2012, through a strategic consolidation within the domestic chemical sector, the company became a core asset of the Grupa Azoty Group, one of the largest fertilizer and chemical holdings in the European Union. Shares of the enterprise were historically traded on the Warsaw Stock Exchange under the ticker ZAB.WA before subsequent corporate restructuring and consolidation within the parent group altered its public listing status.
