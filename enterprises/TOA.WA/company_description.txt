The ticker TOA.WA refers to Torpol S.A., a prominent Polish construction company listed on the Warsaw Stock Exchange. Established in 1990 and headquartered in Poznań, the company specializes in the modernization and construction of railway infrastructure, including tracks, traction networks, and associated engineering structures. Torpol operates primarily as a general contractor, executing large-scale public infrastructure projects funded largely by state bodies and the European Union to enhance national and regional rail transport efficiency. Over the decades, the firm has grown into one of the market leaders in Poland's railway engineering sector, recognized for its technical expertise in delivering complex transportation projects while maintaining high standards of safety and project management.
