COG.WA is the stock ticker for Cogedim Polska, though more accurately associated with Cognor Holding S.A., a major Polish capital group operating in the steel industry. Cognor Holding S.A. specializes in the production of steel products, primarily reinforcing bars, billets, and wire rods, as well as the collection and management of steel scrap. Headquartered in Poręba, Poland, the company traces its origins back to the restructuring and consolidation of various entities within the Polish metallurgical sector, eventually listing on the Warsaw Stock Exchange to finance its modernization and expansion. Cognor operates electric arc furnaces, positioning itself as a key player in the European steel recycling and manufacturing market by emphasizing eco-friendly production methods. Over the decades, the company has navigated the cyclical nature of the global metals industry through strategic asset optimization, acquisitions, and a strong focus on operational efficiency.
