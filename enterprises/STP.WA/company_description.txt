The ticker STP.WA refers to Stella Pack S.A., a prominent Polish manufacturer and distributor of household and hygiene products. Founded in 1995, the company has grown from a regional distributor into a leading European player in the non-woven, plastic, and paper goods sectors. Stella Pack’s extensive product portfolio encompasses waste bags, cleaning cloths, foil packaging, baking papers, and various sanitary items supplied to major retail chains. Headquartered in Lubartów, Poland, the enterprise operates advanced manufacturing and warehousing facilities, emphasizing sustainable development through the integration of recycled materials into its production processes. In 2020, Stella Pack successfully completed its initial public offering, listing its shares on the Warsaw Stock Exchange under the ticker STP.WA, which marked a significant milestone in its corporate evolution and regional expansion strategy.
