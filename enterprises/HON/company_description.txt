Honeywell International Inc., traded under the ticker HON, is a leading American multinational conglomerate that invents and manufactures technologies to address critical global challenges surrounding energy, safety, security, productivity, and global urbanization. Traced back to the 1906 incorporation of the Minneapolis Honeywell Regulator Company, the modern enterprise was formed through the 1999 merger of AlliedSignal and Honeywell Inc., with AlliedSignal assuming the Honeywell name. Today, the company operates primarily through four core business segments: Aerospace, Honeywell Building Technologies, Performance Materials and Technologies, and Safety and Productivity Solutions. Honeywell delivers industry-specific solutions that combine physical products with software to help aircraft, buildings, manufacturing plants, supply chains, and workers become more connected, efficient, and sustainable.
