The ticker PKO.WA corresponds to Powszechna Kasa Oszczędności Bank Polski S.A. (PKO Bank Polski), the largest commercial bank in Poland and a leading financial institution in Central and Eastern Europe. Traded on the Warsaw Stock Exchange, the bank provides a comprehensive range of financial services, including retail, corporate, and investment banking, as well as asset management and insurance products, serving millions of individual and institutional clients. 

Tracing its origins back to the founding of Pocztowa Kasa Oszczędności in 1919 by decree of Chief of State Józef Piłsudski, the institution played a foundational role in building Poland's modern banking system. Despite severe disruptions during World War II and the restructuring necessitated by the post-communist transition to a market economy, the bank maintained its core mission of promoting savings and financial inclusion. It underwent significant modernization and was successfully privatized through an initial public offering on the Warsaw Stock Exchange in 2004. Today, PKO Bank Polski leverages its rich heritage alongside advanced digital banking platforms to maintain its dominant market position and drive regional financial innovation.
