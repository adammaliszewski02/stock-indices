The ticker BNP.WA represents BNP Paribas Bank Polska S.A., a prominent financial institution listed on the Warsaw Stock Exchange and a core subsidiary of the global French banking group, BNP Paribas. The bank provides a comprehensive range of commercial, retail, and corporate banking services, alongside specialized financial solutions including asset management, leasing, and factoring. Operating extensively throughout Poland, the institution serves individual consumers, small and medium-sized enterprises, and major corporations. 

The bank’s heritage in the Polish market traces back to the establishment of Bank Gospodarki Żywnościowej (BGŻ), which historically focused on the agricultural sector. Through a series of strategic mergers and acquisitions—most notably the integration of BGŻ with BNP Paribas Polska in 2015 and the subsequent acquisition of Raiffeisen Polbank’s core banking business in 2019—the institution consolidated its market position to become one of the largest universal banks in Poland. Today, BNP Paribas Bank Polska combines its deep domestic market roots with the international network and financial strength of its parent group, driving innovation in digital banking and sustainable finance within the region.
