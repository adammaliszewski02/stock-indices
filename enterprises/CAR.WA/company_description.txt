The ticker CAR.WA corresponds to Inter Cars S.A., a leading Central and Eastern European distributor of automotive spare parts. Founded in Poland in 1990 as a limited liability company, the enterprise initially focused on supplying components for passenger cars. It achieved significant early milestones by opening its first foreign branch in the Czech Republic in 2004, followed by its initial public offering on the Warsaw Stock Exchange in 2004. Over subsequent decades, Inter Cars expanded its portfolio to include replacement parts for commercial vehicles, motorcycles, and agricultural machinery, alongside workshop equipment and diagnostic tools. Today, the company operates an extensive logistics network and digital ordering platforms, serving professional repair shops and retail customers across numerous European markets.
