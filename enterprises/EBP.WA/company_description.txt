The ticker EBP.WA refers to Erbud S.A., a prominent construction group listed on the Warsaw Stock Exchange. Founded in 1990, the company has grown into one of Northern Poland's leading general contractors, operating across diverse sectors including commercial, residential, public utility, and energy infrastructure. Erbud expanded its market presence through strategic diversification, notably entering the renewable energy sector via its subsidiary ONDE S.A., which specializes in the construction of wind and photovoltaic farms, as well as developing a strong footprint in the modular wooden construction market in Western Europe. Over more than three decades, the group has established a reputation for executing complex engineering projects, maintaining operations both domestically and internationally, and adapting to the evolving demands of sustainable construction and modern infrastructure development.
