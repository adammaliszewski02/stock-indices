Intel Corporation, traded under the ticker INTC, is a leading American multinational corporation and technology company. Founded in 1968 by semiconductor pioneers Robert Noyce and Gordon Moore, the company originally specialized in memory chips before inventing the world’s first commercial microprocessor, the Intel 4004, in 1971. This breakthrough catalyzed the personal computer revolution, establishing Intel as a dominant force in computing hardware. 

Today, Intel designs and manufactures microprocessors, motherboards, chipset components, and integrated circuits for global original equipment manufacturers. The company’s core business centers on providing advanced semiconductor products and data center solutions that power personal computers, cloud infrastructure, and enterprise networks. In recent years, Intel has significantly expanded its business model to include contract semiconductor manufacturing, known as foundry services, aiming to diversify revenue and strengthen domestic semiconductor supply chains.
