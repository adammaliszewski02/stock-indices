UnitedHealth Group Incorporated, traded under the ticker UNH, is a diversified American healthcare and well-being company operating through two distinct platforms, UnitedHealthcare and Optum. Tracing its origins to the foundation of its predecessor, Charter Med Incorporated, in 1974, the organization officially restructured into UnitedHealth Group in 1977. Headquartered in Minnetonka, Minnesota, it has evolved into the largest healthcare company in the world by revenue. UnitedHealthcare provides comprehensive health benefit plans and insurance services to individuals, employers, and Medicare and Medicaid beneficiaries. Concurrently, Optum delivers information and technology-enabled health services, operating pharmacy care services, care delivery systems, and data analytics. Together, these complementary business segments enable the enterprise to serve hundreds of millions of people globally, streamlining clinical operations, enhancing care quality, and reducing overall system costs.
