Netflix, Inc., traded under the ticker NFLX, is a leading global entertainment streaming service offering a vast library of feature films, documentaries, television series, and mobile games across a wide variety of genres and languages. Founded in 1997 by Reed Hastings and Marc Randolph in Scotts Valley, California, the company initially operated as a DVD-by-mail rental service. Netflix fundamentally disrupted the traditional home entertainment industry by pioneering the subscription-based digital streaming model in 2007. Over the following decade, the company transitioned from primarily distributing licensed content to becoming a premier producer of original programming, marked by the release of its first major original series, *House of Cards*, in 2013. Today, Netflix operates on a subscription-based revenue model, delivering on-demand media to hundreds of millions of paid members in over 190 countries. The company continues to evolve its platform through technological innovation, international market expansion, and the ongoing integration of advertising tiers and interactive gaming experiences.
