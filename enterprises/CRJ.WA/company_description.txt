The stock ticker CRJ.WA corresponds to CrowdedHero S.A., a dynamic financial technology company listed on the Warsaw Stock Exchange. The business operates a prominent crowd-investing platform designed to connect ambitious, high-growth startups and small-to-medium-sized enterprises with a diverse community of retail and institutional investors. By democratizing access to private equity markets, the company enables everyday individuals to invest in carefully vetted European ventures starting from low capital thresholds, while simultaneously providing emerging businesses with alternative capital-raising solutions. The enterprise leverages digital infrastructure to streamline the investment process, offering comprehensive services ranging from deal sourcing and regulatory compliance to portfolio management. While maintaining its core focus on equity crowdfunding, the firm continues to expand its technological capabilities and market reach within the European financial ecosystem, facilitating cross-border investment opportunities and supporting the growth of the entrepreneurial economy.
