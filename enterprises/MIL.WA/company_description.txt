The ticker MIL.WA refers to Millennium Bank SA, a prominent commercial bank listed on the Warsaw Stock Exchange. Operating primarily in Poland, the institution offers a comprehensive range of financial services to retail, corporate, and public sector clients, including banking accounts, loans, investment products, and insurance. The bank traces its origins to 1989, founded as Bank Inicjatyw Gospodarczych SA, making it one of the pioneering private banks established in Poland following the transition to a market economy. In 2003, Portugal’s Banco Comercial Português (BCP), which had been steadily increasing its stake since the 1990s, became the majority shareholder, leading to a rebranding under the Millennium name. Today, Millennium Bank SA operates as a universal bank, combining digital innovation with an extensive nationwide branch network to serve millions of customers, supported by the international expertise and backing of its Portuguese parent group.
