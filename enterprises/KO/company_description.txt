The Coca-Cola Company, traded under the ticker KO, is the world's largest beverage corporation, specializing in the manufacture, retail, and marketing of non-alcoholic beverage concentrates and syrups. Founded in 1892 by pharmacist John Stith Pemberton and later scaled into a global enterprise by businessman Asa Griggs Candler, the company is globally renowned for its flagship product, Coca-Cola, invented in Atlanta, Georgia. Over more than a century of operations, KO has evolved from a single carbonated soft drink into a total beverage company. Its expansive portfolio now encompasses over 200 brands, ranging from sparkling soft drinks to water, sports drinks, coffee, and teas, including globally recognized names like Sprite, Fanta, Dasani, and Minute Maid. Operating through a unique franchise model, The Coca-Cola Company primarily manufactures and sells concentrates, beverage bases, and syrups to authorized bottling partners worldwide, who then finish, package, merchandise, and distribute the final products.
