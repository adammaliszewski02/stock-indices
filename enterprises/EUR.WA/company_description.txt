The ticker EUR.WA refers to Erbud S.A., a prominent construction and engineering group based in Poland and listed on the Warsaw Stock Exchange. Established in 1990, the company has evolved from a regional contractor into a diversified general builder operating across various sectors, including commercial, residential, public utility, and energy infrastructure. Erbud delivers comprehensive construction services as a general contractor, with a strong emphasis on modern engineering standards and sustainable building practices. In addition to traditional construction, the group has strategically expanded into renewable energy, particularly through its involvement in the photovoltaic and wind sectors, positioning itself to address the growing demand for green infrastructure. The company also operates internationally, executing projects in several European markets, and is recognized for its robust operational capacity and adaptability within the competitive European construction landscape.
