Texas Instruments Incorporated, trading under the ticker TXN, is a global semiconductor company that designs, manufactures, tests, and sells analog and embedded processing chips for industrial, automotive, personal electronics, communications equipment, and enterprise systems markets. Founded in 1930 as Geophysical Service Incorporated, the firm initially provided seismic data for the petroleum industry before entering the defense electronics and semiconductor markets. A pivotal moment in its history occurred in 1958 when engineer Jack Kilby invented the first integrated circuit while working at the company, revolutionizing modern electronics. Texas Instruments subsequently played a major role in consumer technology by developing early commercial silicon transistors and pioneering hand-held calculators. Today, the Dallas-headquartered corporation focuses its business primarily on analog integrated circuits and embedded processors, leveraging its advanced internal manufacturing capacity and extensive portfolio of proprietary technologies to serve tens of thousands of customers worldwide.
