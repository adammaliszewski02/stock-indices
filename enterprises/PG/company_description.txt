The stock ticker PG refers to Procter & Gamble, a multinational consumer goods corporation headquartered in Cincinnati, Ohio. Founded in 1837 by William Procter and James Gamble as a small, family-operated soap and candle business, the company has evolved into one of the world's largest and most successful consumer packaged goods enterprises. Procter & Gamble operates globally, specializing in a wide range of branded personal care, family care, and household products. Its extensive portfolio features market-leading brands organized across key segments such as beauty, grooming, health care, fabric and home care, and baby, feminine, and family care. Well-known brand names under the corporate umbrella include Tide, Pampers, Gillette, Crest, Head & Shoulders, and Charmin. The company distributes its products in approximately 180 countries primarily through mass merchandisers, e-commerce platforms, grocery stores, membership club stores, and specialty beauty stores. Throughout its long history, Procter & Gamble has been recognized as a pioneer in modern marketing, brand management, and research and development within the consumer goods sector.
