General Electric (GE) is a global industrial leader that has undergone a significant transformation to focus on the energy sector. Following its strategic separation into independent entities, the company now operates primarily as GE Vernova, a powerhouse in power generation, wind energy, and electrification technologies. Historically, GE was founded in 1892 through the merger of Edison General Electric Company and Thomson-Houston Electric Company, with Thomas Edison serving as one of its original founders. For over a century, the company was a cornerstone of American industry, pioneering advancements in aviation, healthcare, and power systems while becoming a symbol of corporate diversification. Today, the company is dedicated to accelerating the global energy transition by providing the infrastructure necessary to deliver reliable, affordable, and sustainable electricity. By leveraging its deep engineering expertise and extensive installed base, GE continues to play a critical role in modernizing the world’s power grids and supporting the shift toward decarbonization.
