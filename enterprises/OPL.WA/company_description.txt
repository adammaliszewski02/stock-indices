The ticker OPL.WA corresponds to Orange Polska S.A., a leading telecommunications provider in Poland. Listed on the Warsaw Stock Exchange, the company traces its origins back to the state-owned enterprise Polska Poczta, Telegraf i Telefon, and later operated as Telekomunikacja Polska before rebranding to Orange Polska in 2013 following majority acquisition by the France Télécom group (now Orange SA). Orange Polska provides a comprehensive range of fixed and mobile telecommunication services, including high-speed fiber-optic internet, mobile telephony, television, and advanced ICT solutions for both individual consumers and enterprise clients. Operating one of the largest telecommunications infrastructures in the country, the company also delivers wholesale services to other market operators. In recent years, Orange Polska has strategically focused on expanding its fiber network footprint, digitizing customer service channels, and driving sustainable business practices to maintain its position as a cornerstone of Poland's digital economy.
