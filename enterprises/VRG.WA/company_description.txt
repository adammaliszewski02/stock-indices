The ticker VRG.WA represents VRG S.A., a prominent Polish retail group operating in the fashion and jewelry sectors. Headquartered in Kraków, the company traces its origins to 1989 with the establishment of Vistula, which later expanded through strategic acquisitions. Over the decades, VRG has evolved into a leading lifestyle enterprise in Poland, managing a diversified portfolio of well-known brands that includes Vistula, W.Kruk, Deni Cler, Wólczanka, and Bytom. The company’s core business model focuses on the design, distribution, and retail of high-end men’s and women’s apparel, tailored suits, accessories, and luxury jewelry through an extensive nationwide network of brick-and-mortar stores as well as robust e-commerce platforms. VRG S.A. is publicly listed on the Warsaw Stock Exchange, where it remains a recognized name in the Central European retail landscape.
