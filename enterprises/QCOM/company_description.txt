Qualcomm Incorporated, trading under the ticker QCOM, is a multinational semiconductor and telecommunications equipment company that designs and markets wireless telecommunications products and services. Founded in 1985 by Irwin Jacobs and six others, the company originally operated as a provider of satellite-based mobile communications systems before pioneering foundational Code Division Multiple Access (CDMA) digital wireless technology in the 1990s. Today, Qualcomm is a world leader in the development of 3G, 4G, and 5G wireless technologies. The company’s primary business model centers on licensing its extensive intellectual property portfolio to mobile device manufacturers globally and designing advanced integrated circuits, most notably its Snapdragon system-on-chip platforms, which power a vast majority of the world's smartphones. In recent years, Qualcomm has strategically expanded its operations beyond mobile telephony into high-growth sectors, including automotive platforms, the Internet of Things, and high-performance computing, thereby diversifying its revenue streams and reinforcing its position as a cornerstone of the modern connected economy.
