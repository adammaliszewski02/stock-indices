Salesforce, Inc., trading under the ticker CRM, is a global leader in customer relationship management technology. Founded in 1999 by Marc Benioff, Parker Harris, Dave Moellenhoff, and Frank Dominguez, the company pioneered the software-as-a-service delivery model by launching one of the earliest enterprise cloud computing platforms. Salesforce offers an integrated CRM platform, known as Customer 360, which unites sales, service, marketing, commerce, and IT teams to provide a single, shared view of every customer. Over the years, the company has significantly expanded its enterprise software portfolio through strategic acquisitions, including the additions of MuleSoft, Tableau, and Slack. Furthermore, Salesforce incorporates advanced artificial intelligence capabilities across its ecosystem through innovations like Einstein and its newer generative AI offerings. Driven by a foundational commitment to stakeholder capitalism and corporate philanthropy through its 1-1-1 model, Salesforce continues to empower organizations of all sizes to digitally transform, optimize operations, and deepen customer connections in an evolving digital economy.
