XTB S.A., identified by the stock ticker XTB.WA, is a prominent Polish multinational financial brokerage firm specializing in online trading and investment services. Founded in 2002 in Warsaw as X-Trade Brokers, the company initially focused on the over-the-counter derivatives market before expanding its operations internationally. Over the years, XTB evolved into a leading European fintech institution, driven by the 2004 launch of its proprietary trading platform, xStation. The firm completed its initial public offering on the Warsaw Stock Exchange in 2016. Today, XTB provides retail clients worldwide with access to a diverse range of financial instruments, including currencies, indices, commodities, cryptocurrencies, stocks, and exchange-traded funds. Operating through multiple regulated subsidiaries across Europe, Latin America, and the Middle East, the company combines advanced technological infrastructure with comprehensive educational resources to support global investors.
