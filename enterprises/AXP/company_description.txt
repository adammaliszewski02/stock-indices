American Express Company (AXP) is a globally integrated payments company that provides customers with access to products, insights, and experiences that enrich lives and build business success. Operating primarily through its eponymous brand, the company is a world leader in credit and charge cards, travel services, and digital payment solutions for consumers, small businesses, and large corporations. Founded in 1850 in Buffalo, New York, as an express mail transportation business by the merger of companies including Henry Wells and William G. Fargo, American Express eventually transitioned into financial services. It issued its first paper money order in 1882, introduced its signature Travelers Cheque in 1891, and launched its iconic charge card in 1958. Today, AXP generates revenue principally from discount revenue on merchant transactions, cardmember fees, and net interest income from its lending portfolios. Headquartered in New York City, the company differentiates itself through a closed-loop network model that directly connects buyers and sellers, an emphasis on premium lifestyle services, and a dedicated loyalty rewards program centered on cardmember engagement.
