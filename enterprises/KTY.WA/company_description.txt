The ticker KTY.WA refers to Kęty SA, a prominent industrial enterprise listed on the Warsaw Stock Exchange and headquartered in Kęty, Poland. Founded in 1953 as a state-owned aluminum plant originally named Zakłady Wyrobów Aluminium, the company underwent privatization and was subsequently listed on the Warsaw stock market in 1996. Today, Kęty operates as a holding company managing a diverse capital group specialized in the processing of aluminum profiles, the production of architectural systems, packaging materials, and flexible products, as well as the manufacturing of PVC window profiles. The company serves a wide array of industrial, construction, and consumer sectors across European and international markets. Through continuous modernization, strategic acquisitions, and a strong emphasis on export growth, Kęty has established itself as a leading Central European manufacturer in the light metals processing industry, recognized for its operational efficiency, technological advancement, and consistent commitment to sustainable business practices.
