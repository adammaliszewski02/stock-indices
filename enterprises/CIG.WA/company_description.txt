The ticker CIG.WA corresponds to CI Games SE, a prominent Polish video game developer, publisher, and distributor founded in 2002 under the name City Interactive. Headquartered in Warsaw, the company initially focused on budget-friendly titles before shifting its strategy toward developing high-profile, globally recognized role-playing games and tactical shooters. CI Games achieved international recognition with its critically acclaimed franchise *Sniper Ghost Warrior*, which launched in 2010 and grew into the company's flagship intellectual property. Building upon this success, the company expanded its portfolio into dark fantasy action role-playing games with the release of *Lords of the Fallen* in 2014, followed by a major reboot of the same title in 2023. CI Games operates globally, distributing its interactive entertainment content across major gaming platforms including personal computers and current-generation consoles. The company has been publicly traded on the Warsaw Stock Exchange since 2007, reflecting its growth from a local budget publisher into an established player in the international video game development sector.
