The ticker AGO.WA refers to Agora S.A., a prominent media group listed on the Warsaw Stock Exchange. Founded in 1989 and headquartered in Warsaw, Poland, the company initially established its market presence by launching *Gazeta Wyborcza*, which grew to become one of the country's leading daily newspapers. Over the decades, Agora evolved from a traditional print publisher into a diversified modern media conglomerate. 

Today, Agora's business operations span multiple sectors of the Polish media and entertainment landscape. Its portfolio includes the management of prominent radio stations such as Radio ZET, and the operation of Helios, which constitutes one of the largest cinema chains in Poland. Additionally, the company engages in outdoor advertising through its subsidiary AMS, digital and internet activities, and film production and distribution via Next Film. Through this integrated ecosystem, Agora delivers comprehensive content, news, and entertainment across print, digital, audio, and cinematic platforms, maintaining a significant influence on the Polish media market.
