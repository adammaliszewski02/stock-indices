Ticker MMM represents 3M Company, a prominent American multinational conglomerate. Founded in 1902 in Two Harbors, Minnesota, as the Minnesota Mining and Manufacturing Company, the enterprise initially focused on mining abrasive minerals before evolving into a diversified manufacturing powerhouse. Today, 3M operates across multiple sectors, including safety and industrial, transportation and electronics, health care, and consumer goods. The company is globally recognized for its culture of continuous innovation and research and development, having invented iconic products such as Scotch-Brite scouring pads, Post-it Notes, and Scotch brand transparent tape. Headquartered in Maplewood, Minnesota, 3M applies science and technology to develop thousands of transformative products designed to improve daily life, serving customers in nearly every major industry worldwide.
