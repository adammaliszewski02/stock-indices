Abbott Laboratories (ticker: ABT) is a leading, globally diversified healthcare company headquartered in Abbott Park, Illinois. Founded in 1888 by physician Wallace C. Abbott, the company originally specialized in producing alkaloid granules. Over more than a century, Abbott evolved from a pharmaceutical manufacturer into a multifaceted enterprise. Today, its core business operations are structured around four primary segments: medical devices, established pharmaceuticals, diagnostic testing systems, and nutritional products. The company is widely recognized for developing breakthrough health technologies, including advanced glucose monitoring systems, cardiovascular devices, and molecular diagnostics. Abbott operates in more than 160 countries, employing tens of thousands of people dedicated to improving global health outcomes through continuous scientific innovation and accessible healthcare solutions.
