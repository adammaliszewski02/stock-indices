The stock ticker BA corresponds to The Boeing Company, a prominent American multinational corporation founded in 1916 by William Boeing in Seattle, Washington. Boeing is a global aerospace leader primarily engaged in the design, development, manufacture, and sale of commercial jetliners, military aircraft, satellites, and missile defense systems. Operating through major divisions including Commercial Airplanes and Defense, Space & Security, the company also provides extensive global fleet support, maintenance, and training services to airline and government customers in more than 150 countries. As one of the world's largest aerospace manufacturers and a leading defense contractor, Boeing is a major exporter in the United States and a foundational component of the nation's industrial and technological infrastructure.
