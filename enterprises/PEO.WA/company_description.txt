Bank Pekao S.A., traded under the ticker PEO.WA on the Warsaw Stock Exchange, is one of the largest financial institutions in Central and Eastern Europe. Founded in 1929, the bank has a long-standing history as a cornerstone of the Polish banking sector, originally established to provide financial services to Poles living abroad. Today, it operates as a universal bank offering a comprehensive range of retail, corporate, and investment banking services. Its business model focuses on providing digital banking solutions, credit products, and asset management services to millions of individual and institutional clients. As a systemic entity in the Polish economy, Bank Pekao maintains a significant market share in deposits and loans, supported by an extensive network of branches and advanced technological infrastructure. The bank is widely recognized for its iconic bison logo, which has served as a symbol of its stability and strength for decades.
