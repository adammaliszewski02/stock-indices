ASML Holding N.V., traded under the ticker ASML, is a multinational corporation headquartered in Veldhoven, Netherlands, and a leading global supplier to the semiconductor industry. The company specializes in the development and manufacturing of advanced photolithography systems, which are essential equipment used by chipmakers to etch microscopic circuit patterns onto silicon wafers. ASML was founded in 1984 as a joint venture between ASM International and Philips, originally operating under the name ASM Lithography. Over the decades, the company pioneered the commercialization of ultraviolet and extreme ultraviolet lithography technologies. These technological breakthroughs enabled the continuous miniaturization and increased processing power of microchips found in virtually all modern electronic devices, including smartphones, computers, and artificial intelligence infrastructure. Today, ASML plays a foundational and irreplaceable role in the global semiconductor supply chain, partnering with major integrated circuit manufacturers worldwide to sustain the advancement of Moore's Law.
