Lowe’s Companies, Inc. (NYSE: LOW) is a prominent American home improvement retailer that operates a comprehensive network of retail stores offering products for maintenance, repair, home remodeling, and property decoration. The company caters to diverse customer segments, including do-it-yourself homeowners, renters, and professional contractors, providing an extensive inventory ranging from building materials and appliances to tools, hardware, and lawn and garden care supplies. 

The enterprise traces its origins back to 1921 when Lucius Smith Lowe founded a small hardware store in North Wilkesboro, North Carolina. Following Lowe's death, the business was inherited by his daughter Ruth and managed by her brother James G. Buchan, who later sold his share to his brother-in-law, Carl Buchan, in 1940. Carl Buchan was instrumental in modernizing the company, pioneering the shift toward a cash-and-carry retail model that bypassed traditional wholesaler markups, which fueled rapid expansion across the American Southeast. Lowe’s officially incorporated in 1952, went public in 1961, and has since grown into a Fortune 50 multinational corporation operating hundreds of stores across North America while integrating robust omnichannel digital platforms to support modern consumer demands.
