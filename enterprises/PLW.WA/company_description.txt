PLW.WA is the stock ticker for PlayWay S.A., a prominent Polish video game publisher and holding company based in Warsaw, founded in 2002 by Krzysztof Kostowski. PlayWay operates a unique and highly decentralized business model centered on the incubation and production of independent video games, primarily within the simulation, strategy, and builder genres. The company functions through a network of dozens of semi-independent development studios operating under the PlayWay umbrella, allowing multiple games to be produced simultaneously with low initial budgets. PlayWay gained widespread international acclaim and commercial success with the 2018 release of *House Flipper*, followed by other popular titles such as *Car Mechanic Simulator* and *Gunsmith Simulator*. The company successfully debuted on the Warsaw Stock Exchange in 2016, transitioning from a local distributor and developer into a major global player in the PC and console gaming markets, renowned for its prolific output and efficient cost-management strategies.
