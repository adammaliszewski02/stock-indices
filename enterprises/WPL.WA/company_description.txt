The ticker WPL.WA refers to Wirtualna Polska Holding S.A., a prominent Polish media and e-commerce group listed on the Warsaw Stock Exchange. The company traces its origins back to 1995 with the launch of Wirtualna Polska, one of Poland's pioneering web portals. Today, Wirtualna Polska operates a diversified digital business model centered around two main pillars: media and e-commerce. Its media segment encompasses a vast portfolio of prominent news portals, lifestyle websites, and specialized vertical content services, supported by a growing digital advertising network. Concurrently, the e-commerce segment comprises leading marketplace platforms operating in high-growth sectors such as travel, housing, and financial services. Through continuous strategic acquisitions and technological development, the company has successfully transitioned from a traditional web portal into a leading technology-driven digital media and commerce ecosystem in Central and Eastern Europe.
