The stock ticker GS represents The Goldman Sachs Group, Inc., a leading global financial institution founded in 1869 by Marcus Goldman in New York City. The firm transitioned from a pioneer in commercial paper to a dominant global force in investment banking, securities, and investment management. Goldman Sachs operates through four primary business segments: Investment Banking, Global Markets, Asset Management, and Wealth Management. Serving a diverse client base that includes corporations, financial institutions, governments, and high-net-worth individuals, the firm provides strategic advisory services, underwriting, market-making, and asset allocation. Historically a private partnership, Goldman Sachs completed its initial public offering in 1999. During the 2008 global financial crisis, the firm converted to a bank holding company and subsequently expanded its retail banking footprint through the launch of Marcus by Goldman Sachs in 2016, later refining its strategic focus back toward its core institutional and wealth management strengths. Today, Goldman Sachs remains a cornerstone of the international financial system, renowned for its analytical rigor, market influence, and extensive global network.
