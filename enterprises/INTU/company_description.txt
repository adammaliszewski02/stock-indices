Intuit Inc. (ticker: INTU) is a global financial technology platform that provides prominent financial management, tax preparation, and accounting software solutions for consumers, small businesses, and accounting professionals. Founded in 1983 by Scott Cook and Tom Proulx with the creation of the Quicken personal finance program, the company has grown through strategic innovation and key acquisitions. Headquartered in Mountain View, California, Intuit's core product ecosystem currently features widely recognized flagship brands including TurboTax for consumer tax filing, QuickBooks for small business financial management, and Credit Karma for personal credit monitoring and financial services. Additionally, the company acquired Mailchimp in 2021 to expand its offerings into automated marketing and customer growth solutions. By leveraging advanced data analytics and artificial intelligence, Intuit serves approximately 100 million customers worldwide, with a mission to power prosperity around the globe by helping individuals and enterprises overcome their most significant financial challenges.
