The ticker 11B.WA refers to 11 bit studios S.A., a prominent Polish video game development, publishing, and investment company based in Warsaw. Founded in 2010 by industry veterans largely originating from CD Projekt and Metropolis Software, the company focuses on creating critically acclaimed titles that emphasize meaningful storytelling and mature themes. 11 bit studios gained global recognition with the 2014 release of *This War of Mine*, a survival game inspired by the siege of Sarajevo, which was later introduced into the Polish school reading curriculum as the first video game to achieve such status. Their portfolio further expanded with the successful launch of the society survival game *Frostpunk* in 2018, establishing the studio's reputation for high-quality, atmospheric entertainment. Beyond developing its own intellectual properties, the company operates a publishing label, 11 bit publishing, which supports external independent developers with funding, marketing, and production expertise. Listed on the Warsaw Stock Exchange, 11 bit studios continues to be a leading force in the independent and mid-tier gaming sector, balancing artistic vision with strong commercial performance.
