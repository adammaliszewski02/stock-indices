Amazon.com, Inc. (AMZN) is a multinational technology company focusing on e-commerce, cloud computing, online advertising, digital streaming, and artificial intelligence. Founded by Jeff Bezos in a garage in Bellevue, Washington, in July 1994, the enterprise began as an online marketplace for books before rapidly expanding into a vast array of consumer goods and services. A pivotal milestone in its history was the 2006 launch of Amazon Web Services (AWS), which pioneered cloud infrastructure services and became a primary driver of corporate profitability. Over the decades, Amazon transformed global retail through innovations like Prime membership and automated logistics, while also expanding into consumer electronics, grocery retail through the acquisition of Whole Foods, and entertainment production. Today, it ranks among the world’s most valuable brands and largest private employers, continuously shaping the modern digital economy.
