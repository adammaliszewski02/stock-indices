Gilead Sciences, Inc., trading under the stock ticker GILD, is a research-based biopharmaceutical company that discovers, develops, and commercializes innovative medicines in areas of unmet medical need. Founded in 1987 in California, the company initially focused on antiviral technology. Gilead achieved significant historical milestones by transforming the treatment and management of life-threatening infectious diseases, most notably through the introduction of breakthrough therapies for human immunodeficiency virus and chronic hepatitis C. Over the decades, the company has expanded its therapeutic portfolio beyond virology through strategic acquisitions and internal research. Today, Gilead’s global operations encompass advanced treatments in oncology, inflammatory diseases, and cardiovascular conditions, positioning it as a leading force in the modern biopharmaceutical industry dedicated to improving patient outcomes worldwide.
