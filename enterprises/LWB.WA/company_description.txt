The ticker LWB.WA refers to Lubelski Węgiel Bogdanka S.A., a prominent hard coal mining company headquartered in Bogdanka, Poland. The company is primarily engaged in the extraction and commercialization of thermal coal, which is predominantly supplied to the commercial power sector in eastern and northeastern Poland. Founded in 1982, the enterprise began commercial production in 1982 and has since evolved into one of the most cost-efficient and technologically advanced mining operations in the Polish hard coal sector. In 2009, the company completed its initial public offering on the Warsaw Stock Exchange. Since 2015, it has operated as a key subsidiary of the Enea Group, one of Poland's leading energy conglomerates, which integrated Bogdanka’s coal production capabilities to secure its fuel supply chain for electricity and heat generation.
