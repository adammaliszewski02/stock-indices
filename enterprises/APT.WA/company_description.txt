The ticker APT.WA corresponds to Aplisens S.A., a prominent Polish enterprise specializing in the design, manufacture, and distribution of industrial instrumentation and measurement equipment. Founded in 1992, the company has established itself as a leading manufacturer of pressure transmitters, differential pressure transmitters, level probes, and temperature sensors utilized across a diverse range of industries, including power generation, chemical processing, oil and gas, marine, and food production. Aplisens operates through an extensive domestic and international network of subsidiaries and distributors, serving customers worldwide. The company is publicly listed on the Warsaw Stock Exchange, where its shares are traded under the ticker symbol APT.
