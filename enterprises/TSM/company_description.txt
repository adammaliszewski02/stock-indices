Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Company Limited, commonly known as TSM, is the world's largest dedicated independent semiconductor foundry. Founded in 1987 by Morris Chang, the company pioneered the pure-play foundry business model, meaning it manufactures chips designed by other companies rather than designing its own branded products. Headquartered in Hsinchu Science Park, Taiwan, TSM has grown to become a cornerstone of the global technology supply chain. It manufactures advanced integrated circuits used in a vast array of applications, including high-performance computing, smartphones, artificial intelligence, automotive electronics, and the Internet of Things. By continuously leading the industry in advanced manufacturing process technologies, TSM produces chips for major global technology innovators, including Apple, Nvidia, and Qualcomm.
