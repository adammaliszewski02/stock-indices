The ticker MDV.WA refers to Medinex S.A., a company listed on the Warsaw Stock Exchange. Medinex operates primarily within the healthcare sector, focusing on the distribution of medical equipment, supplies, and pharmaceuticals to hospitals, clinics, and individual healthcare providers. The company acts as an intermediary between global medical technology manufacturers and the domestic healthcare market, ensuring the steady supply of essential clinical resources. In addition to medical distribution, Medinex has historically engaged in investment activities and the provision of specialized services tailored to the medical industry, supporting the modernization and operational efficiency of healthcare facilities.
