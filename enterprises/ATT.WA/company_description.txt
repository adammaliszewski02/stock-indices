The stock ticker ATT.WA corresponds to ATM Grupa S.A., a prominent Polish media and entertainment enterprise headquartered in Bielany Wrocławskie. Founded in 1991, the company has evolved from a regional video production facility into one of the largest independent television producers in Poland and Central Europe. ATM Grupa operates primarily in the creation and production of television series, game shows, feature films, and documentaries for major national broadcasters. Additionally, the company manages substantial television broadcasting infrastructure, offers comprehensive post-production and rental services for film equipment, and operates real estate assets that include modern television studios. Over decades of operation, ATM Grupa has built a robust catalog of original entertainment content, successfully adapting to the digital transformation of the media landscape by expanding its production capabilities to meet the growing demands of streaming platforms and modern digital distribution channels.
