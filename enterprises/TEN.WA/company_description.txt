TEN.WA is the stock ticker for Ten Square Games S.A., a prominent Polish developer and publisher of mobile free-to-play games, listed on the Warsaw Stock Exchange. Founded in 2010 by Maciej Popowicz and Arkadiusz Pernal, the company initially focused on browser-based games before successfully transitioning to the mobile market. Ten Square Games is globally recognized for its flagship hobbyist titles, most notably *Fishing Clash*—released in 2017—and *Hunting Clash*, which have driven the company’s significant international growth and revenue. Headquartered in Wrocław, Poland, the enterprise has expanded its studio footprint through strategic acquisitions and equity investments in other gaming companies to diversify its portfolio. Ten Square Games focuses on creating accessible, community-driven simulation games that appeal to outdoor enthusiasts and casual gamers worldwide, supported by in-app purchase monetization models and live-operations strategies.
