RTX Corporation is an aerospace and defense multinational conglomerate headquartered in Arlington, Virginia. Formed in 2020 through the merger of United Technologies Corporation and the Raytheon Company, the enterprise subsequently rebranded as RTX in 2023. Operating through three primary segments, the company is a leading global provider of advanced technology and services for the commercial aerospace, defense, and intelligence industries. Pratt & Whitney designs and manufactures aircraft engines and auxiliary power units, Collins Aerospace delivers integrated aerospace and defense systems, and Raytheon specializes in defense electronics, air defense systems, missiles, and cybersecurity solutions. Serving commercial airlines, militaries, and government agencies worldwide, RTX plays a critical role in global infrastructure, defense readiness, and aviation innovation.
