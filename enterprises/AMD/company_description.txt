Advanced Micro Devices, Inc. (AMD) is a prominent American multinational semiconductor company that develops computer processors and related technologies for business and consumer markets. Founded in 1969 by Jerry Sanders and a group of colleagues, the company initially focused on logic chips before entering the memory chip market in the 1970s and launching its first proprietary processor, the AM9080, in 1975. Over the decades, AMD transitioned from a secondary merchant of semiconductor products to a primary innovator in high-performance computing. Today, the company designs and produces microprocessors, graphics processing units (GPUs), motherboards, and embedded processors. AMD serves a diverse clientele across the personal computing, gaming, and data center sectors. A major milestone in its modern history was the 2006 acquisition of ATI Technologies, which significantly strengthened its capabilities in graphics and visual processing. Furthermore, its acquisition of Xilinx in 2022 expanded its footprint in adaptive computing and artificial intelligence. Driven by continuous technological advancement, AMD remains a key competitor to industry giants like Intel and Nvidia in the global semiconductor landscape.
