LPP SA, traded under the ticker LPP.WA on the Warsaw Stock Exchange, is a prominent Polish multinational retail clothing company headquartered in Gdańsk. Founded in 1991 by Marek Piechocki and Jerzy Lubianiec, the company initially operated under the name Master Sp. z o.o. before adopting its current identity and launching its flagship brand, Reserved, in 1998. 

LPP specializes in the design, distribution, and retail of apparel and accessories. Its diverse portfolio currently encompasses five well-known fashion brands: Reserved, Mohito, House, Cropp, and Sinsay. The company manages a vast omnichannel sales network comprising physical stores across numerous European and Asian countries, alongside robust e-commerce platforms for each of its brands. Over the decades, LPP has evolved from a local importer into one of the fastest-growing clothing retailers in Central and Eastern Europe, driven by strategic investments in logistics and digital transformation.
