The ticker GRX.WA refers to Grodno S.A., a prominent Polish distributor and supplier of electrical equipment, materials, and renewable energy solutions. Founded in 1990, the company has evolved from a regional wholesaler into a major nationwide player in the electrotechnical market. Grodno’s business model centers on the wholesale and retail distribution of cables, wires, lighting systems, switchgear, and industrial automation components. In recent years, the company has strategically expanded its portfolio to capitalize on the green energy transition, becoming a leading distributor of photovoltaic systems, heat pumps, and electric vehicle charging infrastructure. Grodno serves a diverse client base that includes electrical installers, industrial enterprises, and retail customers through both its traditional brick-and-mortar branch network and its advanced e-commerce platforms. The company's shares have been listed on the Warsaw Stock Exchange, where it trades under the ticker GRX.WA.
