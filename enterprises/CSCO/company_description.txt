Cisco Systems, Inc., identified by the ticker CSCO, is a worldwide leader in information technology, networking, and cybersecurity solutions. Founded in December 1984 by Stanford University computer scientists Leonard Bosack and Sandra Lerner, the company revolutionized the industry by developing the first commercially successful multi-protocol router, which enabled disparate computer networks to communicate seamlessly. Headquartered in San Jose, California, Cisco designs, manufactures, and sells Internet Protocol-based networking products and other services related to the communications and information technology industry. Over the decades, the company has expanded its core enterprise networking business through strategic innovation and acquisitions into adjacent markets, including cloud security, collaboration software via Webex, Internet of Things solutions, and observability software. Today, Cisco empowers businesses of all sizes to securely connect, automate, and digitize their operations, serving as a foundational infrastructure provider for the modern global internet and enterprise digital environments.
