The ticker ASB.WA refers to ASBIS Enterprises PLC, a leading value-added distributor, developer, and provider of IT products, solutions, and services across emerging markets. Founded in 1995 in Belarus and subsequently headquartered in Cyprus, the company has grown into a major player in the technology sector, operating extensively throughout Central and Eastern Europe, the Baltic states, the Caucasus, Central Asia, and the Middle East. ASBIS specializes in the distribution of a comprehensive portfolio of IT hardware and software, encompassing components, enterprise storage solutions, servers, networking equipment, and consumer electronics from globally recognized manufacturers. In addition to traditional distribution, the company focuses on value-added services, system integration, and the development of proprietary brands such as Prestigio and Canyon. ASBIS has been listed on the Warsaw Stock Exchange since 2007 under the ticker ASB.WA, leveraging this public platform to support its continued regional expansion and strategic partnerships within the global technology supply chain.
