The ticker BHW.WA represents Bank Handlowy w Warszawie S.A., one of Poland's oldest and most prominent financial institutions. Headquartered in Warsaw, the bank provides a comprehensive range of commercial and retail banking services. Its core business encompasses corporate banking, investment banking, asset management, and personal financial services, serving both individual consumers and major domestic and international enterprises. 

Founded in 1870 by industrialist Leopold Kronenberg as Bank Handlowy w Warszawie, the institution played a pivotal role in financing Poland's industrial development during the late 19th and early 20th centuries. Despite severe disruptions and nationalization during and after the Second World War, the bank successfully re-established its independent commercial operations in the post-communist transition. In 1997, its shares were listed on the Warsaw Stock Exchange. A major milestone occurred in 2001 when Citibank, through its parent entity Citigroup, acquired a controlling stake in the institution. Consequently, the bank integrated its operations with Citibank's Polish presence and currently operates prominently under the Citibank brand in Poland, combining its rich local heritage with the extensive global network of one of the world's leading financial services corporations.
