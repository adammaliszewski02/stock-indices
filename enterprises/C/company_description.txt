Citigroup Inc., trading under the stock ticker C, is a multinational investment banking and financial services corporation headquartered in New York City. Tracing its origins back to the 1812 founding of the City Bank of New York, and subsequently through the 1998 landmark merger of Citicorp and Travelers Group, the company operates as one of the major legacy institutions of modern American banking. Today, Citigroup provides a comprehensive range of financial products and services, including consumer banking, credit cards, corporate and investment banking, securities brokerage, and wealth management. Serving millions of consumer, corporate, institutional, and government clients worldwide, the firm operates through two primary segments: Institutional Clients Group and Personal Banking and Wealth Management. Through its extensive global network spanning numerous countries and jurisdictions, Citigroup facilitates cross-border commerce and capital flows, solidifying its status as a foundational pillar of the global financial architecture.
