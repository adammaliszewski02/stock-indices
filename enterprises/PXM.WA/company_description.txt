The ticker PXM.WA refers to Polimex-Mostostal S.A., a prominent Polish construction and engineering group headquartered in Warsaw. Traded on the Warsaw Stock Exchange, the company operates primarily in the energy, industrial, infrastructure, and oil and gas sectors, delivering comprehensive investment services from design and engineering to general contracting. 

Tracing its roots back to post-World War II industrial reconstruction efforts, the modern enterprise was formed through the consolidation of several historical Polish construction and manufacturing firms, most notably the merger of Polimex-Cekop and Mostostal Siedlce in the early 2000s. Over the decades, Polimex-Mostostal has played a critical role in executing major national infrastructure projects, including the construction of power plants, motorways, bridges, and industrial facilities. Today, the company remains a key player in Central Europe's construction market, focusing on modernizing energy generation and transport networks to meet contemporary environmental and structural standards.
