PKP Cargo S.A., listed on the Warsaw Stock Exchange under the ticker PKP.WA, is the largest rail freight operator in Poland and the second-largest in the European Union. Established in 2001 following the restructuring of the state-owned Polish State Railways (PKP), the company provides comprehensive logistics services, including rail transport, intermodal shipping, and terminal operations. Its business model centers on the domestic and international carriage of bulk commodities, such as coal, ores, and construction materials, alongside containerized freight. Headquartered in Warsaw, PKP Cargo leverages an extensive network of rolling stock and infrastructure to facilitate trade across the Trans-European Transport Network. While the company has historically served as a cornerstone of Poland’s industrial supply chain, it has faced significant financial and operational challenges in recent years, leading to ongoing restructuring efforts aimed at modernizing its fleet and improving competitive efficiency within the evolving European logistics market.
