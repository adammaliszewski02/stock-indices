The ticker DOM.WA refers to Dom Development S.A., a leading residential real estate developer listed on the Warsaw Stock Exchange. Established in 1996, the company has grown to become one of the largest housing developers in Poland, primarily focusing on large-scale multi-family residential projects. Its core operations encompass the entire development cycle, including land acquisition, architectural design, construction management, and post-sales customer service. Dom Development primarily targets the middle-to-upper-income residential segments in Poland's major metropolitan areas, most notably Warsaw, Wrocław, and the Tri-City region. Over the decades, the company has delivered tens of thousands of apartments, earning a strong market reputation for financial stability, construction quality, and corporate governance. As a publicly traded entity, it has consistently remained a key component of Poland's main stock market indices, reflecting the broader growth and maturation of the Polish residential property market since the country's economic transition.
