GOOGL is the ticker symbol for Alphabet Inc., a multinational technology conglomerate founded in October 2015 as the corporate parent of Google and several former subsidiaries. Google itself originated in 1998 as a web search engine developed by Stanford University doctoral students Larry Page and Sergey Brin. Over the past two decades, the company has expanded far beyond its foundational search capabilities to become a dominant force in global technology. Alphabet’s core business operations encompass internet search, online advertising, cloud computing, software, hardware, and artificial intelligence. Key revenue drivers include the Google advertising network, Google Cloud services, and consumer hardware products like Pixel devices and Nest home systems. Additionally, Alphabet operates "Other Bets," a portfolio of early-stage and exploratory ventures focused on breakthrough technologies, which includes the autonomous driving company Waymo and the life sciences research organization Verily. Guided by its mission to organize the world's information and make it universally accessible and useful, Alphabet maintains a leading global market position through continuous innovation in digital services and enterprise solutions.
