The stock ticker AMB.WA corresponds to Ambra SA, a leading producer, importer, and distributor of wines in Central and Eastern Europe. Based in Poland, the company operates primarily in Poland, the Czech Republic, Slovakia, and Romania. Ambra’s business encompasses a diverse portfolio of alcoholic beverages, ranging from sparkling wines and vermouths to ciders and table wines. Notable brands within its portfolio include Fresco, Piccolo, Dorato, and Cidre Lubelski. 

The company's origins trace back to the privatization and restructuring of the Polish wine industry in the early 1990s, evolving from a domestic distributor into a prominent regional market leader. Ambra has been publicly traded on the Warsaw Stock Exchange under the ticker AMB since 2005. The company focuses on brand building, continuous product innovation tailored to changing consumer preferences, and sustainable operational practices across its manufacturing and distribution supply chain.
