AbbVie Inc., trading under the ticker ABBV, is a leading global biopharmaceutical company dedicated to discovering, developing, and delivering advanced therapies for some of the world's most complex and serious medical conditions. The company focuses its research and commercial efforts primarily on immunology, oncology, neuroscience, eye care, and aesthetics. AbbVie’s core business model centers on creating innovative prescription medicines that address significant unmet medical needs. Historically, AbbVie was established in January 2013 as a spin-off from Abbott Laboratories, inheriting Abbott's research-based pharmaceutical operations and its flagship blockbuster drug, Humira. Following its separation, AbbVie significantly expanded its therapeutic portfolio and market presence, notably through its strategic acquisition of Allergan in 2020. This major acquisition diversified the company's offerings into medical aesthetics and neuroscience while reinforcing its leadership in immunology. Headquartered in North Chicago, Illinois, AbbVie continues to invest heavily in research and development to sustain its robust drug pipeline and maintain its position as a premier enterprise in the global healthcare sector.
