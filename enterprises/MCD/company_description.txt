MCD is the stock ticker for McDonald's Corporation, the world's leading global foodservice retailer. The company primarily operates and franchises McDonald's restaurants, which are globally renowned for quick-service meals centered around hamburgers, french fries, and breakfast items. 

The enterprise was founded in 1940 by brothers Richard and Maurice McDonald when they opened their first restaurant in San Bernardino, California. The business model transformed significantly after businessman Ray Kroc joined the company as a franchise agent in 1955, eventually purchasing the enterprise in 1961. Kroc pioneered standardized restaurant operations, rapid service, and stringent quality controls, laying the foundation for modern fast-food franchising. 

Today, McDonald's generates revenue through a combination of company-operated restaurant sales and franchise fees, rent, and royalties paid by independent franchisees. Operating in over 100 countries, the corporation utilizes its massive real estate portfolio and iconic brand recognition to maintain its position as a dominant force in the global hospitality and retail sectors.
