Danaher Corporation, trading under the ticker DHR, is a globally diversified science and technology innovator that designs, manufactures, and markets professional, medical, industrial, and commercial products and services. Founded in 1984 by brothers Steven and Mitchell Rales, the company initially operated as a real estate investment trust under the name DMG, Inc., before adopting the Danaher name in 1984, inspired by a Montana river where the founders fished. Danaher subsequently executed a strategic transformation into an industrial manufacturing conglomerate, eventually pivoting toward the life sciences, diagnostics, and biotechnology sectors. Today, the corporation operates through three primary segments: biotechnology, life sciences, and diagnostics. Its portfolio comprises globally recognized brands that provide analytical instruments, consumables, and software utilized in drug discovery, biopharmaceutical production, and clinical healthcare laboratories. Danaher’s operational success is widely attributed to the Danaher Business System, a proprietary continuous improvement framework focused on driving sustainable excellence in quality, delivery, and cost.
