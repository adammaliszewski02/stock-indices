The ticker TPE.WA represents Tauron Polska Energia S.A., one of the largest energy enterprises in Poland and a dominant player in the country’s power sector. Headquartered in Katowice, the company is listed on the Warsaw Stock Exchange and operates primarily as a holding company managing an integrated energy supply chain. Tauron's core business encompasses the generation of electricity and heat, coal mining, as well as the distribution and supply of electricity to millions of end-users, including residential, commercial, and industrial clients. The group has increasingly focused on developing renewable energy sources to align with broader European energy transition goals. Tracing its origins to the consolidation of key regional energy assets in southern Poland, Tauron officially formed as a joint-stock company in 2006 and completed its initial public offering on the Warsaw Stock Exchange in 2010. Today, the Polish State Treasury remains the majority shareholder, reflecting the strategic importance of the company to Poland's national energy security and infrastructure.
