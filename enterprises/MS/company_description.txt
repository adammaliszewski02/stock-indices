Morgan Stanley, identified by the stock ticker MS, is a leading global financial services firm headquartered in New York City. The company operates through three primary segments: Institutional Securities, Wealth Management, and Investment Management. It provides a comprehensive range of financial products and services to a diverse client base, including corporations, governments, financial institutions, and individuals. 

Tracing its roots back to 1935, the firm was founded by Henry Sturgis Morgan and Harold Stanley, alongside other partners, following the passage of the Glass-Steagall Act, which required the separation of commercial and investment banking. Morgan Stanley initially established itself as a premier investment bank, playing a pivotal role in major corporate underwritings and restructuring transactions. Over the decades, the company significantly expanded its footprint through strategic acquisitions, most notably the 1997 merger with Dean Witter, Discover & Co., which bolstered its retail brokerage capabilities, and the 2009 acquisition of a controlling stake in Smith Barney. Today, Morgan Stanley remains a cornerstone of the global financial architecture, continually adapting to market evolution and regulatory changes while maintaining its prominence in global capital markets and wealth advisory services.
