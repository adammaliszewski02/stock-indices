ConocoPhillips, traded under the ticker COP, is one of the world's largest independent upstream oil and gas exploration and production companies. Headquartered in Houston, Texas, the company focuses on the discovery, development, and extraction of crude oil and natural gas across global assets. ConocoPhillips traces its corporate roots back to 1875 with the founding of Continental Oil and Transportation by Isaac E. Blake. Over the decades, the enterprise expanded through major industry consolidations, most notably the 2002 merger of Conoco Inc. and Phillips Petroleum Company. In 2012, the organization executed a strategic restructuring by spinning off its downstream refining and marketing operations into an independent entity known as Phillips 66, allowing ConocoPhillips to operate as a pure-play exploration and production company. Today, the corporation manages a diverse global portfolio encompassing conventional and unconventional reservoirs, liquefied natural gas projects, and oil sands developments, positioning itself as a central player in the international energy sector.
