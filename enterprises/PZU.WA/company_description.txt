Powszechny Zakład Ubezpieczeń S.A., traded under the ticker PZU.WA, is the largest financial institution and insurance company in Poland, as well as one of the largest financial groups in Central and Eastern Europe. Tracing its origins back to 1803 with the establishment of Poland's first mutual insurance institution, the modern company operates primarily through the PZU Group. The enterprise provides a comprehensive range of insurance products, including life, health, and property insurance, alongside asset management, banking, and pension services. Headquartered in Warsaw, PZU has historically played a foundational role in the development of the Polish insurance market and has been publicly listed on the Warsaw Stock Exchange since 2010.
