SLB, historically known as Schlumberger, is the world's leading oilfield services company, providing comprehensive technology, integrated project management, and information solutions to the global oil and gas exploration and production industry. Founded in 1926 by brothers Conrad and Marcel Schlumberger in the Alsace region of France, the company pioneered electrical well logging to locate subsurface oil and gas reserves. Over nearly a century of operations, SLB expanded its portfolio to encompass well construction, reservoir performance, well intervention, and production systems. In recent years, the company has leveraged its technological expertise to transition toward a broader energy portfolio, actively investing in digital operations, carbon capture and sequestration, geothermal energy, and new energy ventures. Headquartered globally across Houston, Paris, The Hague, and London, SLB operates in more than 100 countries, helping its customers access energy safely, efficiently, and with a growing focus on environmental sustainability.
