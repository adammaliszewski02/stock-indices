The company identified by the stock ticker KGH.WA is KGHM Polska Miedz S.A., a major multinational mining and metallurgical enterprise based in Poland. Founded in 1961, the company has evolved into one of the world's leading producers of refined copper and silver, while also significant in the global extraction of molybdenum, gold, and platinum. Its core operations historically center on the Lower Silesian copper basin in Poland, anchored by major underground mines and advanced processing facilities. Over the decades, KGHM has expanded its international footprint significantly, most notably acquiring Quadra FNX Mining Ltd. in 2012, which added large-scale mining assets in North and South America, including the Sierra Gorda mine in Chile. KGHM operates as a vertically integrated business, managing the entire production chain from geological exploration and ore extraction to smelting, refining, and the manufacturing of advanced copper products. Controlled in majority by the Polish State Treasury, the company plays a vital strategic role in both the domestic and global industrial sectors, consistently investing in sustainable mining technologies and energy transition minerals.
