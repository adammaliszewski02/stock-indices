The Walt Disney Company, trading under the ticker DIS, is a diversified worldwide family entertainment and media enterprise operating through four primary segments: entertainment, sports, experiences, and products. Founded in 1923 by brothers Walt and Roy Disney as a modest animation studio, the company pioneered the field of feature-length animation with the release of Snow White and the Seven Dwarfs in 1937. Over the decades, Disney expanded its operations beyond traditional animation into live-action filmmaking, television broadcasting, and theme parks, opening its first resort, Disneyland, in California in 1955. Today, the corporation encompasses prominent media networks, motion picture studios, direct-to-consumer streaming services including Disney+ and Hulu, and a vast global network of theme parks, cruise lines, and consumer products. Disney continues to generate revenue by leveraging its extensive intellectual property portfolio, which includes iconic characters and franchises from Pixar, Marvel, Star Wars, and 20th Century Fox.
