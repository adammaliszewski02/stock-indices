Microsoft Corporation, trading under the ticker MSFT, is a multinational technology enterprise that develops, licenses, and supports a vast range of software products, services, devices, and hardware solutions. Founded in 1975 by Bill Gates and Paul Allen, the company initially gained global prominence through the development and commercialization of the MS-DOS and Windows operating systems. Over subsequent decades, Microsoft successfully expanded its portfolio to include the Microsoft Office productivity suite, enterprise software, cloud computing services through Microsoft Azure, and video gaming via the Xbox brand, alongside hardware offerings like the Surface line of personal computers. In recent years, Microsoft has positioned itself at the forefront of artificial intelligence innovation through strategic partnerships, notably with OpenAI, integrating advanced machine learning capabilities across its entire ecosystem. Today, the company operates as one of the world's most valuable enterprises, serving both individual consumers and large-scale corporate, governmental, and educational clients globally.
