PepsiCo, Inc., traded under the ticker PEP, is a leading global food and beverage corporation renowned for its iconic brand portfolio, which includes Pepsi-Cola, Gatorade, Quaker Oats, Frito-Lay, and Tropicana. The company's origins date back to 1898 when pharmacist Caleb Bradham formulated "Brad’s Drink" in New Bern, North Carolina, later renaming it Pepsi-Cola after the digestive enzyme pepsin and kola nuts. The modern enterprise was formed through the historic 1965 merger of the Pepsi-Cola Company and Frito-Lay, Inc., a strategic combination that established its dual foundation in both carbonated beverages and convenient snacks. Over subsequent decades, PepsiCo expanded its global footprint and diversified its product offerings through major acquisitions, including Tropicana in 1998 and the Quaker Oats Company in 2001. Today, PepsiCo operates on a global scale, delivering its products to consumers in more than 200 countries and territories. The company remains committed to sustainable long-term value creation through its strategic transformation initiative, pep+, which integrates environmental sustainability and health considerations directly into its core business operations, supply chain management, and brand portfolio development.
