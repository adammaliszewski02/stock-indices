The ticker MBK.WA represents mBank S.A., one of Poland's largest financial institutions. Founded in 1986 as Bank Rozwoju Eksportu (BRE Bank), the institution has evolved from a corporate and export finance bank into a modern, universal financial services provider. Following a major rebranding in 2013, the organization unified its retail, corporate, and investment banking operations under the mBank brand, pioneering digital banking innovations in the Polish market. Today, mBank operates as a publicly traded company on the Warsaw Stock Exchange, serving millions of individual and corporate clients across Poland, the Czech Republic, and Slovakia. Majority-owned by Germany's Commerzbank, the bank offers a comprehensive suite of financial services, including retail banking, asset management, leasing, and factoring, with a strategic emphasis on mobile-first solutions and digital user experience.
