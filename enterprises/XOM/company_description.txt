Exxon Mobil Corporation, trading under the ticker XOM, is a multinational oil and gas corporation headquartered in Spring, Texas, and is one of the largest energy companies in the world. The company’s primary operations encompass the exploration, production, transportation, and sale of crude oil and natural gas. Additionally, ExxonMobil is a major manufacturer and marketer of petrochemicals, power generation products, and specialized commodities, while increasingly investing in lower-emission technologies such as carbon capture and biofuels. 

The company’s corporate lineage traces back to John D. Rockefeller’s Standard Oil trust, founded in 1870. Following the landmark 1911 antitrust dissolution of Standard Oil, the business split into several entities, most notably the Standard Oil Company of New Jersey (later known as Exxon) and the Standard Oil Company of New York (later known as Mobil). These two foundational pillars officially reunited in a historic merger in 1999 to form ExxonMobil. Today, the corporation leverages its extensive global infrastructure and historical legacy to navigate the complexities of traditional energy markets while adapting to the evolving global demand for sustainable energy solutions.
