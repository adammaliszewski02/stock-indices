Develia S.A., traded under the ticker DVL.WA on the Warsaw Stock Exchange, is one of Poland's leading residential and commercial real estate developers. The company specializes in the construction and sale of multi-family residential apartments in major Polish metropolitan areas, including Warsaw, Wrocław, Kraków, Katowice, and Gdańsk. Additionally, Develia engages in the commercial real estate sector, developing and managing modern office and retail spaces. 

Tracing its roots to the early 2000s, the company was originally established as LC Corp S.A. by Polish entrepreneur Leszek Czarnecki. It underwent a significant strategic transformation and rebranding to Develia S.A. in 2019, shifting its primary focus toward expanding its residential housing portfolio while progressively divesting from certain commercial assets to optimize its business model. Over the decades, Develia has successfully delivered thousands of residential units and landmark commercial projects, establishing a strong reputation for quality, financial stability, and sustainable urban development within the Central European real estate market.
