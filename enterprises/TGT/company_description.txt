Target Corporation, trading under the stock ticker TGT, is one of the largest general merchandise retailers in the United States. The company operates a vast chain of discount department stores offering a comprehensive selection of apparel, beauty products, electronics, home furnishings, and grocery items. Target emphasizes an upscale shopping experience, blending national brands with popular in-house private labels. 

The company's origins trace back to 1902 when George Draper Dayton founded the Dayton Dry Goods Company in Minneapolis, Minnesota. The enterprise evolved into the Dayton Hudson Corporation before opening its first discount store, branded as Target, in Roseville, Minnesota, in 1962. The venture was conceived to combine the best features of general merchandise retailing with the low prices of discount stores. Following decades of steady expansion and the divestment of its traditional department store divisions, the company officially changed its name to Target Corporation in 2000. Today, Target serves millions of consumers both through its brick-and-mortar locations and its robust digital e-commerce platform, supported by an integrated supply chain focused on convenient fulfillment options like same-day delivery and curbside pickup.
