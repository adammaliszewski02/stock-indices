Starbucks Corporation, trading under the ticker SBUX, is a premier multinational coffeehouse chain and roastery conglomerate. Founded in Seattle, Washington, in 1971 by Jerry Baldwin, Gordon Bowker, and Zev Siegl, the company has evolved from a single local store selling high-end coffee beans and equipment into the largest coffeehouse company in the world. Howard Schultz, who joined the company in 1982 and later assumed leadership, played a transformative role by introducing the Italian coffeehouse experience to the American market, which catalyzed the company's rapid global expansion. Today, Starbucks generates revenue primarily through company-operated and licensed stores, offering a diverse portfolio that includes hot and cold artisan beverages, whole-bean coffees, espresso, micro-roasted coffees, a variety of food items, and ready-to-drink beverages through global retail partnerships. The corporation also markets several subsidiary brands, including Teavana, Seattle's Best Coffee, and Evolution Fresh. Headquartered in Seattle, Starbucks operates tens of thousands of retail locations internationally, maintaining a prominent market presence driven by continuous digital innovation, customer loyalty programs, and a steadfast commitment to ethical sourcing and environmental sustainability across its global supply chain.
