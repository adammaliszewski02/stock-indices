Stock ticker WMT identifies Walmart Inc., an American multinational retail corporation that operates a chain of hypermarkets, discount department stores, and grocery stores. Founded by Sam Walton in 1962 and incorporated in 1969, the company has grown from a single small-town discount store in Rogers, Arkansas, into the world's largest company by revenue. Walmart’s core business model focuses on everyday low prices, supported by an extensive, highly efficient global supply chain and logistics network. The company encompasses several prominent retail formats, including Walmart discount stores, Supercenters, Neighborhood Markets, and Sam's Club warehouse clubs. In recent decades, Walmart has significantly expanded its digital footprint, transforming into an omnichannel retailer through robust e-commerce operations, marketplace platforms, and digital delivery services. Additionally, the company operates internationally through majority-owned subsidiaries and strategic investments, serving millions of customers globally each week while continuously adapting its operations to meet evolving consumer demands in the modern retail landscape.
