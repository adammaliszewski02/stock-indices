Mastercard Incorporated, trading under the stock ticker MA, is a leading multinational technology company in the global payments industry. The company connects consumers, financial institutions, merchants, governments, and businesses across more than 210 countries and territories, enabling them to use digital, electronic, and mobile payment methods instead of cash and checks. Mastercard’s primary business involves processing payment transactions, which includes authorization, clearing, and settlement services. Beyond core payment processing, the company provides a comprehensive suite of value-added services, including cybersecurity and fraud prevention, data analytics, loyalty and rewards programs, and consulting. Tracing its origins back to 1966 when a group of California banks formed the Interbank Card Association to compete with Bank of America's BankAmericard, the network became known as Master Charge in 1969 before officially adopting the name Mastercard in 1979. Mastercard completed its initial public offering on the New York Stock Exchange in 2006, transitioning from a membership organization owned by its financial institutions into a publicly traded corporation. Today, the company continues to drive innovation in the financial services sector, expanding rapidly into open banking, blockchain technology, and cross-border remittance solutions to facilitate secure, fast, and seamless global commerce.
