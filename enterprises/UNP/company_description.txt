Union Pacific Corporation (UNP) is a premier American transportation holding company best known for its principal operating company, Union Pacific Railroad. Established in 1862 under the Pacific Railroad Act signed by President Abraham Lincoln, the company played a vital historical role in the westward expansion of the United States by completing a leg of the historic First Transcontinental Railroad. Today, UNP operates one of the nation's most expansive freight railroad networks, spanning 23 states primarily west of the Mississippi River. The business generates revenue by transporting a diverse mix of agricultural products, industrial goods, chemicals, coal, and intermodal containers. By seamlessly connecting businesses to domestic and global markets, Union Pacific remains a foundational component of modern North American supply chains and logistics infrastructure.
