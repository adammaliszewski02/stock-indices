The ticker APR.WA corresponds to Apator SA, a prominent Polish capital group specializing in the manufacture and supply of modern measurement equipment, systems, and services for the electricity, gas, water, and heat sectors. Headquartered in Toruń, Poland, the company traces its origins back to the interwar period, specifically to the establishment of its foundational manufacturing roots in the early 20th century. Over the decades, Apator has evolved into a leading technology provider in Central and Eastern Europe, focusing heavily on advanced metering infrastructure, billing systems, and renewable energy integration. The company is publicly listed on the Warsaw Stock Exchange, where its shares are traded under the APR ticker.
