Verizon Communications Inc., trading under the ticker VZ, is a premier American multinational telecommunications conglomerate and a global leader in delivering broadband, video, and digital communication services. Headquartered in New York City, the company traces its corporate lineage back to Bell Atlantic Corporation, a regional Bell operating company formed following the 1984 divestiture of AT&T. Bell Atlantic subsequently merged with GTE Corporation in 2000, adopting the name Verizon—a portmanteau of "veritas" and "horizon"—and later acquired full ownership of Verizon Wireless through a landmark transaction with Vodafone in 2014. Today, Verizon operates one of the world's most expansive and reliable wireless networks, serving millions of consumer, business, government, and wholesale customers. The company generates its primary revenue through advanced mobile voice and data services, high-speed fiber-optic broadband via its Fios network, and comprehensive enterprise solutions, including cloud and cybersecurity services, continuously driving technological evolution from 4G LTE to next-generation 5G connectivity.
