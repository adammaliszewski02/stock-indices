BlackRock, Inc., traded under the ticker BLK, is a premier global investment management corporation headquartered in New York City. Founded in 1988 by Larry Fink and a team of executives, the firm initially operated as a fixed-income institutional risk management and fixed-income asset manager before separating from its original parent to establish its current identity. Today, BlackRock operates as the world's largest asset manager, serving institutional, intermediary, and individual investors across global markets. The company provides a broad range of investment and risk management services, encompassing equity, fixed income, cash management, alternative investments, and multi-asset strategies. BlackRock is widely recognized for its proprietary Aladdin technology platform, which integrates risk management, trading, and portfolio analytics for the company and numerous external financial institutions. Additionally, the firm is a pioneer in the exchange-traded fund industry through its globally recognized iShares brand. Operating through a worldwide network of offices and client-focused teams, BlackRock emphasizes long-term value creation, technological innovation, and sustainable investing to help clients achieve their financial objectives.
