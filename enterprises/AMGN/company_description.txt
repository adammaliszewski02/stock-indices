Amgen Inc., trading under the ticker AMGN, is a leading multinational biopharmaceutical company dedicated to discovering, developing, manufacturing, and delivering innovative human therapeutics for severe medical conditions. Founded in Thousand Oaks, California, in 1980 by a venture capital firm originally named Molecular Genetics, Inc., the company adopted the name Applied Molecular Genetics before shortening it to Amgen. Over its history, Amgen has pioneered major advancements in biotechnology, leveraging recombinant DNA technology to produce breakthrough medicines. Its diverse commercial portfolio addresses critical therapeutic areas, including oncology, cardiovascular disease, inflammation, bone health, nephrology, and general medicine. Prominent historical products include Epogen and Neupogen, which established the company's foundational success in supportive cancer care and chronic kidney disease treatment. Amgen continues to invest heavily in research and development, driven by a commitment to serving patients by transforming new biological discoveries into life-saving medicines on a global scale.
