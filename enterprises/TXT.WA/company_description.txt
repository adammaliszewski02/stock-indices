The ticker TXT.WA represents Text S.A., a prominent Polish technology company formerly known as LiveChat Software. Founded in Wroclaw in 2002, the enterprise initially established its market presence through the development of live chat and helpdesk software for customer service operations. Over the years, the company expanded its technological ecosystem to include a suite of online communication tools, such as chatbot solutions and knowledge base platforms, designed to facilitate seamless interactions between businesses and their clients globally. Text S.A. operates primarily on a Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) subscription model, serving tens of thousands of paying customers across numerous countries. The company's shares are publicly traded on the Warsaw Stock Exchange, where it is recognized as a key player in the Central and Eastern European technology sector.
