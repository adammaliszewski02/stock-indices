The ticker PM represents Philip Morris International Inc., a leading international tobacco company engaged in the manufacture and sale of cigarettes, smoke-free products, electronic devices, and nicotine-containing products. Originally established in 1847 as a single London tobacco shop by Mr. Philip Morris, the enterprise expanded globally over the following century. In March 2008, the company was officially spun off from Altria Group, separating its international operations from the domestic United States market. Headquartered in Stamford, Connecticut, and incorporated in Virginia, the firm has strategically sought to transform its business model toward a smoke-free future, highlighted by the 2022 acquisition of Swedish Match AB. Its diverse brand portfolio includes leading global names such as Marlboro alongside innovative reduced-risk alternatives like IQOS, which are currently distributed in approximately 84 markets worldwide.
