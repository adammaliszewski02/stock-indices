Bioton S.A., trading under the ticker BIO.WA, is a prominent Polish biotechnology company listed on the Warsaw Stock Exchange. Established in 1989, the company specializes in the production and commercialization of modern biopharmaceutical products, with a primary focus on the manufacturing of recombinant human insulin and related diabetes management solutions. Over its history, Bioton has evolved into a key regional player in the healthcare and pharmaceutical sector, expanding its operations and strategic partnerships across Europe, Asia, and other international markets. The company integrates research, development, and advanced biotechnological manufacturing to provide accessible therapeutic alternatives, thereby addressing the growing global demand for metabolic and endocrine treatments.
