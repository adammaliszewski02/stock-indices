Bristol-Myers Squibb Company, trading under the ticker BMY, is a leading global biopharmaceutical company dedicated to discovering, developing, and delivering innovative medicines that help patients prevail over serious diseases. Headquartered in Princeton, New Jersey, the company focuses on therapeutic areas including oncology, hematology, cardiovascular disease, and immunology. Bristol-Myers Squibb traces its origins back to the 1858 founding of Squibb by Dr. Edward Robinson Squibb, followed by the establishment of Bristol-Myers in 1887 by William Bristol and John Myers. The two legacy enterprises merged in 1989 to form Bristol-Myers Squibb, creating a pharmaceutical powerhouse. Over the decades, the company has driven significant medical breakthroughs, including the development of baseline antibiotics, platinum-based cancer therapies, and modern immuno-oncology treatments. In 2019, BMY significantly expanded its portfolio and research capabilities through the multi-billion-dollar acquisition of Celgene, reinforcing its position as an industry leader in specialized medicine.
