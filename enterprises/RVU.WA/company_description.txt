The ticker RVU.WA refers to Read-Gene S.A., a pioneering Polish biotechnology company listed on the Warsaw Stock Exchange. Founded in 2008 by renowned geneticist Professor Jan Lubiński, the company specializes in molecular diagnostics and personalized medicine. Read-Gene focuses primarily on identifying genetic predispositions to various cancers, notably breast, ovarian, and prostate cancers. By leveraging advanced genetic testing and research, the company aims to improve early detection, prevention, and targeted therapeutic strategies for oncology patients. Over the years, Read-Gene has established itself as a significant contributor to the life sciences sector in Poland, combining academic research with commercial diagnostic services to advance oncological healthcare outcomes.
