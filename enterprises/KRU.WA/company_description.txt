Kruk S.A., traded under the ticker KRU.WA, is a prominent European debt management company headquartered in Wrocław, Poland. Founded in 1998, the company has grown from a domestic debt collection agency into an international financial services group. Kruk primarily specializes in the management of non-performing retail and corporate debt portfolios, operating across several European markets including Poland, Romania, Italy, Spain, the Czech Republic, Slovakia, and Germany. Its business model centers on purchasing debt portfolios from banks, insurers, leasing companies, and telecommunications providers, alongside offering amicable and legal debt collection services. Kruk successfully transitioned from traditional enforcement methods to a more customer-centric approach, emphasizing amicable settlements and financial counseling for debtors to promote sustainable debt resolution. The company has been listed on the Warsaw Stock Exchange since 2011, reflecting its significant market presence and financial stability within the region's financial sector.
