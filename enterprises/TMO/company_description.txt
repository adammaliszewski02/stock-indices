Thermo Fisher Scientific Inc., trading under the ticker TMO, is a global leader in serving science, providing analytical instruments, life sciences solutions, specialty diagnostics, laboratory equipment, and pharmaceutical services. The company enables its customers to accelerate life sciences research, solve complex analytical challenges, improve patient diagnostics, deliver therapeutics, and increase laboratory productivity. 

The company traces its modern origins to the 2006 merger of Thermo Electron, founded in 1956 as a developer of biomedical and technological products, and Fisher Scientific, a venerable laboratory supply company established in 1902. Through a strategic series of high-profile acquisitions—including Invitrogen in 2008, Life Technologies in 2014, and PPD in 2021—Thermo Fisher has significantly expanded its capabilities across the biopharmaceutical and healthcare sectors. Today, operating through a portfolio of premier brands including Thermo Scientific, Applied Biosystems, Invitrogen, Fisher Scientific, Unity Lab Services, and Patheon, the company supports customers across pharmaceutical and biotech companies, hospitals, clinical diagnostic laboratories, universities, research institutions, and government agencies. Headquartered in Waltham, Massachusetts, Thermo Fisher Scientific remains dedicated to its mission of enabling customers to make the world healthier, cleaner, and safer.
