The Kraft Heinz Company, traded under the ticker KHC, is one of the largest food and beverage companies in the world, operating within the consumer packaged goods sector. Headquartered in Chicago, Illinois, and Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania, the company manufactures and markets a diverse portfolio of globally recognized brands, including condiments, sauces, cheese, dairy, meals, beverages, and infant nutrition products. 

The enterprise was formed through the historic July 2015 merger of H.J. Heinz Holding Corporation and Kraft Foods Group, a transaction orchestrated by 3G Capital and Berkshire Hathaway. This multibillion-dollar combination united iconic heritage brands such as Heinz, Kraft, Oscar Mayer, Philadelphia, Velveeta, and Planters under a single corporate umbrella. Today, Kraft Heinz distributes its products to consumers in over 190 countries and territories, leveraging a vast global supply chain, robust marketing platforms, and a continuous focus on innovation and operational efficiency to meet evolving retail and consumer preferences.
