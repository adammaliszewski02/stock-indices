Eli Lilly and Company, traded under the ticker LLY, is a leading American multinational pharmaceutical corporation dedicated to discovering, developing, and marketing human healthcare products. Founded in 1876 by Colonel Eli Lilly, a pharmaceutical chemist and Union Army veteran, the company has a long history of pioneering significant medical breakthroughs. Notably, it partnered with researchers at the University of Toronto in the 1920s to commercialize insulin, marking one of the earliest mass-production efforts for a vital biologic treatment. Over the decades, Lilly expanded its portfolio significantly, becoming a global leader in areas such as neuroscience, oncology, and immunology. In recent years, the company has achieved substantial commercial and market growth driven by its innovative therapies for type 2 diabetes and obesity, notably through the development and distribution of tirzepatide-based medications. Headquartered in Indianapolis, Indiana, Eli Lilly continues to invest heavily in research and development to address some of the world's most serious medical conditions, reinforcing its legacy as a cornerstone of the modern pharmaceutical industry.
