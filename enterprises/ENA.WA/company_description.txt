The ticker ENA.WA corresponds to ENEA S.A., one of Poland's leading energy groups and a foundational pillar of the country's utility sector. Headquartered in Poznań, the company operates across the entire energy value chain, encompassing hard coal mining, electricity generation, distribution, and commercial retail trading. Enea traces its origins to the post-war development of Poland's power grid, formally taking its current corporate structure in the early 2000s through the consolidation of regional distribution companies. Today, the state-controlled enterprise plays a crucial role in Poland's national energy security while navigating the complex transition toward decarbonization and renewable energy integration. The group serves millions of residential and business customers, supplying electricity and heat primarily derived from conventional sources, alongside growing investments in wind, solar, and modern grid infrastructure to align with European Union climate policies.
