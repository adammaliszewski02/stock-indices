Broadcom Inc., trading under the ticker AVGO, is a global technology leader that designs, develops, and supplies a wide range of semiconductor and infrastructure software solutions. The company’s product offerings serve critical markets including data centers, networking, enterprise software, broadband, wireless, and industrial applications. 

The enterprise traces its origins to Hewlett-Packard’s semiconductor division, which was spun off in 1999 to form Agilent Technologies. Agilent's semiconductor business was subsequently acquired in 2005 to create Avago Technologies. In 2016, Avago acquired the original Broadcom Corporation and adopted the Broadcom name, subsequently expanding its software footprint through major acquisitions including CA Technologies in 2018, Symantec’s enterprise security business in 2019, and cloud software leader VMware in 2023. Headquartered in Palo Alto, California, Broadcom has evolved through strategic consolidation into one of the world's most influential technology infrastructure companies, enabling the foundational connectivity and processing power driving modern digital transformation and artificial intelligence workloads.
