The ticker MRB.WA corresponds to Marvipol Development S.A., a prominent Polish real estate developer listed on the Warsaw Stock Exchange. Established originally as part of the Marvipol group, which began its operations in 1996 as an automotive importer, the company later expanded into the residential property market. In 2017, Marvipol S.A. strategically separated its real estate operations from its automotive business, officially establishing Marvipol Development S.A. as an independent entity focused entirely on housing construction. 

Today, the company's core business revolves around residential real estate development, primarily operating in the Warsaw metropolitan area. Marvipol Development designs, constructs, and sells modern apartment complexes and housing estates, catering to the growing urban demand in Poland. Additionally, the company has occasionally engaged in the commercial real estate sector, specifically warehouse space development. Marvipol Development prides itself on delivering high-standard architectural projects, leveraging decades of market experience to maintain a strong position in the competitive Polish property sector while emphasizing sustainable building practices and customer satisfaction.
