MRK refers to Merck & Co., Inc., a leading global healthcare company headquartered in Rahway, New Jersey. Traded on the New York Stock Exchange under the ticker MRK, the company traces its roots back to 1891 when George W. Merck established the U.S. subsidiary of the German pharmaceutical enterprise E. Merck. Following expropriation during World War I, the American branch became an independent U.S. entity in 1917. 

Merck operates primarily as a research-intensive biopharmaceutical company dedicated to discovering, developing, and delivering innovative medicines, vaccines, and animal health products that address critical unmet medical needs worldwide. Its diverse portfolio includes significant treatments in oncology, vaccines, infectious diseases, and cardiovascular health. Notably, the company is widely recognized for producing Keytruda, a leading immunotherapy used in the treatment of various cancers, as well as crucial vaccines for human papillomavirus and measles. Additionally, Merck maintains a robust animal health division that provides pharmaceuticals, vaccines, and health-monitoring technology for livestock and companion animals.
