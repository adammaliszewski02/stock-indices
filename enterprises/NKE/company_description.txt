NIKE, Inc., traded under the ticker NKE, is a multinational corporation that designs, develops, markets, and sells high-performance athletic footwear, apparel, equipment, and accessories worldwide. Founded in 1964 as Blue Ribbon Sports by Bill Bowerman and Phil Knight, the company officially adopted the name Nike in 1978, taking inspiration from the Greek goddess of victory. It famously introduced its iconic "Swoosh" logo in 1971, designed by graphic design student Carolyn Davidson. Over the decades, Nike expanded its portfolio through strategic acquisitions and organic growth, notably establishing the Jordan brand in collaboration with basketball legend Michael Jordan and acquiring Converse in 2003. Today, Nike operates as the world's largest supplier of athletic shoes and apparel, recognized globally for its innovative product technology, extensive roster of elite athlete endorsements, and the enduring "Just Do It" marketing campaign.
