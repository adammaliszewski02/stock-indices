The Home Depot, traded under the ticker symbol HD, is the world's largest home improvement specialty retailer. The company operates a vast chain of warehouse-format stores across the United States, Canada, and Mexico, offering a comprehensive array of building materials, home improvement supplies, lawn and garden products, and decor. In addition to serving do-it-yourself retail customers, The Home Depot strategically caters to professional contractors, maintenance repair operations, and trade professionals through dedicated fulfillment options and specialized services. 

Founded in 1978 by Bernie Marcus, Arthur Blank, Ron Brill, and Pat Farrah in Atlanta, Georgia, the company revolutionized the retail landscape by pioneering the warehouse-style superstore concept, providing vast product selection and discounted pricing under one roof. The Home Depot went public in 1981, subsequently experiencing rapid expansion and becoming a dominant force in the retail sector. Over the decades, the company has integrated robust digital platforms to create a seamless omnichannel shopping experience, maintaining its industry leadership through continuous supply chain optimization and customer-centric retail strategies. Today, it remains a foundational component of the major stock market indices.
