TSLA is the stock ticker for Tesla, Inc., an American multinational automotive and clean energy company headquartered in Austin, Texas. Founded in 2003 by Martin Eberhard and Marc Tarpenning—with Elon Musk playing a pivotal funding and leadership role from its early stages—the company initially focused on the commercialization of battery electric vehicles. Tesla officially entered public markets via an initial public offering in June 2010. Today, Tesla designs, manufactures, and sells fully electric passenger cars, solar energy generation systems, and stationary battery energy storage products for residential, commercial, and industrial use. Furthermore, the company develops and deploys autonomous driving technologies, artificial intelligence hardware and software, and a proprietary global network of high-output Supercharger stations. Through continuous vertical integration and advanced manufacturing techniques, Tesla has positioned itself as a global leader in the sustainable transportation and renewable energy sectors.
