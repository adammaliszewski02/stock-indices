Neuca S.A., listed on the Warsaw Stock Exchange under the ticker NEU.WA, is a leading healthcare sector enterprise in Poland, primarily recognized as the country's largest pharmaceutical wholesaler. Founded in 1990 as Torfarm, the company has grown from a regional distributor into a comprehensive healthcare group. Its core business focuses on B2B distribution of pharmaceuticals to independent pharmacies, backed by advanced logistics and supply chain infrastructure. Over the years, Neuca has expanded its portfolio beyond wholesale distribution to diversify its revenue streams. The company now operates proprietary pharmacy franchise networks, invests in telemedicine platforms, and provides clinical research organization services to support medical trials. Additionally, Neuca develops its own brands of over-the-counter medications and medical devices, reinforcing its position as an integrated partner in the Polish healthcare ecosystem.
