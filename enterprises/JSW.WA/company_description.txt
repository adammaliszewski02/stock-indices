Jastrzębska Spółka Węglowa (JSW), listed on the Warsaw Stock Exchange under the ticker JSW.WA, is the European Union’s largest producer of high-quality hard coking coal and a significant manufacturer of coke. Headquartered in Jastrzębie-Zdrój, Poland, the company operates primarily within the Upper Silesian Coal Basin. Its business model integrates coal extraction with the production of coke, which is an essential raw material for the steel industry. Established in 1993 through the consolidation of several state-owned coal mines, JSW has evolved into a critical player in the global metallurgical coal market. The company’s operations are vertically integrated, encompassing mining, processing, and logistics, which allows it to supply both domestic and international steel producers. By focusing on high-grade coking coal rather than thermal coal, JSW maintains a strategic position in the industrial supply chain, supporting the production of steel required for infrastructure, automotive, and construction sectors worldwide.
