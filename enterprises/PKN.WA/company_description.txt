The company identified by the stock ticker PKN.WA is PKN Orlen (Polski Koncern Naftowy Orlen), a leading multinational integrated oil and gas company headquartered in Płock, Poland. Traded on the Warsaw Stock Exchange, the corporation has evolved into one of Central Europe's largest industrial enterprises. Its core business encompasses crude oil processing, the production of fuels, petrochemicals, and plastics, as well as extensive wholesale and retail distribution through a vast network of modern service stations across Poland, Germany, the Czech Republic, Slovakia, and Lithuania. 

Historically, the company's roots trace back to the interwar period with the establishment of state-owned refineries, later operating under the socialist economy before undergoing significant restructuring. In 1999, through the consolidation of Petrochemia Płock and Centrala Produktów Naftowych, the modern entity was formed and subsequently privatized, listing on the Warsaw Stock Exchange in 2000. In recent years, PKN Orlen has actively pursued a strategic transition toward sustainable energy, expanding its portfolio into renewable power generation, green hydrogen, and electric vehicle charging infrastructure, while continuing to secure the energy independence of the region.
