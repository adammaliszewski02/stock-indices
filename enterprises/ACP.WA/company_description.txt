The stock ticker ACP.WA refers to Asseco Poland S.A., a prominent European technology company specializing in the development and integration of comprehensive software solutions. Founded in 1991 as Comp Rzeszów, the company has grown organically and through strategic acquisitions to become one of the largest IT enterprises listed on the Warsaw Stock Exchange. Asseco Poland serves a diverse, international client base across key sectors, including banking and finance, healthcare, energy, telecommunications, and public administration. Operating as the holding company for the wider Asseco Group, it manages a federation of independent businesses that deliver localized IT services and proprietary software products spanning multiple continents. The company is particularly recognized for its strong market position in Central and Eastern Europe, where it has played a pivotal role in the digitalization of both commercial enterprises and public sector institutions.
