PGE Polska Grupa Energetyczna S.A., traded under the ticker PGE.WA, is the largest power company in Poland and a dominant enterprise within the Central and Eastern European energy sector. Established in 2007 through the consolidation of state-owned energy assets, the company traces its historical roots back to the post-war development of Poland's centralized power grid. Headquartered in Warsaw and majority-owned by the State Treasury, PGE operates an integrated business model spanning fossil fuel and renewable electricity generation, heat production, wholesale trading, and extensive distribution networks that supply millions of households and industrial clients. In recent years, the company has actively pursued a strategic transition toward decarbonization, focusing significant investments on offshore wind farms, onshore renewables, and modernization of grid infrastructure to align with European Union climate objectives while maintaining national energy security.
