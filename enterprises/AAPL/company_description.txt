AAPL is the stock ticker for Apple Inc., a multinational technology company headquartered in Cupertino, California. Founded in April 1976 by Steve Jobs, Steve Wozniak, and Ronald Wayne, the company originally focused on the development and sale of personal computers, famously introducing the Apple I and Macintosh. Over the decades, Apple evolved into a global leader in consumer electronics, software, and digital services. Its core business centers on the design, manufacture, and marketing of mobile communication and media devices, most notably the iPhone, alongside the Mac computer lineup, iPad tablets, and wearables like the Apple Watch and AirPods. Additionally, the company generates substantial revenue through its digital ecosystems and services, including the App Store, Apple Music, iCloud, and Apple Pay. Apple has grown to become one of the world's most valuable publicly traded corporations, renowned for its distinct brand loyalty, integrated hardware and software ecosystem, and continuous emphasis on innovative design.
