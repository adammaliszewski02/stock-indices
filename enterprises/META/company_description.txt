Meta Platforms, Inc., trading under the ticker META, is a global technology leader that builds products connecting people through mobile devices, personal computers, virtual reality headsets, and home devices. Originally founded in 2004 as TheFacebook by Mark Zuckerberg and college roommates in a Harvard dorm room, the company later adopted the name Facebook, Inc. before rebranding as Meta Platforms in 2021 to reflect its expanding focus beyond social media into the metaverse. The company operates a family of applications that includes Facebook, Instagram, Messenger, WhatsApp, and Threads, which together serve billions of monthly active users worldwide. Meta generates the vast majority of its revenue through the sale of advertising placements to marketers across its platform ecosystem. Additionally, the company invests heavily in artificial intelligence and augmented and virtual reality technologies through its Reality Labs division, developing hardware and software designed to power the next evolution of social technology and immersive digital experiences.
