International Business Machines Corporation, commonly known as IBM and trading under the ticker IBM, is a multinational technology and consulting company headquartered in Armonk, New York. Founded in 1911 as the Computing-Tabulating-Recording Company through a merger of four enterprises, the firm was officially renamed IBM in 1924. Historically renowned for pioneering early computing systems such as the IBM 7090 and the System/360 mainframe, the company has continuously evolved through transformative technological shifts. Today, IBM focuses primarily on hybrid cloud computing, enterprise artificial intelligence, data analytics, and quantum computing. It delivers advanced software and IT services to global businesses, helping organizations modernize their operations and secure their digital infrastructure while maintaining a vast portfolio of intellectual property and research patents.
