LIN is the stock ticker for Linde plc, a leading global industrial gases and engineering company. Traded on the New York Stock Exchange and Frankfurt Stock Exchange, Linde serves a diverse array of industries, including chemicals, healthcare, electronics, energy, and manufacturing. The company supplies atmospheric gases, such as oxygen, nitrogen, and argon, as well as process gases like hydrogen and carbon dioxide, alongside high-performance equipment used in industrial applications. 

Linde’s corporate lineage traces back to the founding of Linde AG in Germany in 1879 by Carl von Linde, who developed a pioneering refrigeration and air separation process. Over the decades, the enterprise expanded internationally through organic growth and major acquisitions. A significant milestone occurred in 2018 when Linde AG merged with the United States-based Praxair, Inc., a company with its own rich history originating from the Linde Air Products Company founded in 1907. This strategic combination created the world's largest industrial gas company by market share and revenue. Today, Linde is headquartered in Woking, United Kingdom, and is recognized for its commitment to sustainability, particularly in advancing clean energy solutions such as green hydrogen.
