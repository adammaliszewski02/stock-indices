Intel Corporation, traded under the ticker INTC, is a leading American multinational corporation and technology company. Founded in 1968 by semiconductor pioneers Robert Noyce and Gordon Moore, the company originally specialized in memory chips before revolutionizing the personal computing industry by developing the world's first commercial microprocessor in 1971. Today, Intel is one of the world's largest semiconductor chip manufacturers by revenue. The company designs, develops, and manufactures advanced microprocessors, chipsets, and other computing and communications components for global original equipment manufacturers. Intel’s product portfolio serves a broad range of markets, including enterprise computing, data centers, artificial intelligence, cloud computing, and the Internet of Things. In recent years, the company has heavily invested in expanding its foundry business under the IDM 2.0 strategy, aiming to manufacture semiconductors for external customers and restore domestic leadership in advanced chip production.
