The stock ticker INTC identifies Intel Corporation, a global leader in the design and manufacturing of semiconductor products and computing technologies. Founded in 1968 by industry pioneers Robert Noyce and Gordon Moore, Intel played a foundational role in the personal computer revolution by establishing the x86 microprocessor architecture, which became the standard for modern computing. 

Headquartered in Santa Clara, California, the company designs central processing units (CPUs), system-on-chips, and related hardware for client personal computers, enterprise servers, and cloud data centers. Intel's product portfolio also encompasses graphics processing units (GPUs), networking components, and specialized accelerators engineered for artificial intelligence workloads. 

Historically operating as an integrated device manufacturer that designed and fabricated its own chips internally, Intel has adapted its business model to include commercial foundry services. Through Intel Foundry, the company now provides advanced semiconductor manufacturing and packaging solutions to third-party designers, positioning itself as a key Western-based manufacturer amid ongoing efforts to diversify the global technology supply chain.
