The stock ticker INTC represents Intel Corporation, a global leader in the design and manufacturing of semiconductor products and computing technologies. Founded in 1968 by Silicon Valley pioneers Robert Noyce and Gordon Moore, Intel played a foundational role in the personal computer revolution by popularizing the x86 architecture, which remains the dominant computing standard for desktops, laptops, and enterprise data centers worldwide.

Headquartered in Santa Clara, California, Intel designs and builds central processing units (CPUs), microprocessors, and chipsets. Its product portfolio spans client computing for consumer and business PCs, data center infrastructure, network and edge solutions, and specialized accelerators for artificial intelligence workloads. 

Unlike many semiconductor peers that rely exclusively on external foundries, Intel is one of the few Western technology firms that both designs and manufactures its own silicon. Through its Intel Foundry business, the company is actively expanding its third-party fabrication services, investing billions of dollars in advanced manufacturing facilities across the United States and Europe to meet global demand for critical microchip production.
