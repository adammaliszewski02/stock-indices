INTC represents Intel Corporation, a pioneering American multinational technology company and one of the world’s largest semiconductor chip manufacturers by revenue. Founded in 1968 by Robert Noyce and Gordon Moore, Intel played a defining role in the rise of Silicon Valley, famously commercializing the first microprocessor, the Intel 4004, in 1971 and establishing the x86 architecture that dominates modern personal and enterprise computing.

Intel’s core business centers on designing, manufacturing, and marketing computing hardware and semiconductor products. Its primary offerings include microprocessors, graphic processing units (GPUs), and chipsets for laptops, desktops, and enterprise servers. The company operates through key segments such as the Client Computing Group, which powers consumer devices, and the Data Center and AI group, which provides hardware solutions for cloud computing and artificial intelligence workloads. Operating predominantly as an integrated device manufacturer (IDM), Intel maintains its own fabrication facilities and is actively expanding its Intel Foundry segment to provide advanced contract manufacturing and packaging services to external global customers.
