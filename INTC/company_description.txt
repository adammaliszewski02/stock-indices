Intel Corporation, traded under the ticker INTC, is a leading American multinational corporation and technology company. Founded in 1968 by Robert Noyce and Gordon Moore, the company pioneered the x86 series of microprocessors, which became the foundational processing units for personal computers. Intel's primary business encompasses the design, manufacture, and sale of computer components and related technologies, with a core emphasis on semiconductors, microprocessors, and motherboards. Over its history, the company transformed the computing landscape through continuous innovation in silicon technology and memory products. Today, Intel serves a diverse global customer base spanning personal computing, data centers, artificial intelligence, and the Internet of Things. In recent years, the company has heavily invested in expanding its foundry services to manufacture advanced semiconductors for external clients, aligning with broader strategic initiatives to revitalize its manufacturing leadership and secure resilient global supply chains.
