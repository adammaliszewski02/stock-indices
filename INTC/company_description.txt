Intel Corporation, trading under the ticker INTC, is a global leader in the semiconductor industry, primarily recognized for designing and manufacturing microprocessors that power the vast majority of the world’s personal computers and data centers. Founded in 1968 by Gordon Moore and Robert Noyce, the company played a pivotal role in the personal computing revolution, most notably through the development of the x86 architecture. Beyond its core processor business, Intel provides a broad portfolio of technology solutions, including integrated graphics, artificial intelligence accelerators, field-programmable gate arrays, and connectivity components. In recent years, the company has undergone a significant strategic transformation under its Integrated Device Manufacturing 2.0 strategy, which involves expanding its internal manufacturing capacity while simultaneously launching Intel Foundry to provide fabrication services for external customers. Headquartered in Santa Clara, California, Intel remains a cornerstone of the technology sector, continuously investing in research and development to advance semiconductor process technology and packaging innovation to support the evolving demands of cloud computing, edge intelligence, and autonomous systems.
