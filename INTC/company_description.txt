Intel Corporation, traded under the ticker INTC, is a leading American multinational technology company and a premier global semiconductor manufacturer. Founded in 1968 by Robert Noyce and Gordon Moore, the company pioneered the x86 series of microprocessors, which became the foundational architecture for personal computers worldwide. Throughout its history, Intel has driven significant advancements in computing, memory technologies, and motherboard chipsets, establishing itself for decades as the dominant force in the semiconductor industry through its integrated design and manufacturing model. 

Today, Intel designs and manufactures a broad portfolio of products, including central processing units for client computing and data centers, artificial intelligence accelerators, graphics processing units, and networking solutions. In recent years, the company has undergone a strategic transformation to restore its technological leadership by aggressively expanding its contract manufacturing operations through Intel Foundry Services. This initiative aims to open its advanced fabrication plants to external customers, positioning the company to compete directly in the global semiconductor foundry market while continuing to serve its traditional core markets in personal computing and enterprise infrastructure.
