Oracle Corporation, trading under the stock ticker ORCL, is a multinational computer technology corporation headquartered in Austin, Texas. Founded in 1977 by Larry Ellison, Bob Miner, and Ed Oates under the original name Software Development Laboratories, the company initially gained prominence as a pioneer of relational database management systems. Over the decades, Oracle has evolved its business model to offer a comprehensive and integrated suite of cloud applications and platform services. Its core offerings include enterprise resource planning software, supply chain management software, human capital management software, and enterprise databases. Oracle serves businesses of all sizes globally, facilitating digital transformation through its autonomous database technologies, industry-specific software solutions, and robust cloud infrastructure. The company continues to expand its market presence through strategic acquisitions, most notably the integration of Sun Microsystems in 2010 and Cerner Corporation in 2022, cementing its status as a foundational pillar of enterprise computing and modern corporate data architecture.
