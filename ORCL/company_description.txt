Oracle Corporation, identified by the stock ticker ORCL, is a multinational computer technology corporation headquartered in Austin, Texas. Founded in 1977 by Larry Ellison, Bob Miner, and Ed Oates under the name Software Development Laboratories, the company initially gained prominence as a pioneer of relational database management systems. Over the decades, Oracle has evolved its business model from a leading provider of on-premises enterprise software and database technology into a major cloud computing services provider. Today, Oracle delivers a comprehensive and fully integrated suite of cloud applications, including enterprise resource planning, human capital management, customer relationship management, and supply chain management, alongside its high-performance Oracle Cloud Infrastructure. Additionally, the company offers database software and engineered systems, serving a vast global clientele of businesses, governments, and educational institutions. Through strategic acquisitions, most notably the integration of Sun Microsystems in 2010 and Cerner in 2022, Oracle has continually expanded its technological capabilities in hardware, enterprise software, and healthcare information systems to maintain its competitive position in the global technology sector.
