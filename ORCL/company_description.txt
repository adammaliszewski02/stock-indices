Oracle Corporation (ORCL) is a global leader in enterprise software and cloud computing solutions. Founded in 1977 by Larry Ellison, Bob Miner, and Ed Oates as Software Development Laboratories, the company initially gained prominence for developing the first commercial relational database management system. Today, Oracle provides a comprehensive suite of integrated cloud applications and platform services, including its flagship Autonomous Database, enterprise resource planning (ERP) software, and human capital management tools. Following its 2022 acquisition of Cerner Corporation, Oracle significantly expanded its footprint in the healthcare technology sector. Headquartered in Austin, Texas, the company serves a diverse global clientele, enabling organizations to manage complex data environments, automate business processes, and leverage artificial intelligence to drive digital transformation. Oracle remains a cornerstone of the technology industry, consistently evolving its infrastructure to support the growing demands of cloud-native computing and large-scale data analytics.
