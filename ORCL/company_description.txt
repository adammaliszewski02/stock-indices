Oracle Corporation, identified by the stock ticker ORCL, is a multinational computer technology corporation headquartered in Austin, Texas. Founded in 1977 by Larry Ellison, Bob Miner, and Ed Oates under the name Software Development Laboratories, the company initially pioneered relational database management systems. Over the decades, Oracle expanded its enterprise software portfolio through significant research, development, and strategic acquisitions, including the high-profile buyouts of PeopleSoft, Sun Microsystems, and Cerner. Today, Oracle provides a comprehensive suite of cloud computing applications, platform services, and engineered systems. Its core business centers on enterprise resource planning software, supply chain management software, and customer relationship management software, alongside its flagship Oracle Database technology. Operating on a global scale, the company empowers organizations across diverse industries to manage critical data, modernize IT infrastructure, and accelerate digital transformation through its secure, scalable cloud environments.
