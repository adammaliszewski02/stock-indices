Oracle Corporation, traded under the stock ticker ORCL, is a multinational computer technology corporation headquartered in Austin, Texas. Founded in 1977 by Larry Ellison, Bob Miner, and Ed Oates under the name Software Development Laboratories, the company initially pioneered relational database management systems. Over the decades, Oracle expanded its enterprise software portfolio through significant research and major strategic acquisitions, including PeopleSoft, Siebel, Sun Microsystems, and Cerner. Today, Oracle's core business revolves around cloud engineered systems, database software, enterprise resource planning software, and supply chain management software. The company operates globally, providing businesses of all sizes with robust infrastructure, platform-as-a-service, and software-as-a-service solutions. Oracle continues to be a dominant force in enterprise technology, helping organizations manage data, automate business processes, and scale their digital operations securely in modern cloud environments.
