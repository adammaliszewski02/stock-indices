Oracle Corporation, identified by the stock ticker ORCL, is a multinational computer technology corporation headquartered in Austin, Texas. Founded in 1977 by Larry Ellison, Bob Miner, and Ed Oates under the name Software Development Laboratories, the company initially pioneered relational database management systems. Over the decades, Oracle expanded its footprint significantly through major acquisitions, notably acquiring Sun Microsystems in 2010—which brought the Java programming language and Solaris operating system into its portfolio—and Cerner Corporation in 2022 to strengthen its healthcare technology offerings. Today, Oracle provides a comprehensive and fully integrated stack of cloud applications and platform services, alongside its market-leading enterprise database software. Serving businesses of all sizes globally, the company operates massive data centers worldwide to deliver scalable cloud infrastructure, enterprise resource planning software, and supply chain management solutions. Oracle remains a foundational pillar of the modern enterprise software industry, driving digital transformation through its autonomous database technologies and enterprise cloud solutions.
