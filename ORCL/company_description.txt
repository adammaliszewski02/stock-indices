The stock ticker ORCL represents Oracle Corporation, a global enterprise technology company headquartered in Austin, Texas. Founded in 1977 by Larry Ellison, Bob Miner, and Ed Oates as Software Development Laboratories, Oracle pioneered the commercial relational database management system using SQL. Through decades of internal innovation and strategic acquisitions—such as Sun Microsystems, PeopleSoft, NetSuite, and Cerner—the company has expanded into a comprehensive enterprise technology provider.

Today, Oracle's business primarily encompasses cloud infrastructure, database software, and enterprise application solutions. Its flagship offerings include Oracle Cloud Infrastructure, autonomous database systems, and enterprise software suites covering enterprise resource planning, human capital management, supply chain management, and customer relationship management. In addition to software-as-a-service and platform-as-a-service solutions, Oracle provides engineered hardware systems, IT consulting, and customer support services. By transitioning legacy on-premises systems to hybrid and multi-cloud environments, Oracle remains a critical provider of high-performance computing and data management architectures for commercial enterprises and government agencies worldwide.
