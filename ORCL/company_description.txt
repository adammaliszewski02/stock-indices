Oracle Corporation, trading under the ticker ORCL, is an American multinational computer technology company specializing in enterprise software, cloud infrastructure, and database management systems. Founded in 1977 by Larry Ellison, Bob Miner, and Ed Oates as Software Development Laboratories, the company gained prominence by releasing the first commercially viable relational database management system using SQL. 

Today, Oracle operates primarily through its cloud services and license support business, delivering solutions such as Oracle Cloud Infrastructure (OCI) alongside enterprise applications for enterprise resource planning (ERP), customer relationship management (CRM), and supply chain management. Historically recognized for its flagship Oracle Database, the company has broadened its footprint through major strategic acquisitions, including Sun Microsystems in 2010, NetSuite in 2016, and healthcare technology provider Cerner in 2022. 

Headquartered in Austin, Texas, Oracle provides integrated cloud applications, platform services, and engineered hardware systems to millions of corporate, governmental, and educational clients worldwide, playing a central role in modern global digital infrastructure.
