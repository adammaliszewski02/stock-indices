The stock ticker KO represents The Coca-Cola Company, one of the world’s most recognizable and enduring non-alcoholic beverage corporations. Founded in 1886 by pharmacist Dr. John Stith Pemberton in Atlanta, Georgia, the company was officially incorporated in 1919 and has since grown into a global industry leader. 

Coca-Cola manufactures, markets, and sells a diverse portfolio of non-alcoholic beverages, which includes sparkling soft drinks, hydration products, juices, dairy, plant-based beverages, and ready-to-drink coffees and teas. In addition to its flagship Coca-Cola brand, the company owns well-known names such as Diet Coke, Sprite, Fanta, Dasani, Minute Maid, Powerade, and Costa Coffee. 

The company operates primarily through a specialized franchise distribution system. Coca-Cola manufactures and sells beverage concentrates and syrups to authorized independent bottling partners around the world. These bottlers then package, market, and distribute the finished branded products to retailers, wholesalers, restaurants, and convenience stores across more than 200 countries and territories.
