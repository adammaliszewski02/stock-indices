The stock ticker KO identifies The Coca-Cola Company, one of the world's largest and most recognized nonalcoholic beverage corporations. Founded in 1886 by pharmacist Dr. John Stith Pemberton in Atlanta, Georgia, the business was later incorporated in 1892 by Asa Griggs Candler, who rapidly expanded its brand reach. The company completed its initial public offering in 1919 and has since grown to serve consumers in more than 200 countries and territories.

The company's core operations involve manufacturing, marketing, and selling beverage concentrates and syrups, as well as finished liquid products. While best known for its flagship sparkling soft drinks such as Coca-Cola, Sprite, and Fanta, its diverse brand portfolio also encompasses water, sports drinks, juice, dairy, plant-based beverages, tea, and coffee, including labels like Dasani, Powerade, Minute Maid, and Costa Coffee. Coca-Cola primarily operates through a network of independent bottling partners, supplying them with proprietary concentrates and syrups. These authorized bottlers subsequently package, market, and distribute the finished beverages to retail and food service customers globally.
