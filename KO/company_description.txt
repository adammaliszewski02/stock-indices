The Coca-Cola Company, traded under the ticker symbol KO, is a premier global beverage corporation renowned for manufacturing, retailing, and marketing non-alcoholic beverage concentrates and syrups. Founded in 1892 in Atlanta, Georgia, following pharmacist John Stith Pemberton’s invention of the iconic soft drink formula in 1886, the enterprise has evolved into a total beverage company. Its extensive portfolio encompasses over 200 brands, ranging from carbonated soft drinks like Coca-Cola, Sprite, and Fanta to water, sports drinks, coffee, and teas, including Dasani, Powerade, and Gold Peak. Operating a distinctive franchise model, the company primarily produces syrup and concentrates, which it sells to master bottling partners worldwide who handle final packaging, distribution, and direct-to-store delivery. Headquartered in Atlanta, Coca-Cola remains one of the world's most recognizable brands and a cornerstone holding in global equities, driven by enduring consumer loyalty and strategic product diversification.
