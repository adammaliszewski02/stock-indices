The Coca-Cola Company, traded under the ticker symbol KO, is a premier global beverage corporation renowned for the manufacture, retail, and marketing of non-alcoholic drink concentrates and syrups. Founded in 1892 in Atlanta, Georgia, by pharmacist John Stith Pemberton, the enterprise evolved from a local soda fountain tonic into a cultural and commercial powerhouse. Its flagship product, Coca-Cola, invented in 1886, remains one of the most recognizable consumer brands in world history. Over the decades, the company expanded its portfolio far beyond its original carbonated soft drink to encompass a vast array of beverages, including sparkling soft drinks, waters, sports drinks, coffees, teas, juices, and plant-based alternatives. Operating a unique franchise model, The Coca-Cola Company primarily manufactures and sells concentrates, beverage bases, and syrups to authorized bottling partners worldwide, who then finish, package, merchandise, and distribute the final products. Today, the corporation manages a vast ecosystem of over 200 brands, serving consumers in more than 200 countries and territories, and maintains a steadfast commitment to global distribution scale, brand loyalty, and continuous product innovation.
