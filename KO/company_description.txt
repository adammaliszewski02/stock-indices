The Coca-Cola Company, traded under the ticker KO, is the world's largest non-alcoholic beverage company. Founded in 1892 in Atlanta, Georgia, following the invention of the iconic beverage formula by pharmacist John Stith Pemberton, the company has evolved into a global enterprise. It manufactures, markets, and sells hundreds of sparkling and still beverage brands across more than 200 countries and territories. Its extensive portfolio encompasses global flagships like Coca-Cola, Diet Coke, and Coca-Cola Zero Sugar, alongside popular brands such as Sprite, Fanta, Dasani, Powerade, Minute Maid, and Gold Peak. The company operates primarily through a franchise model, partnering with local bottling operations to manufacture, package, and distribute finished products, while focusing globally on brand marketing and the production of proprietary beverage concentrates, syrups, and industry-leading merchandising solutions.
