The stock ticker KO represents The Coca-Cola Company, the world’s largest non-alcoholic beverage enterprise. Founded in 1892 by Asa Griggs Candler, who acquired the soda formula created by pharmacist Dr. John Stith Pemberton in 1886, the Atlanta-based company officially went public in 1919. 

Coca-Cola manufactures, markets, and sells an extensive portfolio of beverage concentrates, syrups, and finished drinks across more than 200 countries and territories. While universally recognized for its flagship cola, the company owns or licenses over 200 brands across diverse categories, including sparkling soft drinks, water, sports drinks, juice, dairy, tea, and coffee. Notable brands include Sprite, Fanta, Dasani, Minute Maid, Powerade, and Costa Coffee. 

The company primarily operates through a franchise distribution model: Coca-Cola produces concentrated bases and syrups, which are subsequently sold to independent authorized bottling partners. These partners then manufacture, package, and distribute the finished beverages to retailers, restaurants, and vending operators globally.
