The Coca-Cola Company, traded under the stock ticker KO, is a premier global beverage corporation renowned for manufacturing, retailing, and marketing non-alcoholic beverage concentrates and syrups. Founded in 1892 in Atlanta, Georgia, by pharmacist John Stith Pemberton, the enterprise originally marketed its flagship beverage as a medicinal tonic. Over the decades, it evolved into a dominant consumer goods icon by expanding its portfolio to include hundreds of brands spanning sparkling soft drinks, waters, sports drinks, coffees, and teas, such as Diet Coke, Sprite, Fanta, Dasani, and Minute Maid. Operating through a distinctive franchise model, the company primarily produces syrup concentrate and sells it to authorized bottling partners worldwide, who then complete, package, and distribute the finished products to retailers and consumers. Today, The Coca-Cola Company stands as one of the most recognized and ubiquitous corporate entities globally, operating in over two hundred countries with an enduring focus on marketing, brand equity, and sustainable business practices.
