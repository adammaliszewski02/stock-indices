BlackRock, Inc., trading under the ticker BLK, is a premier global investment management corporation headquartered in New York City. Founded in 1988 by Larry Fink and a team of colleagues with a vision to combine rigorous risk management with fixed-income investing, the firm has grown to become the world’s largest asset manager. BlackRock provides a comprehensive range of investment, advisory, and risk-management services to institutional and retail clients worldwide. Its offerings span equities, fixed income, cash management, alternative investments, and real estate. The company is particularly renowned for its proprietary Aladdin technology platform, which integrates risk management and portfolio analytics, serving not only its own operations but also a vast array of external financial institutions. Additionally, BlackRock operates the iShares brand, the industry leader in exchange-traded funds, which revolutionized passive investing and dramatically broadened global market access. Through its fiduciary commitment, BlackRock manages trillions of dollars in assets on behalf of pensions, foundations, governments, and individual investors, shaping the modern financial landscape while championing long-term value creation and sustainability in global capital markets.
