BlackRock, Inc., traded under the ticker BLK, is a premier global investment management corporation headquartered in New York City. Founded in 1988 by Larry Fink and a team of colleagues initially as part of the Blackstone Group, the firm separated to become an independent entity in 1999 and completed its initial public offering in 1999. Over the decades, BlackRock grew rapidly through both organic expansion and strategic acquisitions, most notably acquiring Barclays Global Investors in 2009, which significantly bolstered its iShares exchange-traded fund platform. Today, BlackRock operates as the world's largest asset manager, serving institutional and retail clients worldwide. The company provides a comprehensive range of investment, advisory, and risk management services across diverse asset classes. Furthermore, it is widely recognized for its proprietary Aladdin technology platform, which integrates risk analytics, portfolio management, and trading capabilities, serving not only its own operations but also a vast array of external financial institutions globally.
