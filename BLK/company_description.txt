BlackRock, Inc. (ticker: BLK) is a premier global investment management corporation headquartered in New York City. Founded in 1988 by Larry Fink and a team of colleagues, the firm initially focused on institutional risk management and fixed-income institutional asset management before pioneering the exchange-traded fund industry through its acquisition of Barclays Global Investors in 2009. Today, BlackRock operates as the world's largest asset manager, providing a comprehensive range of investment, advisory, and risk management services to institutional and retail clients worldwide. The company is particularly renowned for its proprietary Aladdin technology platform, which integrates risk management, trading, and portfolio analytics for financial institutions globally. Driven by a commitment to helping investors build better financial futures, BlackRock manages capital across equities, fixed income, cash management, alternative investments, and real estate, while maintaining a leading role in sustainable investing and corporate stewardship.
