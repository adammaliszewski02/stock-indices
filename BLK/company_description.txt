BlackRock, Inc., trading under the ticker BLK, is a premier global investment management corporation headquartered in New York City. Founded in 1988 by Larry Fink and a team of colleagues with an initial focus on institutional risk management and fixed-income institutional asset management, the firm has grown to become the world’s largest asset manager. BlackRock provides a broad range of investment, advisory, and risk management services to institutional and retail clients worldwide. Its offerings include equity, fixed income, cash management, alternative investment, and real estate strategies. A cornerstone of the company’s modern business is its proprietary Aladdin technology platform, which delivers vital risk analytics and portfolio management capabilities to financial institutions globally. Additionally, BlackRock operates the iShares brand, one of the industry's most extensive ranges of exchange-traded funds, which revolutionized passive investing for millions of investors. As a systemically important financial institution, BlackRock manages trillions of dollars in assets, positioning itself as a central pillar of the modern global financial architecture.
