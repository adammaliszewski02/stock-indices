BlackRock, Inc., traded under the ticker BLK, is a premier global investment management corporation headquartered in New York City. Founded in 1988 by Larry Fink and a team of colleagues initially as part of the Blackstone Group, the firm separated to become an independent entity, completing its initial public offering in 1999. BlackRock provides institutional and individual clients around the world with a broad range of investment and risk management services. The firm operates through a diverse array of asset classes, spanning fixed income, equity, cash management, alternative investments, real estate, and advisory strategies. It is widely recognized as the world's largest asset manager, largely driven by the massive scale of its proprietary technology platform, Aladdin, and its industry-leading family of exchange-traded funds under the iShares brand. BlackRock serves governments, pension funds, insurance companies, foundations, and millions of individual investors, managing capital with a stated mission to help more and more people experience financial well-being.
