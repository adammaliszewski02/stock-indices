BlackRock, Inc., traded under the ticker BLK, is a premier global investment management corporation headquartered in New York City. Founded in 1988 by Larry Fink and a team of colleagues with a vision of combining risk management with institutional asset management, the firm has grown to become the world’s largest asset manager. BlackRock provides a broad range of investment, advisory, and risk management services to institutional and retail clients worldwide. Its offerings include equity, fixed income, cash management, alternative investments, and multi-asset strategies. The company is widely recognized for its proprietary Aladdin technology platform, which integrates risk management and portfolio analytics for financial institutions globally. Additionally, BlackRock operates the iShares brand, which is a global leader in exchange-traded funds, providing investors with efficient access to diverse market segments. As a systemically important financial institution, BlackRock manages capital on behalf of governments, pension funds, foundations, and millions of individual investors, playing a foundational role in the global financial system.
