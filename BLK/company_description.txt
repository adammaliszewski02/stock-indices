BlackRock, Inc., traded under the ticker BLK, is a premier global investment management corporation headquartered in New York City. Founded in 1988 by Larry Fink and seven partners as a fixed-income institutional risk management and asset manager, the firm has grown to become the world’s largest asset manager. BlackRock provides institutional and retail clients worldwide with a comprehensive range of financial services, including investment management, risk assessment, and advisory services. The company is widely recognized for its proprietary Aladdin technology platform, which integrates risk management and portfolio analytics for institutional investors globally. Additionally, BlackRock operates the iShares brand, the industry leader in exchange-traded funds. As a fiduciary, the company manages capital across diverse asset classes, encompassing equities, fixed income, real estate, and alternative investments, serving pension funds, endowments, foundations, and millions of individual investors worldwide.
