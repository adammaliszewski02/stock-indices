BlackRock, Inc. (BLK) is a global investment management corporation headquartered in New York City. Founded in 1988 by Larry Fink and seven partners, the firm initially operated as a subsidiary of The Blackstone Group before becoming an independent entity in 1999. Today, BlackRock stands as the world’s largest asset manager, overseeing trillions of dollars in assets for institutional and retail clients worldwide.

The company provides a comprehensive range of investment, advisory, and risk management solutions. It is perhaps best known for its iShares brand, which is a market leader in exchange-traded funds (ETFs). Beyond its investment products, BlackRock distinguishes itself through its proprietary financial technology platform, Aladdin, which provides sophisticated risk analytics and portfolio management tools to a broad spectrum of financial institutions. By integrating traditional asset management with advanced technological infrastructure, BlackRock plays a central role in the global financial ecosystem, influencing capital allocation across diverse asset classes, including equities, fixed income, and alternative investments.
