Intuit Inc. (NASDAQ: INTU) is an American financial technology company that develops financial management, tax preparation, and business solutions for consumers, small businesses, and self-employed individuals. Founded in 1983 by Scott Cook and Tom Proulx, Intuit originally transformed personal finance with its debut product, Quicken. Over the decades, the company evolved from desktop software into a cloud-based software-as-a-service (SaaS) powerhouse.

Today, Intuit operates an integrated platform powered by several industry-leading brands. Its core offerings include TurboTax, a prominent consumer and business tax-preparation software; QuickBooks, a comprehensive accounting, payroll, and invoicing platform tailored for small-to-midsize businesses; and Credit Karma, a consumer finance platform that provides free credit monitoring and personalized loan and credit card recommendations. In 2021, Intuit broadened its small-business ecosystem by acquiring Mailchimp, an email marketing and customer relationship management platform. Headquartered in Mountain View, California, Intuit increasingly leverages artificial intelligence to automate compliance, optimize cash flow, and deliver actionable financial insights to millions of users worldwide.
