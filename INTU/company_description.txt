Intuit Inc. (ticker: INTU) is a global financial technology platform that serves consumers, small businesses, and self-employed professionals through industry-leading digital products. Founded in 1983 by Scott Cook and Tom Proulx with the creation of the pioneering personal finance software Quicken, the company has evolved to focus on financial management, compliance, and tax preparation. Intuit's flagship offerings include TurboTax for consumer tax filing, QuickBooks for small business accounting and payroll, and Credit Karma, which provides personal finance and credit monitoring services. Additionally, the company acquired Mailchimp in 2021 to expand its capabilities into automated marketing and customer growth solutions. By leveraging advanced data analytics and artificial intelligence, Intuit delivers intuitive software experiences designed to power prosperity and financial confidence for over 100 million customers worldwide.
