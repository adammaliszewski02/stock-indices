Intuit Inc. is a global financial technology platform dedicated to powering prosperity for consumers, small businesses, and the self-employed. Founded in 1983 by Scott Cook and Tom Proulx, the company initially gained prominence by simplifying personal finance management through its flagship product, Quicken. Over the decades, Intuit has evolved into a comprehensive ecosystem centered on its core brands: TurboTax for tax preparation, QuickBooks for small business accounting, and Mailchimp for marketing automation. Following the 2021 acquisition of Credit Karma, the company further expanded its reach into personal finance services, including credit monitoring and banking. Headquartered in Mountain View, California, Intuit leverages artificial intelligence and data-driven insights to help its millions of customers manage their finances, optimize tax outcomes, and streamline business operations. By focusing on digital transformation and cloud-based solutions, Intuit remains a central pillar in the financial software industry, consistently adapting its tools to meet the complex needs of the modern digital economy.
