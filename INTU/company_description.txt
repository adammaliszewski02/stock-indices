Intuit Inc., traded under the ticker INTU, is a leading American multinational software corporation specializing in financial technology, tax preparation, and business management applications. Founded in 1983 by Scott Cook and Tom Proulx in Palo Alto, California, the company initially revolutionized personal finance with the launch of its flagship desktop checkbook-balancing software, Quicken. 

Over the decades, Intuit successfully transitioned its business model to address the evolving needs of consumers, small businesses, and accounting professionals. Today, the company is globally recognized for developing and marketing powerhouse software brands including TurboTax for automated income tax preparation, QuickBooks for comprehensive small business accounting and payroll services, and Mint for personal financial tracking. Additionally, Intuit expanded its market reach through strategic acquisitions, notably adding Credit Karma in 2020 to provide consumers with free credit monitoring, loans, and banking services, and Mailchimp in 2021 to integrate customer relationship management and marketing automation tools. Operating primarily as a cloud-based software-as-a-service provider, Intuit empowers millions of global users to manage their financial lives, streamline operations, and achieve financial confidence.
