Intuit Inc. (ticker: INTU) is a premier global technology platform that provides financial management, tax preparation, and business solutions to consumers, small businesses, and accounting professionals. Founded in 1983 by Scott Cook and Tom Proulx with the creation of the pioneering personal finance software Quicken, the company has since evolved into a cloud-driven ecosystem leader. Intuit’s core operations are anchored by its flagship product lines, which include TurboTax for automated tax filing, QuickBooks for comprehensive small business accounting and invoicing, and Mint, as well as Credit Karma, which offers credit monitoring and financial product recommendations. By leveraging advanced data analytics and artificial intelligence, the corporation delivers intuitive software and expert services designed to simplify complex financial workflows and empower financial confidence for millions of users worldwide. Headquartered in Mountain View, California, Intuit operates primarily through digital subscription models and service fees, consistently adapting its technological infrastructure to meet the shifting demands of modern personal and commercial finance.
