Intuit Inc., traded under the ticker INTU, is a prominent American multinational software corporation specializing in financial, accounting, and tax preparation software for individuals, small businesses, and accounting professionals. Founded in 1983 by Scott Cook and Tom Proulx in Mountain View, California, the company revolutionized personal finance management with the initial release of its flagship product, Quicken. Over the decades, Intuit expanded its portfolio significantly to capture the small business and tax markets, most notably through the development and acquisition of industry-standard platforms such as TurboTax, QuickBooks, and Credit Karma. Headquartered in Mountain View, Intuit has evolved from a desktop software pioneer into a cloud-driven artificial intelligence and financial technology platform. Today, the company utilizes advanced data analytics and machine learning to deliver automated financial solutions, aiming to power prosperity for millions of customers globally through its integrated digital ecosystem.
