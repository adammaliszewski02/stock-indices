Intuit Inc. (ticker: INTU) is a leading American multinational financial technology company that develops software and provides financial, accounting, and tax preparation services for individuals, small businesses, and accounting professionals. Founded in 1983 by Scott Cook and Tom Proulx in Palo Alto, California, the company initially gained prominence with the development and release of its flagship personal finance software, Quicken. Over the subsequent decades, Intuit expanded its portfolio significantly to capture the small business and consumer tax markets. Key milestones in its growth include the 1993 launch of TurboTax, which became the market leader in consumer tax preparation software, and the 1998 acquisition of the web-based accounting software QuickBooks. Today, Intuit operates primarily through major product segments that include Small Business and Self-Employed, Consumer, Credit Karma, and Mailchimp. By leveraging advanced data analytics, artificial intelligence, and cloud-based platforms, the company aims to deliver digital financial products that empower its global customer base to manage money, process payments, and grow their enterprises efficiently.
