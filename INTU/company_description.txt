Intuit Inc. (NASDAQ: INTU) is an American financial technology company that provides financial management, compliance, and marketing software to consumers, small businesses, and accounting professionals. Founded in 1983 by Scott Cook and Tom Proulx in Mountain View, California, the company first revolutionized personal finance with its flagship product, Quicken. Over four decades, Intuit transitioned from desktop software to a robust, cloud-based platform driven by data and artificial intelligence. 

Today, Intuit's business is anchored by several globally recognized brands. TurboTax serves as a premier solution for consumer tax preparation, while QuickBooks offers comprehensive accounting, payroll, and merchant payment processing for small and medium-sized enterprises. The company has aggressively expanded its ecosystem through major acquisitions, notably purchasing Credit Karma in 2020 to provide consumer credit monitoring and financial products, and Mailchimp in 2021 to deliver digital marketing and customer relationship management tools. Headquartered in Mountain View, California, Intuit plays a central role in the global fintech landscape by streamlining complex financial processes for millions of customers worldwide.
