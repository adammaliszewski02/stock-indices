Chevron Corporation, traded under the ticker CVX, is a multinational energy corporation and one of the world's largest integrated petroleum companies. Headquartered in San Houston, Texas, the enterprise traces its origins back to the 1879 formation of the Pacific Coast Oil Company, which later evolved into Standard Oil Company of California before adopting the Chevron name. The business engages in every facet of the oil and gas industries, encompassing hydrocarbon exploration and production, refining, marketing and transportation, petrochemical manufacturing, and power generation. In recent years, Chevron has also expanded its portfolio into renewable energy sectors, including geothermal, biofuels, and hydrogen initiatives, aiming to reduce its carbon intensity while continuing to meet global energy demands.
