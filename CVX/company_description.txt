Chevron Corporation, trading under the ticker CVX, is one of the world's leading integrated energy companies. Headquartered in Houston, Texas, the multinational corporation operates across every facet of the oil and gas industry, including hydrocarbon exploration and production, refining, marketing and transportation, petrochemical manufacturing, and power generation. The company traces its origins back to the 1879 founding of the Pacific Coast Oil Company in California, which later evolved through a series of significant mergers and acquisitions. Most notably, Chevron acquired Texaco in 2001 and merged with Noble Energy in 2020, substantially expanding its global upstream portfolio. In recent years, Chevron has strategically diversified its operations to include lower-carbon initiatives, investing heavily in renewable fuels, hydrogen, geothermal energy, and carbon capture technologies to address global energy transitions while maintaining its core fossil fuel production.
