Chevron Corporation, trading under the ticker CVX, is one of the world's leading integrated energy companies. Headquartered in San Ramon, California, the multinational enterprise engages in every facet of the oil and gas industry, including hydrocarbon exploration and production, refining, marketing and transport, chemical manufacturing, and power generation. The corporation's origins trace back to the formation of the Pacific Coast Oil Company in 1879, which later evolved through a series of significant mergers and acquisitions, most notably the 2001 merger between Chevron and Texaco, and the 2020 acquisition of Noble Energy. Operating globally in dozens of countries, Chevron also actively invests in lower-carbon technologies, such as renewable fuels, hydrogen, and carbon capture, utilization, and storage, aiming to address the dual challenge of meeting growing global energy demand while lowering environmental impacts.
