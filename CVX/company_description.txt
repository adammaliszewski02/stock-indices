Chevron Corporation, traded under the ticker CVX, is a leading multinational energy conglomerate engaged in every facet of the oil and gas industry. The enterprise explores for, produces, and transports crude oil and natural gas; refines, markets, and distributes transportation fuels and lubricants; and manufactures and sells petrochemical products. Headquartered in San Houston, Texas, the company operates globally with significant upstream and downstream assets. Chevron traces its origins back to the 1879 founding of the Pacific Coast Oil Company in California, which was later acquired by Standard Oil. Following the 1911 antitrust dissolution of the Standard Oil trust, the entity evolved through major corporate integrations, notably the 1984 merger with Gulf Oil and the 2001 acquisition of Texaco, eventually adopting the Chevron name. Today, the corporation actively adapts its business model to meet evolving global energy demands while investing in lower-carbon technologies, renewable energy initiatives, and carbon capture solutions to address sustainability and climate change challenges.
