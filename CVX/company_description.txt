Chevron Corporation, trading under the ticker symbol CVX, is a leading American multinational energy corporation and one of the world's largest publicly traded integrated energy companies. Tracing its origins back to the 1879 formation of the Pacific Coast Oil Company in California, the enterprise grew through a series of strategic mergers, most notably absorbing Gulf Oil in 1984 and Texaco in 2001, eventually adopting its current name in 2005. Headquartered in San Houston, Texas, Chevron operates across virtually every facet of the energy sector. The company engages in hydrocarbon exploration, crude oil and natural gas production, refining, marketing and transportation, petrochemical manufacturing, and power generation. Furthermore, Chevron actively invests in lower-carbon initiatives, focusing on renewable fuels, hydrogen energy, carbon capture, and offsets to support the global energy transition while maintaining its traditional oil and gas portfolio.
