Chevron Corporation, traded under the ticker CVX, is one of the world's leading integrated energy companies. Headquartered in Houston, Texas, the multinational corporation operates across every facet of the oil and gas industry, including hydrocarbon exploration and production, refining, marketing and transportation, petrochemical manufacturing, and power generation. The company traces its origins back to the 1879 formation of the Pacific Coast Oil Company in California, which later became Standard Oil Company of California (Socal). Socal subsequently merged with Gulf Oil in 1984 to form Chevron, and later expanded its global footprint through major mergers with Texaco in 2001 and Noble Energy in 2020. In recent years, Chevron has actively diversified its portfolio by investing in lower-carbon initiatives, such as renewable fuels, hydrogen, carbon capture, and geothermal energy, to address the global transition toward sustainable energy sources while maintaining its core conventional operations.
