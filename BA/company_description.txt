The Boeing Company, trading under the ticker BA, is a preeminent global aerospace corporation and the world’s largest manufacturer of commercial jetliners. Headquartered in Arlington, Virginia, the company operates through three primary business segments: Commercial Airplanes, Defense, Space & Security, and Global Services. Boeing designs, develops, and supports a diverse portfolio of products, including the 737 and 787 aircraft families, military rotorcraft, satellite systems, and advanced autonomous vehicles.

Founded in 1916 by William E. Boeing in Seattle, Washington, the company played a pivotal role in the development of modern aviation, notably producing iconic aircraft like the B-17 Flying Fortress during World War II and the 747 "Jumbo Jet," which revolutionized international travel in 1970. Today, Boeing serves as a critical contractor for the U.S. Department of Defense and NASA, while maintaining a vast supply chain that supports airlines and government customers in over 150 countries. Through its integrated business model, Boeing provides comprehensive lifecycle support, including maintenance, modifications, and digital analytics, solidifying its position as a cornerstone of the global aerospace and defense industrial base.
