The Boeing Company (BA) is a preeminent global aerospace corporation and the world's largest manufacturer of commercial jetliners. Headquartered in Arlington, Virginia, the company operates through three primary business segments: Commercial Airplanes, Defense, Space & Security, and Global Services. Boeing designs, manufactures, and supports a diverse portfolio of products, including the 737 and 787 aircraft families, military rotorcraft, satellite systems, and autonomous vehicles.

Founded in 1916 by William E. Boeing in Seattle, Washington, the company played a pivotal role in the development of modern aviation. Throughout the 20th century, Boeing became synonymous with commercial flight, famously introducing the 747 "Jumbo Jet" in 1969, which revolutionized international travel. Today, Boeing remains a cornerstone of the global aerospace and defense industrial base, providing critical technology and services to commercial airlines and government customers in more than 150 countries. The company continues to focus on aerospace innovation, sustainability, and the advancement of next-generation flight technologies.
