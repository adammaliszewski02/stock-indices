The ticker symbol BA corresponds to The Boeing Company, a prominent American multinational aerospace and defense corporation. Founded in 1916 by William E. Boeing in Seattle, Washington, the company evolved from early seaplane manufacturing into a global industry leader. Today, Boeing operates through major business segments that design, manufacture, and service commercial airplanes, defense products, and space systems for customers in over 150 countries. As one of the world's largest aerospace manufacturers, the corporation encompasses the production of commercial jetliners, military aircraft, satellites, and advanced communication systems, while also providing comprehensive fleet support and training services to global airlines and government agencies.
