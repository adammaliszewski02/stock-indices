The stock ticker BA represents The Boeing Company, a leading global aerospace manufacturer founded in Seattle, Washington, in 1916 by William E. Boeing. Boeing operates as the world's largest aerospace company and a prominent defense contractor. Its business is principally organized into three primary segments: Commercial Airplanes, Defense, Space and Security, and Global Services. The company designs, develops, manufactures, and sells commercial jetliners, military aircraft, satellites, human spacecraft, and advanced electronic and defense systems. Furthermore, Boeing provides comprehensive aftermarket support, maintenance, training, and logistical services to airline and government customers worldwide. Over its century-long history, the company has played a foundational role in the evolution of modern commercial aviation and national defense infrastructure.
