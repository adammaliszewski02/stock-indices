The Boeing Company, trading under the ticker BA, is a preeminent global aerospace corporation and the world’s largest manufacturer of commercial jetliners. Headquartered in Arlington, Virginia, the company operates through three primary business units: Commercial Airplanes, Defense, Space & Security, and Global Services. Boeing designs, develops, and supports a diverse portfolio of products, including the 737 and 787 aircraft families, military rotorcraft, satellite systems, and autonomous vehicles.

Founded in 1916 by William E. Boeing in Seattle, Washington, the company has played a foundational role in the evolution of aviation. Throughout the 20th century, Boeing became synonymous with the Jet Age, introducing iconic aircraft such as the 747 "Jumbo Jet." Today, the company remains a critical contractor for the U.S. Department of Defense and a major exporter, providing essential engineering, maintenance, and training services to airlines and government customers in over 150 countries. Boeing continues to focus on advancing aerospace technology, sustainable aviation initiatives, and the modernization of global defense infrastructure.
