The stock ticker BA corresponds to The Boeing Company, a prominent American multinational corporation founded in 1916 by William Boeing in Seattle, Washington. Boeing is a global aerospace leader primarily engaged in the design, development, manufacture, and sale of commercial jetliners, military aircraft, satellites, and missile defense systems. The company operates through major segments including Commercial Airplanes, Defense, Space and Security, and Global Services, serving airline and government customers in more than 150 countries. Recognized as one of the world's largest aerospace manufacturers and a major defense contractor, Boeing has played a foundational role in the history of aviation, achieving significant milestones such as the development of the iconic 747 jumbo jet and supporting major space exploration programs.
