The stock ticker BA corresponds to The Boeing Company, a multinational aerospace corporation founded in 1916 by William Boeing in Seattle, Washington. Boeing is one of the world's largest aerospace manufacturers and a leading defense contractor. The company operates primarily through three main business segments: Commercial Airplanes, Defense, Space and Security, and Global Services. Boeing designs, develops, manufactures, and sells commercial jetliners, military aircraft, satellites, strategic defense systems, and space communication systems globally. Additionally, the company provides extensive fleet support, maintenance, training, and modification services to commercial and governmental airline operators worldwide. Over its century-long history, Boeing has played a foundational role in shaping modern commercial aviation and aerospace technology, though it has navigated significant operational and safety challenges in recent years.
