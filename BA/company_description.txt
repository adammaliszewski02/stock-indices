The Boeing Company, traded under the stock ticker BA, is a premier global aerospace manufacturer and the world's largest defense contractor. Headquartered in Arlington, Virginia, the company operates through three primary segments: Commercial Airplanes, Defense, Space & Security, and Global Services. Boeing designs, develops, manufactures, and sells commercial jetliners, military aircraft, satellites, weapons, and electronic defense systems, while also providing comprehensive fleet support and training services to airlines and government customers worldwide. 

The company traces its origins back to July 1916, when William E. Boeing founded Pacific Aero Products Co. in Seattle, Washington, shortly thereafter renaming it the Boeing Airplane Company. Throughout the twentieth century, Boeing played a pivotal role in the advancement of aviation and aerospace history, most notably introducing the iconic 747 jumbo jet in 1969 and contributing significantly to the Apollo space program. Through strategic acquisitions, including the 1997 merger with McDonnell Douglas, Boeing expanded its portfolio to encompass a vast array of defense and space technologies, establishing its enduring position as a cornerstone of global transportation and national security infrastructure.
