Johnson & Johnson, traded under the ticker JNJ, is a preeminent global healthcare holding company founded in 1886 by Robert Wood Johnson, James Wood Johnson, and Edward Mead Johnson in New Brunswick, New Jersey. The corporation operates through two primary sectors: Innovative Medicine and MedTech. The Innovative Medicine segment focuses on discovering, developing, and delivering advanced therapeutics across complex disease areas including immunology, oncology, neuroscience, cardiovascular disease, and pulmonary hypertension. The MedTech segment designs, manufactures, and distributes a comprehensive portfolio of medical devices and technology used in orthopedic, surgical, cardiovascular, and specialty care interventions. Following the strategic spin-off of its consumer health division, Kenvue, in 2023, Johnson & Johnson completed its evolution into a dedicated, pure-play healthcare company. Driven by continuous research and development, the enterprise serves healthcare professionals and patients globally, maintaining a legacy of scientific innovation and clinical impact spanning over a century.
