Johnson & Johnson (J&J), traded under the ticker JNJ, is a globally prominent American multinational healthcare corporation. Founded in 1886 by the Johnson brothers in New Brunswick, New Jersey, the company initially focused on manufacturing ready-to-use surgical dressings and quickly expanded into antiseptic products. Over the decades, J&J evolved into a comprehensive healthcare titan, pioneering numerous pharmaceutical breakthroughs, medical devices, and consumer health products. Historically recognized for iconic consumer brands, the company executed a major strategic shift in 2023 by spinning off its consumer health division into a separate publicly traded entity named Kenvue. Today, J&J operates primarily through two core sectors: Innovative Medicine and MedTech. The Innovative Medicine segment focuses on discovering, developing, and delivering advanced therapeutics in complex disease areas such as oncology, immunology, neuroscience, and cardiovascular disease. The MedTech segment provides a broad range of surgical, orthopedic, and vision solutions designed to advance patient care and improve surgical outcomes worldwide. Headquartered in New Brunswick, New Jersey, Johnson & Johnson remains a cornerstone of the global healthcare industry, driven by a longstanding corporate credo that prioritizes the well-being of patients, doctors, nurses, and employees.
