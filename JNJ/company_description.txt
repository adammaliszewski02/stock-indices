Johnson & Johnson, traded under the ticker JNJ, is a preeminent global healthcare holding company engaged in the research, development, manufacture, and sale of a broad range of products in the healthcare field. The corporation operates primarily through two major segments: Innovative Medicine, which focuses on immunology, oncology, neuroscience, cardiovascular disease, and pulmonary hypertension; and MedTech, which provides orthopedics, surgery, vision, and interventional solutions. 

Founded in 1886 by Robert Wood Johnson, James Wood Johnson, and Edward Mead Johnson in New Brunswick, New Jersey, the company initially manufactured ready-to-use antiseptic surgical dressings. Over the decades, it expanded globally through organic innovation and strategic acquisitions, establishing a legacy of consumer and clinical health advancements, most notably the invention of Johnson's Baby Powder and first aid kits. In 2023, Johnson & Johnson completed the corporate separation of its consumer health division into a newly traded entity named Kenvue, refocusing its entire operational portfolio entirely on pharmaceuticals and medical technology. Today, JNJ remains a cornerstone of the global healthcare sector, driven by a commitment to advancing health and medical science worldwide.
