Johnson & Johnson, traded under the ticker JNJ, is a preeminent global healthcare holding company headquartered in New Brunswick, New Jersey. Founded in 1886 by Robert Wood Johnson, James Wood Johnson, and Edward Mead Johnson, the enterprise originally specialized in ready-to-use surgical dressings and quickly expanded into antiseptic products and pharmaceuticals. Today, the corporation operates through two primary divisions: Innovative Medicine and MedTech. The Innovative Medicine segment focuses on discovering, developing, and delivering advanced therapeutics in complex therapeutic areas such as immunology, oncology, neuroscience, and cardiovascular disease. The MedTech segment provides a broad portfolio of medical technology solutions, including surgical instruments, orthopedics, and vision care products, designed to improve patient outcomes worldwide. Following the 2023 spin-off of its consumer health division, Kenvue, Johnson & Johnson completed its strategic realignment to concentrate entirely on higher-growth, science-driven pharmaceutical and medical technology markets.
