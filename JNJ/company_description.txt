Johnson & Johnson, trading under the ticker JNJ, is a leading global healthcare corporation focused on pharmaceuticals and medical technologies. Founded in 1886 by the Johnson brothers in New Brunswick, New Jersey, the company originally built its reputation by pioneering mass-produced sterile surgical dressings and sutures. Over the following century, it evolved into one of the world's largest and most diversified healthcare conglomerates. 

Following the 2023 spin-off of its consumer health division into Kenvue, Johnson & Johnson streamlined its operations into two specialized business segments: Innovative Medicine and MedTech. The Innovative Medicine arm researches and develops advanced therapeutics across key areas including oncology, immunology, neuroscience, and cardiovascular health. The MedTech segment delivers innovative technologies for interventional procedures, orthopedics, general surgery, and vision care. 

Headquartered in New Brunswick, the company maintains a premier position in global healthcare, driven by extensive research and development, a vast global commercial footprint, and an exceptional track record of long-term financial stability.
