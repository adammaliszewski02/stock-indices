Johnson & Johnson, trading under the ticker JNJ, is a global healthcare corporation founded in 1886 by brothers Robert Wood, James Wood, and Edward Mead Johnson in New Brunswick, New Jersey. The company initially built its reputation by pioneering commercial sterile surgical dressings and sutures, shaping modern aseptic techniques in medicine. 

Following the 2023 spin-off of its consumer healthcare division into Kenvue, Johnson & Johnson operates as a focused healthcare giant across two core segments: Innovative Medicine and MedTech. The Innovative Medicine business develops advanced pharmaceutical treatments for complex diseases, with a strategic emphasis on oncology, immunology, neuroscience, and cardiovascular health. The MedTech segment designs, manufactures, and distributes an extensive portfolio of medical devices and technologies, including orthopedic implants, digital surgery systems, advanced instrumentation, and cardiovascular intervention tools. Headquartered in New Brunswick, Johnson & Johnson remains one of the world's largest and most financially stable healthcare organizations, consistently investing in research and development to drive clinical advancements across global markets.
