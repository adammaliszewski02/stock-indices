Johnson & Johnson (ticker: JNJ) is a globally prominent American multinational healthcare company. Founded in 1886 by Robert Wood Johnson I, James Wood Johnson, and Edward Mead Johnson, the enterprise has evolved into one of the world's largest and most broadly based healthcare organizations. The company operates primarily through two major sectors: Innovative Medicine, which focuses on the research, development, and marketing of advanced therapeutics for complex diseases in immunology, oncology, neuroscience, and cardiovascular health, and MedTech, which delivers advanced medical devices and surgical solutions. Historically recognized for its consumer health brands, Johnson & Johnson completed the separation of its consumer health segment into a standalone publicly traded company named Kenvue in 2023. Headquartered in New Brunswick, New Jersey, the enterprise serves millions of patients globally, driven by a longstanding corporate philosophy emphasizing scientific innovation, rigorous ethical standards, and a commitment to advancing human health.
