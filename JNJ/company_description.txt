Johnson & Johnson (NYSE: JNJ) is a global healthcare leader that discovers, develops, and delivers advanced healthcare solutions. Following the 2023 spin-off of its legacy consumer health business, Kenvue, the company streamlined its operations into two primary segments: Innovative Medicine and MedTech. The Innovative Medicine division focuses on high-impact therapeutic areas such as oncology, immunology, neuroscience, and cardiovascular diseases. The MedTech division provides advanced medical technologies across orthopedics, interventional solutions, surgery, and vision.

Founded in 1886 by brothers Robert Wood Johnson, James Wood Johnson, and Edward Mead Johnson in New Brunswick, New Jersey, the enterprise originally revolutionized healthcare by mass-producing sterile surgical supplies and commercial first-aid kits. Over the subsequent decades, strategic acquisitions and extensive research and development solidified its position as a pillar of the global pharmaceutical and medical device industries. 

Today, Johnson & Johnson is a component of the Dow Jones Industrial Average and remains one of the few global corporations to maintain a prime AAA credit rating, reflecting its robust financial stability and ongoing role in shaping modern clinical treatments.
