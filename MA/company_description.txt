Mastercard Incorporated, traded under the ticker MA, is a leading global technology company in the payments industry. It operates one of the world’s largest payment-processing networks, facilitating electronic transactions among consumers, financial institutions, merchants, and governments across more than 210 countries and territories. Rather than issuing cards or extending credit directly, Mastercard generates revenue by charging fees based on gross dollar volume and transaction switching, while also offering value-added services such as cybersecurity, fraud prevention, and data analytics.

The company was founded in 1966 as the Interbank Card Association by an alliance of regional banks seeking to create a competitor to BankAmericard, which later became Visa. In 1969, the alliance introduced the Master Charge brand and its iconic interlocking red and yellow circles, officially adopting the name Mastercard in 1979. Headquartered in Purchase, New York, the enterprise operated as a bank-owned membership cooperative until completing its initial public offering on the New York Stock Exchange in May 2006. Today, Mastercard continues to expand its core business by driving innovations in digital commerce, open banking, tokenization, and cross-border payment infrastructure.
