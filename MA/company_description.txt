Mastercard Incorporated, trading under the ticker MA, is a leading multinational technology company specializing in the global payments industry. Founded in 1966 as Interbank Card Association before becoming Master Charge, the company connects consumers, financial institutions, merchants, governments, and businesses across more than 210 countries and territories. Mastercard facilitates secure, digital, and cashless financial transactions primarily through its branded payment processing networks, which include credit, debit, and prepaid cards. Beyond traditional transaction processing, the company provides a comprehensive suite of innovative financial services, including cyber security solutions, fraud prevention, open banking, data analytics, and consulting services. By leveraging advanced technology and robust infrastructure, Mastercard drives financial inclusion and ensures the seamless, fast, and safe flow of money worldwide, continuously adapting to the rapid evolution of digital commerce and modern payment ecosystems.
