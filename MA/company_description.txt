Mastercard Incorporated, trading under the stock ticker MA, is a leading multinational technology company in the global payments industry. Founded in 1966 as Interbank Card Association before becoming MasterCard in 1979, the company facilitates the safe, efficient, and seamless transfer of funds between consumers, financial institutions, merchants, governments, and businesses across more than 210 countries and territories. Rather than issuing cards or extending credit directly, Mastercard operates the foundational processing network that connects these entities. Its core business centers on transaction processing, authorization, and settlement services. Over the decades, the company has evolved beyond traditional credit and debit cards to provide advanced cybersecurity solutions, fraud prevention, open banking, data analytics, and digital identity services. By continuously investing in emerging financial technologies and blockchain initiatives, Mastercard drives financial inclusion and enables the digital economy, ensuring that global commerce remains fast, convenient, and secure.
