Mastercard Incorporated, trading under the stock ticker MA, is a leading multinational technology company in the global payments industry. The company connects consumers, financial institutions, merchants, governments, and businesses across more than 210 countries and territories, facilitating secure digital, electronic, and mobile financial transactions. Mastercard does not issue cards or extend credit; instead, it provides the critical transaction processing infrastructure and financial network that enables seamless value transfer. 

The enterprise traces its origins to 1966, when a group of California banks formed the Interbank Card Association to compete with Bank of America’s BankAmericard. This association was rebranded as Master Charge in 1969 and ultimately adopted the name Mastercard in 1979. In 2006, Mastercard transitioned from a membership organization owned by its constituent financial institutions to a publicly traded corporation on the New York Stock Exchange. Over the subsequent decades, the company has evolved beyond traditional credit and debit processing into cybersecurity, artificial intelligence, open banking, and data analytics solutions, cementing its position as a foundational pillar of modern global commerce and digital finance.
