Mastercard Incorporated, traded under the ticker symbol MA, is a leading global technology company operating in the payments industry. The company maintains one of the world's largest payment processing networks, connecting consumers, financial institutions, merchants, and governments across more than 210 countries and territories. Mastercard facilitates electronic funds transfers through its branded credit, debit, and prepaid cards, as well as an array of digital payment solutions. Rather than acting as a direct lender, Mastercard provides the underlying network infrastructure, generating revenue from transaction processing fees and related services, including cybersecurity, fraud detection, and data analytics.

The company traces its origins to 1966, when several regional bankcard associations established the Interbank Card Association to compete with BankAmericard, which later became Visa. The brand was renamed Master Charge in 1969 before adopting the name Mastercard in 1979. Historically structured as a bank-owned membership association, Mastercard transitioned to a publicly traded corporation with its initial public offering on the New York Stock Exchange in May 2006. Today, it stands as a cornerstone of the global digital economy.
