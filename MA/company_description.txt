Mastercard Incorporated, trading under the stock ticker MA, is a leading multinational technology company in the global payments industry. Founded in 1966 as Interbank Card Association before becoming MasterCard in 1979, the company facilitates the safe, efficient, and seamless transfer of funds between consumers, financial institutions, merchants, governments, and businesses across more than 210 countries and territories. Rather than issuing cards or extending credit directly, Mastercard operates a proprietary global payments network that processes transaction authorizations, clearings, and settlements. Beyond its core credit, debit, and prepaid card processing operations, the company has diversified its business model significantly. It now provides an extensive suite of value-added services, including advanced cybersecurity, fraud prevention, data analytics, consulting, and loyalty and rewards solutions. In recent years, Mastercard has also expanded into open banking, digital identity services, and cross-border B2B payment rails to adapt to the evolving digital economy and capture new revenue streams outside traditional consumer card transactions.
