Stock ticker MA represents Mastercard Incorporated, a multinational technology leader in the global payments industry. Mastercard does not issue credit cards, extend credit, or set consumer interest rates. Instead, it operates a secure global payments processing network connecting consumers, financial institutions, merchants, governments, and businesses across more than 210 countries and territories. The company generates revenue through transaction switching and processing fees, as well as a rapidly growing portfolio of value-added services, including cybersecurity, fraud prevention, data analytics, and digital identity solutions.

Mastercard traces its origins to 1966, when an alliance of regional banks formed the Interbank Card Association to rival BankAmericard, the predecessor to Visa. The network was rebranded as Master Charge in 1969 before adopting the Mastercard name in 1979. Originally operating as a member-owned cooperative, Mastercard transitioned into a publicly traded corporation with its initial public offering on the New York Stock Exchange in 2006. Today, Mastercard remains a foundational pillar of modern financial infrastructure, continually driving the shift toward cashless economies through contactless payments, tokenization, cross-border remittance solutions, and open banking technologies.
