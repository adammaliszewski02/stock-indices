Mastercard Incorporated, trading under the ticker MA, is a leading global technology company in the payments industry. The company connects consumers, financial institutions, merchants, governments, and businesses across more than 210 countries and territories, enabling them to use digital, electronic, and mobile payment solutions. Founded in 1966 as Interbank Card Association before adopting the Master Charge name and subsequently rebranding to Mastercard in 1979, the organization has evolved far beyond its origins as a traditional credit card issuer. Today, it operates a proprietary global payments network that processes transaction authorisations, clearings, and settlements. Beyond core payment processing, Mastercard provides a comprehensive suite of value-added services, including cybersecurity solutions, fraud prevention, data analytics, loyalty programs, and emerging digital identity services. By facilitating secure, convenient, and cost-effective financial transactions, Mastercard drives global commerce and financial inclusion while continuously adapting to the digital economy through investments in open banking, blockchain technology, and real-time account-to-account payment systems.
