TSLA is the stock ticker for Tesla, Inc., an American multinational automotive and clean energy company headquartered in Austin, Texas. Founded in 2003 by Martin Eberhard and Marc Tarpenning—with Elon Musk playing a pivotal role in early funding and leadership—Tesla originally focused on the design and production of electric vehicles. Over time, the company expanded its business model to encompass the entire sustainable energy ecosystem. Tesla currently manufactures battery energy storage systems for home-use to grid-scale, solar panels, and solar roof tiles through its acquisition of SolarCity in 2016. Furthermore, the company develops and deploys advanced driver-assistance systems, marketed as Autopilot and Full Self-Driving, alongside a growing portfolio of robotics and artificial intelligence initiatives. Tesla operates globally with major manufacturing facilities, known as Gigafactories, situated in the United States, China, and Germany.
