TSLA is the stock ticker for Tesla, Inc., an American multinational automotive and clean energy company headquartered in Austin, Texas. Founded in 2003 by Martin Eberhard and Marc Tarpenning—with Elon Musk playing a pivotal role in early funding and leadership starting in 2004—the company designs, manufactures, and sells fully electric vehicles, solar energy generation systems, and stationary battery energy storage products. Tesla achieved significant industry prominence in 2008 with the production of its first vehicle model, the Roadster, which proved the market viability of high-performance electric cars. This was followed by the mass-market success of the Model S sedan, Model X SUV, Model Y crossover, and Model 3 sedan, alongside heavy investment in autonomous driving technology and global supercharger infrastructure. Today, Tesla operates as a publicly traded corporation listed on the NASDAQ exchange, having evolved into one of the world's most valuable automakers and a leading catalyst for the global transition toward sustainable transport and renewable energy solutions.
