TSLA is the stock ticker for Tesla, Inc., an American multinational automotive and clean energy company headquartered in Austin, Texas. Founded in 2003 by Martin Eberhard and Marc Tarpenning—with Elon Musk playing a pivotal funding and leadership role from its early stages—the company designs, manufactures, and sells fully electric vehicles, solar energy generation systems, and stationary battery storage products. Tesla achieved significant historical prominence by accelerating the global automotive industry's transition toward electric mobility, famously introducing the Roadster in 2008 and later scaling production with the mass-market Model S, Model 3, Model X, and Model Y. Beyond automotive manufacturing, Tesla develops advanced driver-assistance systems, including its proprietary Full Self-Driving technology, and invests heavily in artificial intelligence, robotics, and sustainable energy infrastructure to support its long-term mission of expediting the world's transition to sustainable energy.
