TSLA is the stock ticker for Tesla, Inc., an American multinational automotive and clean energy company headquartered in Austin, Texas. Founded in 2003 by Martin Eberhard and Marc Tarpenning—with Elon Musk playing a pivotal role in early funding and leadership—Tesla originally focused on the design and manufacture of all-electric vehicles. The company delivered its first production car, the Roadster, in 2008, followed by the groundbreaking Model S sedan in 2012, which established its reputation for high-performance electric mobility. Over time, Tesla expanded its business model beyond passenger cars into the broader sustainable energy ecosystem. Today, the company designs, manufactures, and sells battery energy storage systems for home, commercial, and utility-scale grids, as well as solar panels and solar roofs following its acquisition of SolarCity in 2016. Furthermore, Tesla develops advanced driver-assistance systems, marketed as Autopilot and Full Self-Driving, and is actively investing in artificial intelligence, robotics, and charging infrastructure. As a pioneer in the mass adoption of electric transportation, Tesla remains a dominant force in the global automotive and renewable energy sectors.
