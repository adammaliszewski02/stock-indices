TSLA is the stock ticker for Tesla, Inc., an American multinational automotive and clean energy company headquartered in Austin, Texas. Founded in 2003 by Martin Eberhard and Marc Tarpenning—with Elon Musk playing a pivotal role in early financing, leadership, and long-term vision—the company designs, manufactures, and sells fully electric vehicles, solar roof panels, and residential and commercial battery energy storage systems. Tesla popularized the modern electric vehicle market through innovations in battery technology, autonomous driving features, and a proprietary global Supercharger network. Beyond its core automotive manufacturing operations, the company operates in the broader energy generation and storage sector, while also developing artificial intelligence software, robotics, and solar technology. As of recent years, Tesla is publicly traded on the Nasdaq Global Select Market and stands as one of the most valuable automotive companies in the world, having fundamentally transformed consumer and industrial adoption of sustainable transport and clean energy solutions.
