TSLA is the stock ticker for Tesla, Inc., an American multinational automotive and clean energy company headquartered in Austin, Texas. Founded in 2003 by Martin Eberhard and Marc Tarpenning—with Elon Musk playing a pivotal funding and leadership role from its early stages—the company initially focused on the commercialization of electric vehicles, beginning with the Roadster in 2008. 

Tesla’s core operations center on the design, development, manufacture, and sale of fully electric passenger cars and commercial vehicles, most notably the Model S, 3, X, and Y, as well as the Cybertruck. Beyond automotive manufacturing, Tesla is a major producer of sustainable energy generation and storage systems, offering residential, commercial, and industrial photovoltaic solar panels and lithium-ion battery energy storage products, including the Powerwall and Megapack. The company is also heavily invested in the development of autonomous driving technology, artificial intelligence, and a proprietary global charging infrastructure known as the Supercharger network. Through continuous technological innovation and vertical integration, Tesla has driven the global transition toward sustainable transportation and renewable energy adoption.
