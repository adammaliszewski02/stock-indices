TSLA is the stock ticker for Tesla, Inc., an American multinational automotive and clean energy company headquartered in Austin, Texas. Founded in 2003 by Martin Eberhard and Marc Tarpenning—with Elon Musk playing a pivotal funding and leadership role from its early stages—Tesla initially focused on the development and commercialization of premium electric vehicles. The company achieved a major industry milestone with the 2008 release of the Roadster, its first production car, which demonstrated the viability of high-performance lithium-ion battery technology. Today, Tesla designs, manufactures, and sells fully electric automobiles, solar energy generation systems, and stationary battery energy storage products for residential, commercial, and industrial use. Furthermore, the company develops advanced autonomous driving software, artificial intelligence solutions, and related services. Tesla operates a vertically integrated business model, encompassing proprietary manufacturing facilities, a global network of direct-to-consumer sales and service centers, and the Supercharger fast-charging infrastructure. Through continuous innovation in battery chemistry, manufacturing automation, and software integration, Tesla has established itself as a global leader in the transition toward sustainable transport and renewable energy ecosystems.
