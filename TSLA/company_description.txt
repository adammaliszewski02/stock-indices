Tesla, Inc., traded under the ticker TSLA, is a leading American multinational automotive and clean energy company headquartered in Austin, Texas. Founded in 2003 by Martin Eberhard and Marc Tarpenning—with Elon Musk playing a pivotal role in early financing, leadership, and vision—the company originally set out to prove that electric vehicles could be better than gasoline-powered cars. Tesla officially entered the public markets via an initial public offering in June 2010. Today, the company designs, manufactures, and sells fully electric vehicles, solar roof tiles, and residential and commercial energy storage products. Furthermore, Tesla develops advanced artificial intelligence technologies, including its proprietary Full Self-Driving software and humanoid robotics. Through continuous innovation in battery technology, manufacturing automation, and a global proprietary Supercharger network, Tesla has driven the mass-market transition toward sustainable transportation and renewable energy ecosystems worldwide.
