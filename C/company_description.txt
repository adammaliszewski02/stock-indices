The stock ticker C represents Citigroup Inc., a prominent multinational investment banking and financial services corporation headquartered in New York City. Tracing its origins to the 1812 founding of the City Bank of New York, and subsequently absorbing institutions such as Salomon Brothers and Travelers Group, the company officially formed through the historic 1998 merger of Citicorp and Travelers Group. Today, Citigroup operates globally through two primary segments, the Institutional Clients Group and Personal Banking and Wealth Management, providing a comprehensive range of financial products and services. These offerings include consumer banking, credit cards, corporate banking, investment banking, securities brokerage, and wealth management to millions of individual, corporate, institutional, and government clients worldwide.
