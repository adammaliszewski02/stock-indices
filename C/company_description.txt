The stock ticker C represents Citigroup Inc., a prominent multinational investment banking and financial services corporation headquartered in New York City. Tracing its origins back to the 1812 founding of the City Bank of New York, and subsequently incorporating the 1895 establishment of the National Bank of Commerce, the institution expanded significantly over the subsequent two centuries. Through a landmark 1998 merger between Citicorp and Travelers Group, the company officially became Citigroup, creating one of the world's first true financial supermarkets. Today, Citigroup operates globally, providing a comprehensive range of financial products and services. Its core business segments include institutional clients group operations, encompassing corporate banking, investment banking, and trading, alongside personal banking and wealth management services for consumers, small businesses, and institutions worldwide.
