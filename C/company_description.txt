The stock ticker "C" identifies Citigroup Inc., a preeminent American multinational investment bank and financial services corporation headquartered in New York City. Tracing its origins to the founding of the City Bank of New York in 1812, the modern institution took shape through the historic 1998 merger of Citicorp and Travelers Group, creating one of the largest financial conglomerates in history before later divesting its insurance operations. 

Today, Citigroup ranks among the largest banks in the United States and is considered a globally systemically important financial institution. Its business is divided into two primary segments: personal banking and wealth management, which encompasses retail banking, branded credit cards, and private wealth advisory; and an institutional clients group, which provides corporate and investment banking, securities trading, and treasury and trade solutions. Serving consumers, corporations, and governments across approximately 160 countries and jurisdictions, Citigroup is particularly distinguished by its expansive global footprint and its focus on cross-border corporate banking and international trade facilitation.
