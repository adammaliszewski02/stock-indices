The stock ticker C represents Citigroup Inc., a prominent American multinational investment banking and financial services corporation headquartered in New York City. Tracing its origins back to the 1812 founding of the City Bank of New York, and subsequently evolving through a landmark 1998 merger of Citicorp and Travelers Group, the company operates as one of the major institutions of the modern financial sector. Citigroup provides a comprehensive range of financial products and services, encompassing consumer banking, credit cards, corporate and investment banking, securities brokerage, and wealth management. Serving millions of consumers, corporations, governments, and institutional clients globally, Citigroup conducts its operations across numerous countries and jurisdictions, positioning itself as a central pillar of the international financial architecture.
