The stock ticker C represents Citigroup Inc., a prominent multinational investment banking and financial services corporation headquartered in New York City. Tracing its origins back to the 1812 founding of the City Bank of New York, the institution subsequently evolved through a series of major mergers and acquisitions, most notably the 1998 landmark union of Citicorp and Travelers Group that formed the modern conglomerate. Today, Citigroup operates globally, providing a comprehensive range of financial products and services to consumer, corporate, institutional, and government clients. Its core business offerings encompass institutional client group services, including investment banking, corporate lending, and treasury and trade solutions, alongside personal banking and wealth management services. Serving millions of customers across numerous countries, Citigroup facilitates international commerce, capital raising, and wealth preservation, solidifying its status as one of the world's primary global systematically important financial institutions.
