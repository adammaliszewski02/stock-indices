The stock ticker C represents Citigroup Inc., a prominent multinational investment banking and financial services corporation headquartered in New York City. Tracing its origins to the 1812 founding of the City Bank of New York, and subsequently absorbing institutions such as Salomon Brothers and Travelers Group, the company grew into one of the world's largest financial establishments. Citigroup operates through two primary segments: Institutional Clients Group and Personal Banking and Wealth Management. It provides a comprehensive range of financial products and services, including consumer banking, credit cards, corporate banking, investment banking, securities brokerage, and wealth management to millions of consumers, corporations, governments, and institutions across global markets.
