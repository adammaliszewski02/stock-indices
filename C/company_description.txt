The ticker symbol C represents Citigroup Inc., a prominent American multinational investment bank and financial services corporation headquartered in New York City. Traded on the New York Stock Exchange, Citigroup traces its corporate lineage back to 1812 with the founding of the City Bank of New York, later evolving through a series of major mergers. Most notably, the company was formed via the historic 1998 union of Citicorp and Travelers Group. Today, Citigroup operates as one of the major global financial institutions, commonly recognized as one of the Big Four banking organizations in the United States. The company provides a comprehensive range of financial products and services to consumer, corporate, institutional, and government clients worldwide. Its core business operations are structured around institutional banking, wealth management, and personal banking services, facilitating capital generation, international trade, and cross-border transactions for clients across numerous global markets.
