Citigroup Inc., trading under the stock ticker C, is a multinational investment banking and financial services corporation headquartered in New York City. Tracing its origins back to the 1812 founding of the City Bank of New York, the institution evolved through a series of historic mergers, most notably the 1998 joining of Citicorp and Travelers Group, which created the modern financial conglomerate. Today, Citigroup operates globally through two primary segments, Institutional Clients Group and Personal Banking and Wealth Management, providing a comprehensive range of financial products and services. These offerings include consumer banking, credit cards, corporate banking, investment banking, securities brokerage, and wealth management to corporations, governments, institutional investors, and individuals worldwide.
