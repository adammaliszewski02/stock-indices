Company description: AAPL

Apple Inc. (NASDAQ: AAPL) is a global technology leader that designs, manufactures, and markets consumer electronics, computer software, and digital services. Its core product lineup includes the flagship iPhone, Mac personal computers, iPad tablets, Apple Watch, and AirPods. Complementing its hardware ecosystem, the company operates a high-margin services segment featuring the App Store, Apple Music, iCloud, Apple Pay, and Apple TV+.

Founded in 1976 by Steve Jobs, Steve Wozniak, and Ronald Wayne as Apple Computer, Inc., the enterprise initially revolutionized the personal computing industry with the Apple II and the Macintosh. Over the decades, Apple transformed consumer technology through landmark product launches, including the iPod in 2001 and the iPhone in 2007, the latter of which reshaped the global smartphone industry and mobile economy. 

Today, Apple operates on a vertically integrated business model, controlling both its hardware engineering and software operating systems (iOS, macOS). The company continues to generate revenue by driving device upgrades, expanding its recurring service subscriptions, and investing heavily in research and development, including proprietary chip architecture and artificial intelligence integration across its global device ecosystem.
