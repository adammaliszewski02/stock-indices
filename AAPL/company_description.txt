Company description: AAPL

Apple Inc., traded under the ticker AAPL, is a global technology leader that designs, manufactures, and markets consumer electronics, computer software, and digital services. Headquartered in Cupertino, California, the company's flagship hardware portfolio includes the iPhone, Mac personal computers, iPad, Apple Watch, and AirPods. Complementing these devices is a robust services ecosystem comprising the App Store, Apple Music, iCloud, and Apple Pay, all integrated seamlessly through its proprietary operating systems like iOS and macOS.

Founded in 1976 by Steve Jobs, Steve Wozniak, and Ronald Wayne as Apple Computer, Inc., the business initially revolutionized personal computing with the Apple II and the Macintosh. The company shortened its name to Apple Inc. in 2007 to reflect its expansion into consumer electronics, coinciding with the launch of the revolutionary iPhone that redefined the modern smartphone industry. 

Today, Apple is renowned for its continuous product innovation, premium design aesthetics, and tightly integrated hardware-software ecosystem. It stands as one of the world's most valuable publicly traded corporations, generating revenue through high-margin hardware sales, a rapidly growing subscription services segment, and digital content distribution globally.
