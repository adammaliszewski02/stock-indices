Company description: AAPL

Apple Inc., traded under the ticker AAPL, is a global technology leader that designs, manufactures, and markets consumer electronics, software, and digital services. Founded in 1976 by Steve Jobs, Steve Wozniak, and Ronald Wayne, the company initially revolutionized personal computing with the Macintosh. It later redefined mobile technology and digital media through landmark innovations like the iPod in 2001 and the iPhone in 2007.

Today, Apple operates a premium, highly integrated ecosystem. Its core hardware offerings include the flagship iPhone, Mac personal computers, iPad tablets, Apple Watch, and AirPods, all powered by proprietary operating systems. Complementing its hardware, Apple has aggressively expanded its services division, generating significant recurring revenue through the App Store, iCloud, Apple Music, Apple Pay, and Apple TV+. 

By controlling both hardware and software, Apple maintains exceptional brand loyalty and strong profit margins. The company continues to drive industry standards through heavy investments in proprietary silicon chips, artificial intelligence, and digital health technologies, solidifying its position as one of the world's most valuable and influential corporations.
