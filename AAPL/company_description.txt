Company description: AAPL

Apple Inc., traded under the ticker AAPL, is a global technology leader that designs, manufactures, and markets premium consumer electronics, software, and digital services. Its flagship hardware portfolio includes the iPhone, Mac computers, iPad tablets, Apple Watch, and the Vision Pro spatial computer. These devices run on proprietary operating systems like iOS and macOS and are supported by a booming services segment, including the App Store, iCloud, Apple Music, and Apple Pay.

Founded in 1976 by Steve Jobs, Steve Wozniak, and Ronald Wayne, Apple initially transformed the personal computer market with the Apple II and Macintosh. The company subsequently revolutionized the global consumer technology landscape in the 21st century with the introductions of the iPod in 2001 and the iPhone in 2007, the latter of which established the modern smartphone industry. 

Today, Apple focuses on deepening customer loyalty through a seamlessly integrated ecosystem. The company is actively investing in custom semiconductor innovation through Apple Silicon, advancing artificial intelligence capabilities via Apple Intelligence, and expanding its high-margin subscription services, reinforcing its status as one of the world's most valuable public corporations.
