Company description: AAPL

Apple Inc., traded under the ticker AAPL, is a global technology leader renowned for designing, manufacturing, and marketing premium consumer electronics, computer software, and digital services. Founded in 1976 by Steve Jobs, Steve Wozniak, and Ronald Wayne, the company initially revolutionized personal computing with the Apple II and Macintosh. Apple later redefined modern communication and digital entertainment through groundbreaking innovations, most notably launching the iPod in 2001, the iPhone in 2007, and the iPad in 2010.

Today, Apple operates a powerful, integrated ecosystem combining hardware, proprietary operating systems, and consumer services. Its core product portfolio includes the flagship iPhone, Mac computers, iPad tablets, and wearable devices such as the Apple Watch and AirPods. Complementing its hardware, Apple has significantly expanded its high-margin services segment, which encompasses the App Store, Apple Music, iCloud, Apple Pay, and Apple TV+. 

Driven by relentless innovation and custom silicon engineering, Apple focuses on seamless hardware-software integration, user privacy, and design excellence. As one of the world’s most valuable publicly traded corporations, Apple continues to shape global consumer behavior while investing heavily in emerging technologies like artificial intelligence, digital health, and immersive media.
