Company description: AAPL

Apple Inc., traded under the ticker AAPL, is a global technology leader that designs, manufactures, and markets consumer electronics, software, and digital services. Headquartered in Cupertino, California, the company is world-renowned for its hardware portfolio, which includes the iPhone, Mac personal computers, iPad, Apple Watch, and AirPods. Complementing its hardware, Apple operates a high-margin services ecosystem encompassing the App Store, Apple Music, iCloud, Apple Pay, and Apple TV+.

Founded in 1976 by Steve Jobs, Steve Wozniak, and Ronald Wayne, the firm initially revolutionized personal computing with the Apple II and Macintosh. Over the decades, Apple transformed into a dominant consumer tech giant through major innovations, notably the iPod in 2001 and the groundbreaking iPhone in 2007, which fundamentally reshaped modern mobile communication. 

Today, Apple drives its revenue through an integrated strategy of selling premium device hardware and growing its recurring digital services subscriber base. The company continuously invests in proprietary silicon architecture, artificial intelligence, and privacy-focused software, solidifying its standing as one of the world's most valuable and influential corporations.
