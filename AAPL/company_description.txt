Company description: AAPL

Apple Inc., traded under the ticker AAPL, is a global technology leader that designs, manufactures, and markets premium consumer electronics, software, and digital services. Founded in 1976 by Steve Jobs, Steve Wozniak, and Ronald Wayne, the company initially revolutionized personal computing with the Apple II and the Macintosh. In 2007, Apple redefined the modern smartphone market with the launch of the iPhone, which remains a primary driver of its business alongside flagship hardware like the Mac, iPad, and Apple Watch.

Beyond hardware, Apple maintains a rapidly growing services division that includes the App Store, Apple Music, iCloud, and Apple Pay, fostering a highly integrated, lucrative user ecosystem. The company continues to drive innovation, recently expanding into spatial computing with the Apple Vision Pro and integrating advanced artificial intelligence through Apple Intelligence. 

Headquartered in Cupertino, California, Apple made financial history in 2018 as the first publicly traded U.S. company to achieve a one trillion dollar market capitalization. Today, it stands as one of the world's most valuable enterprises, recognized for its commitment to design excellence, privacy, and seamless technological integration.
