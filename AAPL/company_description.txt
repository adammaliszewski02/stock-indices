Company description: AAPL

Apple Inc., trading under the ticker symbol AAPL, is a global technology leader that designs, manufactures, and markets premium consumer electronics, personal computers, mobile devices, and software, supported by an expanding ecosystem of digital services. The company is best known for its flagship hardware, including the iPhone, Mac, iPad, Apple Watch, and AirPods, as well as digital platforms like the App Store, iCloud, Apple Music, and Apple Pay.

Founded in 1976 by Steve Jobs, Steve Wozniak, and Ronald Wayne, Apple originally revolutionized personal computing with the Apple II and the Macintosh. The company underwent a dramatic expansion in the early 2000s, reshaping the digital media and telecommunications industries with the launch of the iPod in 2001 and the groundbreaking iPhone in 2007.

Today, Apple focuses on integrating hardware, custom-designed silicon, software, and services to deliver seamless user experiences. Headquartered in Cupertino, California, the firm continues to drive industry innovation through developments in wearable technology, artificial intelligence, digital services, and spatial computing with the Apple Vision Pro, remaining one of the world's most valuable publicly traded corporations.
