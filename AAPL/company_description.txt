Company description: AAPL

Apple Inc., traded under the ticker symbol AAPL, is a global technology leader that designs, manufactures, and markets consumer electronics, computer software, and digital services. 

Founded in 1976 by Steve Jobs, Steve Wozniak, and Ronald Wayne, the company initially revolutionized personal computing with the Apple II and the 1984 Macintosh. Apple redefined the modern technology landscape in the 2000s through a series of iconic product launches, including the iPod in 2001, the smartphone-defining iPhone in 2007, and the iPad in 2010. 

Today, Apple operates a highly lucrative ecosystem powered by hardware offerings such as the iPhone, Mac, iPad, Apple Watch, and AirPods. This hardware foundation is seamlessly integrated with proprietary operating systems and a rapidly expanding Services division, which encompasses the App Store, iCloud, Apple Music, Apple Pay, and Apple TV+. 

Currently, Apple focuses on deepening customer engagement within its ecosystem, advancing its proprietary Apple Silicon chips, growing recurring subscription revenues, and integrating artificial intelligence into its devices. Through continuous innovation and brand loyalty, Apple remains one of the world's most valuable and influential publicly traded companies.
