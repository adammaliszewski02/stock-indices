Company description: AAPL

Apple Inc., trading under the ticker symbol AAPL, is a global technology powerhouse that designs, manufactures, and markets premium consumer electronics, personal computers, software, and digital services. The company’s core business revolves around its flagship hardware products, including the iPhone, Mac, iPad, Apple Watch, and AirPods. These devices are seamlessly integrated with a rapidly growing services segment comprising the App Store, Apple Pay, iCloud, Apple Music, and Apple TV+.

Founded in 1976 by Steve Jobs, Steve Wozniak, and Ronald Wayne, Apple initially disrupted the personal computing landscape with the Apple II and the 1984 release of the Macintosh. The company subsequently redefined modern technology and consumer behavior through pivotal 21st-century launches, most notably the iPod in 2001, the iPhone in 2007, and the iPad in 2010. 

Today, Apple operates a highly lucrative ecosystem powered by proprietary silicon architecture and a strong emphasis on user privacy. The company continues to drive innovation by investing heavily in artificial intelligence, wearable health technology, and spatial computing, maintaining its position as one of the world’s most valuable and influential publicly traded corporations.
