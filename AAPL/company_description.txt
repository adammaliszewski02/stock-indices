Apple Inc., identified by the stock ticker AAPL, is a leading American multinational technology company headquartered in Cupertino, California. Founded in April 1976 by Steve Jobs, Steve Wozniak, and Ronald Wayne, the company originally focused on the development and sale of personal computers, beginning with the Apple I. Over the decades, Apple transformed the consumer electronics industry through landmark product introductions, including the Macintosh in 1984, the iPod in 2001, and the iPhone in 2007, which remains its primary revenue driver. Today, Apple designs, manufactures, and markets a broad ecosystem of mobile communication and media devices, personal computers, and portable digital music players. Its hardware portfolio is complemented by a robust suite of software and services, encompassing the iOS and macOS operating systems, the App Store, iCloud, Apple Music, and Apple Pay. By consistently emphasizing premium design, proprietary hardware-software integration, and user privacy, Apple has established itself as one of the most valuable and influential publicly traded corporations in global commerce.
