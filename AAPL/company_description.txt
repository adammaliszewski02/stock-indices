Company description: AAPL

Apple Inc., traded under the ticker AAPL, is a global technology leader that designs, manufactures, and markets premium consumer electronics, software, and digital services. Founded in 1976 by Steve Jobs, Steve Wozniak, and Ronald Wayne, the company initially revolutionized personal computing with the Apple II and Macintosh. Apple later redefined mobile technology with the introduction of the iPod in 2001 and the flagship iPhone in 2007.

Today, Apple operates a powerful hardware ecosystem featuring the iPhone, Mac personal computers, iPad tablets, Apple Watch, and AirPods. This hardware is seamlessly integrated with proprietary operating systems such as iOS and macOS. Over the past decade, Apple has significantly diversified its revenue by expanding its high-margin services segment, which includes the App Store, iCloud, Apple Music, Apple Pay, and Apple TV+.

Headquartered in Cupertino, California, Apple is renowned for its focus on privacy, design excellence, and custom silicon innovation. As one of the most valuable publicly traded companies in the world, Apple continues to shape the global digital landscape through continuous advancements in hardware, services, and artificial intelligence.
