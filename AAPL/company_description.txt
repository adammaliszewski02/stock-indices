Company description: AAPL

Apple Inc. (NASDAQ: AAPL) is a global technology leader that designs, manufactures, and markets premium consumer electronics, software, and digital services. Its flagship hardware portfolio includes the iPhone, Mac personal computers, iPad, Apple Watch, and Vision Pro spatial computer. These devices run on proprietary operating systems like iOS and macOS, seamlessly integrating with a expanding services business that includes the App Store, Apple Music, iCloud, and Apple Pay.

Founded in 1976 by Steve Jobs, Steve Wozniak, and Ronald Wayne, Apple initially reshaped personal computing with the Apple II and the Macintosh. Following a period of decline in the 1990s, the company orchestrated one of the greatest corporate turnarounds in history, redefining mobile technology through the launch of the iPod in 2001, the iPhone in 2007, and the iPad in 2010. 

Today, Apple operates on a vertically integrated model, leveraging custom-designed silicon to power its hardware and enhance user security. Headquartered in Cupertino, California, the company generates revenue through hardware sales, digital content, and high-margin subscription services, maintaining its position as one of the world's most valuable publicly traded companies by prioritizing design excellence, user privacy, and brand loyalty.
