Company description: AAPL

Apple Inc., traded under the ticker symbol AAPL, is a global technology leader that designs, manufactures, and markets premium consumer electronics, software, and digital services. Headquartered in Cupertino, California, the company's hardware portfolio includes the iconic iPhone, Mac computers, iPad, Apple Watch, and AirPods. Complementing its devices, Apple operates a rapidly growing services division that encompasses the App Store, Apple Music, iCloud, Apple Pay, and Apple TV+.

Founded in 1976 by Steve Jobs, Steve Wozniak, and Ronald Wayne, the company initially revolutionized personal computing with the Apple II and the 1984 Macintosh. Apple fundamentally transformed consumer technology and mobile communications in the 2000s through the launches of the iPod in 2001 and the iPhone in 2007, the latter of which reshaped the global smartphone industry. Demonstrating immense financial growth, Apple became the first publicly traded U.S. company to reach a $1 trillion market capitalization in 2018.

Today, Apple operates a highly integrated hardware and software ecosystem powered by proprietary chip technology. The company continues to drive industry standards through innovation in mobile devices, wearable technology, digital media subscriptions, and an increased strategic focus on artificial intelligence, user privacy, and environmental sustainability.
