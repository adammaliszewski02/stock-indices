Company description: AAPL

Apple Inc. (NASDAQ: AAPL) is a global technology leader that designs, manufactures, and markets premium consumer electronics, software, and digital services. Its primary revenue drivers include hardware products such as the iPhone, Mac computers, iPad tablets, Apple Watch, and AirPods. Complementing its hardware, Apple operates a rapidly growing services segment featuring the App Store, iCloud, Apple Music, Apple Pay, and Apple TV+, while actively integrating artificial intelligence across its ecosystem.

Founded in 1976 by Steve Jobs, Steve Wozniak, and Ronald Wayne as Apple Computer Company, the firm initially revolutionized personal computing with the Apple II and the Macintosh. Under Jobs' leadership in the 2000s, Apple transformed the consumer technology landscape with the launch of the iPod in 2001 and the industry-defining iPhone in 2007. 

Today, Apple is one of the world's most valuable publicly traded corporations. Headquartered in Cupertino, California, the company maintains its market dominance through a seamlessly integrated ecosystem of proprietary hardware and software, a loyal customer base, and a strong strategic focus on user privacy and design innovation.
