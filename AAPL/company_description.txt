AAPL is the stock ticker for Apple Inc., an American multinational technology company headquartered in Cupertino, California. Founded in April 1976 by Steve Jobs, Steve Wozniak, and Ronald Wayne, the company originally focused on the development and sale of personal computers, famously beginning operations in a suburban garage with the Apple I. 

Over the decades, Apple evolved into the world's leading consumer electronics and software enterprise. Its core business centers on the design, manufacture, and marketing of mobile communication and media devices, personal computers, and portable digital music players. Its flagship product lines include the iPhone smartphone, the Mac computer series, the iPad tablet, and the Apple Watch. 

In addition to hardware, Apple operates robust digital services and software ecosystems, including the App Store, Apple Music, iCloud, and Apple Pay, which generate significant recurring revenue. Recognized as the first publicly traded US company to reach a market capitalization of one trillion dollars, Apple remains a dominant force in global technology, prioritizing hardware-software integration, user privacy, and proprietary silicon development.
