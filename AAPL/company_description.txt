AAPL is the stock ticker for Apple Inc., an American multinational technology company headquartered in Cupertino, California. Founded by Steve Jobs, Steve Wozniak, and Ronald Wayne in April 1976, the company initially focused on the development and sale of personal computers, famously launching the Apple I and Macintosh. Over the decades, Apple evolved into a global leader in consumer electronics, software, and online services. Its core business model centers on the design, manufacture, and marketing of mobile communication and media devices, most notably the iPhone, alongside the iPad, Mac computers, Apple Watch, and AirPods. Additionally, the company generates substantial revenue through its expanding digital services ecosystem, which includes the App Store, Apple Music, iCloud, and Apple Pay. Renowned for its integrated hardware and software ecosystems, premium brand positioning, and significant technological innovation, Apple became the first publicly traded US company to reach a market capitalization of one trillion dollars, and it remains one of the most valuable corporations in global history.
