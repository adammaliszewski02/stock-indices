Company description: AAPL

Apple Inc., traded under the ticker symbol AAPL, is a global technology leader that designs, manufactures, and markets premium consumer electronics, proprietary software, and digital services. Founded in 1976 by Steve Jobs, Steve Wozniak, and Ronald Wayne, the company initially revolutionized the personal computer industry with the Apple II and Macintosh. Over the decades, Apple evolved into one of the world's most valuable public companies, driven by groundbreaking product launches including the iPod in 2001, the iPhone in 2007, and the iPad in 2010.

Today, Apple operates a powerful ecosystem defined by hardware, software, and services working seamlessly together. Its core product portfolio features the iPhone, Mac personal computers, iPad tablets, and wearable devices like the Apple Watch and AirPods. The company is increasingly expanding its high-margin Services segment, which includes the App Store, Apple Music, iCloud, Apple Pay, and Apple TV+. Known for its commitment to design, user privacy, and in-house custom silicon engineering, Apple continues to innovate across personal computing, digital services, and emerging technologies like spatial computing with the Apple Vision Pro.
