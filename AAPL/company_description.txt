Company description: AAPL

Apple Inc., traded under the ticker AAPL, is a global technology leader that designs, manufactures, and markets consumer electronics, personal computers, mobile devices, and related software and services. The company is best known for its iconic hardware portfolio, including the iPhone, Mac computers, iPad, Apple Watch, and AirPods. Complementing its hardware ecosystem, Apple operates a rapidly growing services division that encompasses the App Store, Apple Music, iCloud, Apple Pay, and Apple TV+.

Founded in 1976 by Steve Jobs, Steve Wozniak, and Ronald Wayne as Apple Computer Company, the firm initially revolutionized the personal computing industry with the Apple II and the Macintosh. In the 21st century, Apple redefined consumer technology through landmark product launches, notably the iPod in 2001 and the iPhone in 2007, which fundamentally transformed global communication and mobile computing. 

Today, Apple remains at the forefront of digital innovation, continually expanding its ecosystem through advancements in custom silicon chips, spatial computing with the Apple Vision Pro, artificial intelligence, and digital privacy initiatives, maintaining its status as one of the world's most valuable publicly traded corporations.
