Company description: AAPL

Apple Inc., traded under the ticker AAPL, is a multinational technology company that designs, manufactures, and markets consumer electronics, computer software, and digital services. Founded in 1976 by Steve Jobs, Steve Wozniak, and Ronald Wayne, the company initially revolutionized personal computing with the Apple II and Macintosh. In 2007, Apple redefined the mobile communications industry with the launch of the iPhone, establishing a foundation for its current global dominance. 

Today, Apple operates a seamlessly integrated ecosystem centered on flagship hardware devices, including the iPhone, Mac, iPad, Apple Watch, and AirPods. Complementing its hardware, the company drives significant growth through an expanding services portfolio, which features the App Store, iCloud, Apple Music, Apple Pay, and Apple TV+. The firm is actively advancing its capabilities in proprietary silicon engineering, artificial intelligence integration, and digital services while maintaining a strong emphasis on user privacy and environmental sustainability. Headquartered in Cupertino, California, Apple became the first publicly traded U.S. company to reach a $1 trillion market capitalization in 2018 and remains one of the most valuable and influential enterprises in the world.
