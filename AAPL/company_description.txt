Company description: AAPL

Apple Inc., traded under the ticker AAPL, is a global technology leader that designs, manufactures, and markets consumer electronics, software, and digital services. Founded in 1976 by Steve Jobs, Steve Wozniak, and Ronald Wayne, the company initially revolutionized personal computing with the launch of the Macintosh in 1984. It later reshaped the modern mobile ecosystem with the introduction of the iPod in 2001 and the iPhone in 2007.

Today, Apple operates a highly integrated hardware, software, and services ecosystem. Its flagship product line includes the iPhone, Mac personal computers, iPad tablets, and wearable devices like the Apple Watch and AirPods. These hardware offerings are powered by Apple's proprietary operating systems, including iOS and macOS, as well as its custom-designed Apple Silicon processors. 

Beyond hardware, the company has significantly expanded its recurring revenue through a robust services segment, which includes the App Store, iCloud, Apple Music, Apple Pay, and Apple TV+. Headquartered in Cupertino, California, Apple continues to focus on premium hardware innovation, digital privacy, and ecosystem retention, maintaining its position as one of the world's most valuable and influential publicly traded corporations.
