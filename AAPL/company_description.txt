Company description: AAPL

Apple Inc. (NASDAQ: AAPL) is a global technology leader that designs, manufactures, and markets consumer electronics, computer software, and digital services. Its flagship hardware portfolio includes the iPhone, Mac personal computers, iPad tablets, Apple Watch, and AirPods. Complementing its hardware ecosystem, Apple provides operating systems such as iOS and macOS, alongside an expansive services division encompassing the App Store, Apple Music, Apple Pay, iCloud, and Apple TV+.

Founded in 1976 by Steve Jobs, Steve Wozniak, and Ronald Wayne, the company initially revolutionized the personal computer industry with the Apple II and the Macintosh. The launch of the iPod in 2001 and the iPhone in 2007 fundamentally transformed consumer technology and mobile communication, establishing Apple as one of the world's most valuable publicly traded corporations. 

Today, Apple focuses on driving growth through the seamless integration of its hardware, software, and high-margin services. The company continues to innovate by investing heavily in proprietary custom silicon chips, advanced artificial intelligence capabilities, augmented reality, digital health technologies, and sustainable manufacturing initiatives to maintain its market leadership.
