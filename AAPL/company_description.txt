Company description: AAPL

Apple Inc. (NASDAQ: AAPL) is a global technology leader that designs, manufactures, and markets consumer electronics, computer software, and digital services. Founded in 1976 by Steve Jobs, Steve Wozniak, and Ronald Wayne, the company initially revolutionized personal computing with the Apple II and Macintosh. Over the decades, Apple transformed mobile technology and digital entertainment through groundbreaking innovations such as the iPod in 2001 and the iPhone in 2007.

Today, Apple’s core operations center on its premium hardware lineup, which includes the iPhone, Mac personal computers, iPad tablets, Apple Watch, and the Vision Pro spatial computer. Complementing its hardware ecosystem is a rapidly expanding services segment that encompasses the App Store, Apple Music, iCloud, Apple Pay, and Apple TV+. 

By seamlessly integrating proprietary hardware, custom silicon chips, intuitive software, and secure digital services, Apple maintains a powerful global brand with strong customer loyalty. The company continues to drive industry innovation by expanding its digital services portfolio and investing heavily in next-generation technologies, including artificial intelligence, semiconductor development, and spatial computing.
