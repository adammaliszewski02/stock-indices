Company description: AAPL

Apple Inc. (NASDAQ: AAPL) is a global technology leader that designs, manufactures, and markets premium consumer electronics, software, and digital services. Founded in 1976 by Steve Jobs, Steve Wozniak, and Ronald Wayne, the company initially transformed personal computing with the Apple II and Macintosh. Over the following decades, Apple redefined modern technology through groundbreaking innovations, most notably introducing the iPod in 2001, the iPhone in 2007, and the iPad in 2010.

Today, Apple's business centers on its flagship iPhone, alongside the Mac lineup, iPad, Apple Watch, and AirPods, all powered by proprietary operating systems and custom Apple Silicon chips. Beyond hardware, the company has successfully expanded its high-margin services division, which encompasses the App Store, Apple Music, iCloud, Apple Pay, and Apple TV+. 

Headquartered in Cupertino, California, Apple maintains its market leadership by focusing on seamless hardware-software integration, strong brand loyalty, and an interconnected product ecosystem. The company continues to invest heavily in emerging technologies, including artificial intelligence, spatial computing via the Apple Vision Pro, and advanced wearable health features.
