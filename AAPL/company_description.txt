Company description: AAPL

Apple Inc., trading under the ticker AAPL, is a global technology leader that designs, manufactures, and markets consumer electronics, computer software, and digital services. The company is best known for its flagship hardware products, including the iPhone, Mac computers, iPad, and Apple Watch, as well as a robust services portfolio encompassing the App Store, Apple Music, iCloud, and Apple Pay.

Founded in 1976 by Steve Jobs, Steve Wozniak, and Ronald Wayne, the company initially revolutionized personal computing with the Apple II and the 1984 release of the Macintosh. Over the decades, Apple continually redefined consumer technology, most notably with the launch of the iPod in 2001 and the groundbreaking iPhone in 2007, which fundamentally transformed the mobile device industry and global communication. 

Today, Apple operates a tightly integrated ecosystem powered by its proprietary hardware, custom silicon chips, and operating systems like iOS and macOS. Headquartered in Cupertino, California, Apple consistently ranks as one of the world's most valuable publicly traded companies, driving ongoing innovation in personal technology, wearable devices, and digital services.
