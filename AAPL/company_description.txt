AAPL is the stock ticker for Apple Inc., an American multinational technology company headquartered in Cupertino, California. Founded in April 1976 by Steve Jobs, Steve Wozniak, and Ronald Wayne, the company originally focused on the development and sale of personal computers, famously debuting the Apple I and Macintosh. Over the decades, Apple evolved into the world's leading consumer electronics and software enterprise, revolutionizing multiple industries through iconic product lines such as the iPhone, iPad, Mac computers, and Apple Watch. Beyond hardware, the company has significantly expanded its footprint into digital services and software ecosystems, offering platforms including the App Store, Apple Music, iCloud, and Apple Pay. Renowned for its vertically integrated business model and emphasis on design, privacy, and innovation, Apple is one of the most valuable publicly traded corporations in global financial history.
