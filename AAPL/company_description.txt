Company description: AAPL

Apple Inc. (NASDAQ: AAPL) is a global technology leader that designs, manufactures, and markets consumer electronics, software, and digital services. Its flagship hardware portfolio includes the iPhone, Mac personal computers, iPad, and wearable devices such as the Apple Watch and AirPods. Beyond hardware, the company operates a thriving services ecosystem, including the App Store, Apple Music, iCloud, and Apple Pay, all unified by proprietary operating systems like iOS and macOS.

Founded in 1976 by Steve Jobs, Steve Wozniak, and Ronald Wayne, Apple initially revolutionized personal computing with the Apple II and the Macintosh. In 2007, the company dropped "Computer" from its name to reflect a broader pivot into consumer technology, catalyzed by the groundbreaking launch of the iPhone, which fundamentally reshaped the global smartphone industry.

Today, Apple is distinguished by its seamless hardware-software integration, strong customer loyalty, and focus on user privacy. The company continues to expand its market presence through ongoing innovations in custom silicon, digital subscriptions, spatial computing with the Apple Vision Pro, and emerging artificial intelligence capabilities, maintaining its position as one of the world's most valuable publicly traded enterprises.
