Company description: AAPL

Apple Inc. (NASDAQ: AAPL) designs, manufactures, and sells smartphones, personal computers, tablets, wearables, and related services. Its ecosystem includes iPhone, Mac, iPad, Apple Watch, AirPods, iOS, macOS, the App Store, and iCloud. Founded in 1976 and headquartered in Cupertino, California, Apple serves customers worldwide through integrated hardware, software, and services.
