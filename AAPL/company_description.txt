Company description: AAPL

Apple Inc. (NASDAQ: AAPL) is a global technology leader that designs, manufactures, and markets premium consumer electronics, software, and digital services. Founded in 1976 by Steve Jobs, Steve Wozniak, and Ronald Wayne, the company initially revolutionized personal computing with the release of the Apple II and the Macintosh. Over the following decades, Apple transformed into a dominant force in modern consumer technology through landmark product launches, including the iPod, iPhone, and iPad.

Today, the company operates across two primary segments: hardware and services. Its core hardware portfolio features the flagship iPhone smartphone, Mac personal computers, iPad tablets, and an array of wearables and accessories, such as the Apple Watch and AirPods. Complementing its hardware is a robust, rapidly growing Services ecosystem that includes the App Store, iCloud, Apple Music, Apple Pay, and Apple TV+, all seamlessly unified by proprietary operating systems like iOS and macOS. By maintaining strict integration between hardware and software, Apple drives strong brand loyalty and consistently ranks among the most valuable publicly traded companies in the world.
