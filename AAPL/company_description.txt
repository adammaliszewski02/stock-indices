Company description: AAPL

Apple Inc., traded under the ticker symbol AAPL, is a global technology leader that designs, manufactures, and markets consumer electronics, software, and digital services. Founded in 1976 by Steve Jobs, Steve Wozniak, and Ronald Wayne, the company initially revolutionized personal computing with the Apple II and Macintosh. Over the past several decades, Apple transformed from a niche computer manufacturer into one of the world's most valuable public companies, largely driven by the 2007 introduction of the iPhone. 

Today, Apple operates an extensive ecosystem anchored by hardware products including the iPhone, Mac, iPad, Apple Watch, and AirPods, all powered by its proprietary operating systems like iOS and macOS. In recent years, the company has strategically expanded its high-margin services division, which encompasses the App Store, Apple Music, iCloud, Apple Pay, and Apple TV+. Currently, Apple is focused on advancing its custom silicon technology, expanding its subscription services, and integrating artificial intelligence and spatial computing into its device portfolio to drive long-term growth and maintain customer loyalty.
