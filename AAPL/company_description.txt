Company description: AAPL

Apple Inc., trading under the ticker AAPL, is a global technology leader that designs, manufactures, and markets premium consumer electronics, personal computers, software, and digital services. The company operates a high-margin ecosystem driven by flagship hardware products—including the iPhone, Mac, iPad, Apple Watch, and AirPods—supported by proprietary operating systems like iOS and macOS, and a rapidly growing services division comprising the App Store, Apple Music, iCloud, and Apple Pay.

Founded in 1976 by Steve Jobs, Steve Wozniak, and Ronald Wayne in Cupertino, California, Apple initially revolutionized the personal computing industry with the Apple II and the Macintosh. The company later reshaped modern technology and global business with the introduction of the iPod in 2001 and the groundbreaking launch of the iPhone in 2007, which established the smartphone industry as it exists today.

Currently, Apple is focused on enhancing its ecosystem through proprietary silicon chips, expanding its high-margin subscription services, and pioneering new hardware categories, such as spatial computing with the Apple Vision Pro, alongside integrating advanced artificial intelligence across its product suite.
