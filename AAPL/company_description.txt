Company description: AAPL

Apple Inc. (NASDAQ: AAPL) is a global technology leader that designs, manufactures, and markets consumer electronics, software, and digital services. Headquartered in Cupertino, California, the company's flagship hardware portfolio includes the iPhone, Mac personal computers, iPad, Apple Watch, and the Vision Pro spatial computer. Complementing its devices, Apple operates a robust services business encompassing the App Store, Apple Music, iCloud, Apple Pay, and Apple TV+.

Founded in 1976 by Steve Jobs, Steve Wozniak, and Ronald Wayne, Apple initially transformed personal computing with the Apple II and Macintosh. The company subsequently redefined the modern digital landscape in the 21st century through revolutionary innovations, most notably launching the iPod in 2001, the iPhone in 2007, and the iPad in 2010. These breakthroughs propelled Apple to become the first publicly traded U.S. company to reach a $3 trillion market capitalization.

Today, Apple remains focused on seamlessly integrating hardware, software, and services within a unified ecosystem. The company is actively driving growth by embedding artificial intelligence through Apple Intelligence, enhancing digital security, and expanding its wearable technologies, all while working toward a goal of becoming carbon neutral across its entire supply chain and product lifecycle by 2030.
