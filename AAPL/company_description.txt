Company description: AAPL

Apple Inc. (ticker: AAPL) is a global technology leader that designs, manufactures, and markets premium consumer electronics, software, and digital services. The company operates a tightly integrated ecosystem, featuring hardware products such as the iPhone, Mac personal computers, iPad, Apple Watch, and AirPods. Its business is further supported by an expanding services segment, which includes the App Store, Apple Music, iCloud, and Apple Pay.

Founded in 1976 by Steve Jobs, Steve Wozniak, and Ronald Wayne, Apple initially transformed the personal computer industry with the Apple II and Macintosh. The company redefined modern consumer technology in the 2000s, launching the iPod in 2001 and the revolutionary iPhone in 2007, which fundamentally reshaped the global smartphone market and mobile economy. 

Today, Apple is one of the world's most valuable publicly traded corporations. The company generates revenue through direct product sales, subscriptions, digital content distribution, and licensing. Apple continues to focus on hardware innovation, proprietary semiconductor development, and expanding its digital services footprint, maintaining a strong emphasis on user privacy, design, and seamless brand integration across its global customer base.
