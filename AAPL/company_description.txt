Stock ticker AAPL represents Apple Inc., a multinational technology corporation headquartered in Cupertino, California. Founded in 1976 by Steve Jobs, Steve Wozniak, and Ronald Wayne, Apple initially revolutionized the personal computer industry with the Apple II and the Macintosh. The company subsequently reshaped modern consumer technology in the 2000s and 2010s with the launches of the iPod, iPhone, and iPad. 

Today, Apple designs, manufactures, and markets a broad hardware lineup that includes the iPhone, Mac personal computers, iPad tablets, Apple Watch wearables, and various smart accessories. Complementing its hardware, Apple has cultivated a robust, high-margin services division that encompasses the App Store, Apple Music, Apple Pay, Apple TV+, and iCloud storage. By seamlessly integrating proprietary hardware with dedicated operating systems such as iOS and macOS, Apple operates a cohesive digital ecosystem characterized by high brand loyalty. As a result of this strategy, Apple has grown into one of the largest and most valuable publicly traded companies in the world.
