Company description: AAPL

Apple Inc. (NASDAQ: AAPL) is a global technology leader that designs, manufactures, and markets premium consumer electronics, software, and digital services. The company’s flagship hardware portfolio includes the iPhone, Mac computers, iPad tablets, Apple Watch, and AirPods. Complementing these products is a rapidly growing services segment, which encompasses the App Store, Apple Music, iCloud, Apple Pay, and Apple TV+.

Founded in 1976 by Steve Jobs, Steve Wozniak, and Ronald Wayne as Apple Computer Company, the firm initially revolutionized personal computing with the Apple II and Macintosh. In 2007, Apple transformed global communication and mobile technology with the launch of the iPhone, propelling the company to become one of the world's most valuable publicly traded enterprises.

Today, Apple operates a highly integrated ecosystem, generating revenue through high-margin hardware sales, recurring digital subscriptions, and licensing. The company continues to focus on maintaining customer retention through seamless cross-device integration, advancing proprietary custom silicon, expanding its enterprise offerings, and integrating consumer-focused artificial intelligence across its platform.
