Company description: AAPL

Apple Inc. (NASDAQ: AAPL) is a global technology leader that designs, manufactures, and markets smartphones, personal computers, tablets, wearables, and accessories, alongside a robust portfolio of software and digital services. Founded in 1976 by Steve Jobs, Steve Wozniak, and Ronald Wayne, the company initially revolutionized personal computing with the launch of the Apple II and the Macintosh. Apple later redefined modern consumer electronics and mobile communication through iconic innovations like the iPod in 2001 and the flagship iPhone in 2007.

Today, Apple operates a seamless ecosystem powered by its proprietary hardware, software, and silicon architecture. Its core product portfolio includes the iPhone, Mac, iPad, Apple Watch, and Apple Vision Pro. Complementing its hardware, the company generates substantial recurring revenue through its rapidly growing services business, which includes the App Store, Apple Music, iCloud, Apple Pay, and Apple TV+. 

Currently, Apple is focused on expanding its high-margin services segment, strengthening its custom chip capabilities, and integrating advanced artificial intelligence across its platform to enhance user experience and maintain its position as one of the world's most valuable publicly traded companies.
