Company description: AAPL

Apple Inc. (NASDAQ: AAPL) is a global technology leader that designs, manufactures, and markets premium consumer electronics, computer software, and digital services. Founded in 1976 by Steve Jobs, Steve Wozniak, and Ronald Wayne, the company initially revolutionized personal computing with the Apple II and Macintosh. Today, Apple is renowned for its highly integrated ecosystem of hardware and software. 

Its flagship product line includes the iPhone, Mac personal computers, iPad tablets, Apple Watch, and AirPods. Beyond hardware, Apple has strategically expanded its revenue base through a robust services division, which encompasses the App Store, Apple Music, iCloud, Apple Pay, and Apple TV+. 

Driven by continuous innovation, Apple focuses on proprietary silicon development, artificial intelligence integration through Apple Intelligence, and user privacy, while actively working toward a carbon-neutral supply chain. Headquartered in Cupertino, California, Apple remains one of the world's most valuable and influential corporations, distinguished by its strong brand loyalty, supply chain efficiency, and commitment to seamless user experiences.
