Company description: AAPL

Apple Inc. (ticker: AAPL) is a global technology leader that designs, manufactures, and markets consumer electronics, computer software, and digital services. Founded in 1976 by Steve Jobs, Steve Wozniak, and Ronald Wayne, the company initially revolutionized personal computing with the Apple II and Macintosh. Over the decades, Apple transformed the tech landscape through iconic innovations, including the iPod in 2001, the iPhone in 2007, and the iPad in 2010.

Today, Apple operates a powerful hardware ecosystem featuring the iPhone, Mac personal computers, iPad tablets, Apple Watch, and AirPods, all integrated seamlessly through its proprietary operating systems. Beyond hardware, the company has expanded significantly into high-margin digital services, including the App Store, Apple Music, iCloud, Apple Pay, and Apple TV+. Currently, Apple is advancing its ecosystem by pioneering spatial computing with the Apple Vision Pro and integrating artificial intelligence deeply across its hardware and software portfolio. Headquartered in Cupertino, California, Apple remains one of the world's most valuable and influential publicly traded corporations.
