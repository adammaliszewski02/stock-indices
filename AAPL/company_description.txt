Company description: AAPL

Apple Inc., traded under the ticker symbol AAPL, is a global technology leader that designs, manufactures, and markets consumer electronics, mobile devices, personal computers, and software. Headquartered in Cupertino, California, the company’s flagship hardware portfolio includes the iPhone, Mac computers, iPad tablets, Apple Watch, and AirPods. Complementing its hardware, Apple operates a robust services division encompassing the App Store, Apple Music, iCloud, Apple Pay, and Apple TV+.

Founded in 1976 by Steve Jobs, Steve Wozniak, and Ronald Wayne, the firm initially revolutionized the personal computing industry with the Apple II and Macintosh. The company redefined modern technology with the introduction of the iPod in 2001 and the groundbreaking iPhone in 2007, which fundamentally transformed global mobile communications. In 2018, Apple made financial history by becoming the first publicly traded U.S. company to achieve a $1 trillion market capitalization.

Today, Apple remains focused on continuously enhancing its interconnected hardware-software ecosystem, advancing proprietary silicon chip technology, and expanding its subscription-based services while investing heavily in emerging technologies like artificial intelligence.
