The stock ticker AAPL represents Apple Inc., a global technology company headquartered in Cupertino, California. Founded in 1976 by Steve Jobs, Steve Wozniak, and Ronald Wayne, Apple pioneered the personal computing revolution with the Apple II and Macintosh. It later reshaped modern telecommunications and digital media through landmark innovations including the iPod in 2001, the iPhone in 2007, and the iPad in 2010.

Today, Apple designs, manufactures, and markets consumer electronics, software, and online services. Its flagship hardware portfolio includes the iPhone, Mac computers, iPad, Apple Watch, and AirPods, powered by proprietary operating systems and custom Apple silicon. Complementing its hardware is a robust, high-margin services division that encompasses the App Store, Apple Pay, iCloud, and subscription platforms such as Apple Music and Apple TV+. 

Known for its seamless hardware-software ecosystem and intense brand loyalty, Apple operates a global retail network alongside its direct-to-consumer and carrier channels. In 2018, it became the first publicly traded U.S. company to achieve a one trillion dollar market capitalization, and it continues to rank among the world's most valuable and influential corporations.
