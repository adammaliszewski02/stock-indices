The stock ticker AAPL represents Apple Inc., a multinational technology company headquartered in Cupertino, California. Founded in 1976 by Steve Jobs, Steve Wozniak, and Ronald Wayne, Apple played a pioneering role in the personal computer revolution with the Apple II and the Macintosh, later redefining consumer technology through innovation in mobile devices and digital distribution. 

Today, Apple designs, manufactures, and markets a diverse portfolio of consumer electronics, software, and subscription-based digital services. Its primary revenue driver is the iPhone, complemented by hardware offerings such as Mac computers, iPad tablets, the Apple Watch, and AirPods. Beyond hardware, the company has built a high-margin services ecosystem that includes the App Store, Apple Music, Apple Pay, Apple TV+, and iCloud storage. By tightly integrating proprietary hardware with tailored operating systems like iOS and macOS, Apple fosters strong customer retention within a closed ecosystem, making it one of the most profitable and highly capitalized corporations in the world.
