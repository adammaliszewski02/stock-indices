Company description: AAPL

Apple Inc., traded under the ticker symbol AAPL, is a global technology leader that designs, manufactures, and markets premium consumer electronics, software, and digital services. Its flagship hardware ecosystem includes the iPhone, Mac personal computers, iPad tablets, Apple Watch, and AirPods. The company seamlessly integrates this hardware with proprietary software and expanding services, such as the App Store, iCloud, Apple Music, and Apple Pay.

Founded in 1976 by Steve Jobs, Steve Wozniak, and Ronald Wayne in Los Altos, California, Apple initially revolutionized the personal computer industry with the Apple II and the 1984 Macintosh. Following a period of restructuring, the company orchestrated one of the most remarkable turnarounds in corporate history during the early 2000s. Key milestones include the release of the iPod in 2001 and the launch of the iPhone in 2007, the latter of which fundamentally transformed mobile communication and modern technology. 

Today, Apple is headquartered in Cupertino, California, and stands as one of the world's most valuable publicly traded corporations. The company continues to drive industry standards through custom silicon innovation, advanced mobile hardware, and a deeply integrated digital service network.
