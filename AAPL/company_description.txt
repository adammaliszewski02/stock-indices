Company description: AAPL

Apple Inc. (NASDAQ: AAPL) is a global technology leader that designs, manufactures, and markets premium consumer electronics, computer software, and digital services. Founded in 1976 by Steve Jobs, Steve Wozniak, and Ronald Wayne, the company initially revolutionized personal computing with the Apple II and Macintosh. Over the decades, Apple transformed global communication, media, and mobile technology through landmark innovations, including the iPod in 2001, the iPhone in 2007, and the iPad in 2010. 

Today, Apple operates a powerful, integrated ecosystem of hardware, software, and services. Its core product portfolio features the iPhone, Mac personal computers, iPad tablets, and wearable devices such as the Apple Watch and AirPods, all powered by proprietary operating systems. In recent years, the company has significantly expanded its high-margin Services segment, which includes the App Store, Apple Music, iCloud, Apple Pay, and Apple TV+. Headquartered in Cupertino, California, Apple remains one of the world's most valuable publicly traded companies, driven by its focus on continuous innovation, user privacy, premium design, and seamless brand integration.
