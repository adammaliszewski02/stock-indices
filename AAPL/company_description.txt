Company description: AAPL

Apple Inc. (NASDAQ: AAPL) is a global technology leader that designs, manufactures, and markets consumer electronics, personal computers, tablets, wearables, and digital services. Founded in 1976 by Steve Jobs, Steve Wozniak, and Ronald Wayne, the company initially revolutionized personal computing with the Apple II and the Macintosh. Apple later transformed the consumer technology landscape with groundbreaking product launches, including the iPod in 2001 and the flagship iPhone in 2007.

Today, Apple operates a highly integrated ecosystem combining hardware, custom software, and digital services. Its flagship product line features the iPhone, Mac computers, iPad, and Apple Watch, many of which are powered by proprietary Apple Silicon chips. Beyond hardware, the company generates substantial high-margin revenue through its expanding Services division, which includes the App Store, Apple Music, iCloud, Apple Pay, and Apple TV+. Headquartered in Cupertino, California, Apple continues to advance its market position by investing heavily in artificial intelligence, augmented reality, and sustainable technologies, solidifying its standing as one of the most valuable and influential corporations in the world.
