Company description: AAPL

Apple Inc. (NASDAQ: AAPL) is a global technology leader that designs, manufactures, and markets smartphones, personal computers, tablets, wearables, and accessories, alongside a expanding portfolio of digital services. Founded in 1976 by Steve Jobs, Steve Wozniak, and Ronald Wayne, the company initially revolutionized personal computing with the Apple II and Macintosh. In 2007, Apple redefined the modern mobile industry with the launch of the iPhone, which remains its flagship product and primary revenue driver today.

Beyond hardware—which includes the iPhone, Mac, iPad, Apple Watch, and AirPods—Apple operates a thriving services division. This ecosystem encompasses the App Store, Apple Music, iCloud, Apple Pay, and Apple TV+. Driven by its proprietary operating systems, such as iOS and macOS, Apple differentiates itself through tight hardware-software integration, premium design, and a strong emphasis on user privacy. 

Today, Apple continues to innovate by expanding its recurring services revenue, advancing custom silicon architecture, and integrating artificial intelligence across its device ecosystem to maintain its position as one of the world's most valuable and influential corporations.
