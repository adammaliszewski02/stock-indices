Company description: AAPL

Apple Inc., traded under the ticker symbol AAPL, is a global technology leader that designs, manufactures, and markets consumer electronics, software, and digital services. Founded in 1976 by Steve Jobs, Steve Wozniak, and Ronald Wayne, the company initially transformed personal computing with the launch of the Apple II and the Macintosh in 1984. 

Today, Apple is best known for its flagship hardware products, most notably the iPhone, which fundamentally reshaped the mobile industry upon its release in 2007. The company's hardware portfolio also includes the iPad, Mac computers, Apple Watch, and AirPods, all powered by proprietary operating systems such as iOS and macOS. 

Beyond hardware, Apple has expanded significantly into digital services, generating recurring revenue through the App Store, Apple Music, iCloud, Apple Pay, and Apple TV+. By seamlessly integrating custom hardware, advanced software, and services, Apple maintains one of the world's most robust technology ecosystems and stands as one of the most valuable publicly traded corporations globally.
