AAPL is the stock ticker for Apple Inc., a multinational technology corporation headquartered in Cupertino, California, that designs, manufactures, and markets smartphones, personal computers, tablets, wearables, and accessories, alongside a comprehensive suite of related software and services. Founded on April 1, 1976, by Steve Jobs, Steve Wozniak, and Ronald Wayne, the company initially gained prominence with the personal computer revolution, highlighted by the introduction of the Apple II and later the Macintosh in 1984. Apple transformed the consumer technology landscape in the 21st century through groundbreaking innovations such as the iPod, iPhone, and iPad, complemented by its growing ecosystem of digital services, including the App Store, Apple Music, and iCloud. Today, it ranks among the world’s most valuable and influential enterprises, driven by a hardware-and-services business model focused on premium design, user privacy, and vertical integration.
