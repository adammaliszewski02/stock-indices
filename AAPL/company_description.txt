Company description: AAPL

Apple Inc. (NASDAQ: AAPL) is a global technology leader that designs, manufactures, and markets premium consumer electronics, personal devices, and software, alongside a rapidly expanding portfolio of digital services. 

Founded in 1976 by Steve Jobs, Steve Wozniak, and Ronald Wayne, the company initially disrupted personal computing with the Apple II and the Macintosh. Apple later revolutionized modern consumer technology with the launch of the iPod in 2001 and the groundbreaking iPhone in 2007, which fundamentally reshaped the global smartphone industry and mobile economy. 

Today, Apple operates a seamlessly integrated ecosystem built around its hardware lineup—including the iPhone, Mac, iPad, Apple Watch, and AirPods—powered by proprietary operating systems such as iOS and macOS. The company complements its physical products with high-margin services, including the App Store, iCloud, Apple Music, Apple Pay, and Apple TV+. 

Headquartered in Cupertino, California, Apple continues to drive industry innovation through ongoing investments in proprietary silicon chips, artificial intelligence, augmented reality, and subscription services, maintaining its position as one of the world's most valuable and influential corporations.
