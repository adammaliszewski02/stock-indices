Company description: AAPL

Apple Inc., trading under the ticker symbol AAPL, is a multinational technology giant that designs, manufactures, and markets consumer electronics, software, and digital services. Founded in 1976 by Steve Jobs, Steve Wozniak, and Ronald Wayne as a personal computer pioneer, the company reshaped the tech landscape with early innovations like the Apple II and the Macintosh. 

Over the past two decades, Apple evolved into a dominant global brand through groundbreaking hardware, most notably the iPod, iPhone, iPad, and Apple Watch. Beyond hardware, the company develops its own proprietary operating systems, including iOS and macOS, and maintains a thriving digital ecosystem encompassing the App Store, iCloud, Apple Music, and Apple Pay. 

Today, Apple operates as one of the world's most valuable publicly traded companies. Its core business focuses on driving revenue through premium consumer devices, expanding high-margin subscription services, and integrating seamless hardware-software experiences. Moving forward, Apple continues to invest heavily in research and development, advancing into emerging sectors such as spatial computing, health technology, and artificial intelligence.
