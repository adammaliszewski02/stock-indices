Company description: AAPL

Apple Inc. (NASDAQ: AAPL) is a global technology leader that designs, manufactures, and markets consumer electronics, software, and digital services. Founded in 1976 by Steve Jobs, Steve Wozniak, and Ronald Wayne, the company initially revolutionized personal computing with the Apple II and Macintosh. Apple redefined modern mobile technology with the landmark launch of the iPhone in 2007, eventually becoming the first publicly traded company to achieve a trillion-dollar market capitalization.

Today, Apple operates a seamless, premium ecosystem anchored by its flagship hardware, including the iPhone, Mac personal computers, iPad tablets, Apple Watch, and the Vision Pro spatial computer. These devices are powered by custom Apple silicon and proprietary operating systems such as iOS and macOS. Beyond hardware, the company drives substantial growth through its expanding digital services segment, which encompasses the App Store, iCloud, Apple Music, Apple Pay, and Apple TV+. By emphasizing tight hardware-software integration, user privacy, and design excellence, Apple maintains its position as one of the world's most valuable and influential corporations.
