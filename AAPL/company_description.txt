Company description: AAPL

Apple Inc., traded under the ticker symbol AAPL, is a global technology leader that designs, manufactures, and markets smartphones, personal computers, tablets, wearables, and accessories, alongside a robust ecosystem of software and digital services. 

Founded in 1976 by Steve Jobs, Steve Wozniak, and Ronald Wayne as Apple Computer Company, the firm initially revolutionized the personal computing industry with the Apple II and Macintosh. Over the decades, Apple evolved into a global consumer powerhouse, reshaping multiple markets through iconic innovations such as the iPod in 2001, the iPhone in 2007, and the iPad in 2010. 

Today, Apple operates as one of the world's most valuable publicly traded corporations. Its core business revolves around its flagship hardware lineup—led by the iPhone, Mac, and iPad—complemented by a rapidly growing Services division that encompasses the App Store, iCloud, Apple Music, and Apple Pay. Headquartered in Cupertino, California, the company maintains a competitive advantage through its tight integration of custom hardware, proprietary operating systems, and privacy-focused software, while continually expanding into emerging technologies like advanced silicon, augmented reality, and artificial intelligence.
