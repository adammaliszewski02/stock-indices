SLB, formerly known as Schlumberger, is the world’s largest oilfield services company. Founded in 1926 by brothers Conrad and Marcel Schlumberger, the company pioneered the field of electrical well logging, a breakthrough that revolutionized subsurface exploration. Today, SLB provides a comprehensive suite of technology, project management, and information solutions to the global energy industry. Its operations span the entire lifecycle of a reservoir, from exploration and appraisal to production, midstream, and carbon sequestration. Headquartered in Houston, Paris, London, and The Hague, the company maintains a presence in over 100 countries. In recent years, SLB has strategically pivoted toward a "New Energy" portfolio, focusing on digital transformation, decarbonization, and low-carbon technologies to support the global energy transition while maintaining its leadership in traditional oil and gas services.
