SLB, formerly known as Schlumberger, is the world's leading multinational oilfield services company, providing comprehensive technology, project management, and information solutions to the global oil and gas exploration and production industry. Founded in 1926 by brothers Conrad and Marcel Schlumberger in the Alsace region of France, the company revolutionized the energy sector by introducing electrical wireline logging to evaluate subsurface rock formations. Over nearly a century of operation, SLB has expanded its portfolio to encompass well construction, reservoir performance, and surface production systems. Headquartered in Houston, Paris, and The Hague, the company currently operates in more than 100 countries. In recent years, SLB has strategically adapted its business model to address the global energy transition through significant investments in low-carbon technologies, including carbon capture and sequestration, geothermal energy, geo-energy, and hydrogen solutions, while continuing to optimize efficiency and reduce carbon emissions within traditional fossil fuel operations.
