SLB, formerly known as Schlumberger, is the world's leading multinational energy technology company, providing comprehensive services to the global oil and gas industry. Founded in 1926 by brothers Conrad and Marcel Schlumberger in the Alsace region of France, the company revolutionized the energy sector by introducing electrical wireline logging to locate subsurface oil and gas reservoirs. Today, SLB delivers a broad range of products and services spanning hydrocarbon exploration, well construction, production optimization, and large-scale integrated reservoir management. Headquartered globally across Houston, Paris, The Hague, and London, the company operates in more than 100 countries. In recent years, SLB has strategically evolved its business model to address the global energy transition, expanding its portfolio into digital integration, carbon capture and storage, geothermal energy, and lower-carbon emissions technologies to help industrial customers achieve sustainability and net-zero goals.
