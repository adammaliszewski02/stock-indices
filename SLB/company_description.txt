SLB, formerly known as Schlumberger, is the world's leading oilfield services company, providing technology, project management, and information solutions to the global oil and gas exploration and production industry. Founded in 1926 by brothers Conrad and Marcel Schlumberger in France as the Société de Prospection Électrique, the company pioneered electrical well logging to locate subsurface hydrocarbon reserves. Over the decades, SLB expanded its portfolio to encompass a comprehensive range of upstream services, including seismic imaging, well construction, directional drilling, and reservoir evaluation. Headquartered globally across Houston, Paris, The Hague, and London, the company operates in more than 100 countries. In recent years, SLB has actively diversified its business model to address the global energy transition, investing significantly in lower-carbon technologies such as carbon capture and sequestration, geothermal energy, hydrogen systems, and energy storage. Through its extensive research and development network, digital integration, and comprehensive suite of subsurface and surface solutions, SLB enables energy operators to optimize hydrocarbon recovery while simultaneously reducing their environmental impact.
