SLB, formerly known as Schlumberger, is the world's leading oilfield services company, providing comprehensive technology, project management, and information solutions to the global energy industry. Founded in 1926 by the Schlumberger brothers in Alsace, France, the company revolutionized the energy sector by introducing electrical well logging to locate oil and gas reserves. Today, SLB operates in more than 100 countries, helping customers safely and efficiently access hydrocarbons through its core divisions, which encompass reservoir characterization, well construction, production systems, and digital integration. As global energy demands evolve, SLB has strategically expanded its portfolio to include lower-carbon energy solutions, such as carbon capture and sequestration, geothermal energy, and energy storage, while continuing to optimize traditional oil and gas operations through advanced automation and artificial intelligence.
