SLB, formerly known as Schlumberger, is the world's leading oilfield services company, providing comprehensive technology, project management, and information solutions to the global energy industry. Founded in 1926 by the Schlumberger brothers in France, the company revolutionized the energy sector by introducing electrical well logging to oil exploration. Today, SLB operates in over 100 countries, helping customers safely find, develop, and produce oil and gas. In response to global energy transitions, the company has increasingly diversified its portfolio to include low-carbon technologies, such as carbon capture and storage, geothermal energy, and hydrogen solutions. SLB is incorporated in Curaçao and maintains principal executive offices in Houston, Paris, and The Hague.
