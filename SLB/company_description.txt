SLB, formerly known as Schlumberger, is the world's leading multinational energy technology company, providing comprehensive services to the global oil and gas industry. Founded in 1926 by brothers Conrad and Marcel Schlumberger in the Alsace region of France, the company revolutionized the energy sector by introducing electrical wireline logging to evaluate subsurface rock formations. Over the decades, SLB expanded its portfolio to encompass a broad range of operations, including reservoir performance, well construction, and production systems. Headquartered globally across Houston, Paris, The Hague, and London, SLB has continually evolved its business model to address the dual challenge of meeting rising energy demand while reducing environmental impacts. Today, the company leverages advanced digital solutions, artificial intelligence, and extensive research and development to optimize traditional hydrocarbon recovery while simultaneously investing in new energy ventures, such as carbon capture, geothermal energy, and hydrogen systems, positioning itself as a critical enabler of the global energy transition.
