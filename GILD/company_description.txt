Gilead Sciences, Inc., trading under the stock ticker GILD, is a research-based biopharmaceutical company that discovers, develops, and commercializes innovative medicines in areas of unmet medical need. Founded in 1987 in California, the company initially focused on antiviral technology. Gilead achieved significant historical milestones by transforming the treatment and management of life-threatening infectious diseases, most notably through the introduction of groundbreaking therapies for HIV/AIDS and chronic hepatitis C. Over the decades, the company has expanded its therapeutic portfolio beyond virology to address complex oncology, inflammatory conditions, and fibrotic diseases through both internal research and strategic acquisitions, such as the integration of Kite Pharma and Immunomedics. Today, GILD operates on a global scale, supplying life-saving therapeutics to millions of patients worldwide while maintaining a robust pipeline of investigational drugs aimed at advancing global healthcare.
