Gilead Sciences, Inc., trading under the stock ticker GILD, is a research-based biopharmaceutical company that discovers, develops, and commercializes innovative medicines in areas of unmet medical need. Founded in 1987 in Foster City, California, the company initially focused on antiviral technology. Over the decades, Gilead achieved global prominence through groundbreaking therapies that transformed the treatment and management of life-threatening infectious diseases, most notably HIV/AIDS and chronic hepatitis B and C. 

In recent years, Gilead has strategically expanded its scientific portfolio beyond virology into oncology and inflammatory diseases, largely driven by major corporate acquisitions such as Kite Pharma and Immunomedics. Today, the company provides advanced therapeutic options to millions of patients worldwide, operating research and manufacturing facilities across North America, Europe, and Australia while continuously investing a significant portion of its revenue into clinical development and life-saving medical innovation.
