Gilead Sciences, Inc., trading under the ticker GILD, is a research-based biopharmaceutical company that discovers, develops, and commercializes innovative medicines in areas of unmet medical need. Founded in June 1987 by Michael L. Riordan, the company initially focused on antiviral technology. Gilead achieved significant growth and industry prominence through the development of breakthrough therapeutics, notably for human immunodeficiency virus (HIV) and viral hepatitis. Over the decades, the company expanded its therapeutic portfolio significantly, particularly through strategic acquisitions such as Pharmasset in 2012, which added leading hepatitis C treatments, and Kite Pharma in 2017, which established its presence in cell therapy and oncology. Today, Gilead operates globally as a leader in life sciences, dedicated to transforming and simplifying care for people with life-threatening illnesses worldwide.
