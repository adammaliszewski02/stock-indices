Gilead Sciences, Inc. (GILD) is a research-based biopharmaceutical company that discovers, develops, and commercializes innovative medicines in areas of unmet medical need. Founded in California in 1987 by Michael L. Riordan, the company initially focused on antiviral technology. Over the decades, Gilead has achieved global prominence through its groundbreaking treatments for life-threatening infectious diseases, most notably pioneering single-tablet regimens for human immunodeficiency virus (HIV) and curative therapies for hepatitis C. 

In recent years, Gilead has significantly expanded its therapeutic portfolio into oncology and inflammatory diseases, largely driven by strategic acquisitions such as Kite Pharma in 2017 and Immunomedics in 2020. This expansion bolstered its capabilities in cell therapy and antibody-drug conjugates. Additionally, the company played a critical role during the COVID-19 pandemic by developing remdesivir, an antiviral treatment authorized globally. Headquartered in Foster City, California, Gilead continues to leverage scientific innovation to address complex global health challenges.
