Gilead Sciences, Inc., trading under the stock ticker GILD, is a research-based biopharmaceutical company that discovers, develops, and commercializes innovative medicines in areas of unmet medical need. Founded in 1987 in Foster City, California, the company initially focused on antiviral technology. Over the decades, Gilead achieved global prominence through groundbreaking therapies that transformed the treatment landscape for infectious diseases, most notably introducing revolutionary single-tablet regimens for HIV/AIDS, as well as highly effective cures for Hepatitis C. In recent years, the company has strategically expanded its scientific footprint into oncology and inflammatory diseases through significant internal research and major corporate acquisitions, notably incorporating advanced cell therapies and antibody-drug conjugates. Today, Gilead operates on a global scale, supplying life-saving therapeutics to millions of patients worldwide while maintaining a robust clinical pipeline aimed at addressing complex viral, oncological, and immunological conditions.
