Gilead Sciences, Inc., trading under the ticker GILD, is a research-based biopharmaceutical company that discovers, develops, and commercializes innovative medicines in areas of unmet medical need. Founded in 1987 in Foster City, California, the company initially focused on antiviral technology. Over the decades, Gilead has achieved global leadership in the fields of virology and immunology, transforming the treatment and management of life-threatening diseases such as human immunodeficiency virus (HIV) and viral hepatitis through breakthrough therapies like Truvada, Harvoni, and Biktarvy. 

In recent years, Gilead has strategically expanded its portfolio beyond antivirals into oncology and inflammatory diseases, largely driven by significant corporate acquisitions, including Kite Pharma in 2017 and Immunomedics in 2020. Today, the company operates on a global scale, supplying therapeutics to patients in over thirty-five countries. Gilead remains committed to advancing scientific research and delivering transformative medicines to improve healthcare outcomes worldwide.
