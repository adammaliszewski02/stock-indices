Gilead Sciences, Inc. (ticker: GILD) is a research-based biopharmaceutical company that discovers, develops, and commercializes innovative medicines in areas of unmet medical need. Founded in 1987 in Foster City, California, the company initially focused on antiviral technology. Over the decades, Gilead has grown into a global leader, achieving major scientific breakthroughs in the treatment and prevention of life-threatening diseases, including human immunodeficiency virus (HIV), hepatitis B and C, and COVID-19. The company significantly expanded its oncology portfolio through strategic acquisitions, notably the 2017 purchase of Kite Pharma, which advanced its leadership in cell therapy, and the 2020 acquisition of Immunomedics. Today, Gilead continues to direct its research efforts toward advancing therapies in virology, oncology, and inflammatory diseases, serving millions of patients worldwide.
