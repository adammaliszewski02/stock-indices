Gilead Sciences, Inc., trading under the stock ticker GILD, is a research-based biopharmaceutical company that discovers, develops, and commercializes innovative medicines in areas of unmet medical need. Founded in 1987 in Foster City, California, the company initially focused on antiviral technology. Over the decades, Gilead achieved global prominence through groundbreaking treatments for life-threatening infectious diseases, most notably revolutionizing the management and cure of human immunodeficiency virus (HIV) and hepatitis C. 

In recent years, the company has strategically expanded its scientific footprint into oncology and inflammation through significant acquisitions and internal research. Gilead's portfolio now features numerous industry-leading therapeutics, including single-tablet regimens for HIV, cell therapies for cancer, and antiviral treatments such as remdesivir. Driven by a mission to create a healthier world for all people, the company continues to leverage advanced scientific platforms to address complex global health challenges, operating in more than thirty-five countries worldwide.
