Verizon Communications Inc., trading under the ticker VZ, is a premier American multinational telecommunications conglomerate and a global leader in broadband, video, and wireless communications services. Headquartered in New York City, the enterprise traces its origins to the 1984 breakup of the original AT&T, which created Bell Atlantic Corporation. Bell Atlantic subsequently merged with GTE Corporation in 2000, formally adopting the name Verizon—a portmanteau of "veritas" (Latin for truth) and "horizon." Today, Verizon operates primarily through two segments: Verizon Consumer Group and Verizon Business Group. The company delivers advanced voice, data, and video services across its award-winning nationwide wireless and fiber-optic networks to millions of consumers, businesses, government agencies, and wholesale customers. Additionally, Verizon maintains a robust digital media portfolio and is a pioneer in the ongoing deployment of high-speed 5G network infrastructure.
