Verizon Communications Inc., trading under the ticker VZ, is a premier American multinational telecommunications conglomerate and a global leader in delivering broadband, video, and digital communication services. Headquartered in New York City, the company traces its corporate lineage back to the 1984 breakup of AT&T, originally forming as Bell Atlantic Corporation. Following a series of strategic consolidations, including the landmark 2000 merger with GTE, the entity was rebranded as Verizon—a portmanteau of "veritas" (truth) and "horizon." Today, Verizon operates primarily through two major segments: Verizon Consumer Group and Verizon Business. The company provides wireless voice, SMS, and data services across its extensive 4G LTE and growing 5G networks, alongside advanced fiber-optic broadband through its Fios services. Serving millions of retail consumers, businesses, and government agencies, Verizon remains a foundational pillar of modern global connectivity, continuously investing in network infrastructure, cloud solutions, and digital entertainment to drive sustained industry leadership.
