Verizon Communications Inc., trading under the ticker VZ, is a premier American multinational telecommunications conglomerate and a global leader in delivering broadband, video, and digital communication services. Headquartered in New York City, the company traces its origins to the 1984 breakup of AT&T, which led to the formation of Bell Atlantic, one of the original Regional Bell Operating Companies. Bell Atlantic subsequently merged with GTE in 2000 to officially form Verizon, a portmanteau of "veritas" and "horizon." Today, Verizon operates primarily through two segments: Verizon Consumer Group and Verizon Business. The company provides wireless voice, data, and video services across its advanced 4G LTE and nationwide 5G networks to millions of consumers, businesses, and government entities. Additionally, it offers fiber-optic-based Fios internet and television services. Through continuous infrastructure investment and strategic acquisitions, Verizon remains a foundational pillar of modern global connectivity and digital infrastructure.
