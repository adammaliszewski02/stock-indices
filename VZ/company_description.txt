The stock ticker VZ identifies Verizon Communications Inc., one of the world's largest telecommunications and technology service providers. Headquartered in New York City, Verizon delivers wireless connectivity, broadband internet, voice, and video services across its nationwide wireless networks and fiber-optic infrastructure. The company serves hundreds of millions of consumers, enterprise clients, and government entities through its two primary segments: Verizon Consumer Group and Verizon Business Group.

Verizon traces its origins to the 1984 breakup of the original AT&T Bell System, initially operating as the regional telephone company Bell Atlantic. In 2000, Bell Atlantic merged with GTE Corporation to establish Verizon Communications. A defining historical milestone occurred in 2014 when the company acquired Vodafone's 45 percent stake in Verizon Wireless for $130 billion, consolidating complete control over the premier mobile network operator. Today, as a component of the Dow Jones Industrial Average, Verizon focuses primarily on expanding its 5G mobile networks, fiber broadband offerings like Fios, and specialized enterprise digital transformation solutions.
