Verizon Communications Inc., trading under the ticker VZ, is a premier American multinational telecommunications conglomerate and a global leader in delivering broadband, video, and digital communication services. Headquartered in New York City, the enterprise traces its origins to the 1984 divestiture of AT&T, which led to the creation of Bell Atlantic Corporation. Bell Atlantic subsequently merged with GTE Corporation in 2000, officially adopting the name Verizon—a portmanteau of "veritas" (truth) and "horizon." Today, the company operates primarily through two segments: Verizon Consumer and Verizon Business. It provides wireless voice, data, and broadband services to millions of retail customers, businesses, and government agencies across the United States. Renowned for constructing and maintaining extensive 4G LTE and cutting-edge 5G Ultra Wideband networks, the corporation also offers advanced cloud, security, and IoT solutions. Furthermore, Verizon expanded its digital footprint through strategic historical acquisitions, including AOL in 2015 and Yahoo! in 2017, though it has since divested those media properties to refocus intensely on its core telecommunications and connectivity infrastructure.
