Verizon Communications Inc., trading under the ticker VZ, is a premier American multinational telecommunications conglomerate and a global leader in delivering broadband, video, and wireless communications services. Formed in 2000 through the historic merger of Bell Atlantic Corp. and GTE Corp.—with roots tracing back to the Bell Telephone Company founded by Alexander Graham Bell in 1877—the company has evolved from a traditional regional telephone provider into a massive digital and media infrastructure powerhouse. Verizon operates primarily through two segments: Verizon Consumer Group and Verizon Business Group. It delivers reliable voice, data, and video services over its award-winning nationwide wireless and fiber-optic networks to millions of consumers, businesses, and government agencies. Renowned for its strategic investments in cutting-edge technology, Verizon was a pioneer in deploying 5G ultra-wideband networks across the United States. In addition to its core telecommunications offerings, the company provides advanced enterprise solutions, including cloud computing, cybersecurity, and internet of things applications, reinforcing its foundational role in modern global connectivity.
