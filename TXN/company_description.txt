Texas Instruments Incorporated, trading under the ticker TXN, is a leading global semiconductor company headquartered in Dallas, Texas. The company specializes in the design, manufacturing, and sale of analog chips and embedded processors, which collectively account for the vast majority of its revenue. These foundational components manage power, measure physical signals, and process digital data across diverse markets, notably industrial, automotive, personal electronics, and communications infrastructure. 

Originally founded in 1930 as Geophysical Service Inc. to provide seismic exploration for the petroleum industry, the firm reorganized and adopted the name Texas Instruments in 1951 as it pivoted toward electronics. The company holds a storied place in technological history, having commercialized the first silicon transistor in 1954 and invented the integrated circuit in 1958 through Nobel laureate Jack Kilby. Texas Instruments also pioneered early consumer handheld calculators and educational electronics. Today, the enterprise maintains extensive internal manufacturing capabilities, focusing heavily on durable, long-lifecycle analog technologies that underpin modern electronic systems worldwide.
