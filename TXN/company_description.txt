Stock ticker TXN identifies Texas Instruments Incorporated, a global semiconductor design and manufacturing enterprise. The company focuses primarily on analog chips and embedded processors, which generate the vast majority of its revenue. Analog semiconductors process real-world signals—such as sound, temperature, and pressure—and manage power distribution, while embedded processors act as the computational brains for specialized electronic devices. Texas Instruments serves tens of thousands of customers worldwide, predominantly targeting the industrial and automotive sectors, as well as personal electronics and enterprise systems.

Originally founded in 1930 as Geophysical Service Inc., a seismic exploration company for the petroleum industry, the firm reorganized as Texas Instruments in 1951. Throughout its history, it has served as a foundational pioneer in modern computing; notably, TI engineer Jack Kilby co-invented the working integrated circuit in 1958, and the company later introduced the first handheld electronic calculator in 1967. Headquartered in Dallas, Texas, the company differentiates itself through extensive internal manufacturing facilities, enabling cost-efficient production and a resilient global supply chain.
