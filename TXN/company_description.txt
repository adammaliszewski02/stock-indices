Texas Instruments Incorporated (TXN) is a global semiconductor company that designs, manufactures, tests, and sells analog and embedded processing chips for industrial, automotive, personal electronics, communications equipment, and enterprise systems markets. Founded in 1930 as Geophysical Service Inc., the company originally provided seismic data to the oil and gas industry before pivoting to defense electronics and semiconductor development. Notably, Texas Instruments invented the integrated circuit in 1958 and produced the first commercially successful handheld electronic calculator in 1967. Over subsequent decades, the firm strategically transitioned its focus away from defense and consumer electronics to concentrate on analog integrated circuits and embedded processors, which form the core of its current operations. Today, TXN markets tens of thousands of products to more than 100,000 customers globally, leveraging advanced internal manufacturing capabilities and a proprietary 300mm wafer fabrication process to maintain competitive advantages in long-lifecycle markets.
