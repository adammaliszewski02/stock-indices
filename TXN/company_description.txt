Texas Instruments Incorporated (TXN) is a global semiconductor manufacturer that designs, innovates, manufactures, and sells analog and embedded processing semiconductors for industrial, automotive, personal electronics, communications equipment, and enterprise systems markets. Founded in 1930 as Geophysical Service Inc., the company originally provided seismic data to the oil industry before entering the defense electronics sector and pioneering key semiconductor technologies. A major milestone in its history occurred in 1958 when Texas Instruments engineer Jack Kilby invented the integrated circuit, fundamentally transforming modern electronics. The company later introduced the first commercial silicon transistor and revolutionized consumer mathematics with the introduction of handheld electronic calculators in the 1970s. Today, TXN focuses its business primarily on long-lifecycle analog chips and embedded processors, operating its own manufacturing facilities to maintain a competitive manufacturing and technology advantage. Headquartered in Dallas, Texas, the company continues to serve a vast base of global customers through sales and manufacturing operations worldwide.
