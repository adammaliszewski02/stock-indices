Texas Instruments Incorporated, trading under the ticker TXN, is a global semiconductor design and manufacturing company headquartered in Dallas, Texas. The company specializes in developing analog chips and embedded processors, which represent the vast majority of its revenue. These critical components manage power, sense conditions, and process data across tens of thousands of products, primarily serving the industrial, automotive, personal electronics, communications, and enterprise systems markets.

Originally founded in 1930 as Geophysical Service Inc., a seismic exploration enterprise for the petroleum industry, the business reorganized as Texas Instruments in 1951 to focus on electronics. TI has played a pivotal role in the modern technology landscape, highlighted by employee Jack Kilby’s co-invention of the integrated circuit in 1958, the commercialization of the silicon transistor in 1954, and the development of the first handheld calculator in 1967. 

Today, Texas Instruments operates as one of the world’s leading semiconductor manufacturers. The company sets itself apart through a vast portfolio of tens of thousands of products, direct customer channels, and significant internal manufacturing capabilities, focusing heavily on cost-efficient 300-millimeter wafer fabrication and long-term free cash flow generation.
