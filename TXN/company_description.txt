Texas Instruments Incorporated (TXN) is a global semiconductor design and manufacturing company that develops analog integrated circuits and embedded processors for industrial, automotive, personal electronics, communications equipment, and enterprise systems markets. Founded in 1930 as Geophysical Service Incorporated, the firm initially provided seismic data services for the petroleum industry before pivoting to defense electronics and semiconductor development. A major pioneer in microelectronics, Texas Instruments invented the integrated circuit in 1958 and the handheld calculator in 1967. Over subsequent decades, the company divested its defense and consumer electronics divisions—including its iconic calculator and digital light processing businesses—to focus entirely on semiconductor technology. Today, TXN leverages its proprietary manufacturing and wafer fabrication assets, including 300mm analog production facilities, to deliver long-lifecycle components to tens of thousands of customers worldwide.
