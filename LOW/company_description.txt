Lowe’s Companies, Inc., trading under the ticker LOW, is a premier American home improvement retailer that serves millions of do-it-yourself and professional customers. The company offers a comprehensive assortment of products for maintenance, repair, home improvement, and remodeling, spanning categories such as appliances, tools, lumber, building materials, and lawn and garden supplies. 

The enterprise traces its origins to 1921 when Lucius Smith Lowe founded a small hardware store in North Wilkesboro, North Carolina. Following his death in 1940, the business was inherited by his daughter Ruth and managed by her brother, James Marcus Buchan. Buchan modernized the operation, pioneering the shift toward a retail-focused model by discontinuing dry goods and focusing exclusively on hardware and building materials. Lowe’s incorporated in 1952 and went public in 1961, a crucial milestone that fueled its rapid expansion across the United States. Today, operating a vast network of stores alongside robust digital platforms, Lowe's stands as a Fortune 500 entity and one of the largest home improvement retailers globally, dedicated to helping customers make their homes better.
