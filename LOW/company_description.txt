Lowe’s Companies, Inc., trading under the ticker LOW, is a premier Fortune 50 home improvement retailer dedicated to serving homeowners, renters, and commercial business customers. The company offers a comprehensive array of products and services for maintenance, repair, remodeling, and home decor, spanning major categories such as appliances, tools, lumber, and building materials. Headquartered in Mooresville, North Carolina, Lowe's operates a vast network of hundreds of big-box retail stores across the United States. 

The company's origins trace back to 1921 when Lucius Smith Lowe established North Wilkesboro Hardware in North Wilkesboro, North Carolina. Following Lowe's death, the business was inherited by his daughter Ruth and managed by her husband, James Stormont Buchan. In 1940, Buchan's brother-in-law, H. Carl Buchan, bought the business and strategically shifted its focus exclusively to retail hardware and building materials, phasing out dry goods and groceries. This pivotal decision laid the groundwork for modern home improvement retailing. Lowe's expanded regionally in the following decades and became a publicly traded company in 1961, eventually growing into a dominant global retail institution and a foundational component of the retail sector.
