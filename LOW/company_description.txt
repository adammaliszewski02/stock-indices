Lowe’s Companies, Inc., trading under the ticker symbol LOW, is a premier American home improvement retailer that operates a comprehensive network of retail and hardware stores across the United States. The company provides a vast array of products for home maintenance, repair, remodeling, and property decoration, catering to both do-it-yourself residential customers and professional contractors. Founded in 1921 by Lucius Smith Lowe in North Wilkesboro, North Carolina, the business originated as a small hardware store before expanding regionally and eventually nationally. It achieved a major milestone in 1961 by becoming a publicly traded company. Over the subsequent decades, Lowe’s evolved into a Fortune 50 enterprise and one of the largest home improvement retailers globally, driven by strategic store expansions, supply chain enhancements, and a growing emphasis on omnichannel retail capabilities that integrate physical stores with digital e-commerce platforms.
