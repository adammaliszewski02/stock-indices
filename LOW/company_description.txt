Lowe’s Companies, Inc., trading under the ticker symbol LOW, is a premier American home improvement retailer that serves millions of customers weekly. The company operates and services a vast network of home improvement and hardware stores across the United States, offering a comprehensive array of products for maintenance, repair, remodeling, home decor, and property management. Catering to both do-it-yourself customers and professional contractors, Lowe’s provides building materials, appliances, tools, lawn and garden equipment, and related installation services. 

Founded in 1921 by Lucius Smith Lowe in North Wilkesboro, North Carolina, the enterprise began as a small hardware store before expanding regionally and nationally. It achieved a major milestone by going public in 1961, which fueled its rapid nationwide growth. Over the decades, Lowe’s evolved from a regional supplier into a Fortune 50 retail giant. Today, the corporation continuously adapts to modern consumer demands through robust e-commerce platforms and strategic supply chain enhancements, maintaining its position as a cornerstone of the North American retail landscape.
