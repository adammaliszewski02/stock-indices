Lowe’s Companies, Inc., trading under the ticker symbol LOW, is a premier American home improvement retailer serving homeowners, renters, and commercial business customers. Founded in 1921 as a small hardware store in North Wilkesboro, North Carolina, by Lucius Smith Lowe and later expanded by his brother-in-law Carl Buchan, the company evolved from a rural general merchant into a nationwide powerhouse. Today, Lowe’s operates a vast network of retail and distribution facilities across the United States, offering a comprehensive assortment of products for home maintenance, repair, remodeling, and decoration. The company provides a broad selection of building materials, appliances, tools, hardware, and seasonal goods, complemented by installation and project support services. Through its integrated omnichannel strategy, Lowe’s serves millions of customers weekly, combining physical storefronts with a robust digital e-commerce platform to maintain its position as a global leader in the home improvement sector.
