Lowe’s Companies, Inc., trading under the stock ticker LOW, is a prominent American home improvement retailer. The company operates a vast network of retail stores providing a comprehensive range of products for home maintenance, repair, remodeling, and decorating. Its inventory includes building materials, hardware, tools, appliances, and lawn and garden products, catering to both do-it-yourself and professional contractors. 

The enterprise traces its origins back to 1921, when Lucius Smith Lowe established a hardware store in North Wilkesboro, North Carolina. Following his death in 1940, the business was inherited by his daughter Ruth and managed by her husband, James Neel Buchan, who later sold his share to his brother-in-law, H. Carl Buchan. Carl Buchan is widely recognized as the visionary who modernized the business by shifting the focus exclusively to retail hardware and building materials, eliminating dry goods and groceries, and introducing the cash-and-carry model. Lowe’s incorporated in 1952 and experienced rapid growth, eventually becoming a publicly traded company in 1961. Today, it stands as a Fortune 500 company and one of the largest home improvement retailers globally, maintaining its commitment to helping customers improve their living spaces.
