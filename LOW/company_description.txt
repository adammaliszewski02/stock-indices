Lowe’s Companies, Inc., trading under the ticker LOW, is a premier American home improvement retailer that serves millions of customers weekly. Founded in 1921 as a small hardware store in North Wilkesboro, North Carolina, by Lucius Smith Lowe and later expanded by his brother-in-law, Carl Buchan, the company grew from a localized merchant into a national retail powerhouse. Today, Lowe’s operates a vast network of retail home improvement stores across the United States and Canada, offering an extensive assortment of products for maintenance, repair, remodeling, and home decor. The company caters to both do-it-yourself retail customers and professional contractors, providing building materials, appliances, tools, and lawn and garden care products. Alongside its physical store footprint, Lowe’s leverages a robust omnichannel platform to integrate digital shopping with in-store services. As a Fortune 500 company, Lowe’s remains a cornerstone of the consumer retail sector, driven by a strategic focus on supply chain modernization, digital engagement, and localized inventory tailored to regional housing markets.
