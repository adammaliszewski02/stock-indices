Lowe’s Companies, Inc., trading under the ticker LOW, is a premier American home improvement retailer that serves millions of customers weekly. Founded in 1921 as a small hardware store in North Wilkesboro, North Carolina, by Lucius Smith Lowe and later expanded by his brother-in-law Carl Buchan, the company evolved into a publicly traded corporation in 1961. Today, Lowe's operates an extensive network of retail home improvement stores across the United States, offering a comprehensive array of products for maintenance, repair, remodeling, home decor, and property management. Catering to both do-it-yourself customers and professional contractors, the company provides building materials, appliances, tools, hardware, and landscaping supplies. By emphasizing omnichannel capabilities, supply chain optimization, and localized product offerings, Lowe's has established a durable market presence as one of the largest home improvement retailers globally.
