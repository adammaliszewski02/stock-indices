Eli Lilly and Company (LLY) is a global pharmaceutical leader headquartered in Indianapolis, Indiana. Founded in 1876 by Colonel Eli Lilly, a pharmaceutical chemist and veteran, the company has a long-standing history of medical innovation, notably becoming the first to mass-produce insulin in the 1920s. Today, Lilly focuses on discovering, developing, and marketing human pharmaceuticals across several core therapeutic areas, including diabetes, oncology, immunology, neuroscience, and cardiovascular health. The company is currently at the forefront of the biopharmaceutical industry, driven by a robust pipeline of treatments for chronic conditions such as obesity and Alzheimer’s disease. By integrating advanced biotechnology with traditional drug development, Eli Lilly continues to address significant unmet medical needs on a global scale, maintaining its position as one of the world’s most valuable healthcare organizations.
