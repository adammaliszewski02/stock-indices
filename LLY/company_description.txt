Eli Lilly and Company (ticker: LLY) is a leading global pharmaceutical enterprise dedicated to discovering, developing, and delivering human therapeutics that make life better for people worldwide. Founded in Indianapolis in 1876 by Colonel Eli Lilly, a pharmaceutical chemist and veteran of the American Civil War, the company has a long history of medical innovation. It introduced several historic milestones to healthcare, including pioneering the commercial mass production of penicillin in the 1940s and bringing Humulin, the world's first genetically engineered biosynthetic human insulin, to market in 1982. Today, Eli Lilly operates primarily in major therapeutic areas such as neuroscience, immunology, oncology, and cardiometabolic health. The company invests heavily in research and development to address complex global health challenges, commercializing breakthrough treatments for diabetes, obesity, and Alzheimer’s disease. Headquartered in Indianapolis, Indiana, Eli Lilly distributes its diverse portfolio of medicines in over one hundred countries, employing tens of thousands of people globally to sustain its mission of uniting caring with discovery to create medicines that make life better.
