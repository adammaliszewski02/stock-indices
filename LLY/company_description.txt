Eli Lilly and Company (ticker: LLY) is a leading global pharmaceutical enterprise dedicated to discovering, developing, and delivering innovative human therapeutics that improve global health. Founded in Indianapolis in 1876 by Colonel Eli Lilly, a pharmaceutical chemist and veteran of the American Civil War, the firm established an enduring legacy of scientific firsts. It introduced several monumental medical advancements, including being the first company to mass-produce the polio vaccine in the 1950s and commercially launch insulin in the 1920s. Today, the company’s core business centers on researching and marketing prescription medications across critical therapeutic areas, notably diabetes care, oncology, immunology, and neuroscience. In recent years, Eli Lilly has experienced significant market growth driven by its cutting-edge portfolio of cardiometabolic treatments, such as therapies addressing obesity and type 2 diabetes. Operating research and manufacturing facilities worldwide, the enterprise continues to invest heavily in research and development to address complex, high-demand global health challenges.
