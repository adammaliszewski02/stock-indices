Eli Lilly and Company (LLY) is a global pharmaceutical leader headquartered in Indianapolis, Indiana. Founded in 1876 by Colonel Eli Lilly, a pharmaceutical chemist and veteran, the company has a long-standing history of medical innovation, notably becoming the first to mass-produce insulin in the 1920s. Today, Lilly focuses on discovering and developing high-quality medicines to address some of the world’s most urgent medical needs. Its diversified portfolio spans key therapeutic areas, including diabetes, oncology, immunology, neuroscience, and cardiovascular health. The company is currently at the forefront of the biopharmaceutical industry, driven by significant advancements in metabolic treatments and Alzheimer’s disease research. By integrating cutting-edge biotechnology with a commitment to patient-centered care, Eli Lilly continues to expand its global footprint, aiming to improve health outcomes for millions of people while maintaining a robust pipeline of transformative therapies.
