Eli Lilly and Company (ticker: LLY) is a leading global pharmaceutical enterprise dedicated to discovering, developing, and delivering human therapeutics that improve global health. Founded in Indianapolis in 1876 by Colonel Eli Lilly, a pharmaceutical chemist and veteran of the American Civil War, the company pioneered several industry milestones. Notably, it partnered with researchers to mass-produce insulin in the 1920s, significantly altering the management of diabetes, and later became a major commercial producer of penicillin during the 1940s. 

Today, Lilly’s business model centers on research-driven drug development across core therapeutic areas, including diabetes, obesity, oncology, immunology, and neuroscience. The company operates research facilities globally and markets its portfolio of prescription medications and healthcare solutions in approximately 120 countries. In recent years, Lilly has experienced substantial growth driven by innovations in metabolic and weight-management treatments, reinforcing its position as one of the most valued healthcare corporations in the world.
