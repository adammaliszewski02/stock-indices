Eli Lilly and Company, traded under the stock ticker LLY, is a leading American multinational pharmaceutical corporation headquartered in Indianapolis, Indiana. The company discovers, develops, and globally markets human healthcare products, with a primary strategic focus on high-need therapeutic areas including diabetes care, oncology, immunology, neuroscience, and obesity. Founded in 1876 by Colonel Eli Lilly, a Union Army veteran and pharmaceutical chemist, the company has a distinguished history of medical innovation. It introduced several world-changing therapeutics, most notably being the first company to mass-produce the polio vaccine in the 1950s and human insulin (Humulin) using recombinant DNA technology in the early 1980s. In recent years, LLY has experienced significant growth driven by its advanced portfolio of therapies for type 2 diabetes and weight management.
