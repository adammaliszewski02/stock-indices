Eli Lilly and Company (ticker LLY) is a leading American multinational pharmaceutical company headquartered in Indianapolis, Indiana. Founded in 1876 by Colonel Eli Lilly, a pharmaceutical chemist and Union Army veteran, the company has a long history of medical innovation. It introduced the world’s first commercially successful smallpox vaccine in the late nineteenth century and was a pioneer in the mass production of insulin in the 1920s, which revolutionized diabetes care. Over the decades, LLY expanded its portfolio to include significant breakthroughs in psychiatry with the introduction of Prozac in the late twentieth century, as well as major advancements in oncology and immunology. Today, Eli Lilly focuses on discovering, developing, and marketing human healthcare products that treat diabetes, obesity, cancer, and autoimmune conditions. The company invests heavily in research and development to address some of the world's most significant medical challenges, positioning itself at the forefront of the global biopharmaceutical industry.
