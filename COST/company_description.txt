Costco Wholesale Corporation, trading under the ticker COST, is a prominent global membership-only warehouse club operator. Founded in Seattle in 1983 by James Sinegal and Jeffrey Brotman, the company pioneered the warehouse club concept with the opening of its first location. Costco operates a chain of large-format membership warehouses that offer high-quality, nationally branded, and selected private-label goods, notably under the Kirkland Signature brand, at significantly lower prices than traditional wholesalers or retailers. 

The company's core business model relies on generating high-volume sales through limited product assortments, efficient inventory management, and minimal operational overhead. Rather than relying on traditional retail markups, Costco derives a substantial portion of its profitability from annual membership fees paid by its loyal consumer and business customer base. Over the decades, Costco has expanded internationally, establishing a significant presence in North America, Asia, Europe, and Australia, and evolving into one of the largest retailers in the world while maintaining a reputation for strong employee relations and customer satisfaction.
