Costco Wholesale Corporation, trading under the ticker COST, operates an international chain of membership-only warehouse clubs that offer a wide selection of quality, brand-name, and select private-label merchandise at low prices. The company's business model focuses on driving high-volume sales through limited stock keeping units, passing operational savings directly to its members via discounted pricing, and generating the majority of its profits from annual membership fees. 

The enterprise traces its origins to July 1976 when Sol Price opened the first FedMart-like Price Club warehouse in San Diego, California. Building upon this pioneering warehouse club concept, founders Jeffrey Brotman and James Sinegal opened the first Costco location in Seattle, Washington, in September 1983. A decade later, in 1993, Costco and Price Club merged under the name PriceCostco before officially rebranding as Costco Wholesale Corporation in 1997. Today, headquartered in Issaquah, Washington, the company has grown into one of the largest retailers globally, operating hundreds of warehouses across the United States, Canada, Mexico, and numerous international markets, alongside a rapidly expanding digital e-commerce platform.
