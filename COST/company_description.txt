Costco Wholesale Corporation, trading under the ticker COST, operates an international chain of membership-only big-box retail warehouse clubs. The company’s business model focuses on offering a curated selection of nationally branded and private-label products, notably under its Kirkland Signature brand, at significantly lower markups than traditional wholesalers or retailers. Costco generates a substantial portion of its operating income from membership fees, which allows the company to price its merchandise near cost to drive high-volume sales. Founded by James Sinegal and Jeffrey Brotman, the company opened its first warehouse in Seattle, Washington, in 1983. It subsequently expanded rapidly, merging with Price Club in 1993 to form PriceCostco before officially adopting the Costco Wholesale name in 1997. Today, Costco is one of the largest retailers in the world, operating hundreds of locations across the United States and internationally, supported by an increasingly robust e-commerce platform and business delivery services.
