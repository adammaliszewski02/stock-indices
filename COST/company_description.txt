Costco Wholesale Corporation (COST) is a prominent American multinational corporation that operates a chain of membership-only big-box retail stores. Founded in Seattle, Washington, in 1983 by James Sinegal and Jeffrey Brotman, the company pioneered the warehouse club concept, offering members deeply discounted bulk goods, ranging from groceries and electronics to apparel and gasoline. Costco differentiates itself through its unique business model, which generates the vast majority of its profit from annual membership fees rather than traditional retail markups, allowing the company to sell merchandise at near-wholesale prices. Over the decades, Costco has expanded globally, establishing hundreds of locations across the United States, Canada, Mexico, the United Kingdom, Asia, and Australia. In addition to name-brand products, the company is widely recognized for its highly successful private-label brand, Kirkland Signature, introduced in 1995. Today, Costco remains one of the largest retailers in the world, consistently praised for its employee-centric workplace culture, high customer retention rates, and steady financial performance.
