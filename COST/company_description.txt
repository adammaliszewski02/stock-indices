Costco Wholesale Corporation operates a global network of membership-based warehouse clubs, dedicated to offering members high-quality goods at significantly lower prices than traditional wholesale or retail outlets. The company's business model relies on high-volume sales, minimal markups, and substantial annual membership fees, which account for a significant portion of its profitability. Costco’s product offerings span a vast array of categories, including dry groceries, fresh foods, appliances, electronics, apparel, and in-house gasoline stations, alongside proprietary private-label brands such as Kirkland Signature. 

The company's origins trace back to the opening of its first warehouse in Seattle, Washington, in 1983 under the leadership of founders James Sinegal and Jeffrey Brotman. In 1993, Costco merged with Price Club, the pioneer of the membership warehouse concept founded by Sol Price in San Diego in 1976. This merger united the two largest warehouse club operators at the time, establishing the foundation for Costco's current international scale. Headquartered in Issaquah, Washington, Costco has since expanded operations globally, serving hundreds of millions of cardholders across North America, Asia, Europe, and Australia, while maintaining a reputation for strong employee relations and operational efficiency.
