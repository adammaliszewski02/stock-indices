Advanced Micro Devices, Inc. (AMD) is a prominent multinational semiconductor company that develops high-performance computing, graphics, and visualization technologies. Founded in 1969 by Jerry Sanders and several colleagues, the company initially focused on logic chips before entering the microprocessor market in the 1970s. AMD is widely recognized for creating the Am9080 microprocessor and later introducing the groundbreaking x86-compatible Athlon and Ryzen processors, which challenged industry monopolies. Over the decades, the company has evolved from a traditional chipmaker into a fabless semiconductor leader through strategic acquisitions, notably the purchases of graphics pioneer ATI Technologies in 2006 and data center solutions provider Xilinx in 2022. Today, AMD operates through Data Center, Client, Gaming, and Embedded segments. It designs advanced central processing units, graphics processing units, and adaptive system-on-chip products that power personal computers, gaming consoles, cloud computing infrastructure, and supercomputers globally.
