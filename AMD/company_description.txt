Advanced Micro Devices, Inc. (AMD) is a prominent multinational semiconductor company that develops high-performance computing, graphics, and visualization technologies. Founded in 1969 by Jerry Sanders and a group of colleagues, the company initially focused on logic chips before entering the memory chip market in the 1970s and launching its first proprietary processor, the Am9080, in 1975. Over the subsequent decades, AMD established itself as a primary competitor to Intel in the personal computer microprocessor market through key innovations such as the AMD Athlon processor line. 

In the modern era, AMD designs cutting-edge microprocessors, motherboards, embedded processors, and graphics processors for the server, workstation, personal computer, and gaming markets. The company's core business revolves around its Ryzen desktop and mobile processors, EPYC server processors, and Radeon graphics cards, alongside custom silicon solutions powering modern gaming consoles. AMD significantly expanded its technological capabilities and market reach through the strategic acquisitions of programmable logic leader Xilinx in 2022 and server manufacturer ZT Systems in 2024. Today, AMD is a critical infrastructure provider for global cloud computing, artificial intelligence, and scientific research.
