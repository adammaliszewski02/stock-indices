Advanced Micro Devices, Inc. (AMD) is a global semiconductor company that specializes in the design and development of high-performance computing, graphics, and visualization technologies. Founded in 1969 by Jerry Sanders and a group of former Fairchild Semiconductor colleagues, the company initially focused on second-sourcing integrated circuits before transitioning into a leading innovator of x86 microprocessors. Today, AMD provides a comprehensive portfolio of products, including central processing units (CPUs), graphics processing units (GPUs), and adaptive system-on-chip (SoC) solutions. These technologies power a diverse range of applications, spanning data centers, personal computing, gaming consoles, and embedded systems. Following its 2022 acquisition of Xilinx, AMD significantly expanded its footprint in the adaptive computing market, further strengthening its competitive position against industry peers. Headquartered in Santa Clara, California, the company continues to drive advancements in artificial intelligence and high-performance computing, serving enterprise, cloud, and consumer markets worldwide.
