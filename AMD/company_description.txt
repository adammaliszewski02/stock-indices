Advanced Micro Devices, Inc. (AMD) is a prominent multinational semiconductor company that develops computer processors and related technologies for business and consumer markets. Founded in 1969 by Jerry Sanders and a group of colleagues, the company initially focused on logic chips before entering the memory chip market in the 1970s and launching its first proprietary processor, the Am9080, in 1975. Over subsequent decades, AMD established itself as a primary competitor to Intel in the personal computer processor industry through key innovations such as the x86-compatible K-series and the groundbreaking AMD64 architecture. 

Today, AMD’s core business centers on designing high-performance central processing units (CPUs) and graphics processing units (GPUs). The company serves a diverse clientele across the personal computing, gaming, and data center sectors. Major product lines include Ryzen processors for desktop and mobile computers, Radeon graphics cards, and EPYC server processors. Furthermore, AMD significantly expanded its footprint in high-performance computing and adaptive accelerators through its strategic acquisition of Xilinx in 2022, positioning itself as a comprehensive provider of advanced computing infrastructure for modern cloud, enterprise, and artificial intelligence workloads.
