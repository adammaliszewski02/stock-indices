Advanced Micro Devices, Inc. (AMD) is a global semiconductor company that specializes in the design and development of high-performance computing, graphics, and visualization technologies. Founded in 1969 by Jerry Sanders and a group of former Fairchild Semiconductor colleagues, the company initially focused on second-sourcing logic chips before establishing its own proprietary architecture. Today, AMD is a critical player in the technology sector, providing central processing units (CPUs) and graphics processing units (GPUs) for personal computers, gaming consoles, and data centers. Following its 2022 acquisition of Xilinx, the company significantly expanded its portfolio to include adaptive and embedded computing solutions. AMD operates as a fabless semiconductor firm, partnering with external foundries to manufacture its advanced silicon products. Its current business strategy emphasizes leadership in high-performance computing and artificial intelligence, positioning the company as a primary competitor to industry incumbents in both the consumer and enterprise markets.
