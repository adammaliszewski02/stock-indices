Advanced Micro Devices, Inc. (AMD) is a global semiconductor company specializing in the design of high-performance computing and graphics technologies. Operating as a fabless chipmaker, AMD serves diverse sectors, including cloud infrastructure, artificial intelligence, personal computing, and gaming. Its core product portfolio features Ryzen microprocessors, Radeon graphics cards, and EPYC server processors, alongside custom silicon for gaming consoles like the PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X.

Founded in 1969 by Jerry Sanders and former colleagues from Fairchild Semiconductor, AMD began as a manufacturer of logic chips before emerging as Intel's primary competitor in the x86 microprocessor market. A pivotal strategic shift occurred in 2009 when AMD spun off its manufacturing operations to create GlobalFoundries, pivoting fully to a design-focused business model. 

Under the leadership of CEO Lisa Su, appointed in 2014, AMD executed a notable financial and technological turnaround, propelled by its high-efficiency Zen CPU architecture. The company further diversified its technological capabilities through the 2022 acquisition of Xilinx, integrating field-programmable gate arrays (FPGAs) and adaptive computing solutions into its hardware ecosystem to solidify its footprint in the enterprise data center and generative AI markets.
