Advanced Micro Devices, Inc. (AMD) is a multinational semiconductor company that develops computer processors and related technologies for business and consumer markets. Founded in 1969 by Jerry Sanders and several colleagues, the company initially focused on logic semiconductors before entering the memory chip market in 1975 and launching its first proprietary processor, the Am9080, in 1976. 

Today, AMD is a primary global designer of high-performance microprocessors, graphics processing units (GPUs), and adaptive computing solutions. The company operates through primary segments catering to data center, client, gaming, and embedded computing markets. A major milestone in its modern history was the 2006 acquisition of graphics giant ATI Technologies, which significantly enhanced its capabilities in GPU design. Furthermore, AMD expanded its footprint in enterprise and artificial intelligence infrastructure through its 2022 acquisition of Xilinx, a leader in adaptive computing. AMD fabless operational model relies on advanced third-party semiconductor manufacturing partnerships, most notably with TSMC, to produce advanced silicon for personal computers, servers, gaming consoles, and cloud infrastructure worldwide.
