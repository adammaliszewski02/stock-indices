The Home Depot, traded under the ticker HD, is the world's largest home improvement specialty retailer. Founded in 1978 by Bernie Marcus, Arthur Blank, Ron Brill, and Pat Farrah in Atlanta, Georgia, the company pioneered the warehouse-style home improvement concept, offering a vast assortment of building materials, home improvement supplies, lawn and garden products, and décor. Serving both do-it-yourself customers and professional contractors, The Home Depot operates a massive network of retail stores across the United States, Canada, and Mexico, complemented by a robust digital e-commerce platform. Over the decades, the company has driven significant industry growth through strategic acquisitions, supply chain expansion, and a commitment to customer service, solidifying its position as a dominant component of the American retail landscape and a constituent of the Dow Jones Industrial Average.
