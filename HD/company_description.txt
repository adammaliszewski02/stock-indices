The Home Depot, traded under the ticker HD, is the world's largest home improvement retailer. Founded in 1978 by Bernie Marcus, Arthur Blank, Ron Brill, and Pat Farrah in Atlanta, Georgia, the company revolutionized the hardware sector by pioneering the warehouse-style big-box store concept, offering a vast assortment of building materials, home improvement products, lawn and garden supplies, and related services. 

Serving a diverse customer base that includes do-it-yourself homeowners, professional contractors, and maintenance professionals, The Home Depot operates thousands of stores across the United States, Canada, and Mexico. Over the decades, the company has significantly expanded its business model beyond traditional brick-and-mortar retail by developing a robust interconnected e-commerce platform and acquiring major supply chain networks to better serve the professional contractor market. As a component of the Dow Jones Industrial Average and the S&P 500, The Home Depot remains a cornerstone of the global retail and housing sectors, driven by a continuous commitment to customer service, product innovation, and operational efficiency.
