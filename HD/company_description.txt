The Home Depot, traded under the ticker HD, is the world's largest home improvement specialty retailer. Founded in 1978 by Bernard Marcus, Arthur Blank, Ron Brill, and Pat Farrah in Atlanta, Georgia, the company experienced rapid early growth, going public in 1981. The business operates a vast chain of warehouse-format stores across the United States, Canada, and Mexico. These locations stock an extensive assortment of building materials, home improvement supplies, lawn and garden products, and decor, alongside a wide array of services that include home installation and tool rental. The Home Depot primarily serves two distinct customer segments, catering to do-it-yourself retail consumers as well as professional contractors, builders, and tradespeople. Through strategic investments in its supply chain and digital platforms, the company has successfully integrated its brick-and-mortar locations with robust e-commerce capabilities to support a seamless omnichannel retail experience.
