The Home Depot, traded under the stock ticker HD, is the world's largest home improvement retailer. Founded in 1978 by Bernie Marcus, Arthur Blank, Ron Brill, and Pat Farrah in Atlanta, Georgia, the company pioneered the warehouse-style home improvement superstore concept. 

The enterprise operates a vast network of retail stores across the United States, Canada, and Mexico, offering a comprehensive assortment of building materials, home improvement supplies, lawn and garden products, and decor. In addition to serving do-it-yourself retail customers, The Home Depot has strategically expanded its market reach to cater heavily to professional contractors, tradespeople, and maintenance professionals through specialized services and commercial account capabilities. Driven by both brick-and-mortar locations and an advanced digital e-commerce platform, the company generates revenue by providing products and installation services designed to facilitate residential and commercial construction, renovation, and maintenance projects.
