The Home Depot, traded under the ticker HD, is the world's largest home improvement specialty retailer. Founded in 1978 by Bernie Marcus, Arthur Blank, Ron Brill, and Pat Farrah in Atlanta, Georgia, the company pioneered the warehouse-style home improvement concept, offering a vast assortment of building materials, home improvement supplies, lawn and garden products, and décor. 

The business model serves two primary customer segments: do-it-yourself consumers and professional contractors, supported by an extensive network of retail stores across the United States, Canada, and Mexico, alongside a robust digital commerce platform. Over the decades, The Home Depot has driven significant industry growth through strategic acquisitions, supply chain investments, and technological integration, cementing its position as a dominant force in the retail sector and a component of the Dow Jones Industrial Average.
