The Home Depot, traded under the stock ticker HD, is the world's largest home improvement specialty retailer. The company operates a massive chain of warehouse-style stores offering a comprehensive array of building materials, home improvement supplies, lawn and garden products, and décor items, complemented by an extensive suite of services that includes home installation and tool rental. Headquartered in Atlanta, Georgia, the company was founded in 1978 by Bernie Marcus, Arthur Blank, Ron Brill, and Pat Farrah, with financial backing from investment banker Ken Langone. Their revolutionary vision was to build massive warehouse-format stores that offered deep product variety at lower prices than traditional hardware shops. The company completed its initial public offering in 1981 and has since grown into a dominant component of the American retail sector, serving both do-it-yourself consumers and professional contractors through its physical footprint and advanced digital platforms.
