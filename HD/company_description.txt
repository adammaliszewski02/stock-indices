The Home Depot, traded under the stock ticker HD, is the world's largest home improvement specialty retailer. Founded in 1978 in Atlanta, Georgia, by Bernard Marcus, Arthur Blank, Ron Brill, and Pat Farrah, the company pioneered the warehouse-style home improvement superstore concept. 

The enterprise operates a vast network of stores across the United States, Canada, and Mexico, offering a comprehensive array of building materials, home improvement supplies, lawn and garden products, and decor. Alongside its extensive retail footprint catering to do-it-yourself customers, The Home Depot has significantly expanded its capabilities to serve professional contractors, maintenance specialists, and trade professionals through dedicated supply chain networks, digital platforms, and strategic acquisitions.
