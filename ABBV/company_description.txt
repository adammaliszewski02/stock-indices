AbbVie Inc., trading under the ticker ABBV, is a leading global biopharmaceutical company dedicated to discovering, developing, and delivering advanced therapies that address some of the world's most complex and serious health conditions. Headquartered in North Chicago, Illinois, the enterprise officially commenced operations in January 2013 as a spin-off from Abbott Laboratories, inheriting a rich legacy of pharmaceutical innovation dating back to Abbott's founding in 1888. AbbVie primarily focuses its extensive research and commercialization efforts on immunology, oncology, neuroscience, eye care, and virology. Its flagship product, Humira, historically drove the company's unprecedented revenue growth and became one of the best-selling pharmaceutical drugs globally. Following the strategic 2020 acquisition of Allergan, AbbVie further diversified its robust portfolio to include world-renowned aesthetics and gastroenterology therapeutics, notably Botox. Today, AbbVie sustains its industry leadership by leveraging cutting-edge science and a dynamic pipeline to transform standards of care for patients worldwide.
