AbbVie Inc., trading under the ticker ABBV, is a leading global biopharmaceutical company dedicated to developing and delivering advanced therapies to address some of the world's most complex and serious health conditions. Headquartered in North Chicago, Illinois, the company was established in January 2013 following its separation from Abbott Laboratories as an independent research-based pharmaceutical enterprise. AbbVie’s core therapeutic focus areas include immunology, oncology, neuroscience, eye care, and virology. Its historical foundation rests significantly on the blockbuster drug Humira (adalimumab), a monoclonal antibody that became one of the best-selling pharmaceutical products globally for treating various autoimmune diseases. Building upon this legacy, AbbVie expanded its portfolio through major strategic acquisitions, most notably the 2020 purchase of Allergan, which integrated well-known aesthetics and therapeutic brands such as Botox into the enterprise. Today, AbbVie continues to drive medical innovation through a robust pipeline of targeted therapies and biologics, operating commercial facilities and research centers worldwide to improve patient outcomes and address critical unmet medical needs.
