AbbVie Inc., trading under the stock ticker ABBV, is a prominent global biopharmaceutical company dedicated to developing and delivering advanced therapies that address some of the world's most complex and serious health conditions. The enterprise focuses its research and commercialization efforts primarily across key therapeutic areas, including immunology, oncology, neuroscience, eye care, and aesthetics. 

AbbVie was established in January 2013 as an independent spin-off from Abbott Laboratories, a corporate restructuring designed to separate Abbott’s research-based pharmaceutical business from its diversified medical devices, diagnostics, and nutritional products. Following its inception, the company achieved significant commercial success driven largely by Humira, an immunosuppressive drug that became one of the best-selling pharmaceutical products in history. To diversify its revenue base and mitigate the eventual loss of exclusivity for its flagship product, AbbVie executed a major strategic expansion, most notably acquiring Allergan in May 2020. This landmark acquisition integrated leading aesthetics and therapeutic brands, such as Botox, into the corporate portfolio. Today, AbbVie continues to advance a robust pipeline of innovative medicines, operating globally to improve patient outcomes while maintaining its position as a leader in the healthcare sector.
