AbbVie Inc., trading under the ticker ABBV, is a leading global biopharmaceutical company dedicated to discovering, developing, and delivering advanced therapies for some of the world's most complex and serious medical conditions. Headquartered in North Chicago, Illinois, the company operates primarily in immunology, oncology, neuroscience, eye care, and virology. AbbVie was established in January 2013 as a spin-off from Abbott Laboratories, inheriting a rich legacy of pharmaceutical innovation dating back to Abbott's founding in 1888. The company achieved global prominence largely through the commercial success of Humira, one of the world's best-selling prescription medications, which treats various autoimmune diseases. Following the loss of exclusivity for its flagship product, AbbVie successfully diversified its portfolio through strategic acquisitions, most notably the 2020 acquisition of Allergan, which integrated blockbuster treatments like Botox into its offerings. Today, AbbVie continues to invest heavily in research and development to expand its pipeline of targeted biologics and advanced therapeutics, employing tens of thousands of professionals globally to improve patient health outcomes.
