AbbVie Inc. (ticker: ABBV) is a prominent, research-based global biopharmaceutical company dedicated to developing advanced therapies for some of the world's most complex and serious medical conditions. The company's diverse portfolio addresses critical therapeutic areas, including immunology, oncology, neuroscience, eye care, virology, and gastroenterology. 

AbbVie was established in January 2013 following its separation as an independent spin-off from Abbott Laboratories, a corporate legacy dating back to 1888 when it was founded by Chicago physician Wallace C. Abbott. Despite its recent structural independence, AbbVie inherited a rich history of pharmaceutical innovation, most notably anchored by its flagship biologic therapy, Humira, which became one of the best-selling pharmaceutical products globally. In addition to its blockbuster immunology footprint, AbbVie expanded its strategic capabilities significantly through the major acquisition of Allergan in 2020, which broadened its portfolio to include market-leading aesthetics and therapeutic products like Botox. Headquartered in North Chicago, Illinois, AbbVie continues to invest heavily in research and development to sustain its robust pipeline, addressing unmet medical needs and delivering long-term value to patients and shareholders worldwide.
