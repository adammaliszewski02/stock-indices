AbbVie Inc., trading under the ticker ABBV, is a leading global biopharmaceutical company dedicated to discovering, developing, and delivering advanced therapies that address some of the world's most complex and serious health conditions. Headquartered in North Chicago, Illinois, the enterprise officially commenced operations in January 2013 as a spin-off from Abbott Laboratories, a corporate separation designed to allow the newly formed entity to focus entirely on proprietary pharmaceutical research and commercialization. AbbVie’s extensive therapeutic portfolio spans key areas including immunology, oncology, neuroscience, eye care, and aesthetics. Its flagship product, Humira, historically achieved status as one of the world's best-selling prescription medications, driving significant corporate growth and enabling substantial reinvestment into pipeline diversification. To sustain its market leadership beyond the patent expiration of its foundational therapies, the company has strategically expanded through major acquisitions, most notably the 2020 purchase of Allergan. Today, AbbVie continues to leverage cutting-edge science and robust clinical development capabilities to address unmet medical needs globally, reinforcing its position as a cornerstone of the modern healthcare sector.
