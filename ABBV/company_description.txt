AbbVie Inc. (ABBV) is a global, research-based biopharmaceutical company focused on developing advanced therapies for some of the world’s most complex and serious health conditions. The company maintains a robust portfolio across key therapeutic areas, including immunology, oncology, neuroscience, eye care, and aesthetics. Its flagship product, Humira, has historically been one of the world’s best-selling medications, significantly shaping the company’s financial trajectory.

AbbVie was established on January 1, 2013, following its separation from Abbott Laboratories. This strategic spin-off was designed to allow the new entity to focus exclusively on research-driven pharmaceutical development while Abbott retained its diversified medical device and diagnostics businesses. Since its inception, AbbVie has expanded its reach through significant strategic acquisitions, most notably the 2020 purchase of Allergan, which integrated the Botox franchise into its operations. Headquartered in North Chicago, Illinois, AbbVie continues to prioritize innovation in high-growth markets, leveraging its extensive pipeline to address unmet medical needs and drive long-term shareholder value.
