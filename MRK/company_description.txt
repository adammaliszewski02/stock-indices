Merck & Co., Inc., known as MSD outside the United States and Canada, is a leading global biopharmaceutical company headquartered in Rahway, New Jersey. The company focuses on discovering, developing, and providing innovative medicines and vaccines to address significant unmet medical needs. Its diverse portfolio spans oncology, infectious diseases, cardiovascular health, and animal health. Merck is perhaps best known for its breakthrough work in immunotherapy, particularly with Keytruda, which has become a cornerstone in cancer treatment.

The company’s roots trace back to 1668, when Friedrich Jacob Merck established a pharmacy in Darmstadt, Germany. The American subsidiary was established in 1891 and eventually became an independent entity following World War I. Throughout the 20th century, Merck solidified its reputation for scientific excellence, contributing to major medical advancements such as the development of streptomycin for tuberculosis and the creation of the first mumps vaccine. Today, Merck continues to leverage its extensive research and development capabilities to advance global health, maintaining a strong commitment to scientific innovation and the delivery of life-saving therapies to patients worldwide.
