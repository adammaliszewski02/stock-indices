MRK refers to Merck & Co., Inc., a leading global healthcare company headquartered in Rahway, New Jersey. Traded on the New York Stock Exchange, the company traces its origins to a German pharmacy established by Friedrich Jacob Merck in Darmstadt in 1668, with its American branch founded in 1891 by George Merck. Today, Merck operates as a research-intensive biopharmaceutical enterprise dedicated to discovering, developing, and delivering innovative medicines, vaccines, and biologic therapies. Its core business focuses on addressing significant global health challenges, encompassing major therapeutic areas such as oncology, infectious diseases, cardiovascular health, and immunology. A cornerstone of its commercial success is Keytruda, a breakthrough immunotherapy widely used in cancer treatment. Additionally, Merck maintains a robust animal health division that provides veterinarians, farmers, and pet owners with a comprehensive suite of pharmaceuticals, vaccines, and digital identification and monitoring technology. Through continuous investment in research and development, Merck remains committed to advancing global health security and improving patient outcomes worldwide.
