Merck & Co., Inc., trading under the ticker MRK on the New York Stock Exchange, is a leading global healthcare company that delivers innovative health solutions through its prescription medicines, vaccines, biologic therapies, and animal health products. Tracing its origins to 1891 as the United States subsidiary of the historic German firm E. Merck, the American enterprise was seized during World War I and subsequently established as an independent company in 1917. Over the decades, Merck has solidified its position as a research-driven pharmaceutical powerhouse, significantly advancing global health through breakthroughs in penicillin production, streptomycin, and modern oncology, most notably with the development of its flagship cancer immunotherapy, Keytruda. Operating across more than 140 countries, the company focuses its research and development efforts on addressing critical unmet medical needs, including oncology, infectious diseases, cardiovascular disorders, and immunology, while continuing to provide essential veterinary pharmaceuticals and vaccines worldwide.
