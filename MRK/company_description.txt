MRK refers to Merck & Co., Inc., a leading global healthcare company headquartered in Rahway, New Jersey. Traded on the New York Stock Exchange, the company traces its lineage back to 1891 as the United States subsidiary of the historic German firm Merck. Following expropriation of its assets during World War I, it became an independent American enterprise and merged with Sharp & Dohme in 1953. Today, Merck operates as a research-intensive biopharmaceutical enterprise dedicated to discovering, developing, and delivering innovative medicines, vaccines, and animal health products. Its core business focuses on oncology, vaccines, infectious diseases, and cardiovascular health. The company is widely recognized for its portfolio of breakthrough therapies, most notably Keytruda, an immuno-oncology treatment that has become one of the world's top-selling pharmaceutical products. In addition to human health, Merck maintains a robust animal health division that provides veterinarians and farmers with pharmaceuticals, vaccines, and digital identification technology.
