MRK refers to Merck & Co., Inc., a leading global healthcare company headquartered in Rahway, New Jersey. Traded on the New York Stock Exchange, the company traces its lineage back to 1891 as the United States subsidiary of the historic German firm Merck. Following expropriation of its assets during World War I, it became an independent American enterprise and merged with Sharp & Dohme in 1953. Today, Merck operates as a research-intensive biopharmaceutical enterprise dedicated to discovering, developing, and delivering innovative medicines, vaccines, and animal health products. Its core business focuses on oncology, vaccines, hospital acute care, and immunology. A primary driver of its modern commercial success is Keytruda, a breakthrough immunotherapy widely used in cancer treatment. The company also maintains a significant footprint in veterinary pharmaceuticals through its Merck Animal Health division.
