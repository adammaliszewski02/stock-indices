MRK refers to Merck & Co., Inc., a leading global healthcare company headquartered in Rahway, New Jersey. Traded on the New York Stock Exchange, the company traces its lineage back to 1891 as the United States subsidiary of the historic German firm Merck. Following expropriation during World War I, it became an independent American enterprise in 1917 and merged with Sharp & Dohme in 1953. 

Merck operates primarily in the biopharmaceutical sector, discovering, developing, and manufacturing innovative prescription medicines, vaccines, biologic therapies, and animal health products. The company is widely recognized for its robust oncology portfolio, led by the blockbuster immunotherapy drug Keytruda, as well as essential vaccines for preventable infectious diseases. Additionally, its animal health division provides pharmaceuticals, vaccines, and health management software for livestock and companion animals. Merck's overarching corporate mission is focused on applying scientific innovation to save and improve lives worldwide, underpinned by sustained investment in global research and development.
