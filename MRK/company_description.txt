MRK refers to Merck & Co., Inc., a leading global healthcare company headquartered in Rahway, New Jersey. Traded on the New York Stock Exchange under the ticker MRK, the company traces its origins back to 1891 as the American subsidiary of the German firm Merck KGaA, though it was seized and established as an independent American enterprise during World War I. Merck focuses on the discovery, development, and delivery of innovative prescription medicines, vaccines, biologic therapies, and animal health products. Its core business addresses critical global health needs, including oncology, infectious diseases, cardiovascular disorders, and immunology. A cornerstone of its commercial success and scientific impact is Keytruda, a breakthrough immunotherapy widely used in cancer treatment. Additionally, the company operates a robust animal health division that provides pharmaceuticals, vaccines, and health monitoring technology for livestock and companion animals. Through continuous investment in research and development, Merck maintains its position as a vital pillar of the global pharmaceutical industry, dedicated to improving health outcomes and saving lives worldwide.
