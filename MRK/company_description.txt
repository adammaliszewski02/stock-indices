Merck & Co., Inc., known as MSD outside the United States and Canada, is a leading global biopharmaceutical company headquartered in Rahway, New Jersey. The company focuses on discovering, developing, and providing innovative prescription medicines, vaccines, and animal health products. Its diverse portfolio addresses critical medical challenges, including oncology, infectious diseases, cardiovascular health, and immunology.

The company traces its origins to 1668, when Friedrich Jacob Merck established a pharmacy in Darmstadt, Germany. The American subsidiary was founded in 1891 and became an independent entity following World War I. Throughout its history, Merck has been a pioneer in medical research, notably contributing to the development of streptomycin, the first effective treatment for tuberculosis, and the creation of vaccines for mumps, rubella, and measles. Today, Merck remains a cornerstone of the healthcare industry, consistently investing heavily in research and development to advance global health outcomes and improve the quality of life for patients worldwide.
