AMZN is the stock ticker for Amazon.com, Inc., a multinational technology company focused on e-commerce, cloud computing, online advertising, digital streaming, and artificial intelligence. Founded by Jeff Bezos in July 1994 as an online bookstore operating out of a garage in Bellevue, Washington, the company initially expanded into the sale of various media before diversifying into a vast array of consumer goods, digital services, and enterprise solutions. 

Over the decades, Amazon achieved massive global scale, driven in part by the 2006 launch of Amazon Web Services, which established the company as a dominant provider of cloud infrastructure services. It also revolutionized logistics through major investments in supply chain management and automated fulfillment networks. Furthermore, the company expanded its footprint into consumer electronics with the Kindle and Echo product lines, digital entertainment through Prime Video and Amazon Studios, and physical retail via the acquisition of Whole Foods Market in 2017. Today, Amazon ranks among the world's most valuable publicly traded corporations and remains a foundational pillar of the modern digital economy.
