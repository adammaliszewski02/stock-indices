Amazon.com, Inc., identified by the ticker AMZN, is a multinational technology conglomerate focusing on e-commerce, cloud computing, online advertising, digital streaming, and artificial intelligence. Founded by Jeff Bezos in July 1994 as an online bookstore operating out of a garage in Bellevue, Washington, the company rapidly expanded its product offerings to include media, electronics, apparel, and eventually a vast array of consumer goods. 

A pivotal milestone in the company's evolution was the 2006 launch of Amazon Web Services, which pioneered modern cloud computing infrastructure and became a major driver of corporate profitability. Furthermore, the introduction of Amazon Prime in 2005 revolutionized retail logistics by offering expedited shipping, fostering immense customer loyalty. Today, Amazon operates as one of the world's most valuable and influential economic entities, continuously innovating across logistics, hardware, grocery, and entertainment sectors on a global scale.
