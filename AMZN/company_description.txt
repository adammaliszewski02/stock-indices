Amazon.com, Inc. (AMZN) is a multinational technology company focusing on e-commerce, cloud computing, online advertising, digital streaming, and artificial intelligence. Founded by Jeff Bezos in a Seattle garage in July 1994 as an online bookstore, the company steadily expanded its product offerings to become the world's largest online retailer. A significant milestone in its history was the 2006 launch of Amazon Web Services (AWS), which pioneered cloud computing and currently serves as a primary driver of the company’s profitability. Additionally, Amazon revolutionized logistics and consumer electronics with innovations like Amazon Prime, the Kindle e-reader, and Alexa-powered smart devices. The company also operates major subsidiaries, including the Whole Foods Market supermarket chain and the film studio MGM. Today, Amazon leverages its vast logistics network, proprietary technologies, and global digital infrastructure to maintain a dominant position across multiple consumer and enterprise markets.
