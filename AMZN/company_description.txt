Amazon.com, Inc., identified by the ticker AMZN, is a multinational technology conglomerate focusing on e-commerce, cloud computing, online advertising, digital streaming, and artificial intelligence. Founded by Jeff Bezos in July 1994 as an online bookstore operating out of a garage in Bellevue, Washington, the company expanded rapidly into a diverse range of product categories before launching Amazon Web Services in 2006 to pioneer cloud infrastructure services. Today, Amazon is recognized as one of the world's most valuable brands and largest internet companies by revenue. Its business operations span global retail marketplaces, logistics and fulfillment networks, consumer electronics development including Kindle and Echo devices, grocery retail following the acquisition of Whole Foods Market, and entertainment production through Amazon MGM Studios.
