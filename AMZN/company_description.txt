AMZN is the ticker symbol for Amazon.com, Inc., a multinational technology company that focuses on e-commerce, cloud computing, online advertising, digital streaming, and artificial intelligence. Founded by Jeff Bezos in a garage in Bellevue, Washington, in July 1994, the company initially operated as an online marketplace for books before progressively expanding into a vast array of product categories. This core retail operation evolved into the world's largest online sales enterprise. In 2006, Amazon significantly diversified its business model with the launch of Amazon Web Services, which has grown to become the world’s leading cloud infrastructure provider. Furthermore, the company revolutionized consumer electronics with products like the Kindle e-reader and Echo smart speakers, and it established a dominant presence in entertainment through Amazon Prime Video and Amazon Studios. Today, Amazon is recognized as one of the most influential economic and cultural forces globally, employing over one million people and continuously expanding its footprint into logistics, grocery, healthcare, and advanced technologies.
