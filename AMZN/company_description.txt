Amazon.com, Inc. (AMZN) is a multinational technology company focusing on e-commerce, cloud computing, online advertising, digital streaming, and artificial intelligence. Founded by Jeff Bezos in July 1994 as an online bookstore operating out of a garage in Bellevue, Washington, the company rapidly expanded its product offerings to become the world's largest online retailer. 

In 2006, Amazon revolutionized the technology sector by launching Amazon Web Services (AWS), which pioneered the cloud computing industry and became a primary driver of the company's profitability. Over subsequent decades, Amazon diversified its operations through major strategic acquisitions, including the purchase of Whole Foods Market in 2017, and developed proprietary consumer hardware such as the Kindle e-reader and Echo smart devices powered by the Alexa virtual assistant. Today, Amazon operates globally, maintaining dominant market positions in digital commerce, enterprise cloud infrastructure, and logistics, while continuing to invest heavily in emerging technologies such as generative AI, autonomous vehicles, and entertainment production.
