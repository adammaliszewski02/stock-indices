Amazon.com, Inc., identified by the ticker AMZN, is a multinational technology conglomerate focusing on e-commerce, cloud computing, online advertising, digital streaming, and artificial intelligence. Founded by Jeff Bezos in July 1994 as an online bookstore operating out of a garage in Bellevue, Washington, the company rapidly expanded its product offerings to become the world's largest online retailer. In 2006, Amazon revolutionized the technology sector by launching Amazon Web Services (AWS), which pioneered the cloud computing industry and became a primary driver of the company’s profitability. Over the decades, Amazon diversified its operations through major strategic acquisitions, including Whole Foods Market in 2017 and MGM in 2022, while innovating in consumer electronics with products like the Kindle and Echo. Today, Amazon operates globally, serving millions of consumers, third-party sellers, and enterprise clients through its sophisticated logistics network and technological infrastructure, remaining a dominant force in the global digital economy.
