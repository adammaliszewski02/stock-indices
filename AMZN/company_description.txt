Amazon.com, Inc. is a multinational technology conglomerate that serves as a global leader in e-commerce, cloud computing, digital streaming, and artificial intelligence. Founded by Jeff Bezos in 1994 as an online bookstore operating out of a garage in Bellevue, Washington, the company has evolved into one of the world’s most influential economic forces. Its business model is anchored by its massive online marketplace, which connects third-party sellers with a global consumer base, and Amazon Web Services, the industry-leading cloud infrastructure platform that powers a significant portion of the modern internet. Beyond retail and technology, Amazon has expanded into entertainment through Amazon MGM Studios, consumer electronics with the Kindle and Echo product lines, and logistics through its extensive global fulfillment network. By prioritizing long-term growth and customer-centric innovation, Amazon has fundamentally transformed retail habits and enterprise computing standards, consistently ranking among the most valuable companies in the world.
