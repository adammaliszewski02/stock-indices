The Goldman Sachs Group, Inc., trading under the ticker GS, is a leading global financial institution that delivers a wide range of financial services to a substantial and diversified client base that includes corporations, financial institutions, governments, and individuals. Founded in New York City in 1869 by Marcus Goldman, the firm has evolved from a small commercial paper business into a preeminent global investment banking, securities, and investment management powerhouse. 

The company operates through four core business segments: Investment Banking, Global Markets, Asset Management, and Wealth Management. Through these divisions, Goldman Sachs advises on major corporate mergers, acquisitions, and restructurings, underwrites debt and equity securities, provides market-making and execution services across global financial exchanges, and manages capital for institutional and high-net-worth clients worldwide. Throughout its history, the firm has played a pivotal role in the development of global capital markets, transitioning from a private partnership to a publicly traded corporation in 1999.
