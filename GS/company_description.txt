The Goldman Sachs Group, Inc., trading under the ticker GS, is a leading global financial institution that delivers a wide range of financial services to a substantial and diversified client base. Founded in New York City in 1869 by Marcus Goldman, the firm began as a small commercial paper business before pioneering the use of commercial paper for entrepreneurs. Over the decades, Goldman Sachs evolved into a premier investment banking powerhouse, famously advising on major corporate mergers, public offerings, and financial restructurings. Today, its core business operations are structured into four primary segments: Investment Banking, Global Markets, Asset Management, and Platform Solutions. The firm serves corporations, financial institutions, governments, and high-net-worth individuals worldwide by providing comprehensive underwriting, securities sales and trading, wealth management, and strategic advisory services. Throughout its history, Goldman Sachs has navigated significant economic transformations, maintaining its prominent standing at the center of global capital markets while continuously adapting its service models to meet evolving financial landscapes.
