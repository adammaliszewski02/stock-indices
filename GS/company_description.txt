The Goldman Sachs Group, Inc., trading under the stock ticker GS, is a leading global financial institution delivering a broad range of financial services to a substantial and diversified client base that includes corporations, financial institutions, governments, and individuals. Established in 1869 by Marcus Goldman in New York City, the firm evolved through key milestones, including the 1906 partnership with Samuel Sachs and its 1999 initial public offering, transitioning from a private partnership to a publicly traded corporation. Today, Goldman Sachs operates across four primary business segments: Global Banking & Markets, Asset & Wealth Management, and Platform Solutions. The firm provides essential services encompassing investment banking, institutional client execution, asset management, and consumer financial platforms. Headquartered at 200 West Street in New York City, Goldman Sachs maintains a prominent presence in major international financial centers, consistently advising on complex corporate transactions and managing significant global capital assets.
