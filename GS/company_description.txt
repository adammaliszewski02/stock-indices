The Goldman Sachs Group, Inc. (ticker: GS) is a leading global investment banking, securities, and investment management firm headquartered in New York City. Founded in 1869 by Marcus Goldman and joined by Samuel Sachs in 1882, the firm pioneered the use of commercial paper for businesses and transitioned from a private partnership to a publicly traded company in 1999. In response to the 2008 financial crisis, it became a bank holding company, significantly broadening its regulatory oversight and access to liquidity.

Today, Goldman Sachs organizes its core activities into three primary segments: Global Banking & Markets, Asset & Wealth Management, and Platform Solutions. The firm provides strategic advisory services for mergers, acquisitions, restructurings, and capital raising, while actively underwriting debt and equity offerings. It serves as a major market maker across fixed income, currencies, commodities, and equities, offering prime brokerage and financing to institutional clients. Furthermore, Goldman Sachs delivers comprehensive investment management, wealth planning, and lending solutions to institutional investors, corporations, and high-net-worth individuals worldwide.
