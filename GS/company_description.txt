The Goldman Sachs Group, Inc., trading under the ticker GS, is a leading global financial institution that delivers a wide range of financial services to a substantial and diversified client base that includes corporations, financial institutions, governments, and individuals. Founded in New York City in 1869 by Marcus Goldman, the firm has evolved from a small commercial paper business into a preeminent global investment banking, securities, and investment management firm. 

Goldman Sachs operates across four primary business segments: Investment Banking, Global Markets, Asset Management, and Wealth Management. Through these divisions, the firm provides advisory services on mergers and acquisitions, underwrites debt and equity offerings, executes trades in financial markets, manages investments for institutional and private clients, and offers comprehensive consumer and commercial banking solutions. Headquartered in New York with offices in major financial hubs worldwide, the company is recognized for its significant influence on global capital markets and its legacy of financial innovation.
