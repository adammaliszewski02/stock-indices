The stock ticker GS refers to The Goldman Sachs Group, Inc., a leading global financial institution founded in New York City in 1869 by Marcus Goldman. The firm delivers a broad range of financial services to a substantial and diversified client base that includes corporations, financial institutions, governments, and high-net-worth individuals. Goldman Sachs operates primarily through four core business segments: Investment Banking, Global Markets, Asset Management, and Wealth Management. Historically recognized as a premier partnership in private investment banking, the firm underwent a significant evolution by becoming a publicly traded corporation through an initial public offering in 1999. Over the decades, Goldman Sachs has expanded its traditional underwriting, mergers and acquisitions advisory, and securities brokerage operations into comprehensive global asset management and consumer-adjacent financial technologies. Today, the company is globally acknowledged as one of the world's most influential systematically important financial institutions, shaping capital markets and economic strategy worldwide.
