The Goldman Sachs Group, Inc., trading under the stock ticker GS, is a leading global financial institution delivering a broad range of financial services across investment banking, securities, asset management, and consumer banking. Founded in 1869 by Marcus Goldman in New York City, the firm evolved from a modest commercial paper business into a preeminent global partnership. It was joined in 1882 by Samuel Sachs, solidifying the foundational name and expanding its capabilities into initial public offerings and foreign exchange. Following decades of growth as a private partnership, Goldman Sachs became a publicly traded company through its initial public offering in May 1999. Today, the firm serves corporations, financial institutions, governments, and high-net-worth individuals worldwide, operating through four core segments: Global Banking and Markets, Asset Management, Wealth Management, and Platform Solutions. Headquartered at 200 West Street in New York City, Goldman Sachs maintains a vital role in global capital markets, combining deep historical roots with continuous innovation in financial advisory, underwriting, and risk management.
