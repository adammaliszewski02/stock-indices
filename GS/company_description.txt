The Goldman Sachs Group, Inc., trading under the ticker GS, is a leading global financial institution that delivers a wide range of financial services to a substantial and diversified client base. Established in 1869 by Marcus Goldman in New York City, the firm has evolved from a small commercial paper business into a preeminent global investment banking, securities, and investment management firm. 

Goldman Sachs operates primarily through four key business segments: Global Banking & Markets, Asset Management, Wealth Management, and Platform Solutions. The firm provides advisory services on mergers and acquisitions, underwrites debt and equity securities, executes trades across global capital markets, and manages assets for institutional and high-net-worth clients. Throughout its history, the company has played a central role in shaping modern global finance, notable for its rigorous analytical culture, pioneering advisory work in major corporate transactions, and its transition into a bank holding company during the 2008 financial crisis. Today, Goldman Sachs leverages its extensive global footprint and deep market expertise to facilitate capital formation and economic growth worldwide.
