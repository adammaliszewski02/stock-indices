Honeywell International Inc. (ticker: HON) is a prominent American multinational conglomerate that invents and manufactures technologies to address critical global challenges surrounding energy, safety, security, productivity, and urbanization. Tracing its roots back to 1906 with the invention of the thermostat by Mark Honeywell, the modern enterprise was formed through the landmark 1999 merger of AlliedSignal and Honeywell Inc., with AlliedSignal assuming the Honeywell name. Operating today through four primary business segments—Aerospace, Building Technologies, Performance Materials and Technologies, and Safety and Productivity Solutions—Honeywell serves a diverse global customer base across aviation, industrial automation, and smart building infrastructure. The company leverages advanced software and industrial automation capabilities to help customers improve operational efficiency, sustainability, and safety. Headquartered in Charlotte, North Carolina, Honeywell remains a fixture of American industrial history and a major component of major stock market indices.
