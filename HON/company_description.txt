Honeywell International Inc. (ticker: HON) is a multinational conglomerate and technology leader that invents and manufactures technologies to address critical challenges linked to global macrotrends, including energy efficiency, clean energy, safety, security, and global urbanization. Operating through four primary segments—Aerospace, Building Technologies, Performance Materials and Technologies, and Safety and Productivity Solutions—the company delivers industry-specific solutions ranging from aircraft engines and cockpit avionics to advanced software, process automation, and connected building infrastructure. 

With roots tracing back to 1906 when inventor Albert Butz patented the damper flapper, which led to the creation of the Honeywell Heating Specialty Company, the modern enterprise was formed through the 1999 merger of AlliedSignal and Honeywell Inc., with AlliedSignal retaining the Honeywell name. Today, Honeywell leverages its rich industrial heritage and proprietary Honeywell Forge enterprise performance management software to drive digital transformation and sustainability across diverse industrial sectors worldwide.
