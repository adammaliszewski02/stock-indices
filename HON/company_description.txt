Honeywell International Inc. (HON) is a diversified technology and manufacturing leader that operates through four primary segments: Aerospace, Building Automation, Industrial Automation, and Energy and Sustainability Solutions. Headquartered in Charlotte, North Carolina, the company provides integrated solutions that enhance productivity, safety, and energy efficiency for customers worldwide. Its portfolio ranges from advanced avionics and aircraft engines to sophisticated building control systems, industrial software, and sustainable technology platforms.

The company’s origins trace back to 1885, when Albert Butz patented the furnace regulator and alarm, eventually leading to the formation of the Honeywell Heating Specialty Co. In 1999, the company underwent a transformative merger with AlliedSignal, which retained the Honeywell name due to its strong brand recognition. Today, Honeywell is a constituent of the Dow Jones Industrial Average and remains a pivotal player in the global industrial sector, consistently evolving its operations to address complex challenges in automation, digitalization, and the global energy transition.
