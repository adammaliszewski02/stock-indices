Honeywell International Inc. (ticker: HON) is a prominent American multinational conglomerate that invents and manufactures technologies to address critical challenges linked to global macrotrends such as safety, security, and energy. Operating primarily through four business segments—Aerospace, Honeywell Building Technologies, Performance Materials and Technologies, and Safety and Productivity Solutions—the company delivers industry-specific solutions ranging from aircraft engines and cockpit avionics to advanced industrial software, building automation, and personal protective equipment. 

The company's origins trace back to 1906 when innovator Mark Honeywell founded the Honeywell Heating Specialty Company to manufacture the thermostat. Decades later, a significant corporate evolution occurred in 1999 through the multi-billion-dollar merger of AlliedSignal Inc. and Honeywell Inc., with the combined entity retaining the Honeywell name while relocating its headquarters to Charlotte, North Carolina. Today, Honeywell leverages its rich industrial heritage alongside cutting-edge digital capabilities, blending physical products with software to drive smart, safe, and sustainable operations for commercial, industrial, and aerospace customers worldwide.
