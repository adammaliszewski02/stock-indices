Honeywell International Inc. (ticker HON) is a prominent American multinational conglomerate operating primarily in the aerospace, building technologies, performance materials, and industrial automation sectors. Founded as Honeywell Inc. in 1906, the enterprise initially manufactured thermostat controls and heating innovations before expanding significantly through technological research and key mergers, most notably its combination with AlliedSignal in 1999. Today, Honeywell delivers advanced software and hardware solutions designed to enhance operational efficiency, safety, and sustainability for commercial, industrial, and residential customers worldwide.
