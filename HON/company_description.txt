Honeywell International Inc. (HON) is a diversified technology and manufacturing leader headquartered in Charlotte, North Carolina. The company operates through four primary business segments: Aerospace, Honeywell Building Technologies, Performance Materials and Technologies, and Safety and Productivity Solutions. Honeywell provides a wide array of products and services, including aircraft engines, avionics, building control systems, advanced materials, and industrial automation software.

The company’s origins date back to 1885, when Albert Butz patented the furnace regulator and alarm, eventually leading to the formation of the Honeywell Heating Specialty Company. Through a series of strategic mergers and acquisitions—most notably the 1999 merger between AlliedSignal and Honeywell Inc.—the organization evolved into the global industrial conglomerate it is today. Currently, Honeywell focuses on integrating physical products with software-based solutions to address complex challenges in energy efficiency, safety, and global infrastructure. By leveraging its extensive industrial heritage and commitment to digital transformation, the company serves a broad customer base across the aviation, defense, manufacturing, and commercial building sectors.
