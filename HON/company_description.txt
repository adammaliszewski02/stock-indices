Honeywell International Inc. (ticker: HON) is a prominent American multinational conglomerate that invents and manufactures technologies to address critical challenges linked to global macrotrends such as safety, security, and energy. Operating primarily through four business segments—Aerospace, Honeywell Building Technologies, Performance Materials and Technologies, and Safety and Productivity Solutions—the company delivers industry-specific solutions ranging from aircraft engines and cockpit avionics to advanced software, industrial automation, and sensing materials. Honeywell’s historical roots trace back to 1906 with the incorporation of the Minneapolis Honeywell Regulator Company, which later merged with AlliedSignal in 1999. Following the merger, the combined entity adopted the Honeywell name and relocated its corporate headquarters to Charlotte, North Carolina. Today, Honeywell is a Fortune 100 company recognized for integrating physical products with advanced software to create smarter, safer, and more sustainable operations for commercial and industrial clients worldwide.
