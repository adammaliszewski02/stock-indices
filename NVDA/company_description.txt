NVIDIA Corporation, traded under the ticker NVDA, is a pioneering multinational technology company that has transformed from a premier graphics chip designer into a foundational pillar of modern artificial intelligence and accelerated computing. Founded in 1993 by Jensen Huang, Chris Malachowsky, and Curtis Priem, the company initially revolutionized the personal computer gaming market with the 1999 invention of the Graphics Processing Unit, or GPU. Recognizing the parallel processing potential of its technology early on, NVIDIA developed the CUDA programming model in 2006, which enabled GPUs to handle complex, non-graphics computations. This strategic foresight positioned the company at the epicenter of the current generative artificial intelligence boom. Today, NVIDIA designs market-leading hardware, software, and networking systems that power deep learning, autonomous vehicles, robotics, and high-performance data centers globally, establishing itself as an indispensable driver of the digital economy.
