NVIDIA Corporation, traded under the ticker NVDA, is a pioneering American multinational technology company renowned for designing and manufacturing graphics processing units (GPUs) for the gaming and professional visualization markets, as well as system-on-a-chip units for the mobile computing and automotive industries. Founded in 1993 by Jensen Huang, Chris Malachowsky, and Curtis Priem, the company revolutionized the personal computer graphics industry in 1999 with the invention of the GeForce 256. Over the subsequent decades, NVIDIA transformed modern computer graphics and laid the foundation for modern artificial intelligence through the development of its parallel processing architectures. Today, the company is a dominant force in the global technology sector, providing the essential accelerated computing hardware and software ecosystems that power high-performance computing, data centers, autonomous vehicles, and generative artificial intelligence applications worldwide.
