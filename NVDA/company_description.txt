NVIDIA Corporation, traded under the ticker NVDA, is a pioneering technology company globally recognized for designing and manufacturing graphics processing units. Founded in 1993 by Jensen Huang, Chris Malachowsky, and Curtis Priem, the company initially revolutionized the personal computer gaming market with the 1999 introduction of the GeForce 256, widely considered the world's first GPU. Over subsequent decades, NVIDIA successfully adapted its parallel processing architecture to address complex computational challenges beyond traditional graphics. Today, the company is a dominant force in artificial intelligence, deep learning, and high-performance computing, providing the hardware and software ecosystems that power data centers, autonomous vehicles, robotics, and cloud-based AI services worldwide.
