NVIDIA Corporation, traded under the ticker NVDA, is a pioneering American multinational technology company renowned as a global leader in artificial intelligence computing and accelerated graphics. Founded in 1993 by Jensen Huang, Chris Malachowsky, and Curtis Priem, the company initially revolutionized the personal computer gaming industry with the invention of the Graphics Processing Unit in 1999. Over subsequent decades, NVIDIA strategically expanded its architecture into a parallel processing powerhouse, laying the foundational hardware and software ecosystem for modern deep learning, high-performance computing, and autonomous vehicles. Today, the company designs state-of-the-art semiconductor chips, data center infrastructure, and comprehensive software solutions such as CUDA, empowering enterprises, researchers, and cloud providers worldwide to drive the generative AI revolution and advance complex scientific discoveries.
