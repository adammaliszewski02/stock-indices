NVIDIA Corporation, trading under the ticker NVDA, is a global leader in accelerated computing and artificial intelligence. Founded in 1993 by Jensen Huang, Chris Malachowsky, and Curtis Priem, the company initially revolutionized PC gaming by inventing the graphics processing unit (GPU) in 1999. In 2006, NVIDIA launched CUDA, a parallel computing platform that enabled researchers to utilize GPUs for complex general-purpose computing, which ultimately catalyzed the modern artificial intelligence revolution. 

Today, NVIDIA operates as a full-stack computing infrastructure provider, designing high-performance GPUs, central processing units, networking hardware, and specialized software. While maintaining a strong presence in the gaming and professional visualization markets, the core driver of NVIDIA’s business is its data center segment. The company powers the vast majority of global AI workloads, supplying the specialized hardware and software ecosystems required to train and deploy advanced large language models, cloud computing services, and autonomous systems. Headquartered in Santa Clara, California, NVIDIA also expands its reach into robotics, edge computing, and automotive technologies.
