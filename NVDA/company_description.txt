NVIDIA Corporation, traded under the ticker NVDA, is a pioneering American multinational technology company specializing in accelerated computing. Founded in 1993 by Jensen Huang, Chris Malachowsky, and Curtis Priem, the company initially revolutionized the personal computer gaming industry by inventing the Graphics Processing Unit (GPU) in 1999. Over the subsequent decades, NVIDIA successfully expanded its technological scope beyond gaming into professional visualization, data centers, and automotive markets. 

Today, NVIDIA is the dominant global leader in artificial intelligence infrastructure. Its advanced GPUs and proprietary CUDA software platform form the computational backbone for modern generative AI, deep learning, and large language model development, powering cloud service providers, scientific research institutions, and enterprises worldwide. In addition to hardware, the company provides comprehensive software ecosystems and networking solutions tailored for high-performance computing, positioning itself at the forefront of the global digital transformation and autonomous systems industry.
