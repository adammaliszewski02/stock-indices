NVIDIA Corporation, traded under the ticker NVDA, is a pioneering American multinational technology company specializing in accelerated computing. Founded in 1993 by Jensen Huang, Chris Malachowsky, and Curtis Priem, the company initially revolutionized the personal computer gaming industry with the invention of the Graphics Processing Unit in 1999. Over the decades, NVIDIA strategically expanded its architecture to address complex parallel processing challenges. Today, the company is a global leader in artificial intelligence infrastructure, designing advanced hardware, software, and networking systems that power deep learning, data centers, autonomous vehicles, and the metaverse. By fueling the generative AI boom with its industry-standard processors and proprietary CUDA software platform, NVIDIA has evolved from a premier gaming hardware provider into one of the world's most critical drivers of modern technological transformation.
