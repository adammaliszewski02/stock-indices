NVIDIA Corporation is a global technology leader specializing in the design and manufacture of graphics processing units (GPUs) and system-on-a-chip units. Founded in 1993 by Jensen Huang, Chris Malachowsky, and Curtis Priem, the company initially gained prominence by revolutionizing the personal computer gaming market through high-performance graphics hardware. Over the past decade, NVIDIA has successfully pivoted its core architecture to become the foundational engine of the artificial intelligence revolution. Its hardware and software ecosystems, particularly the CUDA parallel computing platform, are now essential to data centers, cloud computing, autonomous vehicles, and professional visualization. By providing the computational power necessary for training and deploying large-scale generative AI models, NVIDIA has established itself as a critical infrastructure provider for the modern digital economy. Headquartered in Santa Clara, California, the company continues to lead the industry in accelerated computing, consistently pushing the boundaries of high-performance processing to solve complex global challenges.
