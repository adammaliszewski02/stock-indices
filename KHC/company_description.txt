The Kraft Heinz Company, traded under the ticker KHC, is a prominent American multinational food and beverage conglomerate. Headquartered in Chicago, Illinois, and Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania, the company is one of the largest food and beverage organizations in the world. Its portfolio encompasses a vast array of globally recognized brands spanning condiments, sauces, cheese, dairy, meals, meats, refreshment beverages, and coffee. 

The enterprise was formed in July 2015 through a landmark merger orchestrated by 3G Capital and Berkshire Hathaway, which combined Kraft Foods Group with H.J. Heinz Holding Company. This strategic union brought together iconic, legacy heritage brands with histories dating back to the late 19th and early 20th centuries, including Heinz ketchup, Kraft Macaroni & Cheese, Oscar Mayer, Jell-O, and Philadelphia cream cheese. Today, KHC operates globally, striving to meet evolving consumer demands for convenient, high-quality nourishment through continuous product innovation, operational efficiency, and sustainable sourcing initiatives across its extensive supply chain.
