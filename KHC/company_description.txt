The Kraft Heinz Company, traded under the ticker KHC, is one of the world's largest food and beverage manufacturers. Dual-headquartered in Chicago, Illinois, and Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania, the company manufactures and markets a diverse portfolio of condiments, sauces, cheese, dairy, meats, packaged meals, and beverages. Its iconic brands include Heinz, Kraft, Oscar Mayer, Philadelphia, Velveeta, Lunchables, and Maxwell House, serving both retail grocery channels and foodservice operators across the globe.

The modern enterprise was formed in 2015 through the merger of Kraft Foods Group and the H.J. Heinz Company, a transaction orchestrated by 3G Capital and Berkshire Hathaway. Both predecessor companies hold significant places in industrial history: H.J. Heinz was founded in 1869 by Henry J. Heinz, initially selling bottled horseradish before introducing its famous tomato ketchup, while Kraft traces its origins to 1903 when J.L. Kraft began a wholesale cheese delivery business in Chicago. Today, Kraft Heinz focuses on brand renovation, supply chain efficiency, and international expansion within the consumer packaged goods sector.
