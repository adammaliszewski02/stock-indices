The Kraft Heinz Company, traded under the ticker KHC, is one of the largest food and beverage companies in the world. Headquartered in Chicago, Illinois, and Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania, the company manufactures and markets a diverse portfolio of globally recognized nutrition, condiment, meal, and beverage products. Its iconic brands include Kraft, Heinz, Oscar Mayer, Philadelphia, Velveeta, and Planters. The enterprise was formed through the historic July 2015 merger of Kraft Foods Group and H.J. Heinz Company, a transaction orchestrated by 3G Capital and Berkshire Hathaway. Today, KHC operates across multiple international markets, leveraging its scale to supply retail and foodservice channels while continually adapting its product offerings to meet evolving consumer preferences for quality, convenience, and health.
