The Kraft Heinz Company, traded under the ticker KHC, is a prominent American multinational food and beverage conglomerate. Headquartered in Chicago, Illinois, and Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania, the enterprise was formed in 2015 through the merger of Kraft Foods Group and H.J. Heinz Company, a transaction orchestrated by Berkshire Hathaway and 3G Capital. 

KHC manufactures and markets a diverse portfolio of globally recognized grocery and food products, spanning condiments, sauces, cheese, dairy, packaged meals, meats, and beverages. Its extensive brand portfolio includes iconic names such as Heinz, Kraft, Oscar Mayer, Philadelphia, Velveeta, Planters, and Maxwell House. The company distributes its products through a vast network of retail supermarkets, mass merchandisers, and foodservice channels across the globe. Driven by a commitment to innovation and consumer-centric marketing, KHC continually adapts its portfolio to meet shifting dietary preferences while maintaining its foundational heritage in the packaged foods sector.
