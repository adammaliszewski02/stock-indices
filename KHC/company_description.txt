The Kraft Heinz Company, traded under the ticker KHC, is one of the largest food and beverage companies in the world. Headquartered in Chicago, Illinois, and Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania, the company manufactures and markets a diverse portfolio of globally recognized nutrition, condiment, meal, and beverage products. Its core portfolio includes iconic household brands such as Kraft, Heinz, Oscar Mayer, Philadelphia, Velveeta, and Planters. 

The company's origins date back to the 19th century through the separate legacies of Henry J. Heinz, who founded his food company in 1869, and James L. Kraft, who began cheese distribution in 1903. The modern iteration of the enterprise was formed in July 2015 through the multi-billion-dollar merger of Kraft Foods Group and H.J. Heinz Holding Corporation, a transaction orchestrated by 3G Capital and Berkshire Hathaway. Today, KHC operates globally, selling its products in over 190 countries and territories, driven by a strategic focus on scale, operational efficiency, and portfolio optimization to meet evolving consumer preferences.
