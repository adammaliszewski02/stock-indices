The Kraft Heinz Company, traded under the ticker KHC, is one of the world’s largest food and beverage manufacturers. Dual-headquartered in Chicago and Pittsburgh, the company produces and markets a vast portfolio of consumer packaged goods, specializing in condiments, sauces, cheese, dairy, prepared meals, and refreshment beverages. Its widely recognized global brands include Heinz, Kraft, Oscar Mayer, Philadelphia, Velveeta, Lunchables, Ore-Ida, and Capri Sun. 

The company assumed its current form in July 2015 following the high-profile merger of Kraft Foods Group and H.J. Heinz Holding Corporation, a transaction engineered by Berkshire Hathaway and the private equity firm 3G Capital. Both legacy entities possess deep industrial roots: Henry J. Heinz founded his namesake business in 1869, famously revolutionizing tomato ketchup, while J.L. Kraft began his wholesale cheese venture in 1903. Today, Kraft Heinz operates globally across both retail and foodservice channels, focusing on supply chain efficiency, brand renovation, and international market expansion.
