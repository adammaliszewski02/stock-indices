The Kraft Heinz Company, traded under the ticker KHC, is one of the largest food and beverage companies in the world. Headquartered in Chicago, Illinois, and Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania, the company manufactures and markets a diverse portfolio of globally recognized brands, including Kraft, Heinz, Oscar Mayer, Philadelphia, and Planters. Its product offerings span condiments and sauces, cheese and dairy, ambient meals, meats, and refreshment beverages, which are distributed to retail and foodservice customers worldwide. 

The company's origins date back to the 19th century, marked by the founding of the H.J. Heinz Company in 1869 and the predecessor of Kraft Foods in 1903. The modern iteration of The Kraft Heinz Company was established in July 2015 through the multi-billion-dollar merger of Kraft Foods Group and H.J. Heinz Holding Corporation, a transaction orchestrated by 3G Capital and Berkshire Hathaway. Today, KHC focuses on driving sustainable growth and operational efficiency while adapting its iconic portfolio to meet evolving consumer preferences toward health, convenience, and global flavors.
