The Kraft Heinz Company, traded under the ticker KHC, is one of the largest food and beverage companies in the world. Headquartered in Chicago, Illinois, and Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania, the company manufactures and markets a diverse portfolio of globally recognized brands, including condiments, sauces, cheese, dairy, packaged meals, and infant nutrition. Its renowned product lineup features iconic names such as Kraft, Heinz, Oscar Mayer, Philadelphia, Velveeta, and Planters. 

The company's origins date back to the 19th century through the separate legacies of the H.J. Heinz Company, founded by Henry J. Heinz in 1869, and the Kraft Foods enterprise, which originated when James L. Kraft began selling cheese in Chicago in 1903. The modern iteration of the enterprise was formed in July 2015 through a landmark merger between Kraft Foods Group and H.J. Heinz Holding Corporation, orchestrated by 3G Capital and Berkshire Hathaway. Today, The Kraft Heinz Company operates on a global scale, supplying products to retailers, foodservice distributors, and consumers across more than 190 countries, while continuously evolving its portfolio to align with changing modern dietary preferences and global consumption trends.
