The stock ticker "V" identifies Visa Inc., a multinational financial services corporation and the world’s largest retail electronic payments network. Headquartered in San Francisco, California, Visa facilitates global commerce by enabling electronic funds transfers between consumers, merchants, financial institutions, and government entities across more than 200 countries and territories. 

Visa does not issue cards, extend credit, or set consumer fees; instead, it provides financial institutions with payment technology and network infrastructure. At the core of its operations is VisaNet, a secure processing network that authorizes, clears, and settles transactions while offering advanced fraud protection. 

The company traces its origins to 1958, when Bank of America launched BankAmericard, the first general-purpose credit card program. In 1976, the system rebranded as Visa to reflect universal acceptance across international markets. After operating for decades as a series of member-owned regional associations, the organizations unified, and Visa Inc. completed one of the largest initial public offerings in U.S. history in March 2008. Today, Visa remains an essential pillar of the modern digital payments ecosystem.
