The stock ticker V refers to Visa Inc., a global digital payments technology company that facilitates electronic funds transfers throughout the world most commonly through branded credit, debit, and prepaid cards. Founded in 1958 by Bank of America as the BankAmericard program, the initiative was later spun off into a cooperative of financial institutions before being reorganized into Visa Inc. and completing an initial public offering in 2008, which was one of the largest in history at the time. Headquartered in San Francisco, California, Visa does not issue cards, extend credit, or set rates and fees for consumers. Instead, it operates the VisaNet processing network, which connects consumers, merchants, financial institutions, and government entities across more than 200 countries and territories, enabling fast, secure, and reliable electronic commerce.
