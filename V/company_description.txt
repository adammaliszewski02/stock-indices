Visa Inc., traded under the ticker V, is a global leader in digital payments, facilitating transactions between consumers, merchants, financial institutions, and government entities across more than 200 countries and territories. Founded in 1958 as BankAmericard by Bank of America, the program expanded internationally in the 1970s, leading to the formation of Visa International Service Association in 1976. The company completed its initial public offering in 2008, which was one of the largest in US history at the time. Visa does not issue cards, extend credit, or set rates and fees for consumers; rather, its core business centers on processing authorization, clearing, and settlement of payment transactions through its proprietary VisaNet network. The company generates revenue primarily from service, data processing, and international transaction fees. Over the decades, Visa has evolved from a traditional credit and debit card association into a technology-driven commerce platform, continually expanding its offerings into value-added services, fraud prevention, digital identity solutions, and business-to-business payment networks to adapt to the modern digital economy.
