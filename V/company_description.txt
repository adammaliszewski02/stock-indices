Visa Inc., trading under the ticker V, is a global leader in digital payments, facilitating transactions among consumers, merchants, financial institutions, and government entities across more than 200 countries and territories. Rather than issuing cards or setting interest rates, the company operates the world’s largest retail electronic payments network, known as VisaNet, which authorizes, clears, and settles payment transactions. 

The enterprise traces its origins to 1958, when Bank of America launched the BankAmericard program in California, which was one of the first general-purpose credit cards for middle-class consumers and small-to-medium-sized merchants. In 1970, the participating banks took control of the program, forming National BankAmericard Inc. The organization was officially rebranded as Visa in 1976 to reflect its growing international scope and universal appeal. Visa subsequently consolidated its regional operations into a single global entity and completed a landmark initial public offering in 2008, which was one of the largest in United States history. Today, the company continues to drive financial commerce by providing secure, reliable, and innovative payment infrastructure across traditional and emerging digital platforms.
