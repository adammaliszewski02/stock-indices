Visa Inc., traded under the ticker V, is a global leader in digital payments, facilitating transactions between consumers, merchants, financial institutions, and government entities across more than 200 countries and territories. Founded in 1958 as BankAmericard by Bank of America, the program expanded internationally in the 1970s, leading to the formation of Visa International Service Association in 1976. The company completed a historic initial public offering in 2008, which was the largest IPO in US history at that time. Rather than issuing cards or extending credit itself, Visa operates the world's most advanced processing networks—specifically VisaNet—which enables secure, fast, and reliable authorization, clearing, and settlement of electronic payments. Its business model centers on earning revenue through service, data processing, and international transaction fees charged to financial institutions. Today, Visa provides a comprehensive suite of financial services, including value-added solutions, fraud prevention, and digital identity services, continually evolving to support emerging payment technologies such as mobile wallets, contactless payments, and business-to-business cross-border transfers.
