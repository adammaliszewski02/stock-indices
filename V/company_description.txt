Visa Inc., identified by the stock ticker V, is a global leader in digital payments, facilitating transactions between consumers, merchants, financial institutions, and government entities across more than 200 countries and territories. The company does not issue cards, extend credit, or set rates and fees for consumers; rather, its core business operates the VisaNet processing network, which securely enables the authorization, clearing, and settlement of payment transactions. 

The enterprise traces its origins to 1958, when Bank of America launched the BankAmericard program in California, which was one of the first general-purpose credit cards for middle-class consumers and small-to-medium-sized merchants. In 1970, the participating banks took control of the program, forming National BankAmericard Inc. The globally unified brand name "Visa" was adopted in 1976 to reflect the company’s international scope and growth. Visa subsequently reorganized into a publicly traded corporation through its historic initial public offering in 2008, which remains one of the largest IPOs in financial history. Today, the company continues to drive innovation in financial technology, expanding beyond traditional credit and debit cards to encompass value-added services, digital wallets, tokenization, and cross-border money movement solutions.
