The ticker symbol V identifies Visa Inc., a global leader in digital payments that facilitates electronic funds transfers throughout more than 200 countries and territories. Founded in 1958 as BankAmericard by Bank of America, the program was eventually spun off and restructured under the name Visa in 1976 before becoming a publicly traded company through its historic initial public offering in 2008. Visa does not issue cards, extend credit, or set rates and fees for consumers. Instead, the company operates the proprietary VisaNet processing network, which securely connects consumers, merchants, financial institutions, and government entities, enabling fast, reliable, and secure global commerce.
