Visa Inc., trading under the ticker V, is a global leader in digital payments, facilitating transactions between consumers, merchants, financial institutions, and government entities across more than 200 countries and territories. Rather than issuing cards or setting interest rates, the company operates the world’s largest retail electronic payments network, known as VisaNet, which authorizes, clears, and settles payment transactions securely and efficiently. 

The enterprise traces its origins to 1958, when Bank of America launched the BankAmericard program in California, which was one of the first all-purpose consumer credit cards in the United States. In 1970, the participating banks took control of the program, subsequently rebranding it as Visa in 1976 to reflect its growing international scope and universal acceptance. Visa completed a historic initial public offering in 2008, which was one of the largest in U.S. history at the time. Today, the company continues to evolve beyond traditional credit and debit cards by investing in digital wallet technologies, business-to-business payment solutions, and advanced fraud-prevention analytics, reinforcing its foundational mission to connect the world through the most secure, reliable, and innovative payment network.
