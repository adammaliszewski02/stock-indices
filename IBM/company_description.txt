International Business Machines Corporation, commonly known as IBM, is a multinational technology and consulting company headquartered in Armonk, New York. Traded on the New York Stock Exchange under the ticker IBM, the company has played a foundational role in the computing industry since its incorporation in 1911 as the Computing-Tabulating-Recording Company, adopting its current name in 1924. Historically renowned for pioneering mainframe hardware and early personal computers, IBM has successfully transitioned its business model to focus on hybrid cloud computing, enterprise artificial intelligence, and advanced IT services. Through its Red Hat subsidiary and the IBM watsonx platform, the company currently empowers global enterprises to digitally transform, secure, and modernize their mission-critical operations.
