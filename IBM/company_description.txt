International Business Machines Corporation, commonly known as IBM and trading under the ticker IBM, is a global technology and consulting enterprise headquartered in Armonk, New York. Founded in 1911 through the amalgamation of several tabulating and computing companies, the firm initially established its legacy in the production of punch-card tabulating systems. Over the decades, IBM transformed into a preeminent pioneer in the computing industry, famously developing the IBM PC in 1981, which standardized personal computing architecture worldwide. 

In the modern era, IBM has successfully shifted its strategic focus away from legacy hardware toward high-value segments, specifically hybrid cloud computing and artificial intelligence. Through its subsidiary Red Hat, acquired in 2019, the company provides open-source enterprise software solutions. Additionally, IBM delivers comprehensive IT infrastructure, enterprise consulting, and advanced quantum computing research. Leveraging its storied technological heritage, the firm enables global businesses to modernize operations, secure digital assets, and harness data-driven insights through platforms such as watsonx.
