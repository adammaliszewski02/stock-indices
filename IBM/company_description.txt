International Business Machines Corporation, commonly known as IBM and trading under the ticker IBM, is a global technology and consulting enterprise headquartered in Armonk, New York. Founded in 1911 as the Computing-Tabulating-Recording Company through the merger of four firms, it was officially renamed IBM in 1924. Historically renowned for pioneering early punch-card tabulating systems and later dominating the mainframe computer market in the twentieth century, IBM has continually reinvented its core operations. Today, the company operates as a leading provider of hybrid cloud infrastructure and artificial intelligence solutions. IBM focuses its business model on enterprise-grade software and services, highlighted by its subsidiary Red Hat, and leverages advanced quantum computing technologies. Through ongoing research and strategic acquisitions, IBM enables global businesses to modernize operations, secure digital assets, and process complex data workloads efficiently.
