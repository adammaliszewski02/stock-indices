The ticker IBM represents International Business Machines Corporation, a global technology and consulting enterprise headquartered in Armonk, New York. Today, IBM focuses primarily on hybrid cloud computing, enterprise software, artificial intelligence through its watsonx platform, and mission-critical IT consulting. The company also maintains a leadership position in specialized enterprise hardware, such as mainframes, and conducts cutting-edge research in fields like quantum computing.

Founded in 1911 as the Computing-Tabulating-Recording Company (CTR) and renamed International Business Machines in 1924 under Thomas J. Watson Sr., IBM has played a foundational role in the digital revolution. Over its history, the company introduced landmark innovations, including punch-card tabulators, the automated teller machine, the magnetic stripe card, the System/360 mainframe, and the IBM Personal Computer. 

In recent decades, IBM has systematically shifted away from commoditized hardware toward higher-value software and cloud solutions. Key milestones in this strategic transition include the landmark acquisition of open-source leader Red Hat in 2019 and the 2021 spin-off of its legacy managed infrastructure services business into a separate public entity, Kyndryl.
