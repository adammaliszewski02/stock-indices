International Business Machines Corporation, commonly known as IBM and historically recognized as Computing-Tabulating-Recording Company prior to its 1924 renaming, is a global technology and consulting enterprise headquartered in Armonk, New York. Founded on a legacy of pioneering punch-card tabulating systems, the company evolved into a dominant mainframe computer manufacturer during the twentieth century. Today, IBM focuses its business on hybrid cloud infrastructure and enterprise artificial intelligence solutions, operating primarily through software, consulting, infrastructure, and financing segments. Leveraging its premier research division, the corporation delivers advanced technological capabilities—including the Red Hat software portfolio and watsonx AI platform—to help global organizations modernize operations, secure data, and drive digital transformation across complex industry landscapes.
