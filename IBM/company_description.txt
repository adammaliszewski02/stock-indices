International Business Machines Corporation, commonly known as IBM, is a global technology and consulting firm headquartered in Armonk, New York. Founded in 1911 as the Computing-Tabulating-Recording Company and rebranded in 1924, the organization has played a pivotal role in the evolution of modern computing. Throughout its history, IBM has been responsible for landmark innovations, including the development of the mainframe computer, the invention of the floppy disk, and the creation of the IBM PC.

Today, IBM focuses its business operations on hybrid cloud computing, artificial intelligence, and enterprise software solutions. By leveraging its extensive research capabilities and global consulting arm, the company assists organizations across various industries in modernizing their digital infrastructure and automating complex business processes. IBM continues to prioritize long-term research and development, maintaining a significant portfolio of patents in areas such as quantum computing and advanced data analytics to address the evolving technological needs of the global marketplace.
