International Business Machines Corporation, commonly known as IBM, is a global technology and consulting enterprise headquartered in Armonk, New York. Traded on the New York Stock Exchange under the ticker IBM, the company has a rich corporate lineage originating from the 1911 merger of three pioneering firms that manufactured commercial scales, tabulating machines, and time-recording devices, later formally adopting its current name in 1924. 

Throughout its storied history, IBM has been a central driver of the information technology revolution, introducing landmark innovations such as the System/360 mainframe architecture, the IBM Personal Computer, and artificial intelligence milestones like Deep Blue and Watson. In the modern era, IBM operates primarily through hybrid cloud infrastructure, enterprise software, and advanced technology services. The company strategically focuses on hybrid cloud computing and artificial intelligence, leveraging its prominent open-source subsidiary, Red Hat, to help global enterprises modernize operations, manage complex data environments, and accelerate digital transformation securely and efficiently.
