International Business Machines Corporation, commonly known as IBM, is a global technology and consulting enterprise headquartered in Armonk, New York. Traded on the New York Stock Exchange under the ticker IBM, the company has a rich corporate lineage originating from the 1911 merger of three pioneering firms, initially establishing its market dominance through the production of punch-card tabulating systems. Throughout its history, IBM has been a foundational architect of the modern computing era, introducing monumental innovations such as the IBM System/360 mainframe in 1964 and the IBM Personal Computer in 1981. Today, IBM operates as a leading hybrid cloud platform and artificial intelligence provider, assisting enterprise clients worldwide in modernizing their critical IT infrastructure, optimizing operations, and scaling data capabilities securely.
