Caterpillar Inc., traded under the ticker CAT, is the world's leading manufacturer of construction and mining equipment, diesel and natural gas engines, industrial gas turbines, and diesel-electric locomotives. Operating through its primary segments of Construction Industries, Resource Industries, Energy and Transportation, along with Financial Products, the company provides vital machinery and technology to support global infrastructure development, energy extraction, and resource management. Headquartered in Deerfield, Illinois, the enterprise traces its origins to 1925 with the merger of the Holt Manufacturing Company and C.L. Best Tractor Co., combining their track-type tractor innovations. Over the following century, Caterpillar expanded globally, evolving from a pioneer in agricultural and earthmoving machinery into a diversified industrial powerhouse. Today, the company sustains a robust international dealer network, delivering heavy equipment and advanced digital solutions that drive productivity and operational efficiency across the mining, agriculture, forestry, marine, and power generation sectors.
