Caterpillar Inc., trading under the ticker symbol CAT, is the world's leading manufacturer of construction and mining equipment, diesel and natural gas engines, industrial gas turbines, and diesel-electric locomotives. The company operates through distinct segments, primarily Construction Industries, Resource Industries, Energy and Transportation, and Financial Products, serving customers globally across infrastructure, forestry, agricultural, oil, gas, and marine sectors. Caterpillar traces its origins to 1925 with the merger of the C.L. Best Tractor Co. and the Holt Manufacturing Company, which pioneered the development of track-type tractors. Headquartered in Irving, Texas, the company expanded rapidly over the twentieth century through product diversification and global acquisitions, establishing an extensive independent dealer network. Today, Caterpillar is a prominent component of the Dow Jones Industrial Average, recognized for its enduring legacy in heavy machinery manufacturing, technological innovation in autonomous mining trucks, and sustained commitment to global infrastructure development.
