Caterpillar Inc., traded under the ticker CAT, is the world's leading manufacturer of construction and mining equipment, diesel and natural gas engines, industrial gas turbines, and diesel-electric locomotives. Operating through key segments including Construction Industries, Resource Industries, and Energy and Transportation, the company also provides specialized financing and related services through its financial products arm. Headquartered in Deerfield, Illinois, the enterprise traces its origins to the 1925 merger of the Holt Manufacturing Company and C.L. Best Tractor Co., which combined their expertise in track-type tractors to form the Caterpillar Tractor Co. Over the subsequent decades, the company expanded globally, diversified its product portfolio through strategic acquisitions, and rebranded as Caterpillar Inc. in 1986 to reflect its broader industrial scope. Today, CAT is a prominent component of the Dow Jones Industrial Average, recognized globally for its iconic yellow machinery and a robust dealer network supporting infrastructure, energy, and natural resource development worldwide.
