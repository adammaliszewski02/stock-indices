Caterpillar Inc., traded under the ticker CAT, is the world's leading manufacturer of construction and mining equipment, diesel and natural gas engines, industrial gas turbines, and diesel-electric locomotives. Operating through its primary segments of Construction Industries, Resource Industries, Energy and Transportation, along with Financial Products, the company provides vital machinery and technology to support global infrastructure, energy, and resource development. The enterprise traces its origins to 1925 through the merger of the Holt Manufacturing Company and C.L. Best Tractor Co., which combined their expertise in crawler tractors to revolutionize agriculture and heavy earthmoving. Over the subsequent decades, Caterpillar expanded globally and diversified its product portfolio through significant acquisitions, most notably the 2010 purchase of Bucyrus International. Today, the corporation is recognized as an iconic American industrial bellwether, distributing its yellow-branded machinery and advanced power solutions through an unparalleled worldwide dealer network, while increasingly integrating automation, autonomy, and sustainability initiatives into its core engineering and manufacturing operations.
