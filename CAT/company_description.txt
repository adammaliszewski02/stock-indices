Caterpillar Inc. (CAT) is the world’s leading manufacturer of construction and mining equipment, diesel and natural gas engines, industrial gas turbines, and diesel-electric locomotives. Founded in 1925 through the merger of the Holt Manufacturing Company and the C.L. Best Tractor Co., the company has evolved into a global industrial powerhouse. Headquartered in Irving, Texas, Caterpillar operates through three primary segments: Construction Industries, Resource Industries, and Energy and Transportation. Its iconic yellow machinery is synonymous with infrastructure development, energy production, and mining operations worldwide. Beyond equipment manufacturing, the company provides comprehensive financial services and support through its global dealer network, ensuring the maintenance and operational efficiency of its products across diverse sectors. With nearly a century of engineering expertise, Caterpillar remains a critical component of the global supply chain, supporting the development of infrastructure, energy, and transportation systems on every continent.
