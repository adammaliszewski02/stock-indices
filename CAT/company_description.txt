Caterpillar Inc., trading under the ticker symbol CAT, is the world's leading manufacturer of construction and mining equipment, diesel and natural gas engines, industrial gas turbines, and diesel-electric locomotives. The company operates through distinct segments, primarily Construction Industries, Resource Industries, Energy and Transportation, and Financial Products, serving customers in infrastructure, forestry, agriculture, oil, gas, and marine sectors globally. Headquartered in Irving, Texas, the enterprise traces its origins to the 1925 merger of the C.L. Best Tractor Co. and the Holt Manufacturing Company, which created the Caterpillar Tractor Co. The founders pioneered the continuous track system for tractors, initially revolutionizing agricultural operations before pivoting heavily to support large-scale infrastructure development and earthmoving projects worldwide. Over the subsequent decades, Caterpillar expanded its portfolio through strategic acquisitions and global manufacturing investments, evolving into an indispensable backbone of industrial and resource extraction economies. Today, the corporation distinguishes itself not only through heavy machinery, but increasingly via advanced technologies, including autonomous mining fleets and digital fleet management solutions that drive operational efficiency and safety for its global clientele.
