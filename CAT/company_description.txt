Caterpillar Inc., trading under the ticker symbol CAT, is the world's leading manufacturer of construction and mining equipment, diesel and natural gas engines, industrial gas turbines, and diesel-electric locomotives. The company operates through distinct segments encompassing machinery, energy and transportation, and financial products, serving diverse global industries including infrastructure, resource extraction, and energy. Caterpillar's origins date back to 1925 with the merger of the Holt Manufacturing Company and C.R. Best Tractor Co., which combined their expertise to create the iconic "Caterpillar" track-type tractor. Over the past century, the enterprise has expanded globally through continuous product innovation, strategic acquisitions, and a robust worldwide dealer network. Today, Caterpillar remains an essential pillar of global industrial development, known for its durable heavy machinery and advanced technological solutions that drive productivity and sustainability across demanding operational environments.
