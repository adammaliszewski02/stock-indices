META is the stock ticker for Meta Platforms, Inc., formerly known as Facebook, Inc. Founded in 2004 by Mark Zuckerberg and his college roommates, the company began as a social networking website for university students before expanding globally to become the world's largest social media conglomerate. Meta’s core business model is centered on digital advertising, generating the vast majority of its revenue by selling targeted ad space across its family of applications, which includes Facebook, Instagram, Messenger, and WhatsApp. In recent years, the company has heavily diversified its operations into artificial intelligence, augmented reality, and virtual reality, most notably through its Reality Labs division, which focuses on developing hardware and software for the metaverse. Additionally, Meta provides enterprise communication tools and hardware devices like Ray-Ban Meta smart glasses and Meta Quest headsets. Driven by massive global user engagement, the company continues to pioneer advancements in open-source AI models and digital infrastructure.
