Meta Platforms, Inc., trading under the ticker META, is a multinational technology conglomerate that constructs products enabling people to connect and share through mobile devices, personal computers, virtual reality headsets, and wearables. Originally founded by Mark Zuckerberg and college roommates as The Facebook in 2004, the company initially focused on its flagship social networking website before expanding its portfolio through strategic acquisitions, including Instagram in 2012 and WhatsApp in 2014. The enterprise officially rebranded from Facebook, Inc. to Meta Platforms in October 2021 to reflect its growing emphasis on the metaverse. Today, Meta generates the vast majority of its revenue through digital advertising sales delivered across its family of applications. Additionally, the company invests heavily in artificial intelligence infrastructure and augmented and virtual reality technologies through its Reality Labs division to shape the next evolution of social technology.
