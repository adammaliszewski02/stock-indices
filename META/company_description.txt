META is the stock ticker for Meta Platforms, Inc., formerly known as Facebook, Inc. Founded in 2004 as a college social networking service, the company has evolved into a global technology conglomerate. Meta’s primary business centers on digital advertising and social media, operating a family of applications that includes Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp, and Messenger. These platforms connect billions of users worldwide and generate the vast majority of the company's revenue through targeted advertising services. In recent years, Meta has significantly expanded its strategic focus beyond social media to encompass the development of virtual and augmented reality technologies. Through its Reality Labs segment, the company invests in hardware, software, and immersive experiences designed to build the metaverse, a collective virtual shared space. Additionally, Meta is a major contributor to open-source artificial intelligence research, integrating advanced machine learning models across its advertising systems and consumer products to enhance user engagement and platform monetization.
