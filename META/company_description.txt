Meta Platforms, Inc., trading under the ticker META, is a multinational technology conglomerate that constructs products enabling people to connect and share through mobile devices, personal computers, virtual reality headsets, and wearables. Founded in February 2004 by Mark Zuckerberg and fellow Harvard college students as The Facebook, the company initially focused on a social networking platform for university students before expanding globally. Over the years, Meta evolved from a single web application into a dominant digital ecosystem encompassing social media platforms such as Instagram, Messenger, WhatsApp, and Threads. In October 2021, the company officially rebranded from Facebook, Inc. to Meta Platforms to reflect its growing strategic emphasis on the metaverse, an integrated network of 3D virtual environments. Today, Meta's primary business model relies on digital advertising, generating substantial revenue by selling targeted ad placements across its family of apps to a global user base. Additionally, the company invests heavily in artificial intelligence infrastructure and consumer hardware, including Ray-Ban Meta smart glasses and Meta Quest headsets, to shape the future of human connectivity and digital interaction.
