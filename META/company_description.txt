Meta Platforms, Inc., trading under the ticker META, is a leading multinational technology conglomerate headquartered in Menlo Park, California. Founded in 2004 by Mark Zuckerberg and his college roommates as Facebook, the company initially focused on its flagship social networking platform before expanding its ecosystem through strategic acquisitions, including Instagram and WhatsApp. Meta’s core business centers on digital advertising, which generates the vast majority of its revenue by connecting businesses with billions of global users across its family of applications. In 2021, the company officially rebranded from Facebook, Inc. to Meta Platforms to reflect its strategic pivot toward building the metaverse, an immersive digital environment integrating virtual and augmented reality. Beyond social media and spatial computing, the company invests heavily in artificial intelligence infrastructure, developing advanced machine learning models and open-source AI technologies to power its platforms, enhance user engagement, and drive future enterprise solutions.
