Meta Platforms, Inc., trading under the ticker META, is a multinational technology conglomerate that constructs products enabling people to connect and share through mobile devices, personal computers, virtual reality headsets, and wearables. Founded in February 2004 by Mark Zuckerberg and fellow Harvard college students as The Facebook, the company initially focused on a social networking platform for university students before expanding globally. 

Today, Meta operates a family of applications, including Facebook, Instagram, Messenger, WhatsApp, and Threads. These platforms support worldwide communication and digital marketing for millions of businesses. Additionally, the company invests heavily in artificial intelligence and the metaverse, developing augmented and virtual reality hardware and software through its Reality Labs segment. Meta generates the vast majority of its revenue by selling advertising placements to marketers across its social media ecosystem.
