Meta Platforms, Inc., trading under the ticker META, is a multinational technology conglomerate that constructs products enabling people to connect and share through mobile devices, personal computers, virtual reality headsets, and wearables. Founded in February 2004 by Mark Zuckerberg and fellow Harvard college students as The Facebook, the company initially focused on a social networking platform for university students before expanding globally. Over the years, Meta transformed from a single social website into a sprawling ecosystem encompassing major social media and messaging applications, including Instagram, WhatsApp, and Messenger. 

In October 2021, the company officially changed its name from Facebook, Inc. to Meta Platforms to reflect its strategic pivot toward building the metaverse, a shared virtual environment blending augmented and virtual reality technologies. Today, Meta generates the vast majority of its revenue through the sale of advertising space to marketers across its family of apps. It also develops consumer hardware, such as Ray-Ban Meta smart glasses and Quest virtual reality headsets, alongside advanced open-source artificial intelligence models through its foundational research division, operating as a dominant force in digital communication, advertising, and emerging immersive technologies.
