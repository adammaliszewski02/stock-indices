Meta Platforms, Inc., trading under the ticker META, is a multinational technology conglomerate that constructs products enabling people to connect and share through mobile devices, personal computers, virtual reality headsets, and wearables. Founded in February 2004 by Mark Zuckerberg and fellow Harvard college students as The Facebook, the company initially focused on a social networking platform for university campuses before expanding globally. Over the past two decades, Meta evolved from a single social website into a dominant ecosystem of digital communication and advertising. The company officially changed its name from Facebook, Inc. to Meta Platforms in October 2021 to reflect its growing strategic focus on the metaverse. Today, Meta's core family of apps includes Facebook, Instagram, Messenger, and WhatsApp, which collectively serve billions of monthly active users worldwide. Additionally, through its Reality Labs division, the company develops augmented and virtual reality hardware and software, pioneering immersive digital environments alongside its traditional strengths in social media and digital advertising technology.
