LIN is the stock ticker for Linde plc, a leading global industrial gases and engineering company. Traded on the New York Stock Exchange and Frankfurt Stock Exchange, Linde serves a diverse array of industries, including chemicals, healthcare, food and beverage, electronics, and manufacturing. The company’s core business involves the production and distribution of atmospheric gases—such as oxygen, nitrogen, and argon—and process gases, including hydrogen and helium, alongside high-performance surface technologies. 

Linde’s corporate lineage traces back to the founding of Linde AG in Germany in 1879 by Carl von Linde, who pioneered the refrigeration and air separation processes that established the industrial gas sector. Over the decades, the enterprise expanded globally through strategic acquisitions and technological innovation. A pivotal milestone occurred in 2018 when Linde AG merged with the United States-based Praxair, a company with historical roots tracing back to Union Carbide's industrial gases division. This transformative merger created the world's largest industrial gas company by market share and revenue. Today, Linde is legally domiciled in Ireland, headquartered in Surrey, United Kingdom, and globally recognized for its commitment to sustainability, clean energy solutions, and advanced decarbonization technologies.
