LIN is the stock ticker for Linde plc, the world's leading industrial gases and engineering company. Traded on the New York Stock Exchange and the Frankfurt Stock Exchange, Linde serves a diverse array of markets including chemicals, refining, electronics, healthcare, manufacturing, and primary metals. The company produces, sells, and distributes atmospheric gases such as oxygen, nitrogen, and argon, process gases including carbon dioxide and helium, and specialty gases. Linde also designs and builds turnkey plant solutions for complex industrial processes. The company’s roots trace back to the founding of Linde AG in Germany in 1879 by Carl von Linde, who patented a refrigeration cycle that enabled the commercial liquefaction of air. In 2018, Linde AG merged with the United States-based Praxair, a company with historical origins dating back to 1907 as part of Union Carbide and the first to commercialize large-scale oxygen production. This historic combination united more than a century of industry pioneering to form the current global entity, which is legally domiciled in Ireland with its corporate headquarters in Surrey, United Kingdom, recognized for its commitment to sustainability, technological innovation, and operational efficiency.
