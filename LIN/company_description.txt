LIN is the stock ticker for Linde plc, the world's largest industrial gases and engineering company by market capitalization and revenue. Headquartered in Woking, United Kingdom, with primary operational roots extending back over a century, the company serves a diverse array of end markets including healthcare, chemicals, energy, manufacturing, and electronics. Linde produces, sells, and distributes atmospheric gases such as oxygen, nitrogen, and argon, as well as process gases including hydrogen and carbon dioxide. The corporation also designs and constructs large-scale scale-up facilities for gas production and offers state-of-the-art gas application technologies to support sustainable development and decarbonization efforts. Linde’s corporate heritage traces back to the 1879 founding by Carl von Linde in Germany, who successfully pioneered the refrigeration and air separation processes that laid the foundation for the modern industrial gas sector. Today, Linde plc operates globally in more than 100 countries, delivering essential products and high-efficiency engineering solutions that help customers reduce their carbon footprint, enhance productivity, and safeguard the environment.
