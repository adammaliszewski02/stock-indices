LIN is the stock ticker for Linde plc, the world's leading industrial gases and engineering company. Traded on the New York Stock Exchange and the Frankfurt Stock Exchange, Linde serves a diverse array of markets including chemicals, energy, food, electronics, healthcare, and manufacturing. Its primary offerings comprise atmospheric gases such as oxygen, nitrogen, and argon, process gases including hydrogen and carbon dioxide, and specialized equipment used in industrial production. The company traces its historical roots back to the late nineteenth century when Carl von Linde invented a revolutionary refrigeration process and founded Linde’s Ice Machine Company in Germany in 1879. Over the decades, the enterprise expanded globally through numerous strategic acquisitions and innovations. A pivotal milestone occurred in 2018 through the merger of equals between Germany-based Linde AG and the United States-based Praxair, a company that itself originated from Union Carbide. This historic combination united more than a century of technological expertise, creating a global powerhouse incorporated in Ireland with operational headquarters in Surrey, United Kingdom. Today, Linde continues to drive industry standards while advancing clean energy solutions, particularly in the production and distribution of green hydrogen.
