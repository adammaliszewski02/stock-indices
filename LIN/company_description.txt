LIN is the stock ticker for Linde plc, the world's leading industrial gases and engineering company. Traded on the New York Stock Exchange and the Frankfurt Stock Exchange, Linde serves a diverse array of markets including chemicals, energy, food, electronics, healthcare, and manufacturing. The company's primary offerings comprise atmospheric gases—such as oxygen, nitrogen, and argon—rare gases, and process gases, alongside comprehensive high-pressure gas delivery systems and related engineering services. 

Linde's corporate lineage traces back to the founding of Linde plc in Germany by Carl von Linde, who successfully liquefied air in 1895. Over the subsequent decades, the enterprise expanded globally through continuous technological innovation in cryogenics and gas processing. A major milestone occurred in 2018 when the original Linde AG merged with U.S.-based Praxair, a company with historical roots tracing back to Union Carbide's industrial gases division. This landmark combination created the current global entity, which is legally domiciled in Ireland with its operational headquarters in Danbury, Connecticut. Today, Linde is recognized not only for its vast industrial reach but also for its commitment to sustainability, pioneering investments in clean hydrogen and carbon capture technologies to drive the global energy transition.
