Linde plc (LIN) is the world’s largest industrial gases and engineering company by market share. Headquartered in Woking, United Kingdom, the company provides atmospheric gases—such as oxygen, nitrogen, and argon—and process gases, including hydrogen and helium, to a diverse range of industries, including healthcare, chemicals, food and beverage, electronics, and manufacturing. Linde also designs and builds large-scale equipment for gas separation and synthesis.

The company’s origins trace back to 1879, when Carl von Linde founded the business after inventing a revolutionary refrigeration process. In 2018, the company underwent a transformative merger between the German-based Linde AG and the American-based Praxair, Inc., creating a global powerhouse in the industrial gas sector. Today, Linde is a constituent of the S&P 500 and is recognized for its commitment to sustainability, particularly through its leadership in the development of clean hydrogen infrastructure and carbon capture technologies. Through its extensive global network of production facilities and distribution channels, Linde remains a critical partner in the global industrial supply chain.
