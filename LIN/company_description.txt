LIN is the stock ticker for Linde plc, a leading global industrial gases and engineering company. Traded on the New York Stock Exchange and the Frankfurt Stock Exchange, Linde serves a diverse array of markets including chemicals, healthcare, manufacturing, and electronics. The company’s core business involves the production and distribution of atmospheric gases, such as oxygen, nitrogen, and argon, as well as process gases, including hydrogen and helium. Linde also designs and builds large-scale chemical plant facilities globally. The company traces its origins to the founding of Linde’s Ice Machine Company in Germany in 1879 by Carl von Linde, who successfully patented a refrigeration cycle that enabled the liquefaction of air. Over the decades, Linde expanded rapidly through international operations and major strategic consolidations. A significant milestone occurred in 2018 through the merger of equals between the historic German enterprise Linde AG and the American corporation Praxair, a descendant of Union Carbide. Today, headquartered in Surrey, United Kingdom, Linde operates as a premier Fortune 500 entity recognized for its commitment to sustainability, clean energy technologies, and operational excellence in industrial gas supply.
