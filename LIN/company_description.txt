The company identified by the stock ticker LIN is Linde plc, the world's largest industrial gases and engineering company by market share and revenue. Tracing its origins to the founding of Gesellschaft für Linde'sche Eismaschinen in Germany in 1879 by Carl von Linde—who pioneered the refrigeration and air liquefaction processes—the modern enterprise was formed through the 2018 merger of Linde AG and Praxair, Inc. Headquartered in Danbury, Connecticut, Linde serves a diverse array of end markets including chemicals, refining, electronics, healthcare, manufacturing, and primary metals. The company produces, sells, and distributes atmospheric gases such as oxygen, nitrogen, and argon, process gases including carbon dioxide and helium, and specialty gases. Additionally, Linde designs and builds equipment for industrial gas production and offers customers comprehensive gas processing solutions. Driven by a commitment to sustainability, the company plays a critical role in the global energy transition by advancing clean hydrogen technologies, carbon capture, and energy-efficient applications that help customers reduce their carbon footprint while maintaining high productivity and safety standards across global operations.
