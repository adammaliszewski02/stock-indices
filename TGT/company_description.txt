Target Corporation (NYSE: TGT) is one of the largest general merchandise retailers in the United States, operating as a suburban- and urban-focused big-box department store chain. The company offers a wide-ranging assortment of merchandise, including apparel, electronics, beauty products, home furnishings, and daily groceries, complemented by an extensive suite of owned and exclusive brands. Target’s business model emphasizes a blend of affordable style and convenience, supported by integrated physical stores and robust digital fulfillment services such as curbside pickup and same-day delivery. The enterprise traces its origins to 1902 when George Draper Dayton founded the Dayton Dry Goods Company in Minneapolis, Minnesota. The first store bearing the Target name opened its doors in Roseville, Minnesota, in 1962, marking the company’s strategic expansion into discount retailing. Dayton Corporation subsequently merged with J.L. Hudson Company to form Dayton Hudson Corporation in 1969, before officially adopting the Target Corporation name in 2000 to reflect the dominance of its primary retail brand. Today, the corporation operates nearly two thousand stores nationwide and remains a cornerstone of American consumer retail.
