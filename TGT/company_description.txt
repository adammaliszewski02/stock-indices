Target Corporation, trading under the ticker TGT, is a prominent American retail company headquartered in Minneapolis, Minnesota. Founded in 1902 as the Dayton Dry Goods Company, the firm evolved significantly with the opening of its first Target discount store in 1962. Today, the company operates as a leading omnichannel retailer, managing nearly 2,000 stores across the United States alongside a robust digital platform. Target distinguishes itself in the competitive retail landscape through a curated product assortment that spans apparel, home decor, electronics, groceries, and beauty essentials, often emphasizing a balance between affordability and design-forward aesthetics. By integrating its physical store footprint with sophisticated supply chain logistics and e-commerce capabilities, Target provides a seamless shopping experience that caters to a broad consumer base. The company remains a cornerstone of the retail industry, consistently focusing on brand partnerships and private-label development to drive customer loyalty and long-term growth.
