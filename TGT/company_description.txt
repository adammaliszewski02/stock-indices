Target Corporation, trading under the ticker TGT, is one of the largest general merchandise retailers in the United States. Headquartered in Minneapolis, Minnesota, the company operates a vast chain of discount department stores offering a comprehensive assortment of apparel, electronics, beauty products, home furnishings, and grocery items. Target emphasizes a strategy of blending affordable everyday essentials with stylish, trend-forward designer partnerships to differentiate itself in the competitive retail landscape. 

The company’s roots trace back to 1902 when George Draper Dayton founded the Dayton Dry Goods Company, which later evolved into the Dayton Hudson Corporation. In 1962, the company opened its first Target discount store in Roseville, Minnesota, pioneering upscale discounting. Dayton Hudson officially changed its name to Target Corporation in 2000 to reflect the growing prominence of its flagship retail brand. Today, Target operates nearly 2,000 stores across the United States and continues to expand its digital footprint, offering robust same-day fulfillment services such as curbside pickup and delivery through its subsidiary, Shipt.
