Target Corporation, trading under the stock ticker TGT, is one of the largest general merchandise retailers in the United States. The company operates a vast chain of discount department stores offering a comprehensive assortment of apparel, electronics, beauty products, home goods, and grocery items through both its physical retail locations and digital e-commerce platforms. Target also emphasizes private-label brands and same-day fulfillment services, including drive-up and home delivery. 

Tracing its origins to 1902 when George Draper Dayton founded the Dayton Dry Goods Company in Minneapolis, Minnesota, the enterprise evolved into the Dayton Hudson Corporation. In 1962, the company opened its first discount store under the Target name in Roseville, Minnesota, pioneering upscale discounting by offering branded merchandise at lower prices within modern, customer-friendly environments. Dayton Hudson officially changed its corporate name to Target Corporation in 2000 to reflect the overwhelming success and national prominence of its flagship retail brand. Today, the corporation employs hundreds of thousands of team members and remains a staple of the American retail landscape, operating over 1,900 stores nationwide.
