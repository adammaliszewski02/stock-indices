Target Corporation, traded under the stock ticker TGT, is a premier American general merchandise retailer operating a vast chain of discount department stores. Founded in 1902 as the Dayton Dry Goods Company in Minneapolis, Minnesota, the enterprise opened its first discount store under the Target name in Roseville, Minnesota, in 1962. Over subsequent decades, the company expanded nationally, dropping its department store roots to focus exclusively on discount retailing and eventually spinning off or selling its non-retail subsidiaries. Today, Target serves millions of consumers both in-store and online through its comprehensive digital platform. The company's business model centers on offering stylish, trend-right apparel, home goods, electronics, and everyday grocery essentials at affordable prices. Target has built a strong brand identity through strategic designer collaborations, exclusive private-label brands, and a commitment to guest experience. Operating primarily within the United States, the corporation manages a robust supply chain network supported by numerous distribution centers and fulfillment hubs, positioning itself as an industry leader in omnichannel retail.
