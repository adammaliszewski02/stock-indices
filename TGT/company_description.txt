Target Corporation, trading under the ticker symbol TGT, is a leading American general merchandise retailer. Headquartered in Minneapolis, Minnesota, the company operates nearly 2,000 stores across the United States alongside a comprehensive digital sales platform. Target offers a diverse, curated assortment of products spanning apparel, home goods, beauty, electronics, food, and beverage. The company differentiates itself through an upscale discount model, combining owned private-label brands and exclusive designer partnerships with robust omnichannel fulfillment options, including in-store pickup and drive-up services.

The business traces its roots to 1902, when George Draper Dayton established Goodfellow Dry Goods, later renamed the Dayton Company. In response to the post-war rise of discount shopping, the firm opened the first Target store in Roseville, Minnesota, in 1962. Following a 1969 merger that created the Dayton-Hudson Corporation, the discount division grew rapidly to become the enterprise's primary revenue driver. In 2000, the company officially rebranded as Target Corporation to reflect its core focus. Today, Target is a constituent of the S&P 500 and ranks among the largest retail chains in the United States.
