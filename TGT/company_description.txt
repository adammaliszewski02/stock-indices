Target Corporation, traded under the stock ticker TGT, is one of the largest general merchandise retailers in the United States. The company operates a vast chain of discount department stores offering a wide assortment of apparel, electronics, home goods, beauty products, and fresh groceries. In addition to physical brick-and-mortar locations, Target maintains a robust digital presence that integrates e-commerce with same-day fulfillment services, including curbside pickup and home delivery. The enterprise traces its origins to 1902 when George Draper Dayton founded the Dayton Dry Goods Company, which later evolved into the Dayton Hudson Corporation. In 1962, the company opened its first discount store in Roseville, Minnesota, under the Target name. Through strategic expansion, the acquisition of various regional department store chains, and a successful corporate rebranding in 2000, Target established itself as an industry leader recognized for its distinct red bullseye logo and focus on trendy, value-oriented merchandise. Headquartered in Minneapolis, Minnesota, Target continues to serve millions of consumers through its commitment to affordable style, convenience, and community involvement.
