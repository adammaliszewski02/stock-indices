Target Corporation (NYSE: TGT) is one of the largest general merchandise discount retailers in the United States. The company offers a diverse assortment of everyday essentials and trend-forward merchandise, encompassing apparel, home goods, beauty products, food and beverage, and electronics through both physical stores and digital channels.

The enterprise traces its origins to 1902, when George Draper Dayton established the Dayton Dry Goods Company in Minneapolis, Minnesota. In 1962, the organization opened the first Target discount store in Roseville, Minnesota, aiming to blend discount pricing with a higher-end department store experience. Previously operating as Dayton-Hudson Corporation, the parent enterprise officially rebranded as Target Corporation in 2000.

Today, Target operates nearly 2,000 stores across all 50 U.S. states and the District of Columbia. The company differentiates itself in the retail sector through a strong portfolio of exclusive owned brands, strategic designer partnerships, and an advanced omnichannel fulfillment network that leverages its retail locations as fulfillment hubs for drive-up, in-store pickup, and direct-to-home shipping.
