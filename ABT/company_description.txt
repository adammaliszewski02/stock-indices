Abbott Laboratories (ticker: ABT) is a leading, globally diversified healthcare company headquartered in Abbott Park, Illinois. Founded in 1888 by physician Wallace C. Abbott, the company operates across four primary segments: medical devices, diagnostics, nutritional products, and established pharmaceutical products. Abbott develops and markets a broad portfolio of health technologies that advance patient care worldwide, ranging from advanced cardiovascular and diabetes management systems, including the FreeStyle Libre glucose monitoring system, to essential diagnostic testing equipment and science-based nutritional brands like Similac and Ensure. Throughout its long history, the company has consistently focused on medical innovation, transitioning from a specialty drug manufacturer into a diversified healthcare leader dedicated to helping people live their best possible health at every stage of life.
