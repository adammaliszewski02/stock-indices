Abbott Laboratories, traded under the ticker ABT, is a globally diversified healthcare company headquartered in Abbott Park, Illinois. Founded in 1888 by Chicago physician Wallace C. Abbott, the firm operates across four primary business segments: medical devices, diagnostics, nutritional products, and established branded pharmaceuticals. Abbott develops, manufactures, and markets a broad portfolio of healthcare solutions, ranging from advanced cardiovascular and neuromodulation devices to life-changing continuous glucose monitoring systems under the FreeStyle Libre brand. Additionally, the company provides essential diagnostic testing systems and trusted nutrition products for infants, children, and adults worldwide. Driven by a long-standing commitment to scientific innovation, Abbott focuses on advancing medical technology and expanding global healthcare access to help people live fuller, healthier lives at all stages of development.
