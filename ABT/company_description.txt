Abbott Laboratories, traded under the ticker ABT, is a globally diversified healthcare company headquartered in Abbott Park, Illinois. Founded in 1888 by physician Wallace C. Abbott, the company has evolved from producing active alkaloid granules into a leading enterprise focused on improving human health through a broad portfolio of medical technologies, diagnostics, branded generic pharmaceuticals, and nutritional products. Abbott operates through four primary segments. Its medical devices division provides advanced solutions for cardiovascular care and diabetes management, notably pioneering the FreeStyle Libre continuous glucose monitoring system. The diagnostics branch delivers essential laboratory equipment and point-of-care testing systems used worldwide to screen, diagnose, and monitor diseases. Additionally, the nutritional business manufactures science-based infant formulas and adult health supplements, while the established pharmaceuticals division supplies trusted medicines primarily to emerging international markets. Driven by a commitment to innovation and accessibility, Abbott continues to shape modern healthcare by developing life-changing technologies that help people live fuller, healthier lives at every stage of development.
