Abbott Laboratories, traded under the ticker ABT, is a globally diversified healthcare company headquartered in Abbott Park, Illinois. Founded in 1888 by Chicago physician Wallace C. Abbott, the company originally specialized in the production of alkaloid granules. Today, Abbott operates across four primary business segments: established pharmaceuticals, nutritional products, diagnostic testing systems, and medical devices. The company is widely recognized for pioneering numerous healthcare advancements, including the introduction of the first commercial radioactive tracer and the creation of the Freestyle Libre continuous glucose monitoring system. Through its extensive portfolio, Abbott delivers science-based solutions that encompass life-changing medical technologies, laboratory diagnostics, and nutritional science, serving healthcare providers and patients in more than 160 countries worldwide.
