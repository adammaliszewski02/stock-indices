Abbott Laboratories, traded under the ticker ABT, is a globally diversified healthcare company headquartered in Abbott Park, Illinois. Founded in 1888 by Chicago physician Dr. Wallace C. Abbott, the company originally focused on the production of active alkaloids from medicinal plants. Today, Abbott operates across four primary business segments: medical devices, established pharmaceuticals, nutritional products, and diagnostics. Its portfolio includes market-leading innovations in cardiovascular devices, diabetes care technology such as the FreeStyle Libre continuous glucose monitoring system, molecular diagnostics, and scientifically formulated nutritional products for all stages of life. Abbott is dedicated to helping people live their best health through the continuous development of life-changing technologies and products that span the continuum of care, serving millions of patients and healthcare providers in more than 160 countries worldwide.
