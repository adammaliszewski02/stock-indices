Abbott Laboratories, traded under the ticker ABT, is a globally diversified healthcare company headquartered in Abbott Park, Illinois. Founded in 1888 by physician Wallace C. Abbott, the company has evolved from producing active alkaloids into a leading enterprise focused on improving human health through a broad portfolio of medical technologies, diagnostics, branded generic pharmaceuticals, and nutritional products. Abbott operates through four primary segments. Its medical devices division provides advanced technologies for rhythm management, electrophysiology, vascular disease, and diabetes care, notably including the FreeStyle Libre continuous glucose monitoring system. The diagnostics business delivers automation, informatics, and rapid testing solutions used worldwide by hospitals, laboratories, and blood banks. Additionally, the nutritional segment manufactures science-based pediatric and adult health powders and liquids, while the established pharmaceuticals division supplies differentiated medicines primarily to emerging markets. Throughout its history, Abbott has demonstrated a steadfast commitment to research and development, pioneering numerous breakthrough medical advancements that have shaped modern healthcare delivery globally.
