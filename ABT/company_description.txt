Abbott Laboratories, traded under the ticker ABT, is a globally diversified healthcare company headquartered in Abbott Park, Illinois. Founded in 1888 by physician Wallace C. Abbott, the company has evolved from pioneering the manufacture of active alkaloids into a leading enterprise spanning medical devices, diagnostics, nutritionals, and branded generic pharmaceuticals. Abbott operates through four primary segments. Its medical devices division provides advanced technologies for rhythm management, electrophysiology, heart failure, vascular disease, and diabetes care, most notably through its FreeStyle Libre continuous glucose monitoring system. The diagnostics business delivers immunoassay and molecular testing systems used worldwide by hospitals, laboratories, and blood banks. Additionally, the nutrition segment produces science-based pediatric and adult nutritional products, including the Similac and Ensure brands, while the established pharmaceuticals division markets a broad portfolio of branded generic medicines primarily in emerging markets. Through continuous innovation, strategic acquisitions, and global reach, Abbott is dedicated to helping people live fuller lives through better health.
