Abbott Laboratories, traded under the ticker ABT, is a globally diversified healthcare company focused on the discovery, development, manufacture, and sale of a broad range of health care products. Founded in Chicago in 1888 by physician Wallace C. Abbott to produce active alkaloids from medicinal plants, the company has evolved into a cornerstone of the medical industry. Headquartered in Abbott Park, Illinois, Abbott operates through four primary segments: established pharmaceuticals, nutritional products, diagnostic systems, and medical devices. Its portfolio features market-leading innovations, including advanced diabetes care technology such as the FreeStyle Libre continuous glucose monitoring system, cardiovascular devices, and essential diagnostic testing equipment. Abbott is committed to expanding global access to healthcare, improving treatment outcomes, and enhancing human health through continuous scientific research and technological advancement.
