Microsoft Corporation, identified by the stock ticker MSFT, is a multinational technology enterprise that develops, licenses, and supports a vast range of software products, services, and hardware devices. Founded in 1975 by Bill Gates and Paul Altos, the company initially gained prominence with its MS-DOS operating system before achieving global market dominance with the Microsoft Windows operating system and the Microsoft Office productivity suite. Over subsequent decades, Microsoft successfully expanded its portfolio into enterprise cloud computing through Microsoft Azure, video gaming via the Xbox brand, professional networking through LinkedIn, and advanced artificial intelligence integration. Today, the corporation operates as one of the world's most valuable technology conglomerates, serving both individual consumers and enterprise clients globally with solutions spanning intelligent cloud services, personal computing, and business process applications.
