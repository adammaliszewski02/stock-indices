Founded in 1975 by Bill Gates and Paul Allen, Microsoft Corporation (NASDAQ: MSFT) is a global technology leader headquartered in Redmond, Washington. The company revolutionized personal computing with MS-DOS and the Windows operating system, followed by the ubiquitous Microsoft Office productivity suite. 

Today, Microsoft operates across three core business segments: Productivity and Business Processes, Intelligent Cloud, and More Personal Computing. Its enterprise portfolio is anchored by the Azure cloud computing platform, Microsoft 365 software-as-a-service applications, the professional network LinkedIn, and Dynamics 365 business management tools. In consumer technology, Microsoft maintains a major presence through Windows, Surface computing hardware, and the Xbox gaming ecosystem, which includes hardware, content, and cloud gaming services. 

Under current leadership, the company has emerged as a dominant force in artificial intelligence. Through a multi-billion-dollar strategic partnership with OpenAI, Microsoft has integrated generative AI capabilities, branded as Copilot, across its search, developer, enterprise, and productivity software offerings, solidifying its role as an essential digital infrastructure provider worldwide.
