Microsoft Corporation, identified by the stock ticker MSFT, is a leading multinational technology company that develops, licenses, and supports a wide range of software products, services, and hardware devices. Founded in 1975 by Bill Gates and Paul Allen, the company initially gained prominence with the development of the MS-DOS operating system and subsequently revolutionized personal computing through the launch of the Microsoft Windows operating system and the Microsoft Office productivity suite. 

Today, Microsoft has evolved into a diversified global technology leader. Its core business segments encompass personal computing, business-process solutions, and intelligent cloud services. Through its Azure platform, the company is a dominant provider of cloud computing solutions for enterprises worldwide. Additionally, Microsoft’s portfolio includes enterprise software, developer tools, gaming hardware and software through Xbox, search advertising, and LinkedIn. Guided by its mission to empower every person and every organization on the planet to achieve more, Microsoft continues to drive industry innovation, heavily investing in artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, and enterprise-grade technological infrastructure.
