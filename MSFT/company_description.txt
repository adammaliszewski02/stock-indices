Microsoft Corporation, identified by the stock ticker MSFT, is a leading multinational technology company founded in 1975 by Bill Gates and Paul Altos. Headquartered in Redmond, Washington, the company develops, manufactures, licenses, supports, and sells computer software, consumer electronics, personal computers, and related services. 

Historically renowned for its MS-DOS operating system and the ubiquitous Microsoft Windows platform, the company has successfully evolved its business model to meet modern technological demands. Today, Microsoft is a dominant force in cloud computing through Microsoft Azure, enterprise productivity via Microsoft 365, and gaming through the Xbox ecosystem. Furthermore, the company has expanded its technological footprint significantly through strategic acquisitions, including LinkedIn and GitHub, and has positioned itself at the forefront of artificial intelligence development through its extensive partnership with OpenAI.
