Microsoft Corporation, identified by the ticker MSFT, is a multinational technology enterprise that develops, licenses, and supports a vast range of software products, services, devices, and hardware solutions. Founded in 1975 by Bill Gates and Paul Allen, the company initially gained global prominence through its MS-DOS operating system and subsequent Windows platforms, alongside the Microsoft Office productivity suite. 

Today, Microsoft operates as a diversified industry leader encompassing cloud computing, artificial intelligence, gaming, and enterprise services. Its core offerings include the Azure cloud platform, LinkedIn, GitHub, and the Xbox gaming ecosystem, supported by significant investments in enterprise software and consumer hardware. Driven by a mission to empower every person and organization on the planet to achieve more, Microsoft continues to pioneer advancements in generative artificial intelligence and digital infrastructure, maintaining its position as one of the world's most valuable and influential technology corporations.
