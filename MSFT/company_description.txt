Microsoft Corporation, trading under the ticker MSFT, is a multinational technology leader founded in 1975 by Bill Gates and Paul Allen. The company initially rose to global prominence by revolutionizing personal computing with MS-DOS and the Windows operating system. Today, Microsoft operates at the forefront of the technology sector, delivering a comprehensive portfolio of software, cloud infrastructure, hardware, and digital services. 

The company's primary business segments include Productivity and Business Processes, Intelligent Cloud, and More Personal Computing. Flagship products and services encompass the Windows operating system, the Microsoft 365 productivity suite, enterprise management tools like Dynamics 365, the LinkedIn professional network, and the Xbox gaming ecosystem. In recent decades, Microsoft successfully pivoted toward enterprise cloud computing with Microsoft Azure, which has become one of its largest revenue drivers. Headquartered in Redmond, Washington, the company is also an aggressive leader in artificial intelligence, heavily investing in proprietary AI research and integrating generative AI capabilities across its entire product lineup through strategic partnerships, notably with OpenAI.
