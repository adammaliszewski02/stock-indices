Microsoft Corporation, traded under the ticker MSFT, is a multinational technology enterprise that develops, licenses, and supports a vast range of software products, services, devices, and solutions. Founded in 1975 by Bill Gates and Paul Altos, the company initially gained prominence with its MS-DOS operating system before achieving global market dominance with the Microsoft Windows operating system and the Microsoft Office productivity suite. 

Today, Microsoft has evolved into a diversified technology titan. Its core operations encompass personal computing, business process solutions, and cloud infrastructure. The company operates the Azure cloud computing platform, which is a major driver of its enterprise revenue, alongside the LinkedIn professional network and Xbox gaming ecosystem. In recent years, Microsoft has established a leading position in artificial intelligence through strategic partnerships and the integration of advanced machine learning capabilities across its product portfolio.
