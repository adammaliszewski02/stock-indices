Microsoft Corporation (NASDAQ: MSFT) is a global technology leader founded in 1975 by Bill Gates and Paul Allen. Initially rising to dominance through its MS-DOS and Windows operating systems, the company has transformed from a personal computer software pioneer into a diversified, cloud-first enterprise. 

Microsoft operates across three primary business segments: Productivity and Business Processes, Intelligent Cloud, and More Personal Computing. Its flagship offerings include the Microsoft 365 software suite, the Azure cloud computing platform, the Windows operating system, Dynamics 365 business solutions, and the LinkedIn professional network. The company also maintains a significant presence in hardware and entertainment through its Xbox gaming brand and Surface line of personal computers. In recent years, Microsoft has positioned itself at the forefront of the artificial intelligence sector, embedding advanced AI capabilities across its ecosystem, bolstered by a major strategic partnership with OpenAI. Today, Microsoft delivers software, infrastructure, platforms, and digital services to consumers, educational institutions, and enterprises worldwide.
