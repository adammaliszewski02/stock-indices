Bristol-Myers Squibb Company, trading under the ticker BMY, is a leading global biopharmaceutical company dedicated to discovering, developing, and delivering innovative medicines that help patients prevail over serious diseases. Headquartered in Princeton, New Jersey, the company focuses primarily on therapeutic areas such as oncology, immunology, cardiovascular disease, and hematology. 

The company's origins trace back to the 1887 founding of Bristol-Myers by William McLaren Bristol and John Ripley Myers, initially operating as a drug and manufacturing firm. Over the decades, the enterprise expanded significantly through internal research and strategic acquisitions. A major milestone occurred in 1989 when Bristol-Myers merged with Squibb Corporation, a venerable pharmaceutical pioneer founded in 1858 by Dr. Edward Robinson Squibb, forming Bristol-Myers Squibb. Further strengthening its position in modern biotechnology, the company completed a landmark acquisition of Celgene Corporation in 2019. 

Today, BMY leverages advanced scientific platforms and strategic collaborations to advance a robust pipeline of novel treatments. By combining its rich heritage of pharmaceutical innovation with cutting-edge research, the company continues to address significant unmet medical needs worldwide and improve global health outcomes.
