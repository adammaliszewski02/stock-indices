Bristol Myers Squibb, trading under the ticker BMY on the New York Stock Exchange, is a leading global biopharmaceutical company dedicated to discovering, developing, and delivering innovative medicines. The company focuses on treating serious conditions across key therapeutic areas, including oncology, hematology, immunology, cardiovascular diseases, and neuroscience. Its commercial portfolio includes widely prescribed treatments such as the anticoagulant Eliquis and the immuno-oncology drug Opdivo.

The modern firm traces its heritage back to the 19th century. Dr. Edward Robinson Squibb founded the Squibb Corporation in 1858, pioneering standardized pharmaceutical manufacturing, while William McLaren Bristol and John Ripley Myers established the business that became Bristol-Myers in 1887. The two legacy companies merged in 1989 to create Bristol-Myers Squibb. 

In recent years, the company has transformed into a specialized specialty biopharma firm, highlighted by its landmark $74 billion acquisition of Celgene in 2019, which dramatically expanded its oncology and cellular therapy pipeline. Headquartered in Princeton, New Jersey, Bristol Myers Squibb continues to invest heavily in scientific research and next-generation therapies to address complex, unmet global medical needs.
