Bristol-Myers Squibb Company (ticker: BMY) is a leading global biopharmaceutical company dedicated to discovering, developing, and delivering innovative medicines that help patients prevail over serious diseases. Headquartered in Princeton, New Jersey, the company focuses on therapeutic areas such as oncology, immunology, cardiovascular disease, and fibrosis. Bristol-Myers Squibb traces its origins to the 1858 founding of Squibb by Dr. Edward Robinson Squibb, followed by the establishment of Bristol-Myers in 1887 by William McLaren Bristol and John Ripley Myers. The two entities merged in 1989 to form Bristol-Myers Squibb. Over the decades, the company has evolved from a traditional pharmaceutical manufacturer into a research-driven biopharma leader, bolstered by major strategic milestones including the transformative 2019 acquisition of Celgene. Today, BMY leverages cutting-edge science and advanced modalities, including cell therapy and immuno-oncology, to address significant unmet medical needs worldwide and drive long-term value for patients and shareholders alike.
