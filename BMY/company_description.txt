Bristol Myers Squibb (ticker: BMY) is a leading global biopharmaceutical company dedicated to discovering, developing, and delivering innovative medicines that help patients prevail over serious diseases. Headquartered in Princeton, New Jersey, the company focuses its therapeutic expertise primarily on oncology, hematology, immunology, and cardiovascular disease. Bristol Myers Squibb traces its roots to 1858 when William McLaren Bristol and John Ripley Myers founded Bristol, Myers Company in Clinton, New York. Over the decades, the firm expanded significantly through strategic acquisitions, most notably merging with E.R. Squibb & Sons in 1989 to form the modern enterprise, and later acquiring Celgene in 2019 to further bolster its oncology and immunology portfolios. Today, BMY leverages cutting-edge science and advanced research platforms to address significant unmet medical needs worldwide, maintaining a robust pipeline of novel therapeutics designed to transform clinical practice and improve patient outcomes globally.
