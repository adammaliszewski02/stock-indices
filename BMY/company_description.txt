Bristol Myers Squibb (ticker: BMY) is a leading global biopharmaceutical company dedicated to discovering, developing, and delivering innovative medicines that help patients prevail over serious diseases. Headquartered in Princeton, New Jersey, the company focuses primarily on therapeutic areas such as oncology, hematology, cardiovascular disease, and immunology. 

The company's origins trace back to 1887, when William McLaren Bristol and John Ripley Myers founded the Bristol, Myers Company in Clinton, New York. Over the decades, the firm expanded significantly through internal research and major strategic acquisitions. A pivotal milestone occurred in 1989 when Bristol-Myers merged with the pharmaceutical giant E.R. Squibb & Sons, forming Bristol-Myers Squibb. More recently, the company significantly bolstered its oncology and immunology portfolios through the multi-billion-dollar acquisition of Celgene in 2019. 

Today, BMY leverages advanced scientific platforms and strategic collaborations to advance a robust pipeline of complex biologics and targeted therapies, maintaining its position as a cornerstone of the modern healthcare industry.
