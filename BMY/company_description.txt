Bristol-Myers Squibb Company (ticker: BMY) is a leading global biopharmaceutical company dedicated to discovering, developing, and delivering innovative medicines that help patients prevail over serious diseases. Incorporated in Delaware and headquartered in Princeton, New Jersey, the company traces its roots to the 1858 founding of pharmaceutical manufacturer Squibb by Dr. Edward Robinson Squibb, and the 1887 founding of Bristol-Myers by William McLaren Bristol and John Ripley Myers. The two legacy enterprises merged in 1989 to form Bristol-Myers Squibb. Today, BMY operates primarily in the areas of oncology, immunology, cardiovascular disease, and fibrosis. By leveraging advanced scientific research and strategic acquisitions—most notably the transformative 2019 purchase of Celgene Corporation—the enterprise continues to advance a robust pipeline of breakthrough therapies designed to address significant unmet medical needs worldwide.
