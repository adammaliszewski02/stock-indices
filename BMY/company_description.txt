Bristol-Myers Squibb Company, trading under the ticker BMY, is a leading global biopharmaceutical company dedicated to discovering, developing, and delivering innovative medicines that help patients prevail over serious diseases. Headquartered in Princeton, New Jersey, the company focuses primarily on therapeutic areas such as oncology, immunology, cardiovascular disease, and hematology. 

The enterprise traces its origins to the 1858 establishment of Bristol-Myers by William McLaren Bristol and John Ripley Myers, alongside the 1887 founding of Squibb by Edward Robinson Squibb. These two foundational entities merged in 1989 to form Bristol-Myers Squibb, creating one of the world's foremost pharmaceutical enterprises. Over the decades, the company has evolved from a broad health and personal care products manufacturer into a specialized, science-driven biopharma leader. This strategic transformation was significantly advanced through major acquisitions, most notably the 2009 purchase of Celgene, which substantially expanded the company's oncology and immunology pipeline. Today, BMY continues to leverage cutting-edge science and strategic collaborations to advance a robust portfolio of breakthrough therapies that address critical, unmet medical needs worldwide.
