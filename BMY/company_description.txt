Bristol-Myers Squibb Company, trading under the ticker BMY, is a leading global biopharmaceutical company dedicated to discovering, developing, and delivering innovative medicines that help patients prevail over serious diseases. Headquartered in Princeton, New Jersey, the company focuses primarily on oncology, immunology, cardiovascular disease, and fibrosis. 

The enterprise traces its origins to the 1887 founding of pharmaceutical firm Bristol, Myers Company by William McLaren Bristol and John Ripley Myers, alongside the 1858 establishment of Squibb by Edward Robinson Squibb. The two entities officially merged in 1989 to form Bristol-Myers Squibb, creating one of the world's foremost healthcare corporations. Over the decades, BMY has significantly expanded its therapeutic pipeline and global research capabilities, most notably through strategic acquisitions such as its landmark 2019 purchase of Celgene. Today, Bristol-Myers Squibb continues to leverage advanced scientific research and biotechnology to address some of the world's most complex medical challenges, maintaining a robust portfolio of breakthrough therapies and an active pipeline of novel treatments.
