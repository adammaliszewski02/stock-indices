NKE is the stock ticker for NIKE, Inc., the world’s leading designer, marketer, and distributor of authentic athletic footwear, apparel, equipment, and accessories. Founded in 1964 as Blue Ribbon Sports by Bill Bowerman and Phil Knight, the company officially adopted the name Nike in 1971, taking inspiration from the Greek goddess of victory. Headquartered near Beaverton, Oregon, the company expanded rapidly through innovative product design, prominent athlete endorsements, and iconic branding, notably introducing the "Swoosh" logo and the "Just Do It" slogan. Today, Nike operates a vast global portfolio that includes its namesake brand as well as Jordan and Converse, serving consumers in approximately 190 countries. The company delivers its products through a robust mix of wholesale partners, digital platforms, and direct-to-consumer retail stores.
