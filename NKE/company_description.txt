NIKE, Inc., trading under the ticker NKE, is a multinational corporation that designs, develops, markets, and sells high-performance athletic footwear, apparel, equipment, and accessories worldwide. Founded in 1964 as Blue Ribbon Sports by Bill Bowerman and Phil Knight, the company officially adopted the name Nike in 1978, taking inspiration from the Greek goddess of victory. Headquartered near Beaverton, Oregon, Nike has grown into the world's largest supplier of athletic shoes and sports apparel. The company operates through a robust portfolio of brands that includes Jordan, Converse, and Hurley. Over the decades, Nike revolutionized sports marketing through iconic branding, groundbreaking technological innovations such as Nike Air, and high-profile partnerships with legendary athletes. Today, Nike distributes its products through a vast global network of company-owned retail stores, digital platforms, and independent distributors, continually adapting its business model to focus on direct-to-consumer digital commerce while maintaining its status as a dominant force in global sports and lifestyle culture.
