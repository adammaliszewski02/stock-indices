Nike, Inc. (NYSE: NKE) is the world's leading designer, marketer, and distributor of authentic athletic footwear, apparel, equipment, and accessories for a wide variety of sports and fitness activities. 

The company was founded in 1964 as Blue Ribbon Sports by University of Oregon track coach Bill Bowerman and his former student Phil Knight. Initially operating as a distributor for Japanese shoemaker Onitsuka Tiger, the business officially adopted the name Nike, Inc. in 1971, named after the Greek goddess of victory, coinciding with the launch of its iconic "Swoosh" logo. 

Headquartered near Beaverton, Oregon, Nike manages a premier brand portfolio that includes the Nike brand, Jordan, and Converse. The company drives growth through continuous product innovation, high-profile athlete endorsements, and a global multi-channel distribution strategy that spans wholesale partnerships, company-owned retail locations, and an expanding direct-to-consumer digital commerce ecosystem. Today, Nike remains the preeminent player in the global sportswear and athletic equipment industry.
