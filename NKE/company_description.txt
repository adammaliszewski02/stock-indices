Nike, Inc., trading under the ticker NKE, is the world's leading designer, marketer, and distributor of authentic athletic footwear, apparel, equipment, and accessories. Founded in 1964 as Blue Ribbon Sports by University of Oregon track coach Bill Bowerman and his former student Phil Knight, the company originally operated as a distributor for Japanese shoemaker Onitsuka Tiger. It officially rebranded as Nike, Inc. in 1971, taking its name from the Greek goddess of victory and introducing the iconic Swoosh logo designed by Carolyn Davidson. 

Today, Nike operates on a global scale, serving athletes and consumers through an omnichannel model that blends wholesale partnerships with a robust direct-to-consumer network of digital platforms and company-owned retail stores. In addition to its flagship namesake brand, the company’s portfolio includes the Jordan Brand and Converse. Nike's enduring market leadership is driven by continuous product innovation, cultural marketing, and high-profile endorsements with premier athletes and sports teams worldwide.
