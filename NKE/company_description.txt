NIKE, Inc., identified by the stock ticker NKE, is a premier global designer, marketer, and distributor of authentic athletic footwear, apparel, equipment, and accessories. Founded in 1964 as Blue Ribbon Sports by Bill Bowerman and Phil Knight, the enterprise officially adopted the name Nike in 1978, drawing inspiration from the Greek goddess of victory. The company achieved early acclaim with the introduction of its iconic "Swoosh" logo in 1971 and revolutionized the industry with the 1979 debut of Nike Air cushioning technology. Today, NKE manages an extensive portfolio that includes the core Nike brand, Jordan Brand, and Converse. The enterprise sells its innovative products worldwide through a vast network of wholesale accounts, digital platforms, and company-operated retail stores, solidifying its position as a dominant force in the global sportswear and lifestyle market.
