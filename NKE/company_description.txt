NIKE, Inc., trading under the ticker NKE, is a multinational corporation that designs, develops, markets, and sells athletic footwear, apparel, equipment, and accessories worldwide. Headquartered near Beaverton, Oregon, the company was founded in 1964 as Blue Ribbon Sports by Bill Bowerman and Phil Knight, officially adopting the Nike name in 1978. Named after the Greek goddess of victory, Nike has grown into the world's largest supplier of athletic shoes and sports apparel. The company operates through a portfolio of powerful brands, which historically includes Converse and Jordan, the latter of which was launched in collaboration with basketball legend Michael Jordan in 1984. Nike distributes its products globally through a combination of owned retail stores, digital platforms, and a vast network of independent wholesale accounts. Furthermore, the company is renowned for its iconic "Swoosh" logo and its influential "Just Do It" marketing campaign, introduced in 1988, which helped cement its status as a dominant cultural and commercial force in the global sporting goods industry.
