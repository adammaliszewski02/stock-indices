Nike, Inc., trading under the stock ticker NKE, is a multinational corporation that designs, develops, markets, and sells high-performance athletic footwear, apparel, equipment, and accessories worldwide. Founded in 1964 as Blue Ribbon Sports by Bill Bowerman and Phil Knight, the company officially adopted the name Nike in 1971, taking inspiration from the Greek goddess of victory. Headquartered near Beaverton, Oregon, Nike distributes its products under its core brand as well as through subsidiaries, including Jordan and Converse. Driven by iconic marketing campaigns, technological innovation, and prominent athlete endorsements, Nike has evolved into the world's largest supplier of athletic shoes and apparel, shaping the global sports culture and retail landscape for decades.
