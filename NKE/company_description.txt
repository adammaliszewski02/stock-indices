NKE is the stock ticker for NIKE, Inc., the world’s leading designer, marketer, and distributor of authentic athletic footwear, apparel, equipment, and accessories. Founded in 1964 as Blue Ribbon Sports by Bill Bowerman and Phil Knight, the company officially adopted the Nike name in 1978, inspired by the Greek goddess of victory. Headquartered near Beaverton, Oregon, the company manages its business through a diverse portfolio of powerhouse brands, including Nike, Jordan, and Converse. Over the decades, Nike revolutionized sports marketing through iconic endorsements with legendary athletes and its globally recognized "Swoosh" logo and "Just Do It" tagline. Today, Nike operates a vast global retail and digital ecosystem, continually driving innovation in athletic performance wear and streetwear culture while maintaining a strong commitment to sustainability and supply chain transparency.
