Pfizer Inc. is a leading American multinational pharmaceutical and biotechnology corporation headquartered in New York City. Founded in 1849 by cousins Charles Pfizer and Charles Erhart as a fine chemical business, the company initially found success in the production of antiparasitic agents and expanded rapidly during World War II to mass-produce penicillin for Allied forces. Over the decades, Pfizer transitioned into a research-based pharmaceutical enterprise, achieving significant commercial milestones with blockbuster medications such as Lipitor, Viagra, and various immunology and oncology treatments. More recently, the company solidified its status as a global healthcare titan by partnering with BioNTech to develop one of the first safe and effective mRNA vaccines against COVID-19. Today, Pfizer discovers, develops, and distributes a broad portfolio of prescription medicines, vaccines, and consumer healthcare products aimed at addressing some of the world's most challenging diseases.
