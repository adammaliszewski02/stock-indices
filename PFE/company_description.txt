Pfizer Inc., traded under the stock ticker PFE, is a leading American multinational pharmaceutical and biotechnology corporation headquartered in New York City. Founded in 1849 by cousins Charles Pfizer and Charles F. Erhart as a fine chemicals business, the company initially focused on the production of parasiticides and industrial chemicals. Over the decades, Pfizer evolved into a research-based pharmaceutical enterprise, achieving major milestones such as mass-producing penicillin during World War II and introducing blockbuster medications including Lipitor. Today, Pfizer discovers, develops, and manufactures a diverse portfolio of healthcare products, including innovative medicines, vaccines, and consumer healthcare solutions. The company operates on a global scale, serving patients in more than 125 countries, and remains at the forefront of medical advancement through significant investments in research and development, most notably demonstrated by its co-development of the first authorized mRNA COVID-19 vaccine.
