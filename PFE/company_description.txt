Pfizer Inc. (ticker: PFE) is a leading American multinational pharmaceutical and biotechnology corporation headquartered in New York City. Founded in 1849 by cousins Charles Pfizer and Charles Erhart as a fine chemicals business, the company initially established its reputation in the production of citric acid and later played a pivotal role in mass-producing penicillin to support Allied forces during World War II. Over the decades, Pfizer transitioned into a research-based pharmaceutical enterprise, achieving significant commercial success through the development of blockbuster medications such as Lipitor and Viagra. Today, the company discovers, develops, and delivers a broad portfolio of healthcare products, including innovative medicines, vaccines, and consumer healthcare solutions. Its extensive global operations focus on addressing complex therapeutic areas such as oncology, immunology, cardiology, and rare diseases. Most recently, Pfizer gained widespread international recognition for its collaborative development of a breakthrough mRNA-based COVID-19 vaccine, reinforcing its longstanding position at the forefront of global medical innovation and public health.
