Pfizer Inc., traded under the ticker PFE, is a leading global biopharmaceutical company engaged in the discovery, development, and manufacture of healthcare products, including innovative medicines and vaccines. Founded in New York in 1849 by cousins Charles Pfizer and Charles Erhart, the company originally began as a fine chemical business, initially producing an antiparasitic compound called santonin. Over the decades, Pfizer evolved into a pharmaceutical powerhouse, achieving significant milestones such as the mass production of penicillin to support Allied forces during World War II and the subsequent development of numerous blockbuster medications, including Lipitor. Today, Pfizer leverages advanced science and global resources to deliver therapies that extend and significantly improve lives, focusing on key therapeutic areas such as immunology, oncology, cardiology, and infectious diseases.
