PFE is the stock ticker for Pfizer Inc., one of the world's premier biopharmaceutical companies. Headquartered in New York City, Pfizer discovers, develops, manufactures, and markets healthcare products, including innovative medicines and vaccines. Founded in 1849 by cousins Charles Pfizer and Charles Erhart as a fine chemicals business in Brooklyn, the company initially found success with the production of santonin, an antiparasitic agent. 

Historically, Pfizer played a critical role in the mass production of penicillin for Allied troops during World War II, establishing its foundation in large-scale fermentation technology. Over the decades, the company expanded significantly through internal research and major acquisitions, such as Warner-Lambert, Pharmacia, and Wyeth, bringing blockbuster drugs like Lipitor to the global market. In recent years, Pfizer achieved historic prominence by partnering with BioNTech to rapidly develop and distribute Comirnaty, one of the first authorized vaccines to combat the COVID-19 pandemic. Today, Pfizer continues to focus on oncology, immunology, cardiology, and rare diseases, leveraging its scientific expertise to address significant global health challenges.
