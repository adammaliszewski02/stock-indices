Pfizer Inc., traded under the stock ticker PFE, is a leading American multinational pharmaceutical and biotechnology corporation headquartered in New York City. Founded in 1849 by cousins Charles Pfizer and Charles Erhart as a fine chemicals business, the company initially gained prominence through the production of santonin and the mass production of penicillin during World War II. Over the decades, Pfizer evolved into a research-based biopharmaceutical enterprise, discovering and developing a broad portfolio of medicines, vaccines, and consumer healthcare products. Key therapeutic areas include oncology, immunology, cardiology, neurology, and rare diseases. Notably, the company partnered with BioNTech to develop Comirnaty, one of the world's primary vaccines against COVID-19. Today, Pfizer operates globally, engaging in the discovery, development, manufacture, and distribution of innovative healthcare solutions designed to prevent and treat complex conditions, thereby improving patient outcomes worldwide.
