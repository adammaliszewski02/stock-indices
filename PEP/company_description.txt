PepsiCo, Inc., trading under the ticker symbol PEP, is a leading global food and beverage corporation headquartered in Purchase, New York. The company’s origins trace back to 1898 with the invention of Pepsi-Cola by pharmacist Caleb Bradham, followed by the 1965 merger of the Pepsi-Cola Company and Frito-Lay, Inc. Today, PepsiCo operates on a global scale, manufacturing, marketing, and distributing a diverse portfolio of grain-based snacks, carbonated and non-carbonated beverages, and dairy products. Its iconic brand lineup includes Pepsi, Quaker Oats, Gatorade, Frito-Lay, Tropicana, and Mountain Dew. The enterprise manages a robust operational ecosystem encompassing direct-store delivery networks and manufacturing facilities worldwide. PepsiCo remains steadfastly focused on consumer-driven innovation, operational efficiency, and sustainability initiatives, notably through its pep+ (PepsiCo Positive) transformation agenda, which integrates environmental stewardship and health objectives throughout its entire value chain to drive long-term value for shareholders and society.
