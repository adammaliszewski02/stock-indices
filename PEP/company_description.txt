PepsiCo, Inc. (ticker: PEP) is a multinational food, snack, and beverage corporation headquartered in Purchase, New York. The company manufactures, markets, and distributes a globally recognized portfolio of grain-based snacks, carbonated and non-carbonated beverages, and dairy products. Its diverse brand ecosystem includes Pepsi-Cola, Frito-Lay, Gatorade, Quaker, and Tropicana, serving consumers in more than 200 countries and territories. 

The enterprise traces its modern origins to the 1965 merger of the Pepsi-Cola Company, originally formulated by pharmacist Caleb Bradham in the late 1890s, and Frito-Lay, Inc., which was founded by Herman Lay and C.E. Doolin. Over the subsequent decades, PepsiCo expanded its product offerings significantly through strategic acquisitions, notably adding Tropicana in 1998, Quaker Oats—along with Gatorade—in 2001, and a controlling stake in Pioneer Foods in 2020. Today, PepsiCo operates under a dual-pronged business model centered on convenient foods and beverages, driven by a commitment to sustainable agriculture and packaging through its pep+ (PepsiCo Positive) transformation framework.
