PepsiCo, Inc., traded under the ticker PEP, is a leading global food and beverage corporation with a product portfolio spanning hundreds of iconic brands, including Pepsi-Cola, Quaker Oats, Gatorade, Frito-Lay, and Tropicana. Headquartered in Purchase, New York, the company manufactures, markets, and distributes grain-based snack foods, beverages, and other convenience products in more than 200 countries and territories. PepsiCo’s operational roots trace back to the late 19th century with the creation of Pepsi-Cola by pharmacist Caleb Bradham in 1898. The modern enterprise was established through the pivotal 1965 merger of the Pepsi-Cola Company and Frito-Lay, Inc., a strategic combination that successfully unified the beverage and snack industries. Over subsequent decades, PepsiCo expanded its footprint significantly through major acquisitions, including Tropicana in 1998 and the Quaker Oats Company in 2001, which further integrated Gatorade into its portfolio. Today, the company operates under its strategic framework known as PepsiCo Positive, which focuses on sustainable agriculture, water stewardship, and evolving its product portfolio toward healthier nutritional options, ensuring long-term value creation for shareholders and consumers alike.
