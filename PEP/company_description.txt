PepsiCo, Inc., traded under the ticker PEP, is a leading global food and beverage corporation renowned for its diverse portfolio of iconic brands. Founded in 1965 through the merger of the Pepsi-Cola Company and Frito-Lay, the company has its roots in the late 19th century when pharmacist Caleb Bradham invented the carbonated soft drink Pepsi-Cola in 1898. Headquartered in Purchase, New York, PepsiCo operates globally, manufacturing, marketing, and distributing a wide array of grain-based snacks, carbonated and non-carbonated beverages, and dairy products. Its prominent brand roster includes Pepsi, Lay's, Gatorade, Quaker, Tropicana, 7 Up, and Doritos. The company serves consumers in more than 200 countries and territories, organized across geographic sectors and convenient-foods and beverage divisions. PepsiCo remains committed to sustainable agriculture and reducing its environmental footprint through its strategic transformation initiative, pep+.
