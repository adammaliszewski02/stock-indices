PepsiCo, Inc., traded under the ticker PEP, is a leading global food, beverage, and snack corporation headquartered in Purchase, New York. The company’s origins date back to 1898 when chemist Caleb Bradham created the soft drink Pepsi-Cola, which later merged with snack-food giant Frito-Lay in 1965 to form PepsiCo. Today, the enterprise manages a massive portfolio of iconic brands that generate billions of dollars in annual retail sales, including Pepsi, Gatorade, Quaker, Tropicana, Lay’s, Doritos, and Gatorade. Operating in more than 200 countries and territories, PepsiCo's business model spans both convenient foods and beverage manufacturing, distribution, and marketing. The corporation distributes its products through a vast network of direct-store-delivery systems, independent bottlers, and retail partners, serving millions of consumers daily. In recent years, the company has increasingly focused on sustainability, health-conscious alternatives, and digital transformation to adapt to shifting global consumer preferences while maintaining its position as a dominant force in the fast-moving consumer goods sector.
