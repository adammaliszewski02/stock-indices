The ticker PEP corresponds to PepsiCo, Inc., a leading global food and beverage corporation headquartered in Purchase, New York. The company’s origins date back to 1898 when North Carolina pharmacist Caleb Bradham formulated Pepsi-Cola, which officially merged with the Quaker Oats Company in 2001 and later acquired Tropicana and Gatorade. Today, PepsiCo operates on a global scale, manufacturing, marketing, and distributing a diverse portfolio of grain-based snacks, beverages, and convenience foods. Its business model centers on two primary divisions: convenient foods, featuring prominent global brands like Lay's, Doritos, Cheetos, and Quaker, and the beverage segment, anchored by Pepsi-Cola, Gatorade, Mountain Dew, and Aquafina. Through strategic acquisitions, product innovation, and an extensive direct-store-delivery and warehouse distribution network, PepsiCo serves consumers in more than two hundred countries and territories. The corporation balances its expansive brand portfolio with a growing emphasis on sustainable agriculture, water conservation, and healthier product alternatives to address evolving consumer preferences.
