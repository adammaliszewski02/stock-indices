PepsiCo, Inc., traded under the ticker PEP, is a leading global food, beverage, and snack corporation headquartered in Purchase, New York. The company’s origins trace back to 1898 when chemist Caleb Bradham invented the carbonated soft drink Pepsi-Cola. In 1965, Pepsi-Cola merged with Frito-Lay, Inc., creating PepsiCo and establishing the company's dual foundation in both beverages and convenient foods. Today, PepsiCo operates a massive portfolio that includes world-renowned brands such as Pepsi, Gatorade, Quaker, Tropicana, Lay’s, Doritos, and Quaker Oats. The business operates on a global scale, spanning agricultural sourcing, manufacturing, marketing, and distribution across more than 200 countries and territories. PepsiCo generates revenue through a diverse network of direct-store-delivery systems, independent bottlers, and retail partnerships, serving both wholesale and direct-to-consumer markets. In recent years, the corporation has increasingly emphasized sustainability, health-conscious product formulations, and supply chain efficiency through its strategic pep+ (PepsiCo Positive) transformation framework.
