United Parcel Service, Inc., trading under the ticker symbol UPS, is a global leader in logistics, offering a broad range of solutions including the transportation of packages and freight, the facilitation of international trade, and the deployment of advanced technology to efficiently manage the world's business. Founded in 1907 as the American Messenger Company in Seattle, Washington, by Jim Casey and Claude Ryan, the enterprise initially focused on telegraph message delivery and foot messenger services. Over more than a century, the company evolved from a regional messenger service into a premier multinational package delivery and supply chain management corporation. Today, UPS operates one of the world's largest and most efficient transportation networks, connecting millions of customers daily across more than 200 countries and territories. The company continually adapts its business model to meet the demands of modern commerce, integrating sustainable practices and innovative digital solutions to support global supply chains, small businesses, and enterprise-level logistics operations.
