United Parcel Service, Inc. (UPS) is a global leader in logistics, offering a broad range of solutions that include the transportation of packages and freight, the facilitation of international trade, and the deployment of advanced technology to efficiently manage the world's flow of business. Founded in 1907 in Seattle, Washington, by Jim Casey and Claude Ryan as the American Messenger Company, the enterprise originally specialized in message delivery, baggage handling, and retail store deliveries. Over the decades, it expanded nationwide and internationally, eventually rebranding as United Parcel Service in 1930. Today, UPS operates one of the world's largest and most efficient air and ground transportation networks, serving more than 200 countries and territories. The company leverages its extensive infrastructure, advanced data analytics, and dedicated workforce to provide critical supply chain management, freight forwarding, and small-package delivery services to millions of residential and commercial customers globally.
