United Parcel Service, Inc. (UPS) is a global leader in logistics, offering a broad range of solutions that include the transportation of packages and freight, the facilitation of international trade, and the deployment of advanced technology to efficiently manage the world of business. Founded in 1907 in Seattle, Washington, as the American Messenger Company by 19-year-old Jim Casey and Claude Ryan, the enterprise began as a local courier service. Over more than a century, it evolved from a regional bicycle messenger operation into a premier multinational corporation. Today, UPS operates one of the world's largest and most efficient package delivery networks, serving more than 200 countries and territories. The company leverages an integrated global air and ground network to provide specialized transportation, distribution, and logistics services, continually adapting its infrastructure to meet the demands of modern commerce and supply chain management.
