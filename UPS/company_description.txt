United Parcel Service, Inc. (UPS) is a global leader in logistics, offering a broad range of solutions that include the transportation of packages and freight, the facilitation of international trade, and the deployment of advanced technology to efficiently manage the world's flow of business. Founded in 1907 in Seattle, Washington, as the American Messenger Company by 19-year-old James E. Casey and Claude Ryan, the enterprise initially focused on telegraph delivery. Over the decades, it evolved from a local messenger service into a common carrier, expanding its operations nationwide and eventually establishing a comprehensive global network. Today, headquartered in Atlanta, Georgia, UPS operates one of the world's largest airline fleets and a massive ground delivery infrastructure, serving millions of customers across more than 200 countries and territories. The company continually adapts its business model to meet the demands of modern commerce, emphasizing supply chain optimization, sustainability initiatives, and e-commerce fulfillment services while maintaining its foundational commitment to reliability and operational efficiency.
