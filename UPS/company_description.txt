United Parcel Service, Inc. (UPS) is a global leader in logistics, offering a broad range of solutions that include the transportation of packages and freight, the facilitation of international trade, and the deployment of advanced technology to efficiently manage the world's flow of business. Founded in 1907 in Seattle, Washington, as the American Messenger Company by 19-year-old James E. Casey, the enterprise initially focused on telegraph message deliveries and foot-and-bicycle courier services. Over the decades, it evolved from a regional messenger service into a premier common carrier, expanding its operations nationwide and eventually internationally. Today, UPS operates one of the largest and most efficient global transportation networks, providing comprehensive supply chain management services to millions of customers across more than 200 countries and territories. Headquartered in Atlanta, Georgia, the company continues to drive innovation in sustainable shipping and digital logistics while maintaining its core commitment to reliability and customer service.
