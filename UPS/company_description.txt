United Parcel Service, Inc. (UPS) is a global leader in logistics, offering a broad range of solutions including the transportation of packages and freight, the facilitation of international trade, and the deployment of advanced technology to manage the world's supply chains. Headquartered in Atlanta, Georgia, the enterprise was founded in 1907 as the American Messenger Company by 19-year-old James E. Casey in Seattle, Washington, with a modest capital of one hundred dollars. Over more than a century, the company evolved from a local messenger service into a multi-billion-dollar corporation, driven by strategic expansions into retail shipping, supply chain management, and air freight through its proprietary airline, UPS Airlines. Today, UPS operates one of the most comprehensive and efficient transportation networks in the world, serving millions of customers across more than 200 countries and territories.
