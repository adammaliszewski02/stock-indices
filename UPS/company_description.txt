United Parcel Service, Inc. (UPS) is a global leader in logistics, offering a broad range of solutions that include the transportation of packages and freight, the facilitation of international trade, and the deployment of advanced technology to efficiently manage the world of business. Founded in 1907 in Seattle, Washington, as the American Messenger Company by 19-year-old James E. Casey and Claude Ryan, the enterprise initially focused on telegraph message delivery before expanding into retail store deliveries. Over more than a century, the company evolved through strategic geographic expansion and service diversification into United Parcel Service in 1930, eventually pioneering "common carrier" ground delivery services nationwide. Today, operating under the NYSE ticker UPS, the corporation leverages an integrated global network to provide small package delivery, heavyweight freight forwarding, and specialized supply chain services to millions of customers across more than 200 countries and territories.
