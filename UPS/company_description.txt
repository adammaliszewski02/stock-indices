United Parcel Service, Inc. (UPS) is a global leader in logistics, offering a broad range of solutions that include the transportation of packages and freight, the facilitation of international trade, and the deployment of advanced technology to efficiently manage the world of business. Founded in 1907 in Seattle, Washington, as a messenger service, the company has evolved from a local courier into a multi-billion-dollar enterprise. Headquartered in Atlanta, Georgia, UPS operates one of the world's largest and most efficient air and ground transportation networks. The company provides supply chain management, freight forwarding, customs brokerage, and fulfillment services to millions of customers across more than 200 countries and territories. Driven by a commitment to sustainability and operational innovation, UPS continues to adapt to the demands of modern commerce, particularly the explosive growth of e-commerce, while maintaining its foundational focus on reliability, speed, and customer service.
