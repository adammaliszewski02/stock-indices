Wells Fargo & Company (ticker: WFC) is a leading American multinational financial services corporation and one of the largest banks in the United States. Headquartered in San Francisco, California, the company provides a comprehensive suite of banking, investment, mortgage, and consumer and commercial finance products and services to individuals, businesses, and institutions through a vast network of physical branches, digital platforms, and offices globally. Tracing its origins back to 1852, when it was founded by Henry Wells and William G. Fargo in New York to provide express delivery and banking services in the wake of the California Gold Rush, the institution has evolved over nearly two centuries through numerous mergers and acquisitions. Most notably, its 2008 acquisition of Charlotte-based Wachovia significantly expanded its footprint across the East Coast, cementing its status as a cornerstone of the American banking sector and a constituent of the major financial indices.
