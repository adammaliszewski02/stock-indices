Wells Fargo & Company (ticker: WFC) is a leading American multinational financial services corporation and one of the "Big Four" banks in the United States. Headquartered in San Francisco, California, the company traces its origins back to 1852, when Henry Wells and William G. Fargo founded the enterprise to provide express delivery and banking services during the California Gold Rush. Over the decades, through strategic expansions and major mergers—most notably with Norwest Corporation in 1998 and the acquisition of Wachovia in 2008—the firm evolved into a vast banking conglomerate. Today, Wells Fargo operates through multiple segments, offering a comprehensive suite of financial products and services, including retail banking, commercial banking, wealth and investment management, and corporate and investment banking, serving tens of millions of consumer, business, and institutional clients globally.
