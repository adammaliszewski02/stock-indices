Wells Fargo & Company (ticker: WFC) is a leading American multinational financial services corporation and one of the "Big Four" banks in the United States. Headquartered in San Francisco, California, the company traces its origins back to 1852, when Henry Wells and William G. Fargo founded an enterprise originally established to provide express and banking services to the American West, famously symbolized by its iconic stagecoach. 

Today, Wells Fargo operates through a vast network of branches and digital platforms, serving millions of consumers, small businesses, and major corporations. The company provides a comprehensive suite of financial products and services, including retail and commercial banking, wealth management, investment banking, and mortgage servicing. As a globally systemically important financial institution, Wells Fargo plays a critical role in the U.S. and international economies, continuously evolving its technological infrastructure and risk management frameworks to support its diverse customer base.
