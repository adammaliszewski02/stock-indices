The Walt Disney Company, trading under the stock ticker DIS, is a diversified worldwide family entertainment and media enterprise. Founded in 1923 by brothers Walt and Roy Disney as a small-scale animation studio, the company pioneered the animation industry with the 1937 release of Snow White and the Seven Dwarfs. Over the subsequent decades, Disney expanded its operations far beyond traditional filmmaking. Today, the corporation operates through two primary segments: Entertainment, which encompasses global film and television production, theatrical distribution, and direct-to-consumer streaming services including Disney+ and Hulu; and Experiences, which comprises theme parks, resorts, cruise lines, and consumer products. Through strategic acquisitions of major industry players like Pixar, Marvel Entertainment, Lucasfilm, and 21st Century Fox, Disney has solidified its position as a dominant global leader in contemporary mass media and entertainment.
