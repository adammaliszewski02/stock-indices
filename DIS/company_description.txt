The Walt Disney Company, trading under the ticker DIS, is a diversified worldwide entertainment and media enterprise. Founded in 1923 by brothers Walt and Roy Disney as an animation studio, the company has since evolved into a dominant global conglomerate. Its operations are broadly structured into entertainment, sports, and experiences. The entertainment segment encompasses iconic film studios, television networks, and the direct-to-consumer streaming services Disney+ and Hulu. The sports division operates the cable and digital network ESPN. The experiences segment manages numerous theme parks, resort destinations, cruise lines, and global consumer products licensing. Disney remains a foundational pillar of modern popular culture, continually expanding its intellectual property portfolio through major strategic acquisitions, including Pixar, Marvel Entertainment, Lucasfilm, and 21st Century Fox.
