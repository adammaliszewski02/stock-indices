The Walt Disney Company, trading under the ticker DIS, is a diversified global entertainment and media conglomerate. Founded in 1923 by brothers Walt and Roy Disney as the Disney Brothers Cartoon Studio, the company has evolved from a pioneering animation house into a multifaceted powerhouse. Its business operations are structured primarily around entertainment, which encompasses film and television production, streaming services like Disney+, and theatrical distribution, alongside its experiences segment, which includes theme parks, cruise lines, and consumer products. 

Throughout its history, Disney has expanded its intellectual property portfolio through strategic acquisitions, including Pixar, Marvel, Lucasfilm, and 21st Century Fox. Today, the company leverages its extensive library of iconic characters and franchises to drive engagement across digital platforms, linear networks, and physical destinations. Headquartered in Burbank, California, Disney remains a dominant force in the global media landscape, continuously integrating technology and creative storytelling to maintain its position as a leader in family-oriented entertainment and mass-market media.
