The Walt Disney Company, trading under the stock ticker DIS, is a diversified worldwide family entertainment and media enterprise. Founded in 1923 by brothers Walt and Roy Disney as a small-scale animation studio, the company pioneered the field of animated film production before expanding its operations significantly over the subsequent decades. Today, its core business operations are structured into two primary segments: Entertainment, encompassing film, television, and streaming services such as Disney+; and Sports, anchored by the global cable and broadcasting network ESPN. Additionally, the company operates a massive global portfolio of theme parks, cruise lines, and resort destinations, alongside a vast consumer products division that manages worldwide merchandise licensing, publishing, and retail operations.
