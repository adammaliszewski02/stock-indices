The Walt Disney Company, trading under the ticker DIS, is a diversified worldwide entertainment and media enterprise. Founded in 1923 by brothers Walt and Roy Disney as an animation studio, the company pioneered the field of animated film before expanding its operations significantly over the decades. Today, Disney's business is structured around two primary segments: Entertainment, encompassing film and television production, streaming services including Disney+ and Hulu, and linear networks; and Experiences, which operates global theme parks, cruise lines, resorts, and consumer products. Through strategic acquisitions such as Pixar, Marvel Entertainment, Lucasfilm, and 21st Century Fox, Disney has cemented its position as a global leader in intellectual property and family entertainment.
