The Walt Disney Company, trading under the ticker DIS, is a diversified worldwide entertainment and media enterprise. Founded in 1923 by brothers Walt and Roy Disney as a small animation studio, the company pioneered the animation industry with the 1937 release of *Snow White and the Seven Dwarfs*. Over the subsequent century, Disney evolved into a dominant global conglomerate through strategic expansions into live-action filmmaking, television broadcasting, theme parks, and consumer products. Today, its core operations are structured into Entertainment, Sports, and Experiences. The Entertainment segment encompasses major film studios like Pixar, Marvel, and Lucasfilm, alongside the Disney+ streaming service. The Sports segment is anchored by ESPN, while the Experiences division manages global theme parks, cruise lines, and resort properties. Disney continues to shape modern pop culture through timeless storytelling, technological innovation, and iconic intellectual properties.
