The Walt Disney Company, trading under the stock ticker DIS, is a diversified worldwide family entertainment and media enterprise. Founded in 1923 by brothers Walt and Roy Disney as a small-scale animation studio, the company pioneered the animation industry with the 1937 release of *Snow White and the Seven Dwarfs*. Over the decades, Disney evolved from a traditional cartoon producer into a global powerhouse spanning multiple sectors. 

Today, its operations are organized into three primary segments: Entertainment, Sports, and Experiences. The Entertainment division encompasses film and television production, linear networks, and direct-to-consumer streaming services, including Disney+ and Hulu. The Sports segment is anchored by ESPN, delivering comprehensive sports programming and digital media coverage. The Experiences division operates the company's iconic theme parks, resort destinations, cruise lines, and global consumer products licensing and retail operations. Through strategic acquisitions of major industry players like Pixar, Marvel Entertainment, Lucasfilm, and 21st Century Fox, Disney has solidified its position as a dominant leader in the global entertainment landscape.
