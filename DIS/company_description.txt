The Walt Disney Company, trading under the ticker DIS, is a diversified worldwide family entertainment and media enterprise. Founded in 1923 by brothers Walt and Roy Disney as a small-scale animation studio, the company pioneered the creation of synchronized sound cartoons and full-length animated features, establishing early industry milestones such as *Snow White and the Seven Dwarfs*. Over the decades, Disney expanded its operations from traditional animation into live-action filmmaking, television broadcasting, and theme park development, opening its flagship resort, Disneyland, in California in 1955. Today, the corporation operates through two primary segments: Entertainment, which encompasses film production, television networks, and the direct-to-consumer streaming services Disney+, Hulu, and ESPN+, and Experiences, which comprises global theme parks, cruise lines, resorts, and consumer products. Through strategic acquisitions of major entertainment brands including Pixar, Marvel Entertainment, Lucasfilm, and 21st Century Fox, Disney has solidified its position as one of the world's most prominent and influential media conglomerates.
