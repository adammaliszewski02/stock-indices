General Electric Company, trading under the ticker GE, is an iconic American multinational industrial corporation with a history dating back to 1892, when it was formed through the merger of Edison General Electric Company and Thomson-Houston Electric Company. For over a century, GE was a pioneer and industrial giant, famously co-founded by Thomas Edison, and grew to encompass a vast array of sectors including aviation, power, healthcare, renewable energy, appliances, and financial services. 

In recent years, the company underwent a massive strategic transformation to simplify its operations and unlock shareholder value through a historic split into three independent, publicly traded companies. This restructuring concluded with the successful spin-offs of GE HealthCare in 2023 and the separation of GE Vernova, its energy business, alongside GE Aerospace in early 2024. Today, the legacy GE entity operates primarily as GE Aerospace, focusing on the design and manufacture of commercial and military jet engines, integrated engine components, and avionics systems, thereby continuing its legacy of technological innovation in the global aerospace industry.
