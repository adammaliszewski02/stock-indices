General Electric (ticker: GE) is an American multinational industrial corporation historically recognized as a pioneer in innovation. Founded in 1892 through the merger of Edison General Electric Company and Thomson-Houston Electric Company, GE grew into a sprawling conglomerate over the 20th century, manufacturing everything from household appliances and medical imaging equipment to power generation turbines and aviation engines. It was an original component of the Dow Jones Industrial Average and long symbolized American industrial might, while also operating a massive financial services arm, GE Capital. 

In recent years, the company executed a historic restructuring to streamline its operations and enhance shareholder value. This transformation culminated in the separation of GE into three independent, publicly traded entities: GE Aerospace, focusing on commercial and military jet engines; GE Vernova, managing the company's energy portfolio including wind, gas, and grid technologies; and GE HealthCare, which completed its spin-off in early 2023. Today, the enduring legacy of the original conglomerate continues primarily through GE Aerospace, which retains the historic GE ticker symbol.
