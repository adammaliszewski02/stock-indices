General Electric Company (GE), identified by the stock ticker GE, is an iconic American multinational enterprise founded in 1892 by Thomas Edison, Charles A. Coffin, Elihu Thomson, and Edwin J. Houston. Historically renowned as a sprawling industrial conglomerate, GE shaped the modern technological and economic landscape through leadership in diverse sectors, including electrical equipment, power generation, aviation, healthcare, and financial services. 

Following a strategic multi-year transformation announced in 2021 to optimize operations and unlock shareholder value, the company successfully divested its healthcare division into GE HealthCare and completed a historic structural split in early 2024. Today, the enterprise operates primarily as two standalone, publicly traded entities: GE Aerospace, which focuses on commercial and military aircraft engines and systems, and GE Vernova, which is dedicated to the global energy transition through power, wind, and electrification technologies.
