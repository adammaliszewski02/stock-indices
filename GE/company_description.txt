The stock ticker GE currently represents GE Aerospace, a leading global provider of jet engines, components, and integrated systems for commercial and military aircraft. Following a deliberate multi-year corporate transformation and spinoff strategy, the legacy industrial conglomerate—originally founded in 1892 with Thomas Edison’s backing—completed its historic breakup in early 2024. This restructuring separated its healthcare division into GE HealthCare and its energy portfolio into GE Vernova, leaving the aerospace entity as the direct continuation of the original corporate structure. Today, GE Aerospace focuses on designing, manufacturing, and servicing advanced propulsion technologies while supporting a massive installed base of commercial and defense flight hours worldwide.
