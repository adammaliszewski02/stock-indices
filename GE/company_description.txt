General Electric Company (GE), identified by the stock ticker GE, is a globally recognized American industrial and technology conglomerate with a legacy spanning over a century. Founded in 1892 through the merger of Edison General Electric Company and Thomson-Houston Electric Company, with Thomas Edison as one of its prominent figures, the company historically played a foundational role in the electrification of the United States and the development of modern industrial manufacturing. 

Over the decades, GE expanded its operations into a vast array of sectors, including aviation, power generation, healthcare, renewable energy, and financial services through GE Capital. In recent years, the company underwent a massive strategic restructuring to simplify its operations and unlock shareholder value, culminating in a historic separation into three independent, publicly traded entities. Today, the legacy GE ticker is primarily associated with GE Aerospace, a world leader in commercial and military jet engines and systems. The healthcare division operates independently as GE HealthCare, while the energy portfolio was spun off under the name GE Vernova, completing the transformation of the historic industrial giant into focused, sector-specific enterprises.
