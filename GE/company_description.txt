General Electric (GE) is a global industrial leader that has undergone a significant transformation to focus on the energy sector. Following a multi-year strategic restructuring, the company completed its separation into three independent, publicly traded entities in 2024: GE Aerospace, GE Vernova, and GE HealthCare. Today, the entity trading under the ticker GE is GE Aerospace, a world-leading provider of jet engines, components, and integrated systems for commercial and military aircraft.

Founded in 1892 through the merger of Edison General Electric Company and Thomson-Houston Electric Company, GE was a founding member of the Dow Jones Industrial Average. For over a century, the company was synonymous with American industrial innovation, spanning diverse sectors including consumer appliances, lighting, finance, and power generation. Under the leadership of Thomas Edison, the company pioneered advancements in electricity and infrastructure. Following its recent spin-offs, GE Aerospace now operates as a pure-play aviation company, leveraging its extensive installed base of engines to provide aftermarket services and cutting-edge propulsion technology to the global aerospace industry.
