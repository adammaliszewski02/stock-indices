General Electric Company (GE), historically one of the world's most prominent industrial conglomerates, has recently transformed into a focused, pure-play aerospace and defense enterprise. Founded in 1892 through the merger of Thomas Edison’s electric company with Thomson-Houston Electric Company, GE grew from a pioneer in electricity and lighting into a diversified global giant operating in power generation, renewable energy, healthcare, aviation, and financial services. 

As part of a strategic corporate reorganization announced in 2021, the company has systematically divested its major non-aerospace divisions to unlock shareholder value and optimize operations. GE Healthcare was spun off into an independent publicly traded company in early 2023. Subsequently, in April 2024, the parent organization completed its final structural split, separating its energy portfolio—now known as GE Vernova—from its aviation business. Following this separation, the remaining entity officially rebranded as GE Aerospace, centering its business on the design, manufacture, and servicing of commercial and military jet engines, integrated engine systems, and advanced aviation technologies.
