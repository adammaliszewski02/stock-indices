ConocoPhillips, traded under the stock ticker COP, is one of the world's largest independent upstream oil and exploration and production companies. Headquartered in Houston, Texas, the company focuses on the discovery, development, and production of crude oil, bitumen, natural gas, and liquefied natural gas across a diverse global portfolio spanning North America, Europe, Asia, and Australia. 

The company’s corporate lineage traces back to the 1875 founding of Continental Oil and Transportation Company by Isaac E. Blake, as well as the 1917 establishment of Phillips Petroleum Company by brothers Lee and Frank Phillips. These two foundational entities merged in 2002 to form ConocoPhillips. A major milestone occurred in 2012 when the enterprise executed a strategic corporate split, separating its downstream refining and marketing operations into an independent company known as Phillips 66, thereby allowing ConocoPhillips to concentrate exclusively on upstream exploration and production activities. Today, the company remains a dominant force in the global energy sector, driven by technological innovation, capital discipline, and a commitment to balancing conventional energy supply with sustainable environmental practices.
