ConocoPhillips, traded under the ticker COP, is the world's largest independent upstream oil and exploration and production company. Headquartered in Houston, Texas, the company focuses on the discovery, development, and production of crude oil, bitumen, natural gas, and liquefied natural gas on a global scale. Its operations span diverse resource types, including traditional assets, oil sands, shale plays, and deepwater developments across North America, Europe, Asia, and Australia. 

The company's origins date back to 1875 with the founding of Continental Oil and Transportation Company by Isaac E. Blake. Over the subsequent decades, Continental Oil evolved into Conoco. In 2002, Conoco merged with Phillips Petroleum Company, which had been established in 1917 by brothers Frank and L.E. Phillips, forming ConocoPhillips. A significant corporate restructuring occurred in 2012 when ConocoPhillips spun off its downstream refining and marketing business into an independent entity named Phillips 66, allowing the parent company to concentrate entirely on upstream exploration and production. Today, ConocoPhillips continues to execute a strategy centered on capital discipline, shareholder returns, and maintaining a diversified, low-cost supply portfolio to navigate the evolving global energy landscape.
