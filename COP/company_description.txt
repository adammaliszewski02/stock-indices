ConocoPhillips, traded under the ticker COP, is the world's largest independent upstream oil and exploration and production company. Headquartered in Houston, Texas, the company focuses on the discovery, development, and production of crude oil, bitumen, natural gas, and liquefied natural gas on a global scale. Its diverse operational portfolio includes conventional and unconventional reservoirs, oil sands, and deepwater assets across North America, Europe, Asia, and Australia. 

The company’s origins trace back to the 1875 founding of Continental Oil and Transportation Company by Isaac E. Blake, which later became Conoco. In 2002, Conoco merged with Phillips Petroleum Company, a firm established by Frank and L.E. Phillips in 1917, forming ConocoPhillips. A major corporate restructuring occurred in 2012 when the enterprise separated its downstream refining and marketing operations into an independent entity, Phillips 66, allowing ConocoPhillips to operate entirely as a pure-play exploration and production enterprise.
