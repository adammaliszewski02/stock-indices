ConocoPhillips, traded under the ticker COP, is the world's largest independent upstream oil and exploration company based on production and reserves. Headquartered in Houston, Texas, the company is primarily engaged in the exploration, development, production, and marketing of crude oil, bitumen, natural gas, liquefied natural gas, and natural gas liquids globally. 

The company's roots trace back to the 1875 founding of the Continental Oil and Transportation Company by Isaac E. Blake in Promontory, Utah, which later became Conoco. Following a series of strategic mergers and separations—most notably the 2002 merger between Conoco and Phillips Petroleum Company, and the subsequent 2012 corporate split that separated its downstream refining business into Phillips 66—ConocoPhillips emerged as a pure-play exploration and production enterprise. Today, the company operates in numerous countries, leveraging a diversified portfolio that includes unconventional shale plays, conventional assets, liquefied natural gas projects, and oil sands developments, while navigating the evolving global energy landscape with a commitment to emission reduction and shareholder value.
