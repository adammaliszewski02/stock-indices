ConocoPhillips, traded under the stock ticker COP, is the world's largest independent upstream oil and exploration and production company based on production and reserves. Headquartered in Houston, Texas, the company explores for, produces, transports, and markets crude oil, bitumen, natural gas, liquefied natural gas, and natural gas liquids globally. ConocoPhillips traces its corporate lineage back to the Continental Oil and Transportation Company, founded in 1875 by Isaac E. Blake, which later became Conoco. The modern iteration of the company was formed through the historic August 2002 merger of Conoco Inc. and Phillips Petroleum Company. A significant strategic evolution occurred in May 2012, when ConocoPhillips successfully completed the spin-off of its downstream refining and marketing business into an independent, publicly traded entity known as Phillips 66. This corporate restructuring allowed ConocoPhillips to focus exclusively on its core upstream exploration and production operations. Today, the company operates in numerous countries across North America, Europe, Asia, and Australia, maintaining a disciplined capital allocation strategy focused on shareholder returns and portfolio resilience through commodity price cycles.
