ConocoPhillips, identified by the New York Stock Exchange ticker symbol COP, is one of the world’s largest independent exploration and production (E&P) energy companies. Headquartered in Houston, Texas, the company focuses exclusively on exploring for, producing, transporting, and marketing crude oil, bitumen, natural gas, liquefied natural gas (LNG), and natural gas liquids. Its core operations encompass conventional and unconventional assets across the United States—notably within the Permian, Eagle Ford, and Bakken basins—as well as long-life assets in Alaska, Canada, Europe, the Asia-Pacific region, and the Middle East.

The company's origins date to the late 19th and early 20th centuries through the Continental Oil and Transportation Company, founded in 1875, and Phillips Petroleum Company, founded in 1917. Conoco and Phillips merged in 2002 to create an integrated energy giant. In 2012, ConocoPhillips strategically reshaped its business model by spinning off its downstream refining and marketing operations into Phillips 66, emerging as a pure-play upstream corporation. Today, ConocoPhillips concentrates on disciplined capital allocation, low-cost resource capture, and technological innovation to supply global energy markets efficiently.
