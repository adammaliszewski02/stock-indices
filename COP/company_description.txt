ConocoPhillips, traded under the ticker COP, is one of the world's largest independent upstream oil and gas exploration and production companies. Headquartered in Houston, Texas, the company focuses on the discovery, development, and extraction of crude oil and natural gas across global operations. ConocoPhillips traces its corporate heritage back to the founding of Continental Oil Company in 1875 by Isaac E. Blake. Over the decades, the firm evolved through major industry milestones, most notably the 2002 merger of Conoco Inc. and Phillips Petroleum Company. In 2012, ConocoPhillips executed a strategic transformation by spinning off its downstream refining and marketing business, now known as Phillips 66, allowing the parent company to concentrate exclusively on exploration and production. Today, the company manages a diverse asset portfolio that includes conventional and unconventional plays, liquefied natural gas projects, and oil sands developments across North America, Europe, Asia, and Australia.
