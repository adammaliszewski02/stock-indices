ConocoPhillips, traded under the ticker COP, is the world's largest independent upstream oil and exploration company based on production and reserves. Headquartered in Houston, Texas, the company focuses on the discovery, development, and production of crude oil, bitumen, natural gas, and liquefied natural gas across multiple global regions. ConocoPhillips traces its origins to the 1875 founding of Continental Oil and Transportation Company by Isaac E. Blake, and later expanded significantly through various industry mergers, most notably the 2002 combination of Conoco Inc. and Phillips Petroleum Company. In 2012, the enterprise underwent a major strategic restructuring, separating its downstream refining and marketing operations into an independent entity, Phillips 66, allowing ConocoPhillips to operate as a pure-play exploration and production company. Today, the corporation manages a diverse asset portfolio encompassing traditional oilfields, shale plays, and deepwater developments, while simultaneously pursuing lower-carbon energy initiatives and technological innovations to reduce operational emissions and meet evolving global energy demands.
