Union Pacific Corporation (UNP) is the parent company of Union Pacific Railroad, the largest railroad network in the United States. Founded in 1862 under the Pacific Railroad Act of 1862, the company played a pivotal role in American history by completing the eastern leg of the First Transcontinental Railroad, which was joined with the Central Pacific Railroad at Promontory Summit, Utah, in 1869. Today, Union Pacific operates across 23 states in the western two-thirds of the country, maintaining a vast infrastructure that spans over 32,000 miles of track. Its business model centers on the efficient transportation of diverse freight, including agricultural products, automotive components, chemicals, coal, and industrial commodities. By connecting major West Coast and Gulf Coast ports with eastern gateways and the Mexican border, Union Pacific serves as a critical link in the North American supply chain, facilitating domestic and international trade through an extensive network of intermodal and manifest rail services.
