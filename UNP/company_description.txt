Union Pacific Corporation (UNP) is the parent company of Union Pacific Railroad, the largest railroad network in the United States. Founded in 1862 under the Pacific Railroad Act of 1862, the company played a pivotal role in American history by constructing the eastern portion of the First Transcontinental Railroad, which was completed in 1869. Today, Union Pacific operates across 23 states in the western two-thirds of the country, maintaining a vast infrastructure that spans over 32,000 miles of track. Its business model centers on the efficient transportation of diverse freight, including agricultural products, automotive components, chemicals, coal, industrial materials, and intermodal containers. By connecting major West Coast and Gulf Coast ports to eastern gateways and the Mexican border, Union Pacific serves as a critical link in the global supply chain, facilitating domestic commerce and international trade through a highly integrated rail network.
