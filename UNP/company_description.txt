Union Pacific Corporation, traded under the stock ticker UNP, is a premier American holding company whose primary operating entity is Union Pacific Railroad, one of the nation's most iconic and extensive freight railroads. Established in 1862 under the Pacific Railroad Act signed by President Abraham Lincoln, the company played a pivotal role in the westward expansion of the United States, famously completing the first transcontinental railroad in 1869 at Promontory, Utah. Today, UNP operates a massive rail network spanning 23 states across the western two-thirds of the country. Headquartered in Omaha, Nebraska, the company transports a diverse mix of freight, including agricultural products, industrial goods, chemicals, coal, and intermodal containers. By connecting vital ports to the Midwest and Gulf Coast gateways, Union Pacific serves as a critical backbone of the North American supply chain and freight transportation infrastructure.
