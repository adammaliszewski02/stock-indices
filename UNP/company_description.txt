Union Pacific Corporation, traded under the stock ticker UNP, operates through its principal subsidiary, Union Pacific Railroad Company, as one of America's premier transportation and logistics enterprises. Tracing its origins back to the Pacific Railroad Act of 1862 signed by President Abraham Lincoln, the company played a foundational role in connecting the United States by rail and completing the historic First Transcontinental Railroad. Today, Union Pacific manages a massive rail network spanning 23 states across the western two-thirds of the country. The company links major West Coast and Gulf Coast ports with eastern gateways, moving essential commodities such as agricultural products, automotive goods, chemicals, industrial materials, and intermodal freight. By providing efficient, sustainable, and reliable supply chain solutions, Union Pacific facilitates domestic and international commerce while continuing a legacy of technological innovation and infrastructure development that spans more than 160 years.
