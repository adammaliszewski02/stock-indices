Union Pacific Corporation, traded under the ticker UNP, is a prominent American holding company primarily known for its principal operating subsidiary, Union Pacific Railroad. Established in 1862 under the Pacific Railroad Act signed by President Abraham Lincoln, the company played a critical role in US westward expansion by constructing part of the First Transcontinental Railroad. Today, it operates one of the largest and most strategically vital freight railroads in North America. The company's rail network spans thousands of route miles across the western two-thirds of the United States, connecting the Pacific and Gulf Coast ports with the Midwest and eastern gateways. Union Pacific transports a diverse range of heavy freight, including agricultural products, industrial goods, chemicals, coal, and intermodal containers. By facilitating efficient supply chain logistics and connecting key industrial corridors, the business serves as a foundational component of the domestic and global transportation infrastructure.
