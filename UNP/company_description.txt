Union Pacific Corporation (UNP) is a premier American transportation holding company, best known for its principal operating company, Union Pacific Railroad. Founded in 1862 under the Pacific Railroad Act signed by President Abraham Lincoln, the company played a pivotal role in the nation's westward expansion by completing a crucial link of the historic First Transcontinental Railroad. Today, Union Pacific operates one of the most extensive freight railroad networks in North America. The company's business focuses on the safe, efficient, and reliable transportation of a diverse mix of goods across the western two-thirds of the United States. Its operations are vital to the national supply chain, moving crucial commodities such as agricultural products, industrial chemicals, automotive goods, coal, and intermodal freight containers. By connecting key Pacific and Gulf Coast ports with the Midwest and eastern gateways, Union Pacific facilitates domestic and international commerce, serving as a foundational backbone for the American economy.
