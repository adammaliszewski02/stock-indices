Union Pacific Corporation (UNP) is a premier American transportation holding company, best known for its principal operating company, Union Pacific Railroad. Established in 1862 under the Pacific Railroad Act signed by President Abraham Lincoln, the company played a pivotal role in the nation's westward expansion by completing a crucial leg of the historic First Transcontinental Railroad. Today, UNP operates one of the largest and most strategically vital freight rail networks in North America. Headquartered in Omaha, Nebraska, the company's vast rail system spans over 30,000 route miles across 23 western states, seamlessly connecting the Pacific and Gulf Coast ports with the Midwest and eastern gateways. Union Pacific transports a diverse array of heavy freight, including agricultural products, industrial chemicals, automotive goods, and intermodal containers, serving as a critical backbone for domestic and global supply chains. Through continuous infrastructure investment and operational innovation, the company remains a cornerstone of the modern American economy, balancing a rich 19th-century legacy with contemporary freight logistics.
