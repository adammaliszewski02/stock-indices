Netflix, Inc. is a global streaming entertainment service that provides a vast library of films, television series, and documentaries across a wide variety of genres and languages. Founded in 1997 by Reed Hastings and Marc Randolph, the company originally operated as a DVD-by-mail service. This innovative model disrupted the traditional video rental industry and laid the groundwork for the company’s pivotal transition to digital streaming in 2007. 

Today, Netflix operates as a subscription-based platform, generating revenue primarily through monthly membership fees. Beyond licensing third-party content, the company has become a major production studio, investing heavily in original programming, including award-winning films and series produced in-house. Headquartered in Los Gatos, California, Netflix maintains a significant international presence, delivering content to hundreds of millions of paid subscribers in over 190 countries. By leveraging sophisticated recommendation algorithms and a data-driven approach to content acquisition and creation, the company remains a dominant force in the global media and entertainment landscape.
