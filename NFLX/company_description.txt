Netflix, Inc. (ticker: NFLX) is a global subscription-based streaming entertainment service that offers a diverse catalog of television series, documentaries, feature films, and mobile games across a wide variety of genres and languages. Founded in 1997 by Reed Hastings and Marc Randolph in Scotts Valley, California, the company initially operated as a DVD-by-mail rental service. Netflix revolutionized the media industry in 2007 by introducing video-on-demand streaming, which subsequently catalyzed the global decline of traditional video rental stores and shifted consumer media consumption habits. The company further transformed the entertainment landscape by pioneering the binge-watching model and transitioning into a major content producer, highlighted by the launch of its first original series, *House of Cards*, in 2013. Today, Netflix generates revenue through tiered monthly subscription plans and operates in over 190 countries, serving hundreds of millions of paid memberships worldwide. In recent years, the company has continued to evolve its business model by expanding into mobile gaming and introducing ad-supported subscription tiers to sustain growth in an increasingly competitive streaming market.
