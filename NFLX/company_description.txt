Netflix, Inc., traded under the ticker NFLX, is a global entertainment streaming service offering a wide variety of television series, documentaries, feature films, and mobile games across a vast array of genres and languages. Members can play, pause, and resume viewing as much as they want, anytime, anywhere, and can change their plans at any time. Founded in 1997 by Reed Hastings and Marc Randolph as a DVD-rental-by-mail service in Scotts Valley, California, the company revolutionized media consumption by pioneering digital streaming in 2007. Over the subsequent decades, Netflix transitioned from a purely licensing-based distributor into a powerhouse content creator, producing critically acclaimed original films and series. Today, the company operates on a subscription-based model and continues to innovate within the digital media landscape, serving hundreds of millions of paid memberships in over 190 countries while occasionally experimenting with new formats, such as ad-supported tiers and live programming.
