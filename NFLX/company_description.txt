Netflix, Inc., traded under the ticker NFLX, is a leading global subscription streaming entertainment service. Founded in 1997 by Reed Hastings and Marc Randolph as a DVD-by-mail rental service in Scotts Valley, California, the company transitioned its primary business model to video on demand in 2007. Today, Netflix offers a diverse library of feature films, documentaries, television series, and mobile games across a wide variety of genres and languages, accessible to hundreds of millions of paid members in over 190 countries. In addition to licensing third-party content, the company has significantly expanded into original programming since the premiere of its first original series in 2013, establishing itself as a dominant force in the modern entertainment and media industry.
