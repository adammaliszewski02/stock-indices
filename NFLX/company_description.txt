Netflix, Inc., traded under the ticker NFLX, is a global entertainment service and one of the world's leading streaming subscription platforms. Founded in 1997 by Reed Hastings and Marc Randolph, the company initially operated as a DVD-by-mail rental service in Scotts Valley, California. Netflix transitioned its primary business model to digital streaming in 2007 and subsequently began producing original content in 2013 with the release of the acclaimed series *House of Cards*, which significantly accelerated its global expansion. Today, the company distributes a diverse portfolio of licensed and proprietary feature films, television series, documentaries, and mobile games across more than 190 countries. Generating revenue entirely through paid subscription tiers, Netflix has fundamentally transformed consumer media consumption habits, shifting the entertainment industry away from traditional linear television toward on-demand, internet-delivered programming.
