Netflix, Inc., traded under the ticker NFLX, is a leading global entertainment streaming service offering a vast library of feature films, documentaries, television series, and mobile games across a wide variety of genres and languages. Founded in 1997 by Reed Hastings and Marc Randolph in Scotts Valley, California, the company initially operated as a DVD-by-mail subscription service. In 2007, Netflix revolutionized the media industry by introducing its subscription-based streaming platform, eventually shifting its primary focus to digital distribution. The company subsequently pioneered the original content production model, releasing acclaimed proprietary programming that reshaped modern television and cinema. Over the decades, Netflix expanded its footprint internationally, establishing a subscriber base of hundreds of millions of paid memberships across more than 190 countries. The enterprise continues to evolve its business model through technological innovation, international market penetration, and the recent introduction of advertising-supported subscription tiers.
