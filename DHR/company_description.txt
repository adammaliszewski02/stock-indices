Danaher Corporation, trading under the ticker DHR, is a leading global life sciences and diagnostics innovator committed to helping customers solve complex scientific challenges and improve patient health. Founded in 1969 as a real estate investment trust under the name Massachusetts Pacific Corporation, the enterprise underwent a strategic transformation in 1984, adopting the Danaher name—inspired by a Montana river—and shifting its focus to industrial manufacturing. Over the subsequent decades, the company executed a disciplined pivot through targeted acquisitions and divestitures, systematically transitioning from industrial tools and components into a premier science and technology leader. Today, Danaher operates through three primary segments: Biotechnology, Diagnostics, and Life Sciences. The company’s globally recognized operating model, anchored by continuous improvement, lean manufacturing, and rigorous financial management, drives sustainable competitive advantages across its portfolio of industry-leading brands. Headquartered in Washington, D.C., Danaher continues to advance vital scientific research, pharmaceutical development, and clinical diagnostics worldwide.
