Danaher Corporation (NYSE: DHR) is a leading global life sciences and diagnostics innovator. Founded in 1984 by brothers Steven and Mitchell Rales and named after Danaher Creek in Montana, the company initially expanded as a diversified industrial conglomerate. Danaher's growth has been historically propelled by strategic mergers and acquisitions coupled with the Danaher Business System, a foundational operating philosophy rooted in Japanese Kaizen and lean manufacturing principles.

In recent years, Danaher strategically reshaped its portfolio to focus entirely on healthcare and medical technology. This transformation included the spin-offs of its industrial business as Fortive in 2016, its dental unit as Envista in 2019, and its environmental and applied solutions business as Veralto in 2023. 

Today, Danaher operates primarily across three core segments: Biotechnology, Life Sciences, and Diagnostics. Through prominent operating brands such as Cytiva, Beckman Coulter, Leica Microsystems, and Cepheid, the company delivers research instruments, bioprocessing equipment, consumables, and clinical diagnostic systems. These tools support the development of life-saving therapeutics, accelerate biomedical research, and enhance clinical decision-making for healthcare providers and patients worldwide.
