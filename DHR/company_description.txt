Danaher Corporation, trading under the ticker DHR, is a leading global science and technology innovator dedicated to helping customers solve complex analytical challenges and improve quality of life worldwide. Founded in 1969 as a real estate investment trust under the name DMG, Inc., the enterprise underwent a strategic transformation in 1984, adopting its current name and shifting focus toward industrial manufacturing and tool production through the implementation of the Danaher Business System, a renowned continuous improvement framework. Over subsequent decades, the company successfully divested its industrial holdings to pivot decisively toward the life sciences, diagnostics, and biotechnology sectors. Today, Danaher operates through a portfolio of market-leading operating companies that provide advanced instruments, consumables, and software utilized in biopharmaceutical drug discovery, clinical laboratory testing, and critical medical research.
