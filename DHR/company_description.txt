Danaher Corporation (ticker: DHR) is a leading global life sciences and diagnostics innovator committed to accelerating the power of science and technology to improve human health. Founded in 1984 by brothers Mitchell and Steven Rales, the company originally operated as a real estate investment trust before transforming into an industrial manufacturing firm. Over the decades, Danaher executed a strategic pivot away from cyclical manufacturing toward high-margin, recurring-revenue sectors through a series of major acquisitions, including Beckman Coulter, Pall Corporation, and the biopharma business of GE Life Sciences (now Cytiva). Today, Danaher operates through three primary segments: Biotechnology, Diagnostics, and Life Sciences. The company is widely recognized for pioneering the Danaher Business System, a proprietary framework focused on continuous improvement, lean manufacturing, and operational excellence, which drives its long-term growth and market leadership across the healthcare and scientific research industries.
