Danaher Corporation, trading under the ticker DHR, is a leading global science and technology innovator that designs, manufactures, and markets professional, medical, industrial, and commercial products and services. Founded in 1984 by brothers Mitchell and Steven Rales, the company originally operated as a real estate investment trust before transitioning into an industrial manufacturing firm. Over the subsequent decades, Danaher executed a strategic pivot through targeted acquisitions and divestitures, successfully evolving into a highly focused life sciences and diagnostics powerhouse. Today, the corporation operates primarily through three business segments: biotechnology, life sciences, and diagnostics. By leveraging the Danaher Business System, a proprietary framework centered on continuous improvement, operational excellence, and customer satisfaction, the company drives sustainable growth and delivers critical technological solutions to scientists, medical professionals, and industries worldwide.
