Danaher Corporation, trading under the ticker DHR, is a globally diversified life sciences and diagnostics company committed to innovating science and technology to improve human health. Founded in 1984 by brothers Steven and Mitchell Rales, the company originally operated as a real estate investment trust before transitioning into an industrial manufacturing firm under the name DHR, inspired by a catch-and-release fishing spot in Islamorada, Florida. Over the subsequent decades, Danaher executed a strategic pivot away from cyclical industrial markets through a series of high-profile acquisitions, most notably the 2020 purchase of GE Biopharma, which was rebranded as Cytiva. Today, Danaher operates through three primary segments: Biotechnology, Diagnostics, and Life Sciences. The company empowers its operating companies through the deployment of the Danaher Business System, a proprietary continuous improvement framework focused on driving sustainable operational excellence, rigorous talent development, and superior financial performance.
