Danaher Corporation, trading under the ticker DHR, is a globally diversified life sciences and diagnostics company committed to innovating science and technology to improve human health. Founded in 1969 as a real estate investment trust under the name DMG, Inc., the organization adopted its current moniker in 1984 and transitioned into an industrial manufacturing firm. Over the subsequent decades, Danaher executed a strategic corporate evolution, increasingly shedding cyclical industrial assets to focus on high-margin, recurring-revenue sectors. Today, the corporation operates through three primary segments: biotechnology, life sciences, and diagnostics. By leveraging the Danaher Business System, a proprietary framework focused on continuous improvement, lean manufacturing, and operational excellence, the company has grown into an industry leader. It designs, manufactures, and markets professional, medical, and scientific products and services, empowering customers to accelerate the pace of scientific discovery and tackle complex medical challenges globally.
