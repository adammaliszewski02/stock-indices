Danaher Corporation (NYSE: DHR) is a leading global life sciences and diagnostics innovator. Headquartered in Washington, D.C., the company operates primarily across three core segments: Biotechnology, Life Sciences, and Diagnostics. Danaher develops, manufactures, and markets advanced technologies, consumables, and instruments that accelerate therapeutic discovery, enable biopharmaceutical manufacturing, and enhance critical medical diagnostics.

Originally founded in 1969 as a real estate investment trust, the business was transformed in 1984 by brothers Steven and Mitchell Rales, who renamed it after Montana’s Danaher Creek. The company built a reputation for operational excellence driven by the Danaher Business System, a continuous-improvement framework rooted in lean manufacturing principles. Through decades of strategic acquisitions and operational integration, Danaher expanded rapidly across diverse industrial markets. 

In recent years, the company orchestrated a deliberate pivot to become a focused healthcare and science pure-play. This transformation included landmark acquisitions such as Beckman Coulter, Pall Corporation, and Cytiva, alongside strategic spin-offs of its non-core industrial, dental, and environmental businesses—including Fortive, Envista, and Veralto. Today, Danaher provides essential tools and workflows to biopharmaceutical companies, commercial laboratories, and healthcare facilities worldwide.
