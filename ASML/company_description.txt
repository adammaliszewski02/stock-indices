ASML Holding N.V., traded under the ticker ASML, is a multinational corporation headquartered in Veldhoven, Netherlands, and a critical global supplier to the semiconductor industry. Founded in 1984 as a joint venture between ASM International and Philips, the company specializes in the development and manufacturing of advanced photolithography systems. These complex machines use ultraviolet light to pattern microscopic circuits onto silicon wafers, enabling the production of increasingly powerful and energy-efficient microchips. ASML is the undisputed market leader in extreme ultraviolet lithography technology, a capability essential for manufacturing the world's most advanced semiconductors used in artificial intelligence, smartphones, and high-performance computing. In addition to lithography equipment, the company provides hardware, software, and services that allow chipmakers to continuously improve chip yields and reduce production costs. ASML operates on a global scale, serving major semiconductor manufacturers worldwide, and plays a foundational role in the modern digital economy.
