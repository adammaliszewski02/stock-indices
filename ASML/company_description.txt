ASML Holding N.V., traded under the ticker ASML, is a multinational corporation headquartered in Veldhoven, Netherlands, and a critical global supplier to the semiconductor industry. Founded in 1984 as a joint venture between ASM International and Philips, the company specializes in the development and manufacturing of advanced photolithography systems. These complex machines are essential for microchip manufacturers to pattern microscopic circuits onto silicon wafers. ASML is the undisputed market leader in extreme ultraviolet lithography technology, which enables the production of the world’s most powerful and energy-efficient microchips utilized in artificial intelligence, smartphones, and computing infrastructure. The company generates revenue through the sale of these multi-million-dollar lithography systems, alongside related installation, maintenance, and upgrade services.
