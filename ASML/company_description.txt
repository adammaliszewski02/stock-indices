ASML Holding N.V. is a Dutch multinational corporation and the world’s leading supplier of photolithography systems for the semiconductor industry. Founded in 1984 as a joint venture between Philips and ASM International, the company has evolved into a critical pillar of the global technology supply chain. ASML designs and manufactures complex machines that use deep ultraviolet and extreme ultraviolet light to print nanometer-scale patterns onto silicon wafers, a process essential for producing the advanced microchips found in smartphones, computers, and artificial intelligence hardware. Headquartered in Veldhoven, the company maintains a unique market position as the sole provider of high-end extreme ultraviolet lithography systems globally. By enabling the continuous miniaturization of transistors, ASML plays a foundational role in driving the performance and efficiency gains required for modern computing and digital infrastructure.
