ASML Holding N.V., traded under the ticker ASML, is a multinational corporation headquartered in Veldhoven, Netherlands, and a critical backbone of the global semiconductor industry. The company specializes in the development and manufacturing of advanced photolithography systems, which are essential equipment used by chipmakers to etch microscopic circuits onto silicon wafers. Founded in 1984 as a joint venture between ASM International and Philips, ASML has grown to become the undisputed market leader in its field. The company is particularly renowned for pioneering Extreme Ultraviolet lithography, a groundbreaking technology that enables the production of the world’s most powerful and energy-efficient microchips used in smartphones, artificial intelligence, and high-performance computing. ASML operates on a global scale, serving major semiconductor manufacturers, and acts as a vital bellwether for technological advancement in the modern digital economy.
