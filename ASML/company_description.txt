ASML Holding N.V. is a multinational corporation headquartered in Veldhoven, Netherlands, and a leading global supplier to the semiconductor industry. Founded in 1984 as a joint venture between ASMI and Philips, the company specializes in the development and manufacturing of advanced photolithography systems. These complex lithography machines are essential for the production of microchips, utilizing extreme ultraviolet (EUV) and deep ultraviolet (DUV) light to etch microscopic circuit patterns onto silicon wafers. ASML serves major semiconductor manufacturers worldwide, enabling the continuous miniaturization, increased processing power, and energy efficiency of microprocessors and memory chips. Through decades of technological innovation, including the pioneering commercialization of EUV technology, the company has established a critical, foundational role in the global electronics supply chain, supporting the production of virtually all advanced integrated circuits used in modern computing, communications, and artificial intelligence infrastructure.
