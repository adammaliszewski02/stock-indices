ASML Holding N.V., traded under the ticker ASML, is a multinational corporation headquartered in Veldhoven, Netherlands, and a critical global supplier to the semiconductor industry. Founded in 1984 as a joint venture between ASM International and Philips, the company specializes in the development and manufacturing of advanced photolithography systems. These complex machines are essential for microchip manufacturers to pattern microscopic circuits onto silicon wafers. ASML is the undisputed market leader in extreme ultraviolet lithography technology, which enables the production of the world’s most powerful and energy-efficient microchips used in smartphones, computers, and artificial intelligence infrastructure. By continuously advancing semiconductor scaling, ASML plays a foundational role in enabling the ongoing evolution of modern digital technology.
