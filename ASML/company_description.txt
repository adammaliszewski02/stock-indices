ASML Holding N.V. is a multinational corporation headquartered in Veldhoven, Netherlands, and a critical pillar of the global semiconductor industry. The company specializes in the development and manufacturing of advanced photolithography systems, which are essential equipment used by chipmakers to etch complex micro-circuits onto silicon wafers. ASML is the undisputed global leader in extreme ultraviolet lithography technology, enabling the production of increasingly smaller, faster, and more energy-efficient microchips for computing, mobile, and artificial intelligence applications. Founded in 1984 as a joint venture between ASM International and Philips under the name ASM Lithography, the company achieved complete independence in 1995 and subsequently went public. Today, ASML operates on a global scale, serving major semiconductor manufacturers worldwide and acting as a vital catalyst in the advancement of modern electronics.
