ASML Holding N.V., traded under the ticker ASML, is a multinational corporation headquartered in Veldhoven, Netherlands, and a pivotal supplier to the global semiconductor industry. Founded in 1984 as a joint venture between ASM International and Philips, the company specializes in the development and manufacturing of advanced photolithography systems. These complex systems are essential for microchip manufacturers to physically pattern microscopic circuitry onto silicon wafers. ASML is globally renowned as the market leader in extreme ultraviolet lithography technology, which enables the production of the world’s most powerful, energy-efficient microchips utilized in smartphones, computers, and artificial intelligence applications. The company generates revenue through the sale of these high-value lithography machines, alongside comprehensive customer service, system maintenance, and upgrade solutions.
