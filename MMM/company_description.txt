3M Company, formerly known as the Minnesota Mining and Manufacturing Company, is a diversified global technology leader headquartered in Maplewood, Minnesota. Founded in 1902 as a small-scale mining venture, the company pivoted early to industrial innovation, eventually becoming synonymous with material science and adhesive technology. Today, 3M operates through a vast portfolio that spans safety and industrial products, transportation and electronics, healthcare, and consumer goods. The company is widely recognized for its culture of research and development, which has produced iconic products ranging from Scotch tape and Post-it Notes to advanced filtration systems, dental materials, and reflective signage. By leveraging its proprietary technology platforms, 3M provides essential solutions to businesses and consumers worldwide, maintaining a significant presence in global supply chains through its commitment to science-based innovation and manufacturing excellence.
