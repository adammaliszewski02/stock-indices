The ticker MMM represents 3M Company, a globally diversified multinational conglomerate founded in 1902 as the Minnesota Mining and Manufacturing Company in Two Harbors, Minnesota. Initially established to mine corundum for sandpaper production, the enterprise evolved through continuous scientific innovation into a developer of tens of thousands of industrial, safety, consumer, and healthcare products. Notable historical milestones include the 1925 invention of masking tape and the 1980 introduction of Post-it Notes, which established the firm as a pioneer in commercializing proprietary adhesive and polymer technologies. Today, 3M operates through segments encompassing Safety and Industrial, Transportation and Electronics, Health Care, and Consumer goods. The company leverages advanced research and development to produce high-performance items ranging from filtration systems and abrasives to medical products and optical films. Through a legacy of disciplined material science and a commitment to sustainable technological advancement, 3M maintains a foundational presence across global manufacturing, healthcare, and retail sectors.
