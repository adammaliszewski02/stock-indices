Stock ticker MMM refers to 3M Company, a prominent American multinational conglomerate founded in 1902 as the Minnesota Mining and Manufacturing Company in Two Harbors, Minnesota. Originally established to mine mineral deposits for sandpaper, the enterprise evolved into a global innovation powerhouse. 3M operates across the industrial, worker safety, health care, and consumer goods sectors, leveraging advanced science and proprietary technologies to manufacture tens of thousands of products. Its diverse portfolio ranges from adhesives, abrasives, and laminates to medical products, personal protective equipment, and car care items. Headquartered in Maplewood, Minnesota, the company is globally recognized for its culture of continuous research and development, holding thousands of patents and introducing iconic consumer brands such as Post-it Notes and Scotch Tape.
