MMM is the stock ticker for 3M Company, a prominent American multinational conglomerate founded in 1902 as the Minnesota Mining and Manufacturing Company. Headquartered in Maplewood, Minnesota, the company initially focused on mining abrasive minerals before evolving into a diversified powerhouse of scientific research and product development. 3M applies advanced technology and innovation across a vast spectrum of industries, operating primarily through four business segments: Safety and Industrial, Transportation and Electronics, Health Care, and Consumer. The company’s extensive portfolio encompasses tens of thousands of products, ranging from abrasives, adhesives, and laminates to passive fire protection, personal protective equipment, medical supplies, and iconic consumer goods such as Post-it Notes and Scotch Tape. 3M distinguishes itself through a strong commitment to research and development, holding thousands of patents and continuously translating fundamental science into practical, everyday commercial solutions for global markets.
