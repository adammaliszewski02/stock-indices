Ticker MMM represents 3M Company, a globally recognized multinational conglomerate founded in 1902 as the Minnesota Mining and Manufacturing Company in Two Harbors, Minnesota. Originally established to mine corundum for grinding wheels, the company transitioned into research and product development, pioneering pressure-sensitive adhesives that led to the invention of masking tape and Scotch tape. Today, 3M operates extensively across industrial, safety, consumer, and healthcare sectors. Headquartered in Maplewood, Minnesota, the enterprise leverages advanced material science to manufacture tens of thousands of differentiated products, ranging from filtration systems and abrasives to medical supplies and electronic components. Driven by a long-standing corporate culture of innovation, 3M continues to serve diverse global markets through science-based solutions that enhance everyday life and industrial productivity.
