The ticker symbol MMM represents 3M Company, a prominent American multinational conglomerate founded in 1902 as the Minnesota Mining and Manufacturing Company. Headquartered in Maplewood, Minnesota, the company operates across diverse sectors, including safety and industrial, transportation and electronics, health care, and consumer goods. 

Throughout its history, 3M has evolved from a small-scale mining venture into a global innovation powerhouse renowned for its extensive research and development. The company is perhaps best recognized for inventing iconic products such as Scotch tape, Post-it Notes, and Thinsulate insulation. 3M applies science and manufacturing expertise to deliver thousands of distinct products that serve industrial markets, medical facilities, and everyday household needs. By leveraging a culture of continuous scientific exploration, 3M maintains a vast global footprint, providing essential technologies and materials to customers in nearly every corner of the modern economy.
