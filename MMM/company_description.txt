MMM is the stock ticker for 3M Company, a prominent American multinational conglomerate founded in 1902 as the Minnesota Mining and Manufacturing Company. Headquartered in Maplewood, Minnesota, the company applies science and innovation to collaborate with customers and create impactful solutions. 3M operates across diverse sectors, including safety and industrial, transportation and electronics, health care, and consumer goods. Historically renowned for inventing iconic products such as Scotching Tape, Post-it Notes, and reflective materials for roadways, the company has evolved into a global science and technology powerhouse holding thousands of patents. 3M focuses on continuous research and development to address complex global challenges through advanced manufacturing, filtration, abrasives, and adhesives. The organization remains committed to driving sustainable growth and delivering value to its customers, employees, and shareholders worldwide.
