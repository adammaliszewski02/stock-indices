The ticker MMM represents 3M Company, a globally recognized multinational conglomerate founded in 1902 as the Minnesota Mining and Manufacturing Company in Two Harbors, Minnesota. Originally established to mine corundum for grinding wheels, the enterprise transitioned into industrial research and product development after early mining ventures proved unsuccessful. 

Today, 3M operates across four primary business segments: Safety and Industrial, Transportation and Electronics, Health Care, and Consumer. The company applies advanced science and collaborative innovation to develop thousands of imaginative products that improve daily life. Its diverse portfolio includes well-known brands and essential items such as Post-it Notes, Scotch Tape, Thinsulate insulation, abrasive materials, medical supplies, and advanced personal protective equipment. Headquartered in Maplewood, Minnesota, 3M serves customers in diverse industries ranging from electronics and manufacturing to healthcare and safety, maintaining a legacy of research-driven technological advancement and global manufacturing excellence.
