Bank of America Corporation (BAC) is one of the world's leading financial institutions, providing a comprehensive range of banking, investing, asset management, and other financial and risk-management products and services. Headquartered in Charlotte, North Carolina, the company serves individual consumers, small and middle-market businesses, corporations, and governments through a robust digital platform and an extensive physical branch network. 

The institution's origins trace back to the 1904 founding of the Bank of Italy in San Francisco by Amadeo Giannini, which later became Bank of America to serve immigrants and working-class people. Over the decades, through strategic growth and major mergers—most notably the 1998 merger with NationsBank and the 2008 acquisition of Merrill Lynch during the global financial crisis—the firm expanded its footprint significantly. Today, Bank of America operates as a global systemically important bank, playing a foundational role in the U.S. and international financial systems by facilitating capital formation, liquidity, and economic growth.
