Bank of America Corporation (BAC) is one of the world's leading financial institutions, providing a comprehensive range of banking, investing, asset management, and other financial and risk-management products and services. Headquartered in Charlotte, North Carolina, the company traces its roots back to 1784 with the founding of Massachusetts Bank, alongside Amadeo Giannini’s 1904 establishment of the Bank of Italy in San Francisco, which later became Bank of America. Through a long series of strategic mergers and acquisitions—most notably the 1998 merger of NationsBank and BankAmerica, and the 2008 acquisition of Merrill Lynch during the global financial crisis—the firm expanded into a universal banking powerhouse. Today, Bank of America serves millions of individual consumers, small and middle-market businesses, and large corporations through a robust digital platform and an extensive physical branch network across the United States. The company operates primarily through segments including Consumer Banking, Global Wealth and Investment Management, Global Banking, and Global Markets, playing a foundational role in the domestic and international financial systems while driving innovation in digital banking services.
