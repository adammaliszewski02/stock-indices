Bank of America Corporation (BAC) is one of the world's leading financial institutions, providing a comprehensive range of banking, investing, asset management, and other financial and risk-management products and services. Headquartered in Charlotte, North Carolina, the company serves individual consumers, small and middle-market businesses, corporations, and governments through a robust digital platform and a vast network of financial centers across the United States. 

The institution traces its roots to 1784 with the founding of Massachusetts Bank, though its modern lineage was largely shaped through a series of significant mergers and acquisitions. Most notably, the 1998 merger between NationsBank and BankAmerica created the entity known today as Bank of America. Further expanding its footprint, the company acquired brokerage giant Merrill Lynch and mortgage lender Countrywide Financial during the 2008 financial crisis. Today, BAC operates as a globally systemic bank, committed to driving responsible growth and supporting economic mobility for its clients and communities.
