Bank of America Corporation (BAC) is one of the world's leading financial institutions, headquartered in Charlotte, North Carolina. Operating as a premier financial services company, it serves individual consumers, small and middle-market businesses, corporations, and institutional investors with a full range of banking, investing, asset management, and other financial and risk-management products and services. 

The company's origins trace back to the 1904 founding of the Bank of Italy in San Francisco by Amadeo Giannini, which later became Bank of America to serve the working class ignored by traditional institutions. Through strategic expansions and major historical acquisitions—most notably FleetBoston Financial in 2004 and Merrill Lynch & Co. during the 2008 financial crisis—the firm evolved into its current nationwide and global footprint. Today, Bank of America operates through segments including Consumer Banking, Global Wealth and Investment Management, Global Banking, and Global Markets, leveraging an extensive digital platform alongside a massive branch and ATM network to deliver comprehensive financial solutions globally.
