Bank of America Corporation (BAC) is one of the world's leading financial institutions, providing a comprehensive range of banking, investing, asset management, and other financial and risk-management products and services. Headquartered in Charlotte, North Carolina, the company serves individual consumers, small and middle-market businesses, large corporations, and governments with a full suite of financial services. 

The institution traces its roots to 1784 with the founding of Massachusetts Bank, though its modern lineage was largely shaped through the 1998 merger of NationsBank and BankAmerica, as well as the pivotal 2008 acquisition of Merrill Lynch during the global financial crisis. Today, Bank of America operates one of the largest financial services networks in the United States, driven by a strategic focus on digital banking innovation, responsible growth, and sustainable finance.
