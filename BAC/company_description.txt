Bank of America Corporation (BAC) is one of the world's leading financial institutions, providing a comprehensive range of banking, investing, asset management, and other financial and risk-management products and services. Headquartered in Charlotte, North Carolina, the company serves individual consumers, small and middle-market businesses, corporations, and governments through a robust digital platform and an extensive physical branch network. 

The institution's origins trace back to 1904, when Amadeo Peter Giannini founded the Bank of Italy in San Francisco to serve immigrants and working-class people denied service by traditional banks. Following the 1906 San Francisco earthquake, Giannini famously resumed operations immediately from a makeshift desk, establishing a legacy of resilience. The institution later became Bank of America. Through significant strategic growth—most notably the 1998 merger with NationsBank and the 2008 acquisition of Merrill Lynch—the company expanded its footprint nationwide and solidified its position as a global financial powerhouse. Today, operating under the ticker BAC, the corporation remains a foundational pillar of the global economy, consistently recognized for its commitment to responsible growth, community development, and technological innovation in the financial sector.
