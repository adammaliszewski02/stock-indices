Bank of America Corporation (BAC) is one of the world's leading financial institutions, providing a comprehensive range of banking, investing, asset management, and other financial and risk-management products and services. Headquartered in Charlotte, North Carolina, the company serves individual consumers, small and middle-market businesses, corporations, and governments through a robust digital platform and an extensive physical branch network. 

The institution traces its origins to the 1904 founding of the Bank of Italy in San Francisco by Amadeo Giannini, which later became Bank of America to serve immigrants and working-class people excluded from traditional banking. Through a series of landmark mergers and acquisitions over the subsequent century—most notably the 1998 merger with NationsBank and the 2008 acquisition of Merrill Lynch during the global financial crisis—the company expanded its footprint nationwide and solidified its position as a global financial powerhouse. Today, operating under its iconic ticker on the New York Stock Exchange, Bank of America remains a pillar of the global economy, driven by a commitment to responsible growth, operational excellence, and technological innovation in financial services.
