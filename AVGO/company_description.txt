Broadcom Inc., trading under the ticker AVGO, is a global technology leader that designs, develops, and supplies a broad range of semiconductor and infrastructure software solutions. The company’s core business encompasses enterprise software, data center networking, broadband, wireless, and industrial products that serve the networking, telecommunications, storage, and industrial markets. 

The company's origins trace back to Hewlett-Packard’s semiconductor division, which was spun off in 1999 as Agilent Technologies. Agilent's semiconductor business was subsequently acquired in 2005 by private equity firms and renamed Avago Technologies. In 2016, Avago acquired the original Broadcom Corporation and adopted the iconic Broadcom name, subsequently executing major strategic expansions. These included the 2018 acquisition of CA Technologies, the 2019 purchase of Symantec’s enterprise security business, and the landmark acquisition of VMware in 2023. Headquartered in Palo Alto, California, Broadcom plays a foundational role in modern digital infrastructure by providing the essential silicon components and virtualization software that power cloud computing, artificial intelligence workloads, and global telecommunications networks.
