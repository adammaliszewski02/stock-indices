Broadcom Inc. (AVGO) is a global technology leader that designs, develops, and supplies a broad range of semiconductor and infrastructure software solutions. The company’s extensive product portfolio serves critical markets including data centers, networking, enterprise software, broadband, wireless, and storage. Broadcom’s business model focuses on complex engineering and high-performance hardware, complemented by a robust software division that provides mission-critical infrastructure management and security tools.

The company traces its origins to the semiconductor business of Hewlett-Packard, which was spun off as Agilent Technologies in 1999 and subsequently became Avago Technologies in 2005. In 2016, Avago Technologies acquired the original Broadcom Corporation, adopting the Broadcom name to reflect its expanded scale and market reach. Since then, the company has pursued a disciplined growth strategy through significant acquisitions, including CA Technologies, Symantec’s enterprise security business, and VMware. Today, Broadcom is recognized as a cornerstone of the modern digital economy, providing the essential components and software platforms that power global telecommunications, cloud computing, and enterprise IT environments.
