Broadcom Inc., trading under the ticker AVGO, is a global technology leader that designs, develops, and supplies a wide range of semiconductor and infrastructure software solutions. The company’s product offerings serve critical markets including data centers, networking, enterprise software, broadband, wireless, and industrial storage. 

The company’s origins trace back to 1961 as a semiconductor products division of Hewlett-Packard. It later operated as Agilent Technologies before being spun off as Avago Technologies in 2005 through a private equity buyout. In 2016, Avago acquired the original Broadcom Corporation and adopted the iconic name, subsequently executing several landmark strategic acquisitions, including CA Technologies in 2018, Symantec’s enterprise security business in 2019, and cloud computing giant VMware in 2023. Headquartered in Palo Alto, California, Broadcom plays a foundational role in modern digital infrastructure by providing the essential hardware and software components that power global connectivity and cloud computing.
