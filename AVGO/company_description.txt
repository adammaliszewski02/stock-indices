Broadcom Inc., trading under the ticker AVGO, is a global technology leader that designs, develops, and supplies a broad range of semiconductor and infrastructure software solutions. The company’s core business encompasses enterprise software, data center networking, broadband, wireless connectivity, and storage products that power modern digital infrastructure, cloud computing, and telecommunications. 

The company's origins trace back to Hewlett-Packard’s semiconductor division, which was spun off as Agilent Technologies in 1999. Agilent's semiconductor operations were subsequently acquired in 2005 to form Avago Technologies. In 2016, Avago acquired the original Broadcom Corporation and adopted the Broadcom name, subsequently executing a series of high-profile strategic acquisitions, including CA Technologies, Symantec’s enterprise security business, and VMware in late 2023. Headquartered in Palo Alto, California, Broadcom has evolved into one of the world's most influential technology conglomerates, driving critical innovations in artificial intelligence networking, custom silicon accelerators, and mission-critical enterprise software.
