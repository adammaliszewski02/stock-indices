Broadcom Inc., trading under the ticker AVGO, is a global technology leader that designs, develops, and supplies a wide range of semiconductor and infrastructure software solutions. The company’s core business encompasses enterprise software, data center networking, broadband, wireless connectivity, and storage products that power modern digital infrastructure, cloud computing, and telecommunications networks. 

The enterprise's origins trace back to Hewlett-Packard’s semiconductor division, which was spun off in 1999 to form Agilent Technologies. Agilent subsequently spun off its semiconductor operations in 2005 to create Avago Technologies. In 2016, Avago acquired the iconic enterprise Broadcom Corporation for $37 billion, adopted the Broadcom name, and relocated its legal headquarters to the United States. Over the following decade, Broadcom executed a transformative growth strategy centered on major strategic acquisitions, most notably the 2018 purchase of CA Technologies, the 2019 acquisition of Symantec’s enterprise security business, and the landmark 2023 acquisition of VMware. Today, under the leadership of Chief Executive Officer Hock Tan, AVGO stands as one of the world's most valuable technology conglomerates, bridging foundational silicon hardware with mission-critical infrastructure software.
