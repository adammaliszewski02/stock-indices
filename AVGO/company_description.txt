Broadcom Inc., trading under the ticker AVGO, is a global technology leader that designs, develops, and supplies a wide range of semiconductor and infrastructure software solutions. The company’s product offerings serve critical markets including data centers, networking, enterprise software, broadband, wireless, and industrial applications. 

The enterprise traces its origins to Hewlett-Packard’s semiconductor division, which was spun off as Agilent Technologies in 1999 and subsequently established as Avago Technologies in 2005. A pivotal moment in the company's history occurred in 2016 when Avago acquired the original Broadcom Corporation for $37 billion and adopted the iconic name. Under the leadership of CEO Hock Tan, the company pursued an aggressive growth strategy defined by major acquisitions, most notably the purchases of CA Technologies in 2018, Symantec’s enterprise security business in 2019, and the landmark $69 billion acquisition of cloud computing giant VMware in 2023. Today, Broadcom is recognized as a cornerstone of the modern digital infrastructure, supplying essential components and software that power global connectivity and cloud computing.
