Broadcom Inc. (AVGO) is a global technology leader that designs, develops, and supplies a broad range of semiconductor and infrastructure software solutions. The company’s core business encompasses enterprise software, data center networking, broadband, wireless connectivity, and storage products that power modern cloud computing, telecommunications, and industrial systems. 

The company's roots trace back to Hewlett-Packard’s semiconductor division, which was spun off in 1999 to form Agilent Technologies. Agilent subsequently spun off its semiconductor operations in 2005 to create Avago Technologies. In a pivotal moment of corporate growth, Avago acquired the original Broadcom Corporation in 2016 for $37 billion and adopted the Broadcom name, retaining the iconic AVGO ticker symbol. 

Under the leadership of CEO Hock Tan, Broadcom expanded its strategic footprint through major acquisitions, including CA Technologies in 2018, Symantec’s enterprise security business in 2019, and the landmark $69 billion acquisition of VMware in 2023. Today, Broadcom plays a critical role in the global technology supply chain, delivering advanced custom silicon for artificial intelligence workloads alongside mission-critical infrastructure software for enterprises worldwide.
