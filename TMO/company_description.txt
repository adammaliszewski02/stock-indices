Thermo Fisher Scientific Inc., trading under the ticker TMO, is a global leader in serving science, providing analytical instruments, life sciences solutions, specialty diagnostics, and laboratory services. Headquartered in Waltham, Massachusetts, the company enables customers to accelerate life sciences research, solve complex analytical challenges, improve patient diagnostics, deliver therapeutics, and increase laboratory productivity. 

The company’s modern corporate lineage traces back to 2006 through the merger of Thermo Electron Corporation—founded in 1956 as an independent research and development laboratory—and Fisher Scientific, a venerable distributor of laboratory equipment established in 1902. Over subsequent decades, Thermo Fisher significantly expanded its global reach and scientific portfolio through strategic acquisitions, most notably integrating major industry players such as Life Technologies in 2014, Patheon in 2017, and PPD in 2021. Today, through its premier brands including Thermo Scientific, Applied Biosystems, Invitrogen, Fisher Scientific, Unity Lab Services, and PPD, the company supports a diverse customer base spanning pharmaceutical and biotech companies, hospitals, clinical diagnostic laboratories, universities, research institutions, and government agencies worldwide.
