Thermo Fisher Scientific Inc., traded under the ticker symbol TMO, is a global leader in serving science, providing analytical instruments, life sciences solutions, specialty diagnostics, laboratory equipment, and pharmaceutical services. The company traces its origins to Thermo Electron, founded in 1956 by George Hatsopoulos and Peter Nomikos, and Fisher Scientific, established in 1902 as a laboratory supply distributor by Pittsburgh chemist Chester G. Fisher. The two entities merged in 2006 to form the current enterprise. Headquartered in Waltham, Massachusetts, Thermo Fisher Scientific enables its customers to accelerate life sciences research, solve complex analytical challenges, improve patient diagnostics, deliver therapeutics to market, and increase laboratory productivity. Through its extensive portfolio of premier brands—including Applied Biosystems, Invitrogen, Fisher Scientific, Unity Lab Services, and Patheon—the corporation serves pharmaceutical and biotech companies, hospitals, clinical diagnostic laboratories, universities, research institutions, and government agencies worldwide.
