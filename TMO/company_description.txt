Thermo Fisher Scientific Inc., trading under the ticker TMO, is a global leader in serving science, providing analytical instruments, life sciences solutions, specialty diagnostics, and laboratory services. Headquartered in Waltham, Massachusetts, the company enables customers to accelerate life sciences research, solve complex analytical challenges, improve patient diagnostics, deliver therapeutics, and increase laboratory productivity. 

The company’s modern iteration stems from a landmark 2006 transformational merger between Thermo Electron, a pioneer in analytical instrumentation founded in 1956, and Fisher Scientific, a historic distributor of laboratory equipment established in 1902. Through subsequent strategic acquisitions, including the high-profile integrations of Life Technologies in 2014, Patheon in 2017, and PPD in 2021, Thermo Fisher significantly expanded its capabilities into clinical research, biopharmaceutical services, and genetic sequencing. Today, operating through premier brands such as Thermo Scientific, Applied Biosystems, Invitrogen, Fisher Scientific, and Unity Lab Services, the company leverages its extensive scale and scientific expertise to support pharmaceutical and biotech companies, hospitals, clinical diagnostic laboratories, academic institutions, and government agencies worldwide.
