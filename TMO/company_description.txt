Thermo Fisher Scientific Inc., traded under the ticker TMO, is a global leader in scientific instruments, reagents, consumables, and healthcare services. The company provides advanced analytical technologies, laboratory equipment, software, and specialty diagnostics to pharmaceutical and biotechnology firms, academic institutions, clinical laboratories, and government agencies. Additionally, Thermo Fisher offers comprehensive clinical research and contract development and manufacturing organization (CDMO) services, supporting clients across the drug discovery and manufacturing lifecycle.

The enterprise was formed in November 2006 through the merger of Thermo Electron, founded in 1956, and Fisher Scientific, which originated in 1902. Following the merger, the company significantly expanded its market presence and technical capabilities through major strategic acquisitions, including Life Technologies in 2014, Patheon in 2017, and clinical research provider PPD in 2021. Headquartered in Waltham, Massachusetts, Thermo Fisher conducts its global business across four core segments: Life Sciences Solutions, Analytical Instruments, Specialty Diagnostics, and Laboratory Products and Biopharma Services.
