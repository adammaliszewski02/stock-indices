Thermo Fisher Scientific Inc., trading under the ticker TMO, is a global leader in serving science, providing analytical instruments, life sciences solutions, specialty diagnostics, and laboratory services. Headquartered in Waltham, Massachusetts, the company enables customers to accelerate life sciences research, solve complex analytical challenges, improve patient diagnostics, deliver therapeutics, and increase laboratory productivity. 

The company's roots trace back to the 1956 founding of Thermo Electron, which grew into a prominent instrumentation and technology company. In 2006, Thermo Electron merged with Fisher Scientific, a leading provider of laboratory equipment, chemicals, and supplies, to form Thermo Fisher Scientific. Since this transformative merger, the company has significantly expanded its capabilities and global footprint through strategic acquisitions, including the prominent integrations of Life Technologies in 2014, Patheon in 2017, and PPD in 2021. Today, Thermo Fisher Scientific operates on a global scale, supporting pharmaceutical and biotech companies, hospitals and clinical laboratories, universities, research institutions, and government agencies with a comprehensive portfolio of advanced technologies, consumable supplies, and pharmaceutical services.
