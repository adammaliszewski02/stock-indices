Thermo Fisher Scientific Inc., trading under the ticker TMO, is a global leader in serving science, providing analytical instruments, life sciences solutions, specialty diagnostics, laboratory equipment, and pharmaceutical services. The company traces its origins to the 1956 founding of Thermo Electron, which initially focused on energy-efficiency technologies before pivoting toward biomedical and analytical instrumentation through decades of strategic diversification. A major milestone occurred in 2006 when Thermo Electron merged with Fisher Scientific, a premier provider of laboratory supplies and reagents, establishing the current corporate entity. Today, TMO empowers customers in pharmaceutical and biotechnology companies, hospitals and clinical diagnostic laboratories, universities, research institutions, and government agencies to accelerate life sciences research, solve complex analytical challenges, improve patient diagnostics, and increase laboratory productivity.
