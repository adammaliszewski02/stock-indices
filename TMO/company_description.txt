Thermo Fisher Scientific Inc., trading under the ticker TMO, is a global leader in serving science, providing analytical instruments, life sciences solutions, specialty diagnostics, and laboratory services. Headquartered in Waltham, Massachusetts, the company enables customers to accelerate life sciences research, solve complex analytical challenges, improve patient diagnostics, deliver therapeutics, and increase laboratory productivity. 

The company's roots trace back to Thermo Electron, founded in 1956 by inventor and scientist George Hatsopoulos, and Fisher Scientific, established in 1902 as a laboratory supply distributor. The two entities merged in 2006 to form Thermo Fisher Scientific, creating an expansive enterprise that now supports pharmaceutical and biotech companies, hospitals, clinical diagnostic laboratories, universities, research institutions, and government agencies worldwide. Through continuous strategic acquisitions—including prominent additions such as Life Technologies in 2014, Patheon in 2017, and PPD in 2021—Thermo Fisher has expanded its capabilities from scientific instrumentation into comprehensive biopharmaceutical services and clinical research. Today, the Fortune 500 company drives innovation across multiple scientific disciplines, operating through a network of specialized brands that collectively advance global health and scientific discovery.
