Thermo Fisher Scientific Inc., trading under the stock ticker TMO, is a leading global life sciences company that provides analytical instruments, laboratory equipment, reagents, software, and services. Headquartered in Waltham, Massachusetts, the corporation serves pharmaceutical and biotech companies, hospitals, clinical diagnostic laboratories, universities, and government agencies. 

The company’s modern iteration was formed through the landmark 2006 merger of Thermo Electron Corporation—founded in 1956 as an inventor of high-tech products—and Fisher Scientific, a historic laboratory supply distributor established in 1902. Over subsequent decades, Thermo Fisher significantly expanded its capabilities and market reach through major strategic acquisitions, including Life Technologies in 2014, Patheon in 2017, and PPD in 2021. Today, the enterprise operates through four primary segments: Life Sciences Solutions, Analytical Instruments, Specialty Diagnostics, and Laboratory Products and Biopharma Services. Through this comprehensive portfolio, Thermo Fisher plays a critical role in advancing scientific research, supporting pharmaceutical development, accelerating drug discovery, and improving patient diagnostics worldwide.
