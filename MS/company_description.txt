Morgan Stanley (NYSE: MS) is a prominent global financial services firm headquartered in New York City. Established in 1935 by Henry S. Morgan, Harold Stanley, and their partners in response to the Glass-Steagall Act, the firm was formed to specialize in investment banking following its separation from J.P. Morgan & Co. 

Today, Morgan Stanley operates across three core business segments: Institutional Securities, Wealth Management, and Investment Management. Through Institutional Securities, the firm provides corporate advisory, capital raising, research, and sales and trading services across equities and fixed income for institutions, corporations, and governments. The Wealth Management division offers comprehensive brokerage, financial planning, and lending solutions to individual investors and high-net-worth clients. Complementing these is the Investment Management arm, which delivers diverse asset management strategies across equity, fixed income, and alternative investments. 

Over the past decade, Morgan Stanley has strategically transformed its business model, moving toward more stable, recurring, fee-based revenue. This evolution was significantly accelerated by landmark acquisitions, including E*TRADE Financial and Eaton Vance, cementing the firm’s status as an industry leader in both global investment banking and wealth management.
