The stock ticker MS identifies Morgan Stanley, a leading global financial services firm headquartered in New York City. The company operates through three primary business segments: Institutional Securities, Wealth Management, and Investment Management. It provides investment banking, capital markets, wealth planning, and asset management services to a diverse global client base of corporations, governments, financial institutions, and individuals.

Morgan Stanley was founded in 1935 by Henry Sturgis Morgan and Harold Stanley following the Glass-Steagall Act, which required the separation of commercial and investment banking operations from J.P. Morgan & Co. Over the decades, the institution expanded worldwide and solidified its reputation as a preeminent underwriter and financial advisor. In 1997, it broadened its retail brokerage reach by merging with Dean Witter Discover & Co. Following the 2008 financial crisis, Morgan Stanley restructured as a bank holding company and strategically pivoted toward more stable, fee-based revenues. This transformation was further accelerated in recent years through the major acquisitions of E*TRADE and Eaton Vance, firmly establishing the firm as a dominant force in modern wealth and asset management.
