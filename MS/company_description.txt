Morgan Stanley, identified by the stock ticker MS, is a leading global financial services firm headquartered in New York City. The company operates through three primary segments: Institutional Securities, Wealth Management, and Investment Management. It provides a comprehensive range of financial products and services to a diverse client base, including corporations, governments, financial institutions, and individuals. 

Tracing its origins back to 1935, the firm was founded by Henry S. Morgan and Harold Stanley alongside other partners following the passage of the Glass-Steagall Act, which required the separation of commercial and investment banking. Initially established as an investment bank, Morgan Stanley has continually evolved through strategic expansions and major acquisitions. A milestone occurred in 1997 with its merger with Dean Witter, Discover & Co., which significantly expanded its retail brokerage and asset management footprint. Over the decades, the firm has solidified its position as a dominant force in global capital markets, advisory services, and wealth preservation.
