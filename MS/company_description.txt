Morgan Stanley, identified by the stock ticker MS, is a leading global financial services firm headquartered in New York City. The company operates through three primary segments: Institutional Securities, Wealth Management, and Investment Management. It provides a comprehensive range of financial products and services to a diverse client base, including corporations, governments, financial institutions, and high-net-worth individuals. Traded on the New York Stock Exchange, the firm has a rich history that began with its founding in 1935 by Henry S. Morgan and Harold Stanley, following the Glass-Steagall Act's mandate to separate commercial and investment banking. Over the decades, Morgan Stanley has grown into an industry titan, significantly expanding its footprint through major strategic milestones, most notably its 1997 merger with Dean Witter, Discover & Co. and the pivotal acquisition of Smith Barney in 2009. Today, the firm continues to shape the global financial landscape through advisory, underwriting, wealth advisory, and asset management services.
