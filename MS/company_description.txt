Morgan Stanley, trading under the stock ticker MS, is a preeminent global financial services firm headquartered in New York City. Founded in 1935 as a spin-off of J.P. Morgan & Co. following the Glass-Steagall Act, the company has evolved into a diversified institution operating across three primary segments: Institutional Securities, Wealth Management, and Investment Management. 

The firm provides a comprehensive range of services to corporations, governments, financial institutions, and high-net-worth individuals, including investment banking, securities underwriting, mergers and acquisitions advisory, wealth planning, and asset management. Over the decades, Morgan Stanley has significantly expanded its footprint and capabilities through strategic transactions, most notably the 1997 merger with Dean Witter, Discover & Co. and the transformative acquisitions of Smith Barney and Eaton Vance in the twenty-first century. Today, the company stands as a cornerstone of the modern global financial system, recognized for its leadership in capital markets and advisory services.
