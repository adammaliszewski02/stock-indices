Morgan Stanley (ticker MS) is a leading global financial services firm headquartered in New York City. Founded in 1935 by Henry S. Morgan and Harold Stanley following the Glass-Steagall Act, the firm initially operated as an investment bank. Over the decades, it evolved into a diversified financial institution serving a global client base that includes corporations, governments, financial institutions, and high-net-worth individuals. 

The company operates through three primary business segments: Institutional Securities, Wealth Management, and Investment Management. The Institutional Securities division provides capital raising, financial advisory services, and sales and trading. Wealth Management offers comprehensive financial planning, brokerage, and investment advisory services. Investment Management delivers a broad range of investment strategies and products globally across public and private markets. 

A significant milestone in its modern history was the 1997 merger with Dean Witter, Discover & Co., which greatly expanded its retail brokerage and credit services. In recent years, Morgan Stanley has further reinforced its wealth and investment management capabilities through major strategic acquisitions, notably purchasing Eaton Vance in 2021 and E*TRADE in 2020. Today, the firm is recognized globally for its financial expertise and market influence.
