Morgan Stanley, identified by the stock ticker MS, is a leading global financial services firm headquartered in New York City. The company operates through three primary segments: Institutional Securities, Wealth Management, and Investment Management. It provides a comprehensive range of financial products and services to a diverse client base, including corporations, governments, financial institutions, and high-net-worth individuals. 

The firm's origins date back to 1935, when Henry S. Morgan and Harold Stanley, along with other colleagues, founded the company following the passage of the Glass-Steagall Act, which required the separation of commercial and investment banking. Initially established as a pure-play investment bank, Morgan Stanley steadily expanded its capabilities over the decades. A pivotal moment occurred in 1997 when the firm merged with Dean Witter, Discover & Co., significantly broadening its retail brokerage and asset management footprint. Following the 2008 global financial crisis, Morgan Stanley transitioned into a financial holding company and strategically emphasized its wealth and investment management divisions to achieve a more stable, recurring revenue model. Today, it remains one of the most prominent and influential institutions in the global financial architecture.
