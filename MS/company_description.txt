Morgan Stanley (ticker symbol MS) is a leading global financial services firm headquartered in New York City. Founded in 1935 by Henry S. Morgan and Harold Stanley following the Glass-Steagall Act, the company initially established itself as a premier investment banking firm. Over the decades, it has evolved into a diversified financial institution operating through three primary segments: Institutional Securities, Wealth Management, and Investment Management. 

The firm provides a comprehensive range of services to a large and diversified group of clients and customers, including corporations, governments, financial institutions, and individuals. Its offerings encompass capital raising and financial advisory services, securities underwriting, corporate lending, wealth planning, brokerage services, and asset management. 

Through strategic acquisitions, most notably the integration of Dean Witter Discover & Co. in 1997 and Smith Barney, alongside major recent expansions into digital brokerage and institutional investment management, Morgan Stanley has cemented its position as one of the world's most influential and systemically important financial institutions.
