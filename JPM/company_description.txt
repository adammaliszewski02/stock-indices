JPMorgan Chase & Co. is a leading global financial services firm and one of the largest banking institutions in the world. Headquartered in New York City, the company operates through four primary business segments: consumer and community banking, corporate and investment banking, commercial banking, and asset and wealth management. It provides a comprehensive suite of services, including investment banking, financial services for consumers and small businesses, commercial banking, financial transaction processing, and asset management.

The firm’s lineage traces back over 200 years, rooted in the founding of The Manhattan Company in 1799. The modern entity was formed through the 2000 merger of J.P. Morgan & Co. and The Chase Manhattan Corporation, followed by significant acquisitions during the 2008 financial crisis, including the operations of Bear Stearns and Washington Mutual. Today, JPMorgan Chase serves millions of customers and many of the world’s most prominent corporate, institutional, and government clients, maintaining a dominant position in the global financial landscape through its extensive branch network and sophisticated digital banking platforms.
