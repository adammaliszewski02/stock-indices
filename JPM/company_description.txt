JPM is the stock ticker for JPMorgan Chase & Co., a leading global financial services firm headquartered in New York City. The institution traces its origins back to 1799 with the founding of the Bank of the Manhattan Company, making it one of the oldest financial institutions in the world. Through a series of major historical mergers and acquisitions—including Chase Manhattan, J.P. Morgan & Co., Bank One, and Bear Stearns—the company evolved into its modern form under the leadership of longtime Chief Executive Officer Jamie Dimon. 

Today, JPMorgan Chase operates as a diversified financial powerhouse, organized primarily into four major business segments: Consumer & Community Banking, Corporate & Investment Bank, Commercial Banking, and Asset & Wealth Management. It provides a comprehensive range of services, including investment banking, asset management, treasury services, and personal banking, serving millions of consumers, corporations, governments, and institutional clients globally. Recognized as the largest bank in the United States by total assets, the firm plays a central role in the domestic and international financial systems.
