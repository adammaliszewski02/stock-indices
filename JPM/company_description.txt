JPM refers to JPMorgan Chase & Co., a leading global financial services firm and the largest bank in the United States by assets. Headquartered in New York City, the institution operates through four primary business segments: Consumer & Community Banking, Corporate & Investment Bank, Commercial Banking, and Asset & Wealth Management. It provides a comprehensive range of financial services to millions of consumers, small businesses, corporations, institutional investors, and governments worldwide. 

The company traces its origins back to 1799 with the founding of The Bank of the Manhattan Company, making it one of the oldest financial institutions in the world. Over the centuries, it grew through a series of significant mergers and acquisitions, most notably the 2000 merger between Chase Manhattan Corporation and J.P. Morgan & Co., followed by the pivotal acquisitions of Bear Stearns and Washington Mutual during the 2008 financial crisis. Today, under the leadership of Jamie Dimon, the firm is widely recognized for its strong balance sheet, technological innovation, and dominant position in global investment banking and retail financial services.
