JPMorgan Chase & Co., identified by the ticker JPM, is a leading global financial services firm and one of the oldest banking institutions in the United States, with historical roots tracing back to 1799 through predecessor entities like the Bank of the Manhattan Company. Headquartered in New York City, the corporation operates through four primary business segments: Consumer and Community Banking, Corporate and Investment Bank, Commercial Banking, and Asset and Wealth Management. JPM provides a comprehensive range of financial services to a diverse global client base, including corporations, governments, institutions, and millions of individual consumers. Its extensive operations encompass investment banking, treasury and securities services, asset management, private banking, and retail financial services. Through decades of strategic mergers and acquisitions—most notably the integration of Chase Manhattan Bank, Bank One, and operations of Bear Stearns and Washington Mutual during the 2008 financial crisis—JPMorgan Chase has evolved into the largest bank in the United States by total assets and a foundational pillar of the international financial system.
