JPMorgan Chase & Co. is a leading global financial services firm and one of the oldest banking institutions in the United States, tracing its lineage back to 1799 through predecessor organizations such as the Manhattan Company. Headquartered in New York City, the company operates as a multinational financial powerhouse, delivering a comprehensive suite of services across four primary segments. These include consumer and community banking, corporate and investment banking, commercial banking, and asset and wealth management. Serving millions of consumers, small businesses, corporations, institutional investors, and governments globally, the firm facilitates commercial lending, treasury services, investment banking, market making, and wealth advisory. The modern enterprise is the result of numerous historical mergers and acquisitions, most notably the 2000 merger between Chase Manhattan Corporation and J.P. Morgan & Co., followed by the strategic acquisitions of Bank One, Bear Stearns, and Washington Mutual during the 2008 financial crisis. Today, JPMorgan Chase stands as the largest bank in the United States by total assets and remains a foundational pillar of the global financial architecture.
