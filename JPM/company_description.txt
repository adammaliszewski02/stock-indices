The stock ticker JPM represents JPMorgan Chase & Co., the largest bank in the United States by assets and one of the world's leading financial services firms. Headquartered in New York City, the company operates across four primary business segments: Consumer & Community Banking, Corporate & Investment Bank, Commercial Banking, and Asset & Wealth Management. Through these divisions, it provides retail banking, credit cards, corporate financing, investment banking advisory, capital markets trading, and investment management to millions of consumers, small businesses, and institutional clients globally.

The firm’s history spans more than two centuries, tracing its earliest lineage back to the founding of The Manhattan Company in 1799. The modern institution was shaped by the landmark 2000 merger of J.P. Morgan & Co.—the legacy bank of renowned financier J. Pierpont Morgan—and The Chase Manhattan Corporation. Over the decades, JPMorgan Chase has solidified its market leadership through strategic acquisitions, notably absorbing Bear Stearns and Washington Mutual during the 2008 financial crisis, as well as First Republic Bank in 2023. As a global systemically important bank, it plays a vital role in international finance and global economic infrastructure.
