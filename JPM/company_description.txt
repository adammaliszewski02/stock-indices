JPMorgan Chase & Co. is a leading global financial services firm and one of the oldest banking institutions in the United States, with roots tracing back to 1799 through the Manhattan Company. Headquartered in New York City, the company operates as a pillar of the modern global economy, offering a comprehensive range of financial services to corporations, governments, institutions, and millions of individual consumers worldwide. Its core business activities are structured around four major segments: consumer and community banking, corporate and investment banking, commercial banking, and asset and wealth management. The firm serves its client base through its prominent consumer brand, Chase, as well as its legacy investment banking operations associated with J.P. Morgan. Through decades of strategic mergers and acquisitions, most notably the 2000 merger of Chase Manhattan Corporation and J.P. Morgan & Co., alongside the 2008 acquisitions of Bear Stearns and Washington Mutual during the financial crisis, the company has grown to become the largest bank in the United States by total assets.
