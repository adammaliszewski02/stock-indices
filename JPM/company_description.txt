JPMorgan Chase & Co. is a leading global financial services firm and one of the oldest banking institutions in the United States, with roots tracing back to 1799 through predecessor organizations like the Bank of the Manhattan Company. Headquartered in New York City, the company operates as a multinational financial powerhouse, providing a comprehensive range of services across four primary business segments: consumer and community banking, corporate and investment banking, commercial banking, and asset and wealth management. Serving millions of consumers, small businesses, corporations, institutional clients, and governments worldwide, JPMorgan Chase delivers solutions encompassing retail banking, credit cards, mortgage origination, treasury services, investment banking, and asset management. The firm operates extensively through its primary banking subsidiary, JPMorgan Chase Bank, N.A., alongside its prominent credit card and consumer brand, Chase, maintaining a significant global footprint across major financial centers.
