The ticker PM identifies Philip Morris International Inc., a leading international tobacco company engaged in the manufacture and sale of cigarettes, smoke-free products, electronic devices, and nicotine-containing products. Originally founded in 1847 as a single London tobacco shop by Mr. Philip Morris, the enterprise expanded globally over the decades. A major historical milestone occurred in March 2008, when the company was spun off from Altria Group, establishing Philip Morris International as an independent, publicly traded entity focused exclusively on markets outside of the United States. In recent years, the corporation has undertaken a significant strategic transformation, shifting substantial capital and research toward a smoke-free future, highlighted by the commercialization of heated tobacco products and oral nicotine alternatives alongside its traditional cigarette portfolio.
