The ticker symbol PM identifies Philip Morris International Inc., a leading international tobacco company engaged in the manufacture and sale of cigarettes, smoke-free products, electronic devices, and nicotine-containing alternatives. Originally incorporated in Virginia in 1987, the company's historical roots trace back to a London shop opened by Mr. Philip Morris in 1847. For decades, the enterprise operated as the international tobacco business of Altria Group. In March 2008, Philip Morris International was spun off into an independent, publicly traded entity, allowing it to focus exclusively on markets outside the United States. In recent years, the company has strategically shifted its business model toward a smoke-free future, heavily investing in the development, scientific substantiation, and commercialization of reduced-risk alternatives, most notably its flagship heated tobacco system, IQOS. Headquartered in Stamford, Connecticut, and operationally managed from Lausanne, Switzerland, the company distributes its products in approximately 180 markets worldwide.
