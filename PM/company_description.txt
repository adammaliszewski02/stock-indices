The stock ticker PM identifies Philip Morris International Inc., a leading international tobacco company engaged in the manufacture and sale of cigarettes, smoke-free products, and associated electronic devices and accessories. Originally incorporated in Virginia in 1987 as part of the Altria Group, the company historically operated as the international tobacco business of Philip Morris Companies Inc. In March 2008, Philip Morris International was spun off into an independent, publicly traded entity, officially decoupling its international operations from Altria, which retained the domestic U.S. business. Headquartered in Stamford, Connecticut, and operationally managed from Lausanne, Switzerland, the company’s portfolio features globally recognized brands, most notably Marlboro. In recent years, Philip Morris International has pursued a corporate transformation aimed at achieving a smoke-free future, driven by significant investments in reduced-risk alternatives such as heated tobacco and nicotine pouch products.
