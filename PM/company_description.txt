The stock ticker PM refers to Philip Morris International Inc., a leading international tobacco company. Originally established as a New York corporation in 1987, the enterprise historically operated as the international tobacco business of Philip Morris Companies Inc. (later renamed Altria Group, Inc.). In March 2008, Philip Morris International was spun off from Altria Group as an independent, publicly traded company, officially listing its shares on the New York Stock Exchange under the ticker symbol PM. Headquartered in Stamford, Connecticut, with its operational headquarters in Lausanne, Switzerland, the company engages in the manufacture and sale of cigarettes, smoke-free products, and other nicotine-containing items. Its diverse portfolio features globally recognized brands, most notably Marlboro. In recent years, the company has driven a strategic transformation aimed at achieving a smoke-free future, heavily investing in the research, development, and commercialization of scientifically substantiated reduced-risk alternatives, such as heated tobacco and oral nicotine products, which now account for a substantial and growing portion of its total net revenues.
