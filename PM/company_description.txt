Philip Morris International Inc., trading under the ticker PM, is a leading international tobacco company engaged in the manufacture and sale of cigarettes, smoke-free products, electronic devices, and nicotine-containing accessories. Originally incorporated in Virginia in 1987, the enterprise was historically part of the broader Philip Morris tobacco empire. A significant milestone occurred in March 2008, when Philip Morris International was spun off from Altria Group, establishing it as an independent, publicly traded entity focused exclusively on markets outside of the United States. Headquartered in Stamford, Connecticut, and operationally managed from Lausanne, Switzerland, the company markets its portfolio in over 180 countries. In recent years, PM has strategically transitioned its business model toward a smoke-free future, highlighted by the 2022 acquisition of Swedish Match and the continued global expansion of its flagship heated tobacco system, IQOS. This transformation emphasizes reduced-risk alternatives to traditional combustible cigarettes.
