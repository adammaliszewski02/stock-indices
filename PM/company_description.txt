Philip Morris International Inc., traded under the ticker PM, is a leading international tobacco company engaged in the manufacture and sale of cigarettes, smoke-free products, electronic devices, and nicotine-containing alternatives. Originally established as part of the Philip Morris tobacco business founded in London in 1847, the modern entity was officially formed in 2008 following its corporate spin-off from Altria Group, which separated its domestic US operations from its international business. Headquartered in Stamford, Connecticut, and incorporated in Virginia, the company operates in numerous markets globally. In recent years, Philip Morris International has undergone a significant strategic transformation, shifting its focus toward a smoke-free future by investing heavily in the development and commercialization of reduced-risk products, most notably its heated tobacco system, IQOS, and the 2022 acquisition of the oral nicotine company Swedish Match.
