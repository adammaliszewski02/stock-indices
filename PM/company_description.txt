The ticker symbol PM identifies Philip Morris International Inc., a leading international tobacco company engaged in the manufacture and sale of cigarettes, smoke-free products, and electronic devices. Originally incorporated in Virginia in 1987 as part of the Altria Group, the company’s history traces back to a London tobacco shop opened by Mr. Philip Morris in the mid-nineteenth century. Philip Morris International was officially established as an independent, publicly traded entity in March 2008 following its spin-off from Altria Group, which separated its domestic operations in the United States from its international business. Headquartered in Stamford, Connecticut, and operationally managed from Lausanne, Switzerland, the company today focuses strategically on transitioning its business model toward a smoke-free future, highlighted by the development and global commercialization of heated tobacco and nicotine pouch products.
