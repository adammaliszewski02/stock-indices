The ticker symbol PM identifies Philip Morris International Inc., a leading international tobacco company engaged in the manufacture and sale of cigarettes, smoke-free products, electronic devices, and nicotine-containing products. Originally established in 1847 as a London tobacco shop by Mr. Philip Morris, the enterprise expanded globally over the following century. A significant historical milestone occurred in March 2008, when Philip Morris International was spun off from Altria Group, becoming an independent, publicly traded entity focused exclusively on markets outside the United States. In recent years, the company has undergone a major strategic transformation, committing to a smoke-free future by shifting its substantial resources toward reduced-risk alternatives, most notably its flagship heated tobacco system, IQOS, alongside modern oral nicotine products following its 2022 acquisition of Swedish Match. Today, Philip Morris International markets its diverse portfolio in approximately 180 countries, employing tens of thousands of professionals worldwide to lead the industry's evolution beyond traditional combustion.
