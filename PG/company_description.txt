Company description: PG

The Procter & Gamble Company (NYSE: PG) is a world-leading consumer goods corporation specializing in personal care, hygiene, and household products. The company operates through iconic global brands across key segments, including Fabric & Home Care (Tide), Baby & Family Care (Pampers), Beauty (Olay, Head & Shoulders), Grooming (Gillette), and Health Care (Crest). 

Founded in 1837 by William Procter and James Gamble in Cincinnati, Ohio, the business originated as a modest soap and candle maker. Over its nearly two-century history, P&G expanded globally and revolutionized the consumer packaged goods industry by pioneering modern brand management and advertising techniques in the 20th century. 

Today, Procter & Gamble distributes its products in approximately 70 countries, serving billions of consumers worldwide. The company focuses on driving organic growth through continuous product innovation, supply chain efficiency, and digital transformation, consolidating its long-standing position as an essential cornerstone of the global consumer staples sector.
