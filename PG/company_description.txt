Company description: PG

Procter & Gamble (NYSE: PG) is a world-leading multinational consumer goods corporation specializing in personal care, hygiene, and household products. The company manufactures and markets its portfolio through five primary segments: Beauty, Grooming, Health Care, Fabric & Home Care, and Baby, Feminine & Family Care. Its lineup features globally recognized brands such as Tide, Pampers, Gillette, Crest, Downy, and Head & Shoulders.

Founded in 1837 by candlemaker William Procter and soapmaker James Gamble in Cincinnati, Ohio, the business originated as a modest soap and candle enterprise. Over its nearly two-century history, Procter & Gamble revolutionized the consumer goods industry by pioneering modern brand management, mass media advertising, and product innovation. Key historical milestones include the introduction of Ivory soap in 1879, which floated in water, and the 1961 launch of Pampers, which created the modern disposable diaper category. 

Today, Procter & Gamble leverages its extensive global supply chain, robust market research, and continuous digital innovation to serve billions of consumers across more than 180 countries, remaining a resilient leader in the fast-moving consumer goods sector.
