Company description: PG

The Procter & Gamble Company, trading under the ticker PG, is a global leader in the fast-moving consumer goods industry. Founded in 1837 by William Procter and James Gamble in Cincinnati, Ohio, the business originated as a modest soap and candle maker. Over nearly two centuries, it has expanded into a multinational powerhouse that develops, manufactures, and markets essential personal care, household cleaning, and hygiene products.

Today, Procter & Gamble operates across five primary segments: Beauty, Grooming, Health Care, Fabric & Home Care, and Baby, Feminine & Family Care. Its vast portfolio includes iconic, billion-dollar brands such as Tide, Pampers, Gillette, Crest, Head & Shoulders, and Downy. Driven by continuous innovation and extensive market research, the company sells its products in over 180 countries through mass merchandisers, grocery stores, e-commerce, and distributors. P&G’s strategic focus on daily-use, trusted consumer staples ensures its position as one of the most stable and influential consumer goods enterprises in the world.
