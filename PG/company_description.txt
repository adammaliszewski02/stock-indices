The stock ticker PG refers to Procter & Gamble, a multinational consumer goods corporation headquartered in Cincinnati, Ohio. Founded in 1837 by brothers-in-law William Procter and James Gamble as a small candle and soap manufacturing business, the company has evolved into one of the world’s largest and most influential consumer packaged goods enterprises. Procter & Gamble primarily operates through five core sectors: Beauty, Grooming, Health Care, Fabric and Home Care, and Baby, Feminine and Family Care. Its extensive brand portfolio features globally recognized names such as Tide, Pampers, Gillette, Crest, Head & Shoulders, and Pantene, distributed across approximately 180 countries. The company focuses on delivering superior, high-quality branded products through continuous innovation and disciplined operational execution. Over its nearly two-century history, Procter & Gamble has consistently adapted to shifting market dynamics, maintaining a strong commitment to shareholder value, brand equity, and global corporate sustainability.
