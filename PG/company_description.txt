Company description: PG

The Procter & Gamble Company (NYSE: PG) is a global leader in the fast-moving consumer goods industry, specializing in personal care, hygiene, and household cleaning products. Headquartered in Cincinnati, Ohio, the company operates across five primary segments: Beauty, Grooming, Health Care, Fabric & Home Care, and Baby, Feminine & Family Care. Its portfolio features world-renowned brands such as Tide, Pampers, Gillette, Crest, Bounty, and Head & Shoulders, serving billions of consumers worldwide.

Founded in 1837 by English candlemaker William Procter and Irish soapmaker James Gamble, the enterprise originated as a regional soap and candle business. Over its nearly two-century history, Procter & Gamble transformed into an international commercial powerhouse, credited with pioneering modern brand management and consumer research techniques. 

Today, PG focuses on driving growth through constant product innovation, supply chain optimization, and strategic marketing. By delivering essential, high-quality daily-use items, the company maintains a dominant market presence and generates consistent revenue across both developed and emerging markets globally.
