Company description: PG

The Procter & Gamble Company (NYSE: PG) is a leading American multinational consumer goods corporation specializing in a vast array of personal care, hygiene, and household products. Headquartered in Cincinnati, Ohio, the company operates across five primary segments: Beauty, Grooming, Health Care, Fabric & Home Care, and Baby, Feminine & Family Care. Its portfolio includes world-renowned brands such as Tide, Pampers, Gillette, Crest, Bounty, and Head & Shoulders.

Founded in 1837 by candle maker William Procter and soap maker James Gamble, the business originated as a modest regional partnership utilizing local animal fats from Cincinnati's meatpacking industry. Over nearly two centuries, P&G evolved into one of the largest and most influential consumer packaged goods enterprises globally. Historically, the company is also recognized for pioneering modern brand management and mass media advertising, notably sponsoring early radio and television dramas that birthed the phrase "soap opera." 

Today, Procter & Gamble generates tens of billions of dollars in annual revenue by manufacturing and marketing essential daily-use products to consumers in over 180 countries, maintaining its market leadership through continuous innovation and deep-rooted brand loyalty.
