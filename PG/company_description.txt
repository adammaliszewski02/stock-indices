Company description: PG

The Procter & Gamble Company, trading under the ticker PG, is a global leader in the consumer packaged goods industry. Founded in 1837 by William Procter and James Gamble in Cincinnati, Ohio, the company originated as a modest soap and candle maker. Over nearly two centuries, it has evolved into one of the world's largest and most influential multinational corporations.

Today, Procter & Gamble specializes in the development, manufacturing, and distribution of personal care, hygiene, and household cleaning products. Its business operations are divided into several core segments: Beauty, Grooming, Health Care, Fabric & Home Care, and Baby, Feminine & Family Care. P&G manages a portfolio of globally recognized brands, including Tide, Pampers, Gillette, Crest, Head & Shoulders, and Downy. 

The company operates in more than 180 countries, selling its products through mass merchandisers, grocery stores, pharmacies, e-commerce platforms, and direct-to-consumer channels. By prioritizing product innovation, strategic marketing, and supply chain scale, Procter & Gamble consistently drives stable revenue while fulfilling essential daily consumer needs worldwide.
