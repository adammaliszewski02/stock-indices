Company description: PG

Procter & Gamble (NYSE: PG) is a global consumer goods leader specializing in a wide range of personal care, hygiene, and household cleaning products. The company operates across five primary segments: Beauty, Grooming, Health Care, Fabric & Home Care, and Baby, Feminine & Family Care. Its portfolio features iconic, trusted brands such as Tide, Pampers, Gillette, Crest, and Head & Shoulders, reaching consumers in over 180 countries.

Founded in 1837 by candle maker William Procter and soap maker James Gamble in Cincinnati, Ohio, the business began as a modest regional shop. During the American Civil War, the company gained significant national exposure by securing contracts to supply soap and candles to the Union Army. Over its nearly two-century history, Procter & Gamble evolved into an international powerhouse driven by pioneering research, brand acquisition, and memorable marketing campaigns. 

Today, Procter & Gamble focuses on driving organic growth through continuous product innovation and supply chain optimization, maintaining its status as an essential, defensive staple in global equity markets.
