Company description: PG

The Procter & Gamble Company, trading under the ticker symbol PG, is an American multinational consumer goods corporation specializing in personal health, hygiene, and household products. Founded in 1837 by William Procter and James Gamble in Cincinnati, Ohio, the business originally began as a small candle and soap manufacturer. 

Over nearly two centuries, Procter & Gamble has evolved into one of the world's premier consumer packaged goods leaders, generating tens of billions of dollars in annual revenue. The company operates through five key business segments: Beauty, Grooming, Health Care, Fabric & Home Care, and Baby, Feminine & Family Care. Its portfolio features dozens of globally recognized household brands, including Tide, Pampers, Gillette, Crest, Head & Shoulders, and Downy. 

Today, Procter & Gamble manufactures and markets essential everyday products across more than 180 countries. Driven by ongoing product innovation, brand equity, and massive global supply chains, the company maintains dominant market share across its core product categories while serving billions of consumers worldwide.
