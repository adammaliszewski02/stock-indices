The Procter & Gamble Company, trading under the ticker PG on the New York Stock Exchange, is a leading American multinational consumer goods corporation. Founded in 1837 by William Procter and James Gamble in Cincinnati, Ohio, the business began as a small soap and candle maker. The company expanded significantly during the American Civil War by supplying the Union Army and later revolutionized commercial marketing, famously sponsoring the daytime radio dramas that popularized the term "soap opera."

Today, Procter & Gamble manufactures and markets branded consumer packaged goods across five core segments: Beauty, Grooming, Health Care, Fabric & Home Care, and Baby, Feminine & Family Care. Its portfolio includes some of the world’s most recognized household brands, such as Tide, Pampers, Gillette, Crest, Head & Shoulders, Bounty, and Charmin. P&G sells its products in approximately 180 countries and territories, maintaining its global market leadership through continuous product innovation, extensive retail distribution networks, and massive scale in supply chain operations.
