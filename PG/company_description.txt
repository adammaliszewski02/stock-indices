Company description: PG

The Procter & Gamble Company, trading under the ticker symbol PG, is a leading American multinational consumer goods corporation headquartered in Cincinnati, Ohio. Founded in 1837 by candlemaker William Procter and soapmaker James Gamble, the enterprise originated as a regional soap and candle business before expanding into a global powerhouse known for pioneering modern consumer marketing and product innovation. 

Today, Procter & Gamble specializes in the manufacture and international distribution of essential personal care, hygiene, and household products. The company operates through five primary business segments: Beauty, Grooming, Health Care, Fabric and Home Care, and Baby, Feminine, and Family Care. Its extensive portfolio features dozens of trusted, world-renowned brands, including Tide, Pampers, Gillette, Crest, Bounty, Charmin, and Head & Shoulders. By focusing on everyday necessities driven by continuous research and development, Procter & Gamble serves billions of consumers across more than 180 countries, maintaining a dominant market presence and generating consistent long-term value.
