Company description: PG

Procter & Gamble (NYSE: PG) is a globally renowned consumer goods corporation headquartered in Cincinnati, Ohio. Founded in 1837 by candle maker William Procter and soap maker James Gamble, the company began as a small regional business and evolved into one of the largest and most influential multinational corporations in the world. 

Procter & Gamble specializes in the manufacture and marketing of a vast array of personal care, hygiene, and household consumer products. Its portfolio features iconic, market-leading brands such as Tide, Pampers, Gillette, Crest, Downy, and Head & Shoulders. The company structures its operations into five primary business segments: Beauty, Grooming, Health Care, Fabric & Home Care, and Baby, Feminine & Family Care. 

Serving billions of consumers across more than 180 countries, Procter & Gamble leverages extensive research and development, supply chain efficiency, and brand equity to maintain its industry dominance. Throughout its nearly two-century history, P&G has pioneered modern consumer product manufacturing and brand management, consistently generating stable revenue by providing essential, daily-use items.
