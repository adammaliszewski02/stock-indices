Company description: PG

Procter & Gamble (NYSE: PG) is a global consumer goods leader specializing in the development, manufacturing, and marketing of personal care, hygiene, and household products. Founded in 1837 by candle maker William Procter and soap maker James Gamble in Cincinnati, Ohio, the company originated as a modest soap and candle business. Over nearly two centuries, it has expanded into an international powerhouse operating across five core segments: Beauty, Grooming, Health Care, Fabric & Home Care, and Baby, Feminine & Family Care.

Today, Procter & Gamble manages a portfolio of iconic, trusted brands, including Tide, Pampers, Gillette, Crest, and Head & Shoulders, serving billions of consumers across more than 180 countries. The company drives growth through continuous product innovation, strategic marketing, and supply chain optimization to meet evolving consumer needs. Recognized for its operational scale, brand equity, and financial stability—highlighted by a decades-long track record of dividend growth—Procter & Gamble remains a foundational benchmark within the global consumer staples sector.
