Company description: PG

The Procter & Gamble Company, trading under the ticker PG, is a global leader in the fast-moving consumer goods industry. Founded in 1837 by candle maker William Procter and soap maker James Gamble in Cincinnati, Ohio, the company originated as a small soap and candle business. Over nearly two centuries, it has evolved into a multinational titan focused on personal care, hygiene, and household products. 

Procter & Gamble manufactures and markets its portfolio across several core segments: Fabric and Home Care, Baby, Feminine and Family Care, Beauty, Grooming, and Health Care. Its product line features iconic, daily-use brands such as Tide, Pampers, Gillette, Crest, Pantene, and Downy. 

With operations in approximately 70 countries and products sold in over 180, Procter & Gamble leverages extensive research, supply chain excellence, and strategic marketing to serve billions of consumers worldwide. The company remains a cornerstone of the global consumer staples sector, driven by continuous product innovation and a deeply established retail presence.
