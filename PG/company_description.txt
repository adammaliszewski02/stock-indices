Company description: PG

Procter & Gamble (NYSE: PG) is an American multinational consumer goods corporation headquartered in Cincinnati, Ohio. The company specializes in personal care, hygiene, and household cleaning products across five main business segments: Beauty, Grooming, Health Care, Fabric & Home Care, and Baby, Feminine & Family Care. Its portfolio includes Tide, Pampers, Gillette, Crest, and Head & Shoulders. Founded in 1837, P&G serves consumers in more than 180 countries and focuses on essential everyday products, product innovation, and global supply networks.
