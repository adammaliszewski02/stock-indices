Company description: PG

The Procter & Gamble Company, trading under the ticker PG, is a global leader in the consumer packaged goods industry. Headquartered in Cincinnati, Ohio, the company manufactures and markets a vast portfolio of trusted personal care, household cleaning, and hygiene products. Its world-renowned brands include Tide, Pampers, Gillette, Crest, Downy, and Head & Shoulders, operating across key segments such as Beauty, Grooming, Health Care, Fabric & Home Care, and Baby Care.

Founded in 1837 by candle maker William Procter and soap maker James Gamble, the business originated as a modest regional enterprise. Over its nearly two-century history, P&G transformed into a multinational powerhouse by pioneering modern brand management, consumer research, and iconic innovations, including Ivory soap in 1879 and the mass market's first disposable diaper, Pampers, in 1961. 

Today, Procter & Gamble serves billions of consumers across more than 180 countries and territories. The company generates revenue by distributing its essential everyday items through mass merchandisers, e-commerce platforms, grocery stores, and distributors, maintaining its market leadership through continuous product innovation, strong brand equity, and operational efficiency.
