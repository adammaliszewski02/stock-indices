The Procter & Gamble Company, trading under the stock ticker PG, is a leading multinational consumer goods corporation headquartered in Cincinnati, Ohio. Founded in 1837 by William Procter and James Gamble as a small, family-operated soap and candle manufacturer, the company has evolved into a global powerhouse. P&G specializes in a wide range of branded consumer packaged goods, organized primarily into beauty, grooming, health care, fabric and home care, and baby, feminine, and family care sectors. Its portfolio features globally recognized household names such as Tide, Pampers, Gillette, Crest, and Head & Shoulders. By focusing on consumer-centric innovation, rigorous market research, and iconic brand building, Procter & Gamble has sustained long-term growth and remains one of the world's most stable and influential corporations, serving billions of consumers across more than 180 countries.
