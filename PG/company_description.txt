Company description: PG

The Procter & Gamble Company (NYSE: PG) is a world-leading American consumer goods corporation specializing in a vast array of personal care, hygiene, and household products. Founded in 1837 by candle maker William Procter and soap maker James Gamble in Cincinnati, Ohio, the company originated as a simple soap and candle business before growing into a global enterprise over nearly two centuries.

Today, Procter & Gamble operates across five primary business segments: Beauty, Grooming, Health Care, Fabric & Home Care, and Baby, Feminine & Family Care. Its expansive portfolio features iconic, billion-dollar brands such as Tide, Pampers, Gillette, Crest, Head & Shoulders, and Bounty. Headquartered in Cincinnati, P&G manufactures products in dozens of nations and sells them to consumers in more than 180 countries worldwide. Driven by continuous product innovation, strategic marketing, and extensive distribution networks, the company focuses on providing essential, daily-use consumer goods while maintaining a legendary track record of corporate resilience and consistent dividend growth for its shareholders.
