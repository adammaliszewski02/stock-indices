Company description: PG

The Procter & Gamble Company (NYSE: PG) is a global leader in the fast-moving consumer goods industry, specializing in personal care, hygiene, and household products. Founded in 1837 by William Procter and James Gamble in Cincinnati, Ohio, the enterprise originally began as a small candle and soap manufacturing operation. Over nearly two centuries, it has evolved into one of the world’s most influential consumer product corporations.

Today, Procter & Gamble operates across five primary business segments: Beauty, Grooming, Health Care, Fabric & Home Care, and Baby, Feminine & Family Care. The company manufactures and markets a vast portfolio of iconic, daily-use brands, including Tide, Pampers, Gillette, Crest, and Head & Shoulders. By continually investing in product innovation and expanding its omni-channel retail presence globally, Procter & Gamble delivers essential consumer staples to billions of customers across more than 180 countries, generating sustained value through strong brand equity and operational scale.
