Company description: PG

Procter & Gamble (NYSE: PG) is a leading American multinational consumer goods corporation specializing in personal care, hygiene, and household products. The company manufactures and markets a diverse portfolio of globally recognized brands across key segments, including Fabric & Home Care (Tide, Ariel), Baby & Family Care (Pampers, Bounty), Beauty (Olay, Head & Shoulders), Grooming (Gillette), and Health Care (Crest, Vicks).

Founded in 1837 in Cincinnati, Ohio, by candlemaker William Procter and soapmaker James Gamble, the business originated as a modest soap and candle enterprise. Over nearly two centuries, P&G transformed the consumer landscape through landmark innovations, such as the introduction of Ivory soap in 1879 and pioneering modern brand management and market research techniques in the 20th century. 

Today, Procter & Gamble serves billions of consumers across more than 180 countries. Its primary operations focus on leveraging brand equity, supply chain excellence, and continuous product innovation to drive long-term shareholder value and maintain leadership in the global fast-moving consumer goods sector.
