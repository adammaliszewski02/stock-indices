The ticker symbol PG refers to Procter & Gamble, a multinational consumer goods corporation founded in 1837 by William Procter and James Gamble in Cincinnati, Ohio. The company originally established its reputation by manufacturing candles and soap, leveraging abundant animal fats from the region's meatpacking industry. Over the decades, Procter & Gamble expanded its operations globally and diversified its portfolio to encompass a vast array of everyday household and personal care products. Its core business focuses on developing, marketing, and distributing branded consumer packaged goods across multiple categories, including beauty, grooming, health care, fabric care, and home care. Operating in over 180 countries, the company manages a robust portfolio of well-known global brands, such as Tide, Pampers, Gillette, Crest, and Head & Shoulders. Procter & Gamble employs a centralized research and development strategy to drive product innovation, maintaining its market leadership through continuous adaptation to consumer trends and large-scale retail distribution networks.
