Company description: PG

The Procter & Gamble Company (NYSE: PG) is a global leader in the fast-moving consumer goods industry, specializing in a vast array of personal care, hygiene, and household cleaning products. Founded in 1837 by candle maker William Procter and soap maker James Gamble in Cincinnati, Ohio, the business originated as a modest regional operation. Over nearly two centuries, it has expanded into an international conglomerate.

Today, Procter & Gamble manufactures and markets renowned everyday brands—including Tide, Pampers, Gillette, Crest, Head & Shoulders, and Downy—to consumers in over 180 countries. The company operates through five core business segments: Beauty, Grooming, Health Care, Fabric & Home Care, and Baby, Feminine & Family Care. 

P&G focuses on driving long-term organic growth through continuous product innovation, robust brand management, and massive global supply chain capabilities. By prioritizing essential, high-frequency consumer items, the company maintains a highly resilient business model designed to deliver consistent value to both global consumers and shareholders.
