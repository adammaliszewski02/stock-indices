Company description: PG

Procter & Gamble (NYSE: PG) is a premier global consumer goods corporation specializing in personal care, hygiene, and household products. Founded in 1837 in Cincinnati, Ohio, by candlemaker William Procter and soapmaker James Gamble, the enterprise initially focused on soaps and candles, famously supplying the Union Army during the American Civil War. 

Over nearly two centuries, P&G has evolved into an international powerhouse operating across five main business segments: Beauty, Grooming, Health Care, Fabric & Home Care, and Baby, Female & Family Care. Its vast portfolio features iconic, market-leading brands such as Tide, Pampers, Gillette, Crest, Downy, and Head & Shoulders. 

The company generates revenue by developing, manufacturing, and marketing these fast-moving consumer goods to retailers, mass merchandisers, and e-commerce platforms worldwide. Driven by robust innovation, strategic brand management, and supply chain efficiency, Procter & Gamble maintains a market-leading position in daily-use consumer products and remains a foundational constituent of the S&P 500 index.
