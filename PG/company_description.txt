Company description: PG

Procter & Gamble Co. (NYSE: PG) is a globally recognized American multinational consumer goods corporation headquartered in Cincinnati, Ohio. Founded in 1837 by candle maker William Procter and soap maker James Gamble, the company originated as a small regional business and evolved into one of the world's premier consumer staples companies. Historically, P&G is celebrated for pioneering modern market research, brand management, and commercial advertising techniques.

Today, Procter & Gamble specializes in the manufacture and marketing of personal care, hygiene, and household products across more than 180 countries. Its operations are organized into key segments, including Fabric & Home Care, Baby, Feminine & Family Care, Beauty, Grooming, and Healthcare. P&G manages an extensive portfolio of trusted, billion-dollar flagship brands, including Tide, Pampers, Gillette, Crest, Downy, Head & Shoulders, and Bounty. 

By leveraging deep consumer insights and continuous technological innovation, P&G focuses on providing superior daily-use essentials. The company maintains a robust global supply chain and strong retail partnerships, ensuring its products remain a constant presence in billions of households worldwide.
