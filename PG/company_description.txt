Company description: PG

The Procter & Gamble Company, trading under the ticker symbol PG, is a premier global leader in the consumer packaged goods industry. Founded in 1837 by William Procter and James Gamble in Cincinnati, Ohio, the enterprise originated as a small candle and soap-making business before expanding into one of the world’s most influential multinational corporations. 

Today, Procter & Gamble focuses on the development, manufacturing, and global marketing of a vast array of personal care, hygiene, and household products. The company operates through five core business segments: Beauty, Grooming, Health Care, Fabric & Home Care, and Baby, Feminine & Family Care. Its portfolio features dozens of iconic, billion-dollar brands that consumers rely on daily, including Tide, Pampers, Gillette, Crest, Head & Shoulders, and Downy. 

Headquartered in Ohio, P&G serves billions of consumers across more than 180 countries. The company drives its market leadership through continuous product innovation, robust supply chain operations, and strategic brand management, aiming to deliver superior quality and value in everyday essential items.
