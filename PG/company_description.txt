Company description: PG

The Procter & Gamble Company, trading under the ticker symbol PG, is a global leader in the consumer packaged goods industry. Founded in 1837 by candle maker William Procter and soap maker James Gamble in Cincinnati, Ohio, the company originated as a modest soap and candle business. Over its nearly two-century history, Procter & Gamble has expanded into a multinational powerhouse that manufactures and markets a wide array of personal health, hygiene, and household cleaning products.

Today, the company operates across several core segments, including Fabric and Home Care, Baby, Feminine and Family Care, Beauty, Grooming, and Health Care. Procter & Gamble’s portfolio features many of the world’s most iconic and trusted household brands, such as Tide, Pampers, Gillette, Crest, Downy, and Head & Shoulders. By focusing on consumer-driven innovation, brand building, and extensive global distribution, the company generates robust revenue by providing essential daily-use products to billions of consumers worldwide.
