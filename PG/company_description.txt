The ticker PG refers to The Procter & Gamble Company, a multinational consumer goods corporation headquartered in Cincinnati, Ohio. Founded in 1837 by William Procter and James Gamble as a small, family-operated soap and candle maker, the enterprise has evolved into a global leader in branded consumer packaged goods. The company operates through distinct segments, including Beauty, Grooming, Health Care, Fabric & Home Care, and Baby, Feminine & Family Care. Its expansive portfolio features globally recognized brands such as Tide, Pampers, Gillette, Crest, and Pantene, which are distributed in approximately 180 countries. Driven by a commitment to consumer-focused innovation, sustainable practices, and strategic brand management, Procter & Gamble remains a cornerstone of the fast-moving consumer goods industry, consistently delivering essential everyday products to billions of consumers worldwide.
