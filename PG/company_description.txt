Company description: PG

The Procter & Gamble Company (NYSE: PG) is a global leader in the consumer packaged goods industry, specializing in a broad range of personal care, hygiene, and household cleaning products. Founded in 1837 by candle maker William Procter and soap maker James Gamble in Cincinnati, Ohio, the company has grown over nearly two centuries from a local partnership into one of the world's largest and most valuable corporations. 

Today, Procter & Gamble operates through five main business segments: Beauty, Grooming, Health Care, Fabric & Home Care, and Baby, Feminine & Family Care. Its portfolio includes dozens of internationally recognized, iconic brands such as Tide, Pampers, Gillette, Crest, Head & Shoulders, and Downy. 

Headquartered in Cincinnati, P&G focuses on brand building, product innovation, and large-scale global distribution. By delivering essential, daily-use consumer goods, the company serves billions of consumers worldwide, maintaining a strong market presence across both developed and emerging economies.
