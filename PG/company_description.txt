Company description: PG

Procter & Gamble (NYSE: PG) is a world-leading American multinational consumer goods corporation headquartered in Cincinnati, Ohio. The company specializes in the manufacture and global distribution of a vast array of personal care, hygiene, and household products. Its portfolio features iconic, market-leading brands such as Tide, Pampers, Gillette, Crest, Downy, and Head & Shoulders, organized across core segments including Beauty, Grooming, Health Care, Fabric & Home Care, and Baby, Feminine & Family Care. 

Founded in 1837 by English candle maker William Procter and Irish soap maker James Gamble, the business began as a modest soap and candle producer. Over nearly two centuries, Procter & Gamble evolved into a global powerhouse through continuous product innovation, strategic international expansion, and pioneering marketing strategies, such as sponsoring early radio and television "soap operas." 

Today, Procter & Gamble operates in roughly 70 countries and serves billions of consumers worldwide. The company's business model relies on generating consistent, long-term growth by providing trusted, daily-use essential items that command high brand loyalty across diverse global markets.
