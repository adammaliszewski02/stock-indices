Company description: PG

Procter & Gamble (NYSE: PG) is a global leader in the consumer packaged goods industry, specializing in a vast array of personal care, hygiene, and household products. Founded in 1837 by William Procter and James Gamble in Cincinnati, Ohio, the company originated as a modest candle and soap manufacturer before expanding into an international enterprise. 

Today, P&G operates through five primary business segments: Beauty, Grooming, Health Care, Fabric & Home Care, and Baby, Feminine & Family Care. Its portfolio features dozens of trusted, world-renowned brands, including Tide, Pampers, Gillette, Crest, Bounty, and Head & Shoulders. Still headquartered in Cincinnati, Procter & Gamble serves billions of consumers across more than 180 countries. The company focuses on developing high-quality, daily-use essential items, leveraging continuous product innovation and aggressive marketing to maintain market leadership and deliver consistent value to shareholders.
