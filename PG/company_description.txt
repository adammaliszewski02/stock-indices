Company description: PG

The Procter & Gamble Company, traded under the ticker symbol PG, is a premier American multinational consumer goods corporation specializing in a vast array of personal care, household cleaning, grooming, and hygiene products. 

Founded in 1837 by candle maker William Procter and soap maker James Gamble in Cincinnati, Ohio, the company began as a humble local business. It expanded significantly during the American Civil War after securing contracts to supply the Union Army with soap and candles. Over nearly two centuries, P&G has evolved into one of the world's largest consumer products manufacturers.

Today, Procter & Gamble operates globally through five core business segments: Beauty, Grooming, Health Care, Fabric & Home Care, and Baby, Feminine & Family Care. Its portfolio features dozens of globally recognized billion-dollar brands, including Tide, Pampers, Gillette, Crest, Pantene, and Downy. The company focuses on continuous product innovation, extensive mass-market retail distribution, and strategic brand management to maintain leadership across consumer markets worldwide.
