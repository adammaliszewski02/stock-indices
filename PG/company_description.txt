Company description: PG

Procter & Gamble (NYSE: PG) is a global leader in the fast-moving consumer goods industry, specializing in personal care, hygiene, and household products. Founded in 1837 by candlemaker William Procter and soapmaker James Gamble in Cincinnati, Ohio, the company originated as a small regional enterprise and evolved into one of the world's largest multinational corporations, now operating in approximately 180 countries.

P&G’s business model revolves around developing, manufacturing, and marketing iconic, everyday products. The company divides its operations into five main segments: Beauty, Grooming, Health Care, Fabric & Home Care, and Baby, Feminine & Family Care. Its vast portfolio features globally recognized brands such as Tide, Pampers, Gillette, Crest, Head & Shoulders, and Downy. 

Through relentless focus on product innovation, brand equity, and supply chain efficiency, Procter & Gamble provides essential consumer goods designed to meet daily health, hygiene, and cleaning needs. P&G continues to drive consistent value by adapting to changing consumer preferences, expanding into emerging markets, and maintaining leadership in the global consumer packaged goods sector.
