Company description: PG

Procter & Gamble (NYSE: PG) is a global leader in the fast-moving consumer goods industry, specializing in personal care, hygiene, and household products. Founded in 1837 by candle maker William Procter and soap maker James Gamble in Cincinnati, Ohio, the company began as a modest regional business and subsequently grew into one of the world's largest and most valuable multinational corporations. 

Today, Procter & Gamble manufactures and markets essential everyday items across five core segments: Beauty, Grooming, Health Care, Fabric & Home Care, and Baby, Feminine & Family Care. Its portfolio features iconic, market-leading brands such as Tide, Pampers, Gillette, Crest, Head & Shoulders, and Downy. 

Operating in over 180 countries, the company focuses on consumer-driven innovation, strategic brand management, and scalable supply chain operations. By continually adapting to shifting market demand and delivering superior product performance, Procter & Gamble maintains a prominent position in households worldwide while generating consistent value for its shareholders.
