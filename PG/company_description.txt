Company description: PG

Procter & Gamble Co. (NYSE: PG) is a global leader in the fast-moving consumer goods industry, specializing in personal care, hygiene, and household products. Founded in 1837 by candle maker William Procter and soap maker James Gamble in Cincinnati, Ohio, the company originated as a small regional business before expanding into an international enterprise. 

Today, Procter & Gamble manufactures and markets essential daily-use items across key categories, including grooming, home care, family care, and personal health. The company owns a dominant portfolio of trusted, world-renowned brands such as Tide, Pampers, Gillette, Crest, Downy, Ariel, and Head & Shoulders. 

P&G serves billions of consumers across more than 180 countries and territories. Its products are distributed through mass merchandisers, grocery stores, membership club stores, pharmacies, and rapidly growing e-commerce platforms. Driven by continuous innovation, brand management, and a robust global supply chain, Procter & Gamble remains dedicated to delivering high-quality, superior-performing products that enhance the daily lives of consumers worldwide.
