The Procter & Gamble Company, trading under the ticker PG, is a multinational consumer goods corporation headquartered in Cincinnati, Ohio. Founded in 1837 by William Procter and James Gamble as a small, family-operated soap and candle maker, the company has evolved into one of the world's largest and most influential consumer products enterprises. P&G focuses on providing branded, high-quality products organized across several key consumer segments, including beauty, grooming, health care, fabric and home care, and baby, feminine, and family care. Its globally recognized portfolio features iconic household names such as Tide, Pampers, Gillette, Crest, and Head & Shoulders. The company distributes its extensive product offerings in approximately 180 countries primarily through mass merchandisers, e-commerce platforms, grocery stores, membership club stores, drugstores, and high-frequency stores. P&G operates on a strategic model emphasizing superior product performance, packaging, brand communication, retail execution, and value, underpinned by a continuous commitment to sustainable innovation and operational efficiency.
