Company description: PG

Procter & Gamble (NYSE: PG) is a global consumer packaged goods giant specializing in personal care, hygiene, and household products. Founded in 1837 by candle maker William Procter and soap maker James Gamble in Cincinnati, Ohio, the company originated as a modest soap and candle operation. Over its nearly two-century history, P&G has evolved into a multinational powerhouse, famous for pioneering modern brand management and consumer research. 

Today, the company operates in over 180 countries and is organized into five primary business segments: Fabric & Home Care, Baby, Feminine & Family Care, Beauty, Grooming, and Health Care. P&G manufactures and markets an iconic portfolio of trusted, daily-use brands, including Tide, Pampers, Gillette, Crest, Downy, and Head & Shoulders. 

By focusing on essential, high-frequency consumer needs, Procter & Gamble leverages its massive global scale, robust supply chain, and continuous product innovation to drive growth. The company serves billions of consumers worldwide, maintaining a dominant market position in the fast-moving consumer goods sector while consistently delivering stable returns to its investors.
