Company description: PG

Procter & Gamble (NYSE: PG) is a premier global consumer goods corporation specializing in the manufacture and marketing of personal care, hygiene, and cleaning products. Headquartered in Cincinnati, Ohio, the company operates across five primary business segments: Beauty, Grooming, Health Care, Fabric & Home Care, and Baby, Feminine & Family Care. Its portfolio features world-renowned brands such as Tide, Pampers, Gillette, Crest, and Head & Shoulders.

Founded in 1837 by English candlemaker William Procter and Irish soapmaker James Gamble, the business originated as a modest regional candle and soap enterprise. A significant catalyst in its early growth occurred during the American Civil War, when P&G secured contracts to supply the Union Army with soap and candles, establishing nationwide brand loyalty. 

Over nearly two centuries, Procter & Gamble has evolved into an industrial powerhouse through consistent product innovation, massive scale, and effective global distribution. Today, the company serves billions of consumers worldwide, generating robust revenues and maintaining market leadership in essential everyday consumer categories.
