Company description: PG

The Procter & Gamble Company, trading under the ticker symbol PG, is a premier American multinational consumer goods corporation. Founded in 1837 by William Procter and James Gamble in Cincinnati, Ohio, the business originally began as a small-scale soap and candle maker. Over nearly two centuries, it has expanded into one of the world's largest and most valuable fast-moving consumer goods entities.

Procter & Gamble specializes in the innovation, manufacturing, and global distribution of a vast array of personal health, hygiene, and home care products. Its industry-leading portfolio includes iconic household brands such as Tide, Pampers, Gillette, Crest, Downy, and Head & Shoulders. The company organizes its operations into five main segments: Beauty, Grooming, Health Care, Fabric & Home Care, and Baby, Feminine & Family Care.

Today, P&G serves billions of consumers across more than 180 countries. Through continuous product research, robust global supply chain operations, and strategic brand management, PG maintains a dominant market presence in daily essentials, solidifying its reputation as a steady, reliable leader in the retail and consumer staples sectors.
