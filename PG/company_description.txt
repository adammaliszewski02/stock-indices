Company description: PG

Procter & Gamble (NYSE: PG) is a world-leading fast-moving consumer goods corporation that develops, manufactures, and markets a broad array of personal care, household, and hygiene products. Founded in 1837 by William Procter and James Gamble in Cincinnati, Ohio, the business originally began as a small maker of candles and soap before growing into an international industry leader.

Today, the company serves billions of consumers across more than 180 countries. Its operations are structured around five primary business segments: Fabric & Home Care, Baby, Feminine & Family Care, Beauty, Grooming, and Health Care. Procter & Gamble owns a portfolio of globally recognized, market-leading brands, including Tide, Pampers, Gillette, Crest, Head & Shoulders, and Downy. 

By leveraging deep consumer insights, continuous product innovation, and an extensive global supply chain, the company focuses on delivering high-quality, daily-use essential items. Its resilient business model and long-standing brand equity make it a cornerstone of the global retail market.
