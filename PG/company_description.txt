Company description: PG

Procter & Gamble Co. (NYSE: PG) is a premier global consumer goods corporation specializing in personal care, hygiene, and household products. Founded in 1837 by candle maker William Procter and soap maker James Gamble in Cincinnati, Ohio, the company began as a small regional business. Over nearly two centuries, it has evolved into an international industry leader with products sold in over 180 countries.

P&G operates through five core business segments: Beauty, Grooming, Health Care, Fabric & Home Care, and Baby, Feminine & Family Care. Its vast portfolio includes iconic, category-defining brands such as Tide, Pampers, Gillette, Crest, Head & Shoulders, and Febreze. The company generates revenue by manufacturing and distributing these fast-moving consumer goods to mass retailers, grocery stores, pharmacies, and e-commerce platforms worldwide. 

Renowned for its commitment to product innovation, market research, and brand equity, Procter & Gamble maintains a dominant presence in the consumer staples sector, delivering essential everyday products to billions of consumers globally.
