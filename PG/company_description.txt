Company description: PG

The Procter & Gamble Company, trading under the ticker symbol PG, is a leading multinational consumer goods corporation headquartered in Cincinnati, Ohio. Founded in 1837 by William Procter and James Gamble as a humble candle and soap maker, P&G has grown over nearly two centuries into a global industry giant.

Today, Procter & Gamble specializes in the development, manufacturing, and marketing of a vast array of personal care, hygiene, and household goods. The company operates through five primary business segments: Beauty, Grooming, Health Care, Fabric & Home Care, and Baby, Feminine & Family Care. Its portfolio features iconic global brands such as Tide, Pampers, Gillette, Crest, Downy, and Head & Shoulders.

P&G’s business model centers on continuous product innovation, consumer market research, and an extensive global distribution network that serves billions of customers daily across diverse retail channels. Historically recognized for pioneering modern brand management techniques, Procter & Gamble remains a cornerstone of the global consumer staples sector, driving long-term value through essential, high-demand products designed for everyday life.
