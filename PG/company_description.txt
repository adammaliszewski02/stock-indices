Company description: PG

The Procter & Gamble Company, trading under the ticker symbol PG, is a multinational consumer packaged goods leader. Founded in 1837 by William Procter and James Gamble in Cincinnati, Ohio, the business began as a modest soap and candle maker before evolving into a global corporate powerhouse. 

Today, Procter & Gamble develops, manufactures, and markets a vast array of personal care, hygiene, and household products. The company operates across five core segments: Beauty, Grooming, Health Care, Fabric & Home Care, and Baby, Feminine & Family Care. Its portfolio includes world-renowned brands such as Tide, Pampers, Gillette, Crest, Olay, and Head & Shoulders, reaching consumers in over 180 countries. 

Driven by continuous product innovation, strategic marketing, and an extensive global supply chain, Procter & Gamble focuses on delivering daily essential items that enhance consumer lives. The company is widely recognized for its operational resilience, long history of reliable dividend growth, and strong brand equity, maintaining its position as a cornerstone of the global retail economy.
