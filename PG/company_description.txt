Company description: PG

Procter & Gamble (NYSE: PG) is a world-leading consumer goods corporation that manufactures and markets a vast portfolio of personal care, hygiene, and household cleaning products. Headquartered in Cincinnati, Ohio, the company operates globally across five primary segments: Beauty, Grooming, Health Care, Fabric & Home Care, and Baby, Feminine & Family Care. Its iconic brand lineup includes Tide, Pampers, Gillette, Crest, Bounty, and Head & Shoulders.

Founded in 1837 by candle maker William Procter and soap maker James Gamble, the business initially grew by supplying soaps and candles to the Union Army during the American Civil War. Over its nearly two-century history, P&G transformed the fast-moving consumer goods industry through landmark innovations, such as the introduction of Ivory soap in 1879, and by pioneering modern brand management and market research techniques in the early 20th century.

Today, Procter & Gamble leverages its extensive global supply chain, robust research and development, and strong consumer trust to deliver essential daily products to billions of households worldwide, consistently focusing on premium product performance and sustainable operational growth.
