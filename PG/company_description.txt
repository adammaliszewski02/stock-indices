Company description: PG

Procter & Gamble (NYSE: PG) is an American multinational corporation and a global leader in the consumer packaged goods industry. Founded in 1837 by candle maker William Procter and soap maker James Gamble in Cincinnati, Ohio, the company initially gained prominence supplying soap and candles to the Union Army during the Civil War. Over the past two centuries, it has evolved into one of the world's largest consumer goods companies.

Today, Procter & Gamble focuses on manufacturing and marketing a wide array of personal care, hygiene, and household products. The company’s operations are structured into five main business segments: Beauty, Grooming, Health Care, Fabric & Home Care, and Baby, Feminine & Family Care. Its portfolio includes dozens of globally recognized, trusted brands such as Tide, Pampers, Gillette, Crest, Pantene, and Head & Shoulders. 

Through continuous product innovation, robust marketing, and a vast global supply chain, Procter & Gamble generates revenue by delivering essential daily-use products to billions of consumers across more than 180 countries.
