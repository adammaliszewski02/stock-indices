Company description: PG

The Procter & Gamble Company (NYSE: PG) is an American multinational consumer goods corporation specializing in a vast array of personal care, hygiene, and household products. Founded in 1837 by William Procter and James Gamble in Cincinnati, Ohio, the business originated as a modest candle and soap manufacturer. Over its nearly two-century history, the company has grown into one of the world’s largest and most valuable fast-moving consumer goods enterprises.

Today, Procter & Gamble operates globally across five core business segments: Beauty, Grooming, Health Care, Fabric & Home Care, and Baby, Feminine & Family Care. Its portfolio features iconic, market-leading brands including Tide, Pampers, Gillette, Crest, Head & Shoulders, and Downy. P&G generates revenue by developing, manufacturing, and marketing these essential daily-use products through mass merchandisers, grocery retailers, pharmacies, and e-commerce platforms worldwide. Focused on innovation, brand building, and supply chain excellence, the company continues to maintain a dominant presence in global retail markets.
