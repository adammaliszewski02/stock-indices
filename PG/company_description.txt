Company description: PG

The Procter & Gamble Company (NYSE: PG) is a global leader in the consumer packaged goods industry, specializing in personal care, hygiene, and household products. Founded in 1837 by William Procter and James Gamble in Cincinnati, Ohio, the enterprise began as a humble soap and candle maker before expanding into one of the world's largest consumer products corporations. 

Today, Procter & Gamble operates through five core business segments: Fabric & Home Care, Baby, Feminine & Family Care, Beauty, Grooming, and Health Care. The company’s extensive portfolio features widely recognized, billion-dollar brands such as Tide, Pampers, Gillette, Pantene, Crest, and Downy. 

P&G sells its products in over 180 countries and territories through mass merchandisers, grocery stores, membership club stores, drugstores, and e-commerce channels. Its business strategy centers on driving organic growth through continuous product innovation, superior brand performance, and supply chain efficiency. By delivering essential daily-use items to billions of consumers worldwide, Procter & Gamble maintains its status as an industry cornerstone and a historically reliable blue-chip investment.
