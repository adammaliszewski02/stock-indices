The Procter & Gamble Company (NYSE: PG) is an American multinational consumer goods corporation headquartered in Cincinnati, Ohio. Founded in 1837 by William Procter and James Gamble as a modest soap and candle enterprise, the company has evolved into one of the world's largest and most recognized manufacturers of household and personal care products. 

P&G operates through five primary business segments: Beauty, Grooming, Health Care, Fabric & Home Care, and Baby, Feminine & Family Care. Its portfolio includes dozens of iconic, multi-billion-dollar global brands, such as Tide, Pampers, Gillette, Head & Shoulders, Oral-B, and Crest. The company sells its merchandise in approximately 180 countries, leveraging massive distribution networks, rigorous research and development, and sophisticated marketing strategies. Known for its historical role in developing modern brand management and pioneering market research, Procter & Gamble remains a leading dividend-paying stalwart within the global consumer staples sector.
