Company description: PG

The Procter & Gamble Company (NYSE: PG) is a global leader in the fast-moving consumer goods industry, specializing in high-quality personal care, hygiene, and household products. Headquartered in Cincinnati, Ohio, P&G operates through key business segments including Beauty, Grooming, Health Care, Fabric & Home Care, and Baby, Feminine & Family Care. Its portfolio features trusted, world-renowned brands such as Tide, Pampers, Gillette, Crest, and Head & Shoulders.

Founded in 1837 by candlemaker William Procter and soapmaker James Gamble, the enterprise began as a modest regional soap and candle business. Over its nearly two-century history, P&G transformed the retail landscape through continuous product innovation and pioneering marketing techniques. Notably, the company helped create modern brand management and sponsored early serialized radio and television dramas, which gave rise to the term "soap operas."

Today, Procter & Gamble serves billions of consumers across more than 180 countries. The company focuses on driving sustainable, long-term growth by leveraging its robust global supply chain, extensive market research, and iconic brand equity to deliver essential daily-use products that improve consumer lives worldwide.
