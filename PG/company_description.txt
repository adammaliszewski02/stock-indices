Company description: PG

Procter & Gamble (NYSE: PG) is a world-leading American multinational consumer goods corporation specializing in personal care, hygiene, and household cleaning products. 

Established in 1837 by candle maker William Procter and soap maker James Gamble in Cincinnati, Ohio, the company began as a humble soap and candle business. Over nearly two centuries, it evolved into a global powerhouse, famously pioneering modern brand marketing and sponsoring early radio and television drama series, which gave rise to the term "soap operas."

Today, Procter & Gamble manufactures and markets a diverse portfolio of trusted, essential consumer brands, including Pampers, Tide, Downy, Gillette, Crest, Pantene, and Head & Shoulders. The company organizes its operations into key segments, such as Beauty, Grooming, Health Care, Fabric & Home Care, and Baby, Feminine & Family Care. Operating in approximately 70 countries and selling products in over 180, Procter & Gamble focuses on continuous product innovation and operational efficiency to serve billions of consumers daily, maintaining its position as a cornerstone of the global consumer staples market.
