Company description: PG

Procter & Gamble (NYSE: PG) is a global leader in the fast-moving consumer goods industry, specializing in a broad portfolio of personal care, hygiene, and household products. Founded in 1837 by William Procter and James Gamble in Cincinnati, Ohio, the company began as a modest soap and candle maker before expanding into an international enterprise over its nearly two-century history. 

Today, Procter & Gamble develops, manufactures, and markets essential everyday items through iconic brands such as Tide, Pampers, Gillette, Crest, and Downy. The company operates through five core business segments: Beauty, Grooming, Health Care, Fabric & Home Care, and Baby, Feminine & Family Care. Serving billions of consumers in over 180 countries, Procter & Gamble leverages extensive research and development, sophisticated supply chain management, and strong brand equity to maintain its market-leading position across global retail and digital distribution channels.
