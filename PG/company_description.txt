Company description: PG

Procter & Gamble Co. (NYSE: PG) is a global leader in the fast-moving consumer goods industry, specializing in personal care, hygiene, and household products. The company operates through five core segments: Beauty, Grooming, Health Care, Fabric & Home Care, and Baby, Feminine & Family Care. It manufactures and markets trusted, iconic brands worldwide, including Tide, Pampers, Gillette, Crest, and Head & Shoulders.

Founded in 1837 by candle maker William Procter and soap maker James Gamble in Cincinnati, Ohio, the enterprise began as a modest regional partnership. Over nearly two centuries, Procter & Gamble transformed the consumer market through continuous product innovation and pioneering advertising strategies, highlighted by the groundbreaking launch of Ivory soap in 1879 and the development of modern brand management.

Today, Procter & Gamble serves billions of consumers across more than 180 countries. The company focuses on portfolio optimization, technological innovation, and supply chain efficiency to sustain steady growth and deliver everyday essential items to households globally.
