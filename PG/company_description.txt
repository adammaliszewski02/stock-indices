Company description: PG

The Procter & Gamble Company (NYSE: PG) is an American multinational consumer goods corporation specializing in a vast array of personal care, hygiene, and household products. Headquartered in Cincinnati, Ohio, P&G structures its operations into core segments, including Beauty, Grooming, Health Care, Fabric & Home Care, and Baby, Feminine & Family Care. Its globally recognized brand portfolio includes market leaders such as Tide, Pampers, Gillette, Crest, and Head & Shoulders.

Founded in 1837 by English candlemaker William Procter and Irish soapmaker James Gamble, the enterprise originated as a modest soap and candle business. P&G achieved significant early growth by supplying soap and candles to the Union Army during the American Civil War and later revolutionized consumer marketing with the launch of Ivory soap in 1879. Over nearly two centuries, the company pioneered modern brand management and extensive consumer research techniques.

Today, Procter & Gamble serves billions of consumers across more than 180 countries and territories. Renowned for its innovation and operational scale, P&G remains one of the largest and most stable consumer staples companies globally, maintaining a distinguished history of delivering consistent long-term shareholder value and continuous annual dividend increases for over six decades.
