Company description: PG

The Procter & Gamble Company (NYSE: PG) is a premier American multinational consumer goods corporation specializing in personal care, hygiene, and household products. Headquartered in Cincinnati, Ohio, the company operates across five core segments: Beauty, Grooming, Health Care, Fabric & Home Care, and Baby, Feminine & Family Care. Its portfolio features globally recognized brands such as Tide, Pampers, Gillette, Crest, and Head & Shoulders.

Founded in 1837 by candlemaker William Procter and soapmaker James Gamble, the business originated as a modest regional venture. Over nearly two centuries, Procter & Gamble evolved into an international powerhouse through relentless product innovation, strategic acquisitions, and pioneering marketing strategies, including the sponsorship of early radio and television "soap operas."

Today, Procter & Gamble manufactures and markets essential fast-moving consumer goods in over 180 countries. The company leverages its extensive global distribution network, robust research and development capabilities, and strong brand equity to maintain leadership positions across multiple consumer categories, generating reliable long-term value for shareholders through consistent dividend payments and steady operational growth.
