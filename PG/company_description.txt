Company description: PG

The Procter & Gamble Company (NYSE: PG) is a premier global consumer goods corporation specializing in personal care, hygiene, and household cleaning products. Founded in 1837 by candle maker William Procter and soap maker James Gamble in Cincinnati, Ohio, the business initially gained national prominence by supplying soap and candles to the Union Army during the American Civil War. Over nearly two centuries, it has evolved into one of the world's most influential multinational enterprises.

Today, Procter & Gamble manufactures and markets essential consumer products across five key business segments: Beauty, Grooming, Health Care, Fabric & Home Care, and Baby, Feminine & Family Care. The company holds a massive portfolio of globally recognized, trusted brands, including Tide, Pampers, Gillette, Crest, Downy, and Head & Shoulders. 

Operating in over 180 countries, Procter & Gamble focuses on brand innovation, sustainable manufacturing, and consumer-centric research to maintain its position as an industry leader, consistently generating strong cash flow and delivering essential daily-use products to billions of consumers worldwide.
