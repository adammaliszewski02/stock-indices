The stock ticker PG represents The Procter & Gamble Company, a leading American multinational consumer goods corporation headquartered in Cincinnati, Ohio. Founded in 1837 by candle maker William Procter and soap maker James Gamble, the enterprise originated as a regional soap and candle business and has since evolved into one of the world's largest consumer products manufacturers.

Procter & Gamble operates through five primary business segments: Fabric & Home Care, Baby, Feminine & Family Care, Beauty, Grooming, and Health Care. Its extensive portfolio includes globally recognized household brands such as Tide, Pampers, Gillette, Crest, Head & Shoulders, and Dawn. The company distributes its products worldwide through mass merchandisers, grocery stores, pharmacies, department stores, and direct-to-consumer e-commerce channels. Renowned for its resilient business model, strong brand equity, and heavy investment in research, development, and marketing, Procter & Gamble remains a cornerstone of the global consumer staples sector.
