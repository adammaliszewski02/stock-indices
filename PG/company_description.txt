Company description: PG

The Procter & Gamble Company, trading under the ticker symbol PG, is a leading global consumer goods corporation headquartered in Cincinnati, Ohio. Founded in 1837 by candle maker William Procter and soap maker James Gamble, the enterprise originated as a humble soap and candle business and has evolved over nearly two centuries into one of the world's largest and most trusted consumer staples companies.

Today, Procter & Gamble specializes in the development, manufacturing, and marketing of personal care, health, hygiene, and household products. The company operates through key business segments including Beauty, Grooming, Health Care, Fabric & Home Care, and Baby, Feminine & Family Care. Its portfolio features globally recognized brands such as Tide, Pampers, Gillette, Crest, Head & Shoulders, and Downy. 

Leveraging continuous product innovation, extensive market research, and a sophisticated global supply chain, Procter & Gamble distributes its products to consumers in more than 180 countries. Through a strategy focused on productivity and organic growth, the company maintains a dominant market presence, delivering consistent value to shareholders and everyday consumers worldwide.
