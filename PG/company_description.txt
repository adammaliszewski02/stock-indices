Company description: PG

Procter & Gamble (NYSE: PG) is a global leader in the consumer packaged goods industry, specializing in personal care, hygiene, and household cleaning products. Founded in 1837 by candle maker William Procter and soap maker James Gamble in Cincinnati, Ohio, the company evolved from a humble regional enterprise into a multinational powerhouse operating in approximately 180 countries. 

P&G develops, manufactures, and markets essential daily-use products organized across five core business segments: Beauty, Grooming, Health Care, Fabric & Home Care, and Baby, Feminine & Family Care. Its portfolio features some of the world’s most recognizable household brands, including Tide, Pampers, Gillette, Crest, Bounty, and Head & Shoulders. Driven by consumer-focused innovation, strategic brand building, and a sophisticated global distribution network, Procter & Gamble focuses on delivering superior-performing products to maintain its market leadership and drive consistent long-term financial performance.
