Company description: PG

Procter & Gamble (NYSE: PG) is a global leader in the fast-moving consumer goods industry, specializing in personal care, hygiene, and household products. Founded in 1837 by candle maker William Procter and soap maker James Gamble in Cincinnati, Ohio, the company initially produced soap and candles before expanding into an international consumer product powerhouse.

Today, Procter & Gamble manufactures and markets a vast portfolio of essential, daily-use consumer goods across five key business segments: Beauty, Grooming, Health Care, Fabric & Home Care, and Baby, Feminine & Family Care. Its lineup includes numerous world-renowned brands such as Tide, Pampers, Gillette, Crest, Head & Shoulders, and Downy. 

PG distributes its products to consumers in more than 180 countries through mass merchandisers, e-commerce, grocery stores, and pharmacy channels. The company focuses on driving organic growth through continuous product innovation, robust brand building, and supply chain efficiency, consistently maintaining a dominant market share in its core categories worldwide.
