Company description: PG

The Procter & Gamble Company (NYSE: PG) is a world-leading multinational consumer goods corporation specializing in personal care, hygiene, and household products. Founded in 1837 by candle maker William Procter and soap maker James Gamble in Cincinnati, Ohio, the enterprise grew from a modest regional business into one of the largest and most influential consumer goods companies globally. 

Today, Procter & Gamble operates in approximately 70 countries, serving billions of consumers worldwide through a portfolio of iconic, daily-use brands. The company divides its operations into five main business segments: Beauty, Grooming, Health Care, Fabric & Home Care, and Baby, Feminine & Family Care. Its well-known brand roster includes industry staples such as Tide, Pampers, Gillette, Crest, Head & Shoulders, and Downy. 

Through continuous product innovation, robust marketing, and extensive global supply chain networks, Procter & Gamble focuses on delivering high-quality, branded consumer packaged goods that enhance everyday life. The company remains a cornerstone of the global retail market, driven by consistent consumer demand for essential household and personal health items.
