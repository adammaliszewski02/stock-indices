Company description: PG

Procter & Gamble Co. (NYSE: PG) is a global leader in the consumer packaged goods industry, specializing in the manufacture and sale of personal care, hygiene, and household products. The company operates through five primary business segments: Beauty, Grooming, Health Care, Fabric & Home Care, and Baby, Feminine & Family Care. Its portfolio features world-renowned brands such as Tide, Pampers, Gillette, Crest, Head & Shoulders, and Bounty.

Founded in 1837 by candlestick maker William Procter and soap maker James Gamble in Cincinnati, Ohio, the enterprise began as a modest regional producer of candles and soap. Over its nearly two-century history, P&G evolved into an international powerhouse through continuous product innovation, strategic acquisitions, and pioneering advertising techniques. Milestones include the launch of Ivory soap in 1879, the introduction of Tide detergent in 1946, and the creation of Pampers disposable diapers in 1961, which revolutionized the infant care market.

Today, Procter & Gamble operates in approximately 70 countries and serves billions of consumers worldwide. The company remains focused on delivering high-quality, daily-use consumer products while driving growth through brand building, technological research, and supply chain efficiency.
