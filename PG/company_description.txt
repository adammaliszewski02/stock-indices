Company description: PG

Ticker PG represents The Procter & Gamble Company, an American multinational consumer goods corporation headquartered in Cincinnati, Ohio. Founded in 1837 by candle maker William Procter and soap maker James Gamble, the business began as a small regional enterprise. Over nearly two centuries, it has evolved into one of the world's largest consumer packaged goods giants.

Procter & Gamble specializes in the manufacture and marketing of personal care, hygiene, and household products. The company operates through five primary segments: Beauty, Grooming, Health Care, Fabric & Home Care, and Baby, Feminine & Family Care. Its portfolio includes dozens of iconic, globally recognized brands such as Tide, Pampers, Gillette, Crest, Bounty, and Head & Shoulders. 

P&G serves billions of consumers worldwide by distributing its daily essential staples through mass merchandisers, grocery stores, drugstores, and e-commerce platforms. The company’s core business strategy relies on continuous product innovation, supply chain scale, and targeted brand marketing to maintain market leadership and generate consistent revenue across global markets.
