Company description: PG

Procter & Gamble Co., trading under the ticker PG, is a world-leading American multinational consumer goods corporation. Founded in 1837 by William Procter and James Gamble in Cincinnati, Ohio, the company originated as a modest soap and candle business before evolving into a global industry giant. 

Today, Procter & Gamble manufactures and markets a vast array of daily essential products across five core segments: Beauty, Grooming, Health Care, Fabric & Home Care, and Baby, Feminine & Family Care. Its portfolio features dozens of iconic, billion-dollar brands, including Tide, Pampers, Gillette, Crest, Downy, and Head & Shoulders. 

Driven by extensive research, product innovation, and sophisticated global marketing, the company distributes its products to consumers in over 180 countries. Sales are primarily generated through mass merchandisers, grocery stores, membership clubs, drugstores, and e-commerce channels. As one of the oldest continuously operating companies in the S&P 500 with a history of consistent dividend growth, Procter & Gamble maintains its position as a market leader by delivering trusted, high-quality consumer brands designed to meet daily personal health and hygiene needs.
