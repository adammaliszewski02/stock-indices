Company description: PG

The Procter & Gamble Company (NYSE: PG) is a global leader in the consumer packaged goods industry, specializing in a vast array of personal care, hygiene, and household products. Founded in 1837 by candlemaker William Procter and soapmaker James Gamble in Cincinnati, Ohio, the company has grown from a small regional partnership into one of the world's largest multinational corporations. 

P&G operates through five primary business segments: Beauty, Grooming, Health Care, Fabric & Home Care, and Baby, Feminine & Family Care. Its portfolio features many of the world's most recognizable, household-name brands, including Tide, Pampers, Gillette, Crest, Downy, and Head & Shoulders. Driven by continuous product innovation, extensive market research, and robust global supply chains, Procter & Gamble focuses on manufacturing essential daily-use items designed to improve consumer health and hygiene. Today, the company serves billions of consumers across more than 180 countries, maintaining a dominant market presence and a strong commitment to long-term sustainable growth.
