Company description: PG

Procter & Gamble (NYSE: PG) is a premier global consumer goods corporation specializing in the manufacture and sale of personal care, hygiene, and household cleaning products. Headquartered in Cincinnati, Ohio, the company operates in over 180 countries, delivering essential daily-use items to billions of consumers. Its iconic portfolio features world-renowned brands such as Tide, Pampers, Gillette, Crest, Downy, and Head & Shoulders, categorized across five main business segments: Beauty, Grooming, Health Care, Fabric & Home Care, and Baby, Feminine & Family Care.

Founded in 1837 by candlemaker William Procter and soapmaker James Gamble, the enterprise originated as a small regional soap and candle business. Over its nearly two-century history, the company evolved into an international powerhouse, driven by pioneering market research, continuous product innovation, and strategic brand management. Procter & Gamble notably sustained continuous growth through major historical events, supplying soap and candles to the Union Army during the American Civil War and later revolutionizing commercial advertising through soap operas and modern media sponsorship. Today, Procter & Gamble maintains its market leadership through consumer-centric innovation, operational efficiency, and a focus on sustainable manufacturing practices.
