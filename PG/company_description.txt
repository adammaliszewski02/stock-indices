Company description: PG

Procter & Gamble (NYSE: PG) is a global consumer goods giant specializing in personal care, hygiene, and household products. Founded in 1837 in Cincinnati, Ohio, by candlemaker William Procter and soapmaker James Gamble, the company began as a modest soap and candle business and has evolved over nearly two centuries into one of the world’s largest and most influential corporations. 

P&G operates across five core business segments: Beauty, Grooming, Health Care, Fabric & Home Care, and Baby, Feminine & Family Care. Its portfolio features iconic, market-leading brands such as Tide, Pampers, Gillette, Crest, Downy, and Head & Shoulders. The company’s primary business strategy focuses on developing superior, branded consumer packaged goods, driven by continuous product innovation, robust supply chain operations, and extensive worldwide retail distribution. 

Headquartered in Cincinnati, P&G serves billions of consumers across more than 180 countries. It is widely recognized for its operational efficiency, strong brand equity, and an exceptional track record of dividend growth, having paid a dividend for over a century. Today, Procter & Gamble continues to maintain its industry leadership by adapting to evolving consumer trends and leveraging its massive global scale.
