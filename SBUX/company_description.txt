Starbucks Corporation, identified by the stock ticker SBUX, is a premier multinational coffeehouse chain and roastery conglomerate. Founded in Seattle, Washington, in 1971 by Jerry Baldwin, Zev Siegl, and Gordon Bowker, the company has evolved from a single local store into the world's largest coffeehouse chain. Starbucks primarily engages in the retail sale of specialty hot and cold beverages, whole bean coffees, microground instant coffees, full-leaf teas, and fresh food items. Under the leadership of former CEO Howard Schultz, the company pioneered the "third place" concept, creating a social environment distinct from home and work. Today, Starbucks generates revenue globally through both company-operated stores and licensed partnerships, complemented by a robust consumer packaged goods division. The company has continuously expanded its digital ecosystem through its mobile application and loyalty reward program, while also prioritizing ethical sourcing and sustainable agricultural practices within its global supply chain.
