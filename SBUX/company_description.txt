Starbucks Corporation, identified by the ticker SBUX, is a premier multinational coffeehouse chain and roaster headquartered in Seattle, Washington. Founded in 1971 by Jerry Baldwin, Zev Siegl, and Gordon Bowker as a single store selling high-end coffee beans and equipment, the company underwent a transformative shift in the 1980s under the leadership of Howard Schultz, who introduced the Italian-style espresso bar concept. Today, Starbucks operates as a publicly traded global enterprise, engaging in the retail and wholesale of specialty coffee, teas, handcrafted beverages, and fresh food items. With tens of thousands of company-operated and licensed stores worldwide, the corporation generates revenue through its extensive retail footprint, packaged goods, and foodservice partnerships. Furthermore, Starbucks is widely recognized for pioneering industry standards in ethical sourcing, corporate social responsibility, and customer engagement through its robust digital ecosystem and loyalty rewards program.
