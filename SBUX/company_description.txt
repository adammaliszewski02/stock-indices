Starbucks Corporation, trading under the ticker SBUX, is the world's largest multinational chain of roasteries and coffeehouses. Founded in Seattle, Washington, in 1971 by Jerry Baldwin, Zev Siegl, and Gordon Bowker, the company initially specialized in freshly roasted whole coffee beans before transforming into a global coffeehouse culture pioneer under the leadership of longtime CEO Howard Schultz, who acquired the retail operations in 1987. Headquartered in Seattle, Starbucks generates revenue through the operation and licensing of its namesake brand stores, offering a diverse portfolio of hot and cold coffee beverages, whole bean coffees, espresso drinks, teas, and fresh food items. Additionally, the company markets its packaged goods and ready-to-drink beverages through global retail and grocery channels via strategic partnerships, most notably the Global Coffee Alliance with Nestlé. Operating in numerous international markets, Starbucks continues to emphasize experiential store formats, digital customer engagement through its robust rewards program, and sustainable sourcing practices across its global supply chain.
