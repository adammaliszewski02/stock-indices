Starbucks Corporation, identified by the stock ticker SBUX, is the world's largest multinational chain of coffeehouses and roastery reserves headquartered in Seattle, Washington. Founded in 1971 by Jerry Baldwin, Gordon Bowker, and Zev Siegl as a single store specializing in fresh coffee beans and roasting equipment, the company underwent a transformative period after Howard Schultz acquired the retail operations in 1987 and introduced the Italian coffeehouse concept. Today, SBUX operates tens of thousands of company-operated and licensed stores globally, offering a diverse portfolio of hot and cold beverages, whole bean coffees, micro-ground instant coffees, full-leaf teas, and fresh food items. The company generates revenue through its retail store operations, licensed store royalties, and a robust consumer packaged goods segment distributed through global retail partnerships. Operating under a mission to inspire and nurture the human spirit, Starbucks has evolved into a ubiquitous cultural and commercial enterprise centered on the "third place" experience between home and work.
