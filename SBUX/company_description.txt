Starbucks Corporation, identified by the ticker SBUX, is a premier multinational coffeehouse chain and roaster headquartered in Seattle, Washington. Founded in 1971 by Jerry Baldwin, Zev Siegl, and Gordon Bowker, the company initially operated as a single store selling high-end whole-bean coffee and tea before undergoing a transformative expansion in the 1980s under the leadership of Howard Schultz. Today, Starbucks operates as a dominant global force in the quick-service restaurant industry, generating revenue through company-operated and licensed stores that offer hot and cold coffee beverages, whole-bean coffees, micro-ground instant coffees, full-leaf teas, and fresh food items. In addition to its flagship brand, the company's portfolio includes Teavana, Seattle's Best Coffee, Ethos Water, and various ready-to-drink beverage partnerships. Starbucks is widely recognized for pioneering the "third place" retail concept—a social environment between home and work—and for its industry-leading digital loyalty ecosystem and mobile application integration.
