Starbucks Corporation, traded under the ticker SBUX, is a premier multinational coffeehouse chain and roaster headquartered in Seattle, Washington. Founded in 1971 by Jerry Baldwin, Zev Siegl, and Gordon Bowker as a single store specializing in high-quality coffee beans and equipment, the company underwent a transformative period after Howard Schultz acquired it in 1987. Schultz introduced the Italian coffeehouse concept to the enterprise, shifting its focus toward espresso-based beverages and creating a distinct "third place" customer experience between home and work. 

Today, Starbucks operates as the largest coffeehouse chain in the world, generating revenue through company-operated and licensed stores. Its extensive product portfolio includes whole-bean coffees, brewed coffees, handcrafted espresso beverages, teas, and food items, alongside consumer packaged goods sold globally through grocery and retail channels. The company also owns brands such as Teavana, Seattle's Best Coffee, and Evolution Fresh, and it pioneered customer loyalty programs through its widely adopted mobile application.
