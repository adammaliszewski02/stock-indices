The stock ticker SBUX represents Starbucks Corporation, a premier multinational coffeehouse chain and roaster headquartered in Seattle, Washington. Founded in 1971 by Jerry Baldwin, Zev Siegl, and Gordon Bowker as a single store specializing in fresh-roasted coffee beans, the company underwent a transformative period after Howard Schultz acquired the retail operations in 1987, inspired by the Italian espresso bar experience. Starbucks has since grown into a global retail giant, operating tens of thousands of company-owned and licensed stores worldwide. The company's core business revolves around the sourcing, roasting, and retailing of high-amenity arabica coffee beverages, handcrafted espresso drinks, teas, and fresh food items. Beyond its flagship retail storefronts, Starbucks distributes a broad portfolio of consumer packaged goods, including bottled beverages, ready-to-drink coffees, and roasted whole-bean products through global grocery and retail channels. Additionally, the company generates revenue through licensed store operations, foodservice partnerships, and its digital ecosystem, anchored by its industry-leading mobile application and customer loyalty reward program.
