Salesforce, Inc., trading under the ticker CRM, is a global leader in customer relationship management software. Founded in 1999 by Marc Benioff and three partners, the company pioneered the software-as-a-service (SaaS) model, fundamentally shifting enterprise technology from on-premise installations to the cloud. Headquartered in San Francisco, Salesforce provides a comprehensive suite of integrated applications that enable businesses to connect with their customers across sales, service, marketing, and commerce. Through its flagship Customer 360 platform, the company leverages artificial intelligence, data analytics, and automation to help organizations manage their entire customer lifecycle. Over the past two decades, Salesforce has expanded its ecosystem significantly through strategic acquisitions, including Slack, Tableau, and MuleSoft, solidifying its position as a critical infrastructure provider for digital transformation. Today, it remains a cornerstone of the modern enterprise software landscape, consistently ranking among the world’s largest and most influential technology firms.
