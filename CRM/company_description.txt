The ticker CRM identifies Salesforce, Inc., an American cloud-based software company headquartered in San Francisco, California. Founded in 1999 by Marc Benioff, Parker Harris, Dave Moellenhoff, and Frank Dominguez, the company pioneered the delivery of enterprise software through a software-as-a-service (SaaS) business model. Salesforce is best known for its flagship Customer Relationship Management (CRM) platform, which helps businesses manage customer relationships, sales operations, and marketing campaigns. Over the years, the company has expanded its technological ecosystem through major strategic acquisitions, including Marketing Cloud, Demandware, MuleSoft, Tableau, and Slack. Today, Salesforce operates as a leading global provider of enterprise cloud solutions, integrating advanced data analytics and artificial intelligence capabilities—such as its Einstein platform and Einstein 1—to help organizations connect with their customers in innovative ways.
