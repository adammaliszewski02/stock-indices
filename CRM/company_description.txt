Salesforce, Inc., trading under the ticker CRM, is a global leader in customer relationship management software. Founded in 1999 by Marc Benioff and others, the company pioneered the software-as-a-service (SaaS) model, shifting enterprise applications from on-premise installations to the cloud. Its flagship platform provides a comprehensive suite of tools for sales, service, marketing, and commerce, enabling businesses to connect with their customers through a unified digital interface. 

Over the past two decades, Salesforce has significantly expanded its ecosystem through strategic acquisitions, including Slack, Tableau, and MuleSoft, which have integrated data analytics, communication, and integration capabilities into its core offerings. Today, the company leverages artificial intelligence through its Einstein platform to provide predictive insights and automation across its product lines. Headquartered in San Francisco, Salesforce remains a cornerstone of the modern enterprise software landscape, consistently ranking among the world’s largest and most influential technology companies.
