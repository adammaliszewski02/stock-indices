Salesforce, Inc., identified by the stock ticker CRM, is a leading global cloud-based software company headquartered in San Francisco, California. Founded in 1999 by Marc Benioff, Parker Harris, Dave Moellenhoff, and Frank Dominguez, the company pioneered the Software-as-a-Service delivery model, fundamentally transforming enterprise technology through its flagship Customer Relationship Management platform. Over the years, Salesforce has significantly expanded its technological ecosystem via strategic acquisitions, including MuleSoft, Tableau, and Slack, integrating advanced data analytics, enterprise messaging, and application integration into its portfolio. Today, Salesforce delivers a comprehensive suite of enterprise applications focused on customer service, marketing automation, commerce, and application development. The company further drives industry innovation through its integrated Einstein artificial intelligence platform, empowering organizations worldwide to connect with their customers through intelligent, automated digital workflows and unified cloud infrastructure.
