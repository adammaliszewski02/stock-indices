Identified by the stock ticker CRM, Salesforce, Inc. is a leading global cloud-based software company specializing in customer relationship management (CRM) solutions. Founded in March 1999 by Marc Benioff, Parker Harris, Dave Moellenhoff, and Frank Dominguez, the company pioneered the software-as-a-service (SaaS) delivery model, shifting enterprise applications to the internet. Salesforce expanded its offerings through strategic acquisitions, including the purchases of MuleSoft, Tableau, and Slack, to build an integrated ecosystem. Today, the company provides a comprehensive suite of enterprise applications focused on sales, customer service, marketing automation, analytics, and application development. Furthermore, Salesforce incorporates advanced artificial intelligence into its platform through Salesforce Einstein and Einstein 1, helping businesses automate workflows, derive data-driven insights, and deepen customer engagement across multiple digital channels.
