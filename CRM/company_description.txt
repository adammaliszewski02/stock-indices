The stock ticker CRM represents Salesforce, Inc., a global leader in cloud-based customer relationship management software and enterprise cloud solutions. Founded in 1999 by former Oracle executive Marc Benioff alongside Parker Harris, Dave Moellenhoff, and Frank Dominguez, Salesforce pioneered the Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) business model through its revolutionary "No Software" campaign. The company completed its initial public offering on the New York Stock Exchange in 2004, selecting the ticker CRM to reflect its defining market category. 

Salesforce provides a comprehensive suite of enterprise applications designed to unite sales, customer service, marketing, commerce, and IT teams around a shared view of customer data. Over the decades, the company significantly expanded its platform capabilities through major strategic acquisitions, including MuleSoft for integration, Tableau for business intelligence, and Slack for enterprise collaboration. Today, Salesforce delivers an integrated ecosystem of cloud offerings augmented by proprietary artificial intelligence technologies, such as Agentforce and Einstein, enabling global organizations of all sizes to automate complex workflows and deliver data-driven, personalized customer experiences.
