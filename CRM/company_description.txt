CRM is the stock ticker for Salesforce, Inc., a leading global cloud-based software company headquartered in San Francisco, California. Founded in 1999 by Marc Benioff, Parker Harris, Dave Moellenhoff, and Frank Dominguez, the company pioneered the software-as-a-service (SaaS) delivery model and revolutionized customer relationship management. Salesforce’s core business centers on providing enterprise software solutions that help organizations connect with their customers, streamline sales processes, automate marketing campaigns, and deliver comprehensive customer service. Over the years, the company has significantly expanded its portfolio through major strategic acquisitions, including Tableau, Slack, and MuleSoft, transforming into a comprehensive enterprise ecosystem. Furthermore, Salesforce integrates advanced artificial intelligence capabilities, notably through its Einstein platform, to deliver predictive analytics and automated workflows. Today, the company remains a dominant force in the enterprise technology sector, empowering businesses of all sizes to manage customer data, operations, and digital engagement on a unified cloud platform.
