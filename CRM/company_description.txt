CRM is the stock ticker for Salesforce, Inc., a leading global cloud-based software company headquartered in San Francisco, California. Founded in 1999 by Marc Benioff, Parker Harris, Dave Moellenhoff, and Frank Dominguez, the company pioneered the delivery of enterprise software via a software-as-a-service (SaaS) business model. Salesforce is best known for its flagship Customer Relationship Management platform, which helps businesses manage customer relationships, sales operations, and marketing campaigns. Over the years, the company has significantly expanded its enterprise offerings through strategic acquisitions, including the incorporation of platforms like Tableau, MuleSoft, and Slack. Today, Salesforce integrates advanced artificial intelligence, branded as Einstein, into its ecosystem to deliver automated insights and productivity tools across its comprehensive enterprise suite.
