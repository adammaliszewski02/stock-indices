AMGN is the stock ticker for Amgen Inc., a pioneering multinational biopharmaceutical company founded in California in 1980. Amgen focuses on discovering, developing, manufacturing, and delivering innovative human therapeutics derived from cutting-edge advances in recombinant DNA and molecular biology. The company is dedicated to serving patients suffering from serious, often life-threatening illnesses across major therapeutic areas, including oncology, cardiovascular disease, inflammation, bone health, nephrology, and neuroscience. Amgen achieved early commercial success with breakthrough biologic blockbusters such as Epogen and Neulasta, establishing itself as an independent biotechnology pioneer. Over the decades, it has expanded its global footprint and product portfolio through internal research and strategic acquisitions, most notably the 2002 purchase of Immunex and the recent 2023 acquisition of Horizon Therapeutics. Today, Amgen leverages advanced human genetics and manufacturing capabilities to continuously transform scientific discoveries into breakthrough medicines that improve global health outcomes.
