Amgen Inc., trading under the ticker AMGN, is a leading American multinational biopharmaceutical company headquartered in Thousand Oaks, California. Founded in 1980 as Applied Molecular Genetics, the company pioneered the commercial application of recombinant DNA technology to discover, develop, manufacture, and deliver innovative human therapeutics for serious illnesses. Amgen focuses primarily on areas of high unmet medical need, including oncology, cardiovascular disease, inflammation, bone health, nephrology, and rare diseases. Its portfolio features several blockbuster biologic products, such as Neulasta, Epogen, Enbrel, and Prolia. Over the decades, Amgen has grown into one of the world's largest independent biotechnology firms, continually expanding its pipeline and global market reach through both internal research and strategic acquisitions, most notably its recent integration of Horizon Therapeutics to strengthen its presence in rare and complex diseases.
