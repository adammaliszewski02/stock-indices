Amgen Inc. (AMGN) is a global biotechnology pioneer headquartered in Thousand Oaks, California. Founded in 1980 as Applied Molecular Genetics, the company played a foundational role in the birth of the biotech industry by leveraging recombinant DNA technology to develop innovative human therapeutics. Today, Amgen focuses on addressing serious illnesses in areas including oncology, hematology, cardiovascular disease, inflammation, bone health, and nephrology. The company is recognized for its robust research and development pipeline and its sophisticated large-molecule manufacturing capabilities. By integrating advanced human genetics with complex protein engineering, Amgen aims to transform the lives of patients suffering from high-burden diseases. As a component of the Dow Jones Industrial Average, Amgen remains one of the world’s largest independent biotechnology companies, consistently advancing scientific discovery to provide life-altering medicines to patients across the globe.
