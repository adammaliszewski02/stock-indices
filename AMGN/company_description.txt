Amgen Inc., trading under the ticker AMGN, is a leading multinational biotechnology company dedicated to discovering, developing, manufacturing, and delivering innovative human therapeutics for severe medical conditions. Founded in Thousand Oaks, California, in 1980 as Applied Molecular Genetics, the company was an early pioneer in the commercial application of recombinant DNA technology. Amgen initially established its scientific leadership through the development of breakthrough supportive care products for oncology and nephrology, notably Epogen and Neupogen. Over the decades, the company has significantly expanded its therapeutic footprint into diverse fields, including cardiovascular disease, oncology, inflammation, bone health, nephrology, and neuroscience. By leveraging advanced human genetics and cutting-edge protein engineering, Amgen successfully brings complex biologic medicines to global markets to address serious unmet medical needs. Today, it remains one of the world's premier independent biotechnology firms, continually investing heavily in research and development to sustain its robust pipeline of novel therapeutics and biosimilars.
