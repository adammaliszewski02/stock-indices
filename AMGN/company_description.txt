Amgen Inc., trading under the ticker AMGN, is a leading multinational biopharmaceutical company dedicated to discovering, developing, manufacturing, and delivering innovative human therapeutics for severe medical conditions. Founded in Thousand Oaks, California, in 1980 by a venture capital group that included William K. Bowes Jr. and George B. Rathmann, the company initially operated under the name Applied Molecular Genetics before shortening it to Amgen. Recognized as a pioneer in the biotechnology industry, Amgen leveraged recombinant DNA technology to introduce groundbreaking medicines targeting high-unmet-need areas such as oncology, cardiovascular disease, bone health, nephrology, neuroscience, and inflammation. Over the decades, the company has expanded its global footprint through significant research and development investments and strategic acquisitions, such as its recent integration of Horizon Therapeutics. Amgen continues to advance a robust pipeline of biologic and biosimilar medicines, solidifying its position as a cornerstone of the modern life sciences sector while striving to improve patient outcomes worldwide.
