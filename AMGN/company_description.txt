AMGN is the ticker symbol for Amgen Inc., a pioneering multinational biopharmaceutical company headquartered in Thousand Oaks, California. Founded in 1980 as Applied Molecular Genetics, the company has grown to become one of the world's leading independent biotechnology firms. Amgen specializes in discovering, developing, manufacturing, and delivering innovative human therapeutics derived from advances in recombinant DNA and molecular biology. The company’s diverse product portfolio addresses serious illnesses with high unmet medical needs, focusing primarily on oncology, cardiovascular disease, bone health, inflammation, nephrology, and general medicine. Notable historical milestones include the 1989 launch of Epogen and the 1991 introduction of Neupogen, both of which represented breakthroughs in supportive cancer care and established the company's industry leadership. Driven by a commitment to unlocking the potential of biology for patients suffering from serious diseases, Amgen continues to integrate advanced science with state-of-the-art manufacturing to deliver transformative medicines globally.
