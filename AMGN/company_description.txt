Amgen Inc., trading under the ticker AMGN, is a leading global biotechnology company dedicated to discovering, developing, manufacturing, and delivering innovative human therapeutics. Founded in Thousand Oaks, California, in 1980 as Applied Molecular Genetics, the company pioneered the application of recombinant DNA technology in the pharmaceutical industry. Amgen initially achieved significant clinical and commercial success with the introduction of landmark biologics targeting serious illnesses, including Epogen in 1989 and Neupogen in 1991, which transformed supportive care in oncology and nephrology. Over the decades, the company has expanded its therapeutic portfolio to address areas of high unmet medical need such as oncology, cardiovascular disease, inflammation, bone health, nephrology, and rare diseases. Notable modern blockbusters include Enbrel for inflammatory diseases and Prolia for osteoporosis. By integrating advanced human genetics with cutting-edge protein engineering, Amgen continues to advance a robust pipeline of novel biologic and biosimilar medicines designed to treat complex conditions and improve patient outcomes worldwide.
