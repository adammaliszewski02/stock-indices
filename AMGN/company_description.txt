Amgen Inc., trading under the ticker AMGN, is a leading multinational biotechnology company dedicated to discovering, developing, manufacturing, and delivering innovative human therapeutics. Founded in Thousand Oaks, California, in 1980 as Applied Molecular Genetics, the company was an early pioneer in the commercial application of recombinant DNA technology. Over the decades, Amgen has grown into one of the world's premier independent biotechnology firms, focusing primarily on areas of high unmet medical need, including oncology, cardiovascular disease, inflammation, bone health, nephrology, and neuroscience. Its product portfolio features numerous blockbuster biologic drugs that have transformed treatment standards globally. By leveraging advanced cellular and molecular biology, Amgen continues to advance a robust pipeline of targeted therapies designed to combat serious illnesses.
