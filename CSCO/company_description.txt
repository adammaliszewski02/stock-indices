Cisco Systems, Inc. (CSCO) is a global technology leader specializing in networking hardware, software, and telecommunications equipment. Founded in 1984 by Stanford University computer scientists Leonard Bosack and Sandy Lerner, the company gained early prominence by developing the first multi-protocol router, which allowed disparate computer networks to communicate. This innovation proved foundational to the growth of the modern internet.

Today, Cisco’s business encompasses a broad portfolio of enterprise solutions, including routing, switching, cybersecurity, collaboration tools, and cloud-managed services. The company has strategically evolved from its hardware-centric roots into a software-driven organization, focusing heavily on subscription-based models and recurring revenue streams. Through its comprehensive ecosystem, Cisco provides the critical infrastructure that powers data centers, corporate networks, and service providers worldwide. Headquartered in San Jose, California, the company remains a central pillar of the global digital economy, consistently investing in emerging technologies such as artificial intelligence, silicon innovation, and observability to maintain its competitive edge in an increasingly connected landscape.
