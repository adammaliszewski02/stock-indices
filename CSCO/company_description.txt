Cisco Systems, Inc. (CSCO) is a global leader in networking technology, hardware, and software solutions. Founded in 1984 by Stanford University computer scientists Leonard Bosack and Sandy Lerner, the company gained early prominence by developing the first multi-protocol router, which allowed disparate computer networks to communicate. Today, Cisco designs and sells a wide range of products that form the backbone of the internet, including enterprise-grade routers, switches, cybersecurity platforms, and collaboration tools like Webex.

Headquartered in San Jose, California, Cisco has evolved from a pure hardware manufacturer into a diversified technology powerhouse focused on cloud computing, artificial intelligence, and software-defined networking. The company plays a critical role in global digital infrastructure, providing the connectivity and security necessary for businesses, governments, and service providers to operate in a digital-first economy. By integrating advanced analytics and automation into its networking architecture, Cisco continues to facilitate the secure exchange of data across increasingly complex and distributed environments.
