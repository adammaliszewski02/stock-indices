CSCO is the stock ticker for Cisco Systems, Inc., a multinational technology conglomerate headquartered in San Jose, California. Founded in 1984 by Stanford University computer scientists Leonard Bosack and Sandy Lerner, Cisco pioneered the commercial multi-protocol router, a technology that proved foundational to the growth of the modern Internet. The company went public in 1990 and briefly became the world’s most valuable publicly traded company at the height of the dot-com era in 2000.

Today, Cisco is a global leader in networking, telecommunications, and digital infrastructure. Its core business focuses on designing, manufacturing, and selling networking hardware, including enterprise-grade switches, routers, and wireless systems. In recent years, Cisco has strategically evolved beyond traditional hardware to emphasize software, recurring subscription revenues, and services. Key growth areas include comprehensive cybersecurity solutions, cloud infrastructure management, collaboration platforms such as Webex, and artificial intelligence-driven network observability and optimization tools. Cisco serves businesses of all sizes, government agencies, and service providers worldwide.
