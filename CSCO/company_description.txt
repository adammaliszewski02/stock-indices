Cisco Systems, Inc., identified by the stock ticker CSCO, is a worldwide leader in information technology, networking, and cybersecurity solutions. Founded in December 1984 by Stanford University computer scientists Leonard Bosack and Sandra Lerner, the company revolutionized the industry by developing the first commercially viable multi-protocol router, which allowed disparate computer networks to communicate seamlessly. Headquartered in San Jose, California, Cisco designs, manufactures, and sells Internet Protocol-based networking and other products related to the communications and information technology industry. Over the decades, the company has expanded its core enterprise routing and switching business into adjacent markets, including unified communications, data center infrastructure, cloud management, and advanced security software. Through continuous innovation and strategic acquisitions, Cisco empowers businesses of all sizes to securely connect, collaborate, and automate their digital infrastructure on a global scale.
