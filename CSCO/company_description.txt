Cisco Systems, Inc., identified by the stock ticker CSCO, is a worldwide leader in information technology, networking, and cybersecurity solutions. Founded in December 1984 by Stanford University computer scientists Leonard Bosack and Sandy Lerner, the company revolutionized the industry by developing the first commercially viable multi-protocol router, which allowed disparate computer networks to communicate seamlessly. Headquartered in San Jose, California, Cisco designs, manufactures, and sells Internet Protocol-based networking products and other services related to the communications and information technology industry. Over the decades, the corporation has expanded its core routing and switching portfolio through strategic acquisitions into enterprise networking, collaboration tools, cloud security, and software-defined networking. Today, Cisco enables governments, businesses, and educational institutions to securely connect, communicate, and collaborate on a global scale.
