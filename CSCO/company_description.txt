CSCO represents Cisco Systems, Inc., a multinational technology conglomerate headquartered in San Jose, California. Founded in 1984 by Stanford University computer scientists Leonard Bosack and Sandy Lerner, Cisco pioneered the commercially viable multi-protocol router, playing an instrumental role in the infrastructure buildout of the early internet. 

Today, Cisco is a global leader in enterprise networking, cybersecurity, and telecommunications. The company designs, manufactures, and sells Internet Protocol-based networking hardware, including core switches, routers, and wireless access systems. Over the past decade, Cisco has strategically transitioned toward high-margin software and recurring-revenue models. Its expanded portfolio encompasses cloud infrastructure management, enterprise collaboration tools such as Webex, observability software, and advanced threat-defense cybersecurity platforms. Cisco serves a diverse global client base spanning small enterprises, large corporations, public sector institutions, and telecommunication service providers.
