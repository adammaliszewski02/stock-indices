Cisco Systems, Inc., identified by the ticker symbol CSCO, is a worldwide leader in information technology and networking. Founded in December 1984 by Stanford University computer scientists Leonard Bosack and Sandy Lerner, the company revolutionized the industry by developing the first commercially successful multi-protocol router, which enabled disparate computer networks to communicate seamlessly. Headquartered in San Jose, California, Cisco designs, manufactures, and sells Internet Protocol-based networking and other products related to the communications and information technology industry. Over the decades, the company has expanded its core enterprise routing and switching business into a diversified portfolio spanning cybersecurity, collaboration software such as Webex, cloud management, and observability solutions. Through strategic acquisitions and continuous innovation, Cisco supports businesses of all sizes, service providers, governments, and educational institutions in securely connecting digital infrastructure, facilitating remote work, and managing complex data architectures globally.
