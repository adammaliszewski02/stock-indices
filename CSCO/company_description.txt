CSCO is the stock ticker for Cisco Systems, Inc., a multinational technology conglomerate headquartered in San Jose, California. Founded in 1984 by Stanford University computer scientists Leonard Bosack and Sandy Lerner, Cisco pioneered the commercial multi-protocol router, playing a fundamental role in the growth of the early Internet and briefly becoming the world’s most valuable public company during the dot-com era in 2000. 

Today, Cisco is a global leader in digital communications, networking hardware, and enterprise software. Its traditional core business centers on designing and selling Internet Protocol-based networking equipment, including enterprise-grade switches, routers, and wireless systems. Over recent years, the company has strategically shifted toward recurring software and service models, significantly expanding its footprint in cybersecurity, cloud infrastructure, network observability, and collaboration technologies, such as Webex. Through organic innovation and major acquisitions—including its landmark purchase of Splunk—Cisco remains a critical provider of secure networking architectures, serving enterprise, commercial, and telecommunications clients worldwide.
