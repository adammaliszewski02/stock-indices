The ticker TSM identifies Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Company Limited (TSMC), the world’s largest dedicated semiconductor foundry. Founded in 1987 by Morris Chang in Hsinchu, Taiwan, TSMC pioneered the pure-play foundry business model. By focusing exclusively on manufacturing integrated circuits for third-party clients rather than designing its own branded chips, TSMC catalyzed the rise of the global "fabless" semiconductor industry. 

Today, the company is a critical linchpin of the global technology supply chain, providing advanced manufacturing capabilities to major technology firms such as Apple, NVIDIA, and AMD. TSMC leads the industry in manufacturing cutting-edge microchips using advanced process nodes, including 5-nanometer and 3-nanometer technologies. Its manufactured semiconductors power a vast array of modern applications, including consumer electronics, artificial intelligence accelerators, high-performance computing systems, automotive technologies, and telecommunications infrastructure. Headquartered in Taiwan, TSMC has expanded its global footprint with advanced fabrication facilities under development in the United States, Japan, and Europe.
