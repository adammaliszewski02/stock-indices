The ticker TSM identifies Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Company Limited (TSMC), the world’s largest dedicated pure-play semiconductor foundry. Founded in 1987 by Morris Chang and headquartered in Hsinchu, Taiwan, TSMC revolutionized the tech industry by pioneering a business model focused exclusively on manufacturing chips designed by third parties rather than competing with its customers. 

This model enabled the rapid rise of fabless semiconductor firms, positioning TSMC as the critical production partner for major technology companies, including Apple, Nvidia, AMD, and Qualcomm. Today, TSMC operates advanced fabrication facilities worldwide, producing integrated circuits that power artificial intelligence applications, smartphones, high-performance computing, and automotive electronics. 

By consistently leading the commercialization of cutting-edge manufacturing processes—such as 7-nanometer, 5-nanometer, and 3-nanometer technologies—TSMC commands a dominant market share in advanced logic chips, making it a linchpin of the global technology supply chain and modern digital infrastructure.
