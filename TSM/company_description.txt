Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Company Limited, commonly known as TSM, is the world's largest dedicated independent semiconductor foundry. Founded in 1987 by Morris Chang, the company pioneered the pure-play foundry business model, meaning it manufactures integrated circuits based on designs provided by its customers rather than designing its own branded chips. Headquartered in Hsinchu Science Park, Taiwan, TSM has grown to become a cornerstone of the global technology supply chain. It mass-produces advanced microchips for a vast array of industry leaders, powering smartphones, high-performance computing systems, automotive electronics, and artificial intelligence applications. Renowned for its technological leadership in ultra-fine lithography processes, TSM continues to drive innovation in semiconductor manufacturing, operating state-of-the-art fabrication facilities across Taiwan, with expanding international operations in the United States, Japan, and Europe to meet global demand.
