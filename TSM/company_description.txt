Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Company Limited, commonly known as TSM, is the world's largest dedicated independent semiconductor foundry. Founded in 1987 by Morris Chang, the company pioneered the pure-play foundry business model, meaning it manufactures integrated circuits based on designs provided by its customers without competing against them with proprietary products. Headquartered in Hsinchu Science Park, Taiwan, TSM operates cutting-edge fabrication facilities that produce advanced microchips for a vast array of global technology leaders, encompassing applications in high-performance computing, smartphones, the Internet of Things, and automotive electronics. TSM is recognized globally for its technological leadership in advanced manufacturing nodes, playing a critical and foundational role in the global electronics supply chain.
