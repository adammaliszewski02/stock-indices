Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Company Limited, known globally as TSMC and trading under the ticker TSM, is the world’s largest dedicated semiconductor foundry. Founded in 1987 by Morris Chang in Taiwan, TSMC pioneered the pure-play foundry business model, choosing to exclusively manufacture integrated circuits for other enterprises rather than designing and marketing its own branded chips. 

Today, the company serves as the primary manufacturing backbone for leading global technology firms, including Apple, Nvidia, AMD, and Qualcomm. TSMC commands a dominant share of the worldwide foundry market, standing at the forefront of advanced process technologies with its production of cutting-edge 3-nanometer and 5-nanometer nodes. Its manufactured semiconductors power a vast array of global applications, ranging from smartphones and consumer electronics to artificial intelligence accelerators, high-performance computing systems, and automotive platforms, establishing the company as an indispensable linchpin of the global technology supply chain.
