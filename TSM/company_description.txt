Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Company Limited, commonly known as TSM, is the world's largest dedicated independent semiconductor foundry. Founded in 1987 by Morris Chang, the company pioneered the pure-play foundry business model, meaning it manufactures integrated circuits for other companies without designing its own branded products. Operating primarily from Taiwan, TSM produces advanced logic silicon used across high-performance computing, mobile devices, automotive applications, and the Internet of Things. Serving a vast portfolio of global fabless technology leaders, the company has driven much of the modern semiconductor industry's manufacturing innovation, particularly through continuous scaling and leadership in extreme ultraviolet lithography nodes.
