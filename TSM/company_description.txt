Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Company Limited, commonly known as TSMC and traded under the ticker TSM, is the world's largest dedicated independent semiconductor foundry. Founded in 1987 by Morris Chang, the company pioneered the pure-play foundry business model, meaning it manufactures integrated circuits for other companies without designing its own branded products. Headquartered in Hsinchu Science Park, Taiwan, TSMC operates advanced fabrication facilities that produce chips for a vast array of global technology leaders, powering applications ranging from smartphones and high-performance computing to automotive systems and artificial intelligence. Over the decades, the company has maintained a technological edge through relentless capital investment and research, becoming a critical pillar of the global semiconductor supply chain and manufacturing the vast majority of the world's most advanced microchips.
