Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Company Limited, commonly known as TSM, is the world's largest dedicated independent semiconductor foundry. Founded in 1987 by Morris Chang, the company pioneered the pure-play foundry business model, meaning it manufactures semiconductor chips designed by other companies rather than designing its own products. Headquartered in Hsinchu Science Park, Taiwan, TSM has grown to become a cornerstone of the global technology supply chain. It manufactures advanced integrated circuits utilized across diverse sectors, including high-performance computing, mobile devices, automotive electronics, and the Internet of Things. TSM is globally renowned for its leadership in cutting-edge semiconductor manufacturing process technologies, producing chips for major technology innovators such as Apple, NVIDIA, and AMD.
