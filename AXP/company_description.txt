American Express Company, trading under the ticker AXP, is a globally integrated payments company that provides customers with access to products, insights, and experiences that enrich lives and build business success. Founded in 1850 as an express mail transportation business in Buffalo, New York, the company evolved through the late 19th century into a pioneer of financial services, introducing its iconic paper money orders in 1882 and traveler's cheques in 1891. American Express subsequently revolutionized commerce by launching its flagship charge card in 1958. Today, the company operates primarily through its proprietary closed-loop payment network, offering a comprehensive suite of products including credit and charge cards, digital payment solutions, traveler's checks, and travel services. It serves a diverse global customer base ranging from individual consumers and small businesses to large corporations. Additionally, American Express provides merchant acquisition and processing services, network services, and various banking and insurance products, generating revenue primarily from discount fees paid by merchants, cardmember fees, and interest income on revolving credit balances.
