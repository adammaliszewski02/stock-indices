American Express Company (AXP) is a globally integrated payments company that provides customers with access to products, insights, and experiences that enrich lives and build business success. Operating primarily through its eponymous brand, the company is a world leader in credit and charge cards, travel services, and digital payment solutions for consumers, small businesses, and large corporations. Founded in 1850 in Buffalo, New York, as a joint stock association operating an express mail business, American Express originally specialized in freight forwarding. Over the subsequent decades and well into the twentieth century, the company gradually transitioned its core operations away from express shipping toward financial services and travel-related products. This strategic evolution led to the launch of its iconic paper traveler's cheque in 1891 and, crucially, the issuance of its flagship charge card in 1958. Today, American Express generates revenue primarily through discount revenue from merchant processing, cardmember fees, and net interest income from its lending portfolios, distinguishing itself in the financial sector through a closed-loop network model that directly connects buyers and sellers.
