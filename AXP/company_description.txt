American Express Company, trading under the ticker AXP, is a globally integrated payments company that provides customers with access to products, insights, and experiences that enrich lives and build business success. Founded in 1850 as an express mail transportation business in Buffalo, New York, the company evolved through the late nineteenth century to pioneer financial services, introducing its signature Money Order in 1882, the Travelers Cheque in 1891, and ultimately launching its iconic credit card in 1958. Today, AXP operates primarily as a payment network and issuer, offering credit cards, charge cards, banking, and travel services to consumers, small businesses, and large corporations worldwide. The company generates revenue principally from discount revenue paid by merchants, cardmember fees, and interest income on revolving balances. By combining a closed-loop network model with premium customer service, American Express has established itself as one of the world's most recognized financial brands, facilitating millions of transactions globally while maintaining a strong emphasis on affluent consumer segments and corporate expense management.
