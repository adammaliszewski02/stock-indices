American Express Company, trading under the ticker symbol AXP, is a globally integrated payments company that provides customers with access to products, insights, and experiences that enrich lives and build business success. Founded in 1850 as an express mail transportation business in Buffalo, New York, the company evolved through the late 19th and 20th centuries to become a pioneer in financial services, introducing its iconic paper traveler's cheque in 1891 and its first charge card in 1958. Today, AXP primarily engages in the issuance of credit and charge cards, the operation of a worldwide payment network, and the provision of travelers' cheques, corporate travel services, and digital banking solutions. Operating through segments including Global Consumer Services Group, Global Commercial Services, Global Merchant and Network Services, and Credit and Fraud Risk, the enterprise connects millions of merchants and consumers worldwide, maintaining a reputation for premium customer service, robust rewards programs, and secure transaction processing.
