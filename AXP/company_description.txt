American Express Company, trading under the ticker AXP, is a globally integrated payments company that provides customers with access to products, insights, and experiences that enrich lives and build business success. Founded in 1850 as an express mail transportation company in Buffalo, New York, the firm evolved through the late nineteenth and twentieth centuries to become a leader in financial services, introducing its iconic paper traveler's cheque in 1891 and issuing its first charge card in 1958. Today, American Express primarily operates through its credit card, charge card, and traveler's cheque businesses, connecting consumers and merchants worldwide through a proprietary payments network. The company generates revenue principally from discount revenue, cardmember fees, and net interest income derived from its lending products. Headquartered in New York City, American Express is renowned for its premium customer service, loyalty programs, and strong brand equity, maintaining a prominent position among the world's largest financial institutions.
