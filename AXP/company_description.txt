American Express Company (AXP) is a globally integrated payments company that provides customers with access to products, insights, and experiences that enrich lives and build business success. Operating primarily through its network, credit card, and travel businesses, the company is a world leader in charge and credit payment cards for consumers, small businesses, and large corporations. Founded in 1850 in Buffalo, New York, as an express mail transportation business by the merger of Henry Wells, William G. Fargo, and John Warren Butterfield, the enterprise eventually transitioned into financial services. It introduced its signature paper money orders in 1882, followed by travelers cheques in 1891, and issued its first plastic credit card in 1958. Today, American Express generates revenue principally from discount revenue derived from merchant fees, cardmember services fees, and net interest income on its lending portfolio. Headquartered in New York City, the company is renowned for its premium customer service, proprietary closed-loop network model, and iconic brand value.
