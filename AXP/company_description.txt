American Express Company, trading under the ticker AXP, is a globally integrated payments company that provides customers with access to products, insights, and experiences that enrich lives and build business success. Founded in 1850 as an express mail transportation business in Buffalo, New York, the company evolved through the late nineteenth and twentieth centuries to become a pioneer in financial services, introducing its iconic paper traveler's cheque in 1891 and issuing its first charge card in 1958. Today, AXP primarily engages in the issuance of credit and charge cards, the operation of a worldwide payment network, and the provision of travel-related services for consumers, small businesses, and large corporations. The firm differentiates itself through a closed-loop network model that directly connects buyers and sellers, enabling robust data analytics, premium customer loyalty programs, and high merchant acceptance. Headquartered in New York City, American Express remains one of the world's most recognized financial brands, driven by a dual strategy of expanding its digital capabilities while maintaining its traditional focus on premium lifestyle and travel services.
