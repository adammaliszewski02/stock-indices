American Express Company, trading under the ticker AXP, is a globally integrated payments company that provides customers with access to products, insights, and experiences that enrich lives and build business success. Founded in 1850 as an express mail transportation business in Buffalo, New York, the company later revolutionized commerce by introducing financial services, most notably its pioneering paper money orders, travelers cheques, and subsequently, its iconic charge cards in 1958. Today, AXP operates primarily as a digital payments network and banking institution, offering a broad range of credit and charge cards, travel services, merchant acquisition and processing, network services, and digital banking products to consumers, small businesses, and large corporations worldwide.
